# OpenAI、Proプラン新規登録を一時停止 -- Astraモデルへの需要がインフラに過度な負荷

OpenAI announced on September 9 (US time) that it is temporarily pausing new sign-ups for its $200-per-month Pro plan. Demand for its newest and most powerful model, Astra, has placed excessive strain on the company's infrastructure, forcing the temporary restriction.

## Tibo Sottiaux Announces on X

OpenAI's Head of Product, Tibo Sottiaux -- who leads core products including ChatGPT and Codex -- explained the decision on X: "Pro subscriptions put the most strain on our systems, which is why sign-ups for this service tier are now being disabled." He added, "We wanted to take the smallest step that allows us to continue giving the broadest access possible."

OpenAI had warned on September 8 that such a change might be coming, with Sottiaux noting at the time that "demand for Astra is really unprecedented."

## Other Plans Remain Available

The pause applies only to the Pro plan. Other tiers -- including the API plan, $20-per-month Plus, and $20-per-month Go plans -- remain fully operational and available for new sign-ups. The Pro plan previously offered unlimited access to OpenAI's most powerful models.

The Pro subscription pause reflects the real-world cost of running frontier AI models at scale. Astra, OpenAI's latest flagship model, requires significant compute resources, and demand has consistently outpaced capacity since its launch. The company is working to add more capacity before re-opening Pro plan sign-ups.

## Compute Constraints Hit Consumer Product

This marks one of the first times AI model demand has directly forced a product tier suspension. OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar had warned on the last quarterly earnings call that GPU scarcity was constraining the company's ability to scale. The Pro plan pause represents the first visible consumer-facing impact of those compute constraints, signaling that even well-capitalized AI labs face real infrastructure limits as they race to deploy increasingly capable models.
