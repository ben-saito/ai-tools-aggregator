# OpenAI Anthropic Google Lead 100+ Companies in Coalition Against Rogue AI

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and over 100 other major tech companies and AI startups have signed a joint declaration on AI security. The initiative calls for coordinated measures to defend against malicious use of AI systems.

---

## Joint Declaration Details

The participating companies condemn the current state of cybersecurity and describe a new coordinated solution they are developing to ward off threats from a new generation of powerful AI systems. The statement notes that AI-powered cyberattacks are growing more sophisticated, requiring industry-wide cooperation.

---

## Why Companies Are Uniting

AI-powered cyberattacks have surged over the past year, with threat actors ranging from state-sponsored hackers to individual attackers leveraging AI tools. By cooperating beyond competitive boundaries, companies aim to restore the defensive advantage against these evolving threats.
