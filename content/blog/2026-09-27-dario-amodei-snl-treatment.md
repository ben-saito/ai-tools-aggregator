# Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei Gets the SNL Treatment

---

## Saturday Night Live Pokes Fun at AI Industry's Doom-Warning Tour

Saturday Night Live took on the AI industry's recent warnings of doom last night, as cast member Jane Wickline offered her impression of Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei during the Weekend Update segment.

The sketch opened with host Michael Che introducing the segment with visible uncertainty about how to pronounce Amodei's last name, describing the CEO as having "stumbled through a press tour" where he and other AI lab leaders warned about existential risks from frontier AI systems.

---

## The Impression: Halting Answers and Dark Side Confessions

Wickline's version of Amodei sported an impressive wig and delivered halting answers that occasionally devolved into full-on Gollum-style exchanges with their dark side. At one point, the fictional Amodei confessed: "AI is the devil and I its maker."

The impression leaned heavily into the tension between AI executives publicly warning about catastrophic risks while simultaneously pushing the technology forward. Wickline-as-Amodei assured audiences that AI executives "are all on the same page here: We do not condone what we are doing."

---

## Curing Cancer in 10 Years: 10% Chance

As for the technology's supposed benefits, like potentially curing cancer, the fictional Amodei offered a notably conservative timeline: "Put it this way: In 10 years, there's about a 10% chance that cancer won't be a problem for anyone."

The line reflects a subtle jab at the grandiose claims that often accompany AI product announcements, where transformative benefits are promised on timescales that are difficult to verify or hold accountable.

---

## Cultural Commentary on AI Safety discourse

The sketch arrives amid intensified public debate about AI risk. Amodei and other frontier AI lab leaders including OpenAI's Sam Altman and DeepMind's Demis Hassabis have been on a coordinated tour warning about potential catastrophic outcomes from advanced AI systems. The SNL treatment highlights how that discourse has entered mainstream cultural conversation beyond tech circles.

The comedy's particular target was the apparent contradiction between warning about AI risks while continuing to build increasingly powerful systems -- a tension that even Amodei himself has acknowledged in essays and interviews.

---

## Broader Context: AI Goes Mainstream in Entertainment

The SNL segment is the latest sign that AI has become a fixture in entertainment satire. From cold opens to musical parodies, late-night comedy has increasingly engaged with technology themes as they affect everyday life. The AI-specific treatment, however, marks a new level of engagement with the technical safety discourse that has mostly remained within policy and research circles.
