# Anthropic Updates Usage Policy to Ban Model Abuse and Election Interference

---

Anthropic has updated its usage policy to explicitly prohibit using Claude for model abuse — including training competing models on Claude outputs — and for interfering with elections. The update comes as policymakers push AI companies to take stronger steps against misuse. Anthropic says the policy will be enforced through automated monitoring and that violations could result in API key revocation.
