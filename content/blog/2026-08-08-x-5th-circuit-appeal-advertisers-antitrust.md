# X Asks 5th Circuit to Revive Antitrust Lawsuit Against Advertisers

X Corp. filed an appeal on Tuesday asking the US Court of Appeals for the 5th Circuit to reverse a lower court's dismissal of its antitrust lawsuit against a group of major advertisers. The case centers on the Global Alliance for Responsible Media (GARM), an industry initiative that defined brand-safety standards — and which X claims coordinated a boycott that suppressed ad revenue.

The case was thrown out in March by US District Judge Jane Boyle, who ruled that advertisers did not commit any antitrust violation. Last week, X reached a settlement with the advertiser group that had originally been named as defendants. But the underlying case against the remaining defendants — including Mars, CVS Health, Nestle, Abbott Laboratories, Colgate-Palmolive, Lego, Pinterest, Tyson Foods, Shell, and \u00d8rsted A/S — continues.

---

## X's Core Argument: Coordinated Boycott

"This case involves an unusually brazen group boycott," X said in its filing. "That misconduct has drawn the attention of regulators and Congress. There is no valid reason that this effort by the advertisers succeeded in causing X significant injury."

X's legal theory hinges on the argument that GARM exercised collective power through its membership rules, and that conditioning advertising contracts on compliance with GARM standards constituted illegal coordination under antitrust law.

The boycott "injured X and competition in that market, allowing other social-media platforms to charge rates above truly competitive pricing," the company said. X argued that Boyle's ruling misinterpreted the harm as merely the collective choice of customers to use competitors — a characterization X disputes.

## SpaceX Discloses X Ad Revenue: Down Year-Over-Year

The timing of the appeal is notable given new financial data. SpaceX's earnings report this week disclosed that X generated $367 million in advertising revenue in Q2 2026, down from $426 million in Q2 2025. For the first half of 2026, ad revenue totaled $710 million.

Those figures are a fraction of what Twitter reported before Elon Musk acquired the platform in October 2022. The company has struggled to retain brand advertisers who departed in the months following the acquisition, citing concerns about content moderation changes.

---

## The Settlement and What Remains

X reached a settlement with the Global Alliance for Responsible Media and several major advertisers in the original lawsuit — a deal that effectively ended the coordinated group defendant. But the settlement did not include the individual companies listed above, whose continued participation in the coordinated GARM framework X claims constitutes the underlying antitrust violation.

Boyle's dismissal cited the principle that harm resulting from competitors winning business is not an antitrust injury. X's appeal argues that GARM's structural role in setting market terms for all participants takes the case outside that precedent.

The 5th Circuit's decision will be closely watched by platform companies and advertisers alike, as it may define the boundaries of coordinated brand-safety initiatives in digital advertising markets.
