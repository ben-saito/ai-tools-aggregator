# AI Safety Conversations Have Gotten Unbelievable

This week, two separate conversations about AI safety went viral, highlighting just how difficult it has become to distinguish AI fact from fiction in public discourse. The incidents underscore the widening gap between the technical realities of AI capabilities and the narratives that circulate about them.

---

## Summary

TechCrunch reported on September 19, 2026, that two distinct AI safety conversations captured public attention simultaneously—one involving a claim about AI behavior that proved exaggerated or fabricated, and another where a genuine safety concern was met with disbelief. The dual phenomenon illustrates the information environment surrounding AI safety: both false alarms and genuine risks face similar obstacles to credibility.

The viral spread of both conversations—and the subsequent debates about their validity—demonstrates that the AI safety discourse has reached a point where audience skepticism is nearly automatic, regardless of whether the underlying claim is accurate or not.

---

## The Verification Problem

Separating credible AI safety reports from misinformation has become increasingly difficult as AI capabilities become more widely discussed. Several factors contribute to this:

First, the pace of AI development means that capabilities once considered hypothetical can become real within months, making skeptical responses to safety claims seem reasonable in retrospect. Second, companies have competing incentives to both promote their models' capabilities and manage safety perceptions, creating ambiguity around public disclosures.

Third, the technical complexity of modern AI systems makes independent verification by non-experts essentially impossible, leaving journalists, regulators, and the public dependent on company disclosures—which may be incomplete or self-serving.
