# Figma Debuts Markdown-Based Design Specs to Guide AI Agents at Config India

---

Design platform Figma announced a new Markdown-based editor called **Figma Specs** at its first Config event in India, aiming to provide a definition that both human teams and AI agents can follow when working within a brand's design system. The feature lets users define design language in a simple text format, serving as a centralized reference for design libraries, code libraries, and guidelines. Agents operating within Figma can consult this document to adhere to brand rules while creating with AI.

Figma Specs functions as a standalone file in Figma or can coexist within the canvas. Apart from acting as a text editor, users can reference other Figma elements, including elements on the canvas, embed FigJam diagrams, code layers, and designs. Teams can bring in more context about design, including design libraries, code libraries, and guidelines to manage context for the design system.

In the past week, the company made its agent on canvas available to a wider user base. However, given the non-deterministic nature of AI, the company is providing tools to steer it towards more predictable outcomes. Figma also made its code layers feature, which adds coding to designs on canvas for more interactive prototypes. The company announced this feature in private beta back in June and is now making the function available in public beta.

"We see Figma as the place where software teams come to decide what they build and ship. And so for that, I think one of the key things to keep in mind about what AI has changed and what it made available is the ability to build," Figma's chief design officer Loredana Crisan told TechCrunch. "There are 17-times more lines of code being produced today than in the past. But when you're looking at releases, only 1.3-times more releases. A lot of that code actually is not necessarily built to ship, but really built to discover what the product is. It's built to design."

The company is also supporting over 20 new tools with its Model Context Protocol (MCP), including Open Code, Dia, Grok Bot, and Indian AI startup Sarvam. Last year, Figma acquired open-source headless CMS Payload. Now, the company is integrating it with its Figma Make product for generating dynamic websites and directly managing content. The company said that Payload's downloads have increased sixfold in the last 12 months.

Figma opened its first office in India late last year with sales, partnership, go-to-market, and product support functions. The country is its biggest market in terms of users outside the United States, with the platform working with 40% of India's top 100 companies listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange.
