# ElevenLabs voice AI doubles valuation to $22B in $300M tender

ElevenLabs, the AI voice synthesis company, has doubled its valuation to $22 billion following a $300 million employee tender round co-led by Wellington Management and T. Rowe Price, according to TechCrunch reporting.

---

## The Tender and Valuation

The $300 million secondary offering allowed employees and early investors to sell shares at a valuation that reflects the company's rapid growth in the text-to-speech and voice cloning market. The round values ElevenLabs at approximately $22 billion, up from the $11 billion valuation the company achieved in early 2026.

The strong demand for shares reflects investor confidence in ElevenLabs' position as a leading provider of AI audio infrastructure. The company's voice synthesis technology is used across a range of applications, from content creation to accessibility tools to conversational AI.

---

## Voice AI Market Dynamics

ElevenLabs competes with established players including OpenAI, Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, and Amazon Polly, as well as newer entrants in the voice AI space. The company's differentiation has centered on the naturalness of its output and the granularity of control it offers over voice characteristics.

The market for AI voice synthesis has grown rapidly as companies seek to add voice interfaces to products. Demand has been particularly strong in gaming, entertainment, and accessibility applications, where high-quality synthetic voice can replace traditional voice acting or narration.

---

## What the Valuation Means

A $22 billion valuation positions ElevenLabs among the highest-valued private AI companies globally. The valuation reflects both the scale of the addressable market for voice AI and the company's ability to capture a significant share of it. The tender structure -- allowing existing shareholders to sell without a new capital raise for the company -- suggests the primary goal was liquidity rather than fresh capital for expansion.
