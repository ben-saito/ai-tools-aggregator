# TypeSafe AI's Jev Valued at $7.5B Weeks After Launch

---

## A New Kind of AI Model Takes Off

TypeSafe AI launched Jev just three weeks ago, and already the company has closed a $870 million funding round at a $7.5 billion valuation. Andreessen Horowitz led the round, with participation from Sequoia and existing investor DCVC. The speed of the raise is remarkable even by AI industry standards.

Jev is not a large language model. Unlike LLMs that output text, Jev produces calibrated probabilities — what TypeSafe calls "calibrated decisions." The model is optimized for automation tasks rather than generation, and the company claims it runs significantly faster while consuming far fewer tokens than traditional LLMs.

"We have been super good at human language for four years, but it's not useful for automation because computers speak a different language," co-founder Diogo Almeida told TechCrunch.

## Enterprise Adoption Outpaces Typical B2B Rollouts

TypeSafe claims a third of Fortune 500 companies are already using Jev, an unusually fast enterprise adoption curve. Almeida previously worked as a researcher at OpenAI. He co-founded TypeSafe in 2024 alongside Sasha Sheng, a former Meta research engineer, and Erik Gafni.

The company's positioning — calibrated decision-making for automation rather than text generation — appears to be landing with enterprises looking to deploy AI in high-volume operational workflows. Rather than generating human-readable output, Jev appears designed to make rapid, statistically grounded choices in machine-to-machine contexts.
