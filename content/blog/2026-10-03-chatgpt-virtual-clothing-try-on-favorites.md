# ChatGPT Adds Virtual Clothing Try-On and Favorites Shopping Features

OpenAI has introduced virtual try-on and saved favorites features to ChatGPT's shopping capabilities, using the company's newly launched Images 2.5 model to generate realistic renderings of how clothing and accessories look on individual users. The features position ChatGPT more directly against Pinterest and Google in the fashion discovery and product search market.

---

## How Virtual Try-On Works

Users upload a selfie or full-body photo to ChatGPT, which then generates images showing how specific clothing items look on their actual body. The Try On button appears in ChatGPT's shopping results, and the system uses Images 2.5 to handle lighting, fabric drape, and realistic texture rendering.

OpenAI claims Images 2.5 produces more natural lighting and richer textures than previous models, follows editing instructions more reliably, and reduces image generation latency. The feature is rolling out to ChatGPT users with shopping features enabled.

The Favorites feature lets users save products and try-on images to a library within the ChatGPT app, creating a persistent shopping wishlist that combines visual discovery with AI-generated personalization.

---

## Competing for the Fashion Discovery Market

The move puts ChatGPT in direct competition with Pinterest's visual search capabilities and Google's virtual try-on features, which launched last year. Both incumbents have significant advantages in fashion discovery: users already go to Pinterest for style inspiration, and Google's shopping graph benefits from vast e-commerce data.

OpenAI's edge is the conversational context — users can describe a style they want, ask ChatGPT to shop for components, or upload photos of celebrity outfits and request similar items available for purchase. This conversational shopping layer is novel, though it remains to be seen whether users will adopt ChatGPT as a primary shopping destination.
