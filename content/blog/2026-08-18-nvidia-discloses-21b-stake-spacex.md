# Nvidia Discloses $21B Stake in SpaceX After SpaceX IPO

---

## SEC Filing Reveals Stake

Nvidia disclosed in an SEC filing on Friday that it owned SpaceX stock worth nearly $21 billion at the end of June 2026. The stake has declined in value to approximately $17 billion following SpaceX's sharp stock decline since its June IPO.

The disclosure marks a significant payoff from Nvidia's $500 million investment in xAI completed in January 2026, shortly before Musk combined the AI lab with SpaceX.

---

## Jensen Huang's Strategy

The disclosure underscores Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's strategy of using the financial power of the world's most valuable company ($5.5 trillion market cap) to forge complex financial links across the AI industry - including with some of Nvidia's largest customers.

Musk, during SpaceX's first public earnings call, stated the company has an exclusive relationship with Nvidia: "We've decided to build exclusively on Nvidia because we think its Vera Rubin architecture is the best architecture. We think it's the best AI computer and we greatly value our close cooperation and partnership on many levels with Nvidia."

---

## Wider Investments

Nvidia has committed more than $100 billion to AI companies in the past two years, with investments in cloud computing startups such as CoreWeave, AI labs including Thinking Machines and Safe Superintelligence, and code editor Cursor which SpaceX acquired this week.
