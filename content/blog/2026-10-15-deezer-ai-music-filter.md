# Deezer Building AI Music Filter to Let Users Block Generated Content

---

Streaming platform Deezer remains steadfast in its intent to help users avoid listening to AI-generated music: the company on Tuesday said it is working on a new toggle that lets users filter out AI-generated music uploaded to its platform. The feature is expected to be available before the end of the year.

Deezer's approach differs from YouTube Music's disclosure labels — rather than labeling content, Deezer wants to give users an active choice to exclude AI-generated tracks from their listening experience entirely.

---

## The Filtering Challenge

Detecting AI-generated music is technically non-trivial. Unlike visual content where AI provenance can be watermarked at generation time, music AI systems often produce outputs that don't carry obvious fingerprints. Deezer's engineering team has been developing detection models that analyze audio characteristics for patterns associated with AI synthesis.

The filter toggle will apply to new uploads as well as retroactive scanning of existing catalogs. Users who enable the filter won't see AI-generated tracks in recommendations, search results, or playlists.

---

## Competing Approaches to AI Transparency

Deezer's filter-based approach reflects a broader divergence in how platforms handle AI content disclosure. YouTube Music has opted for labels that inform without restricting, while Deezer is betting that some listeners will prefer an opt-out model. Both approaches acknowledge that AI music is becoming a permanent feature of streaming catalogs.

The streaming giant's announcement positions Deezer as an advocate for listener autonomy in an era when the boundary between human and AI-made music grows increasingly blurry.
