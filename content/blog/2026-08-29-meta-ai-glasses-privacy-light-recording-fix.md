# Meta Updates AI Smart Glasses to Block Recording When Privacy Light Is Covered

Meta has begun rolling out a software update for its AI smart glasses that prevents the camera from functioning when the LED indicator light is covered during recording, closing a loophole that allowed users to record without alerting bystanders.

---

## The Privacy Loophole

Meta's smart glasses include an LED light that is supposed to alert people nearby when the device is capturing photos or video. However, users discovered that covering the light after starting a recording would not trigger the expected shutdown — the LED shutdown only applied when a user attempted to start recording with the light already covered.

This meant users could record continuously while obscuring the indicator light by simply reversing the order of their actions: start recording first, then place a sticker or finger over the light.

The company faced criticism over this design, with privacy advocates arguing it made surreptitious recording too easy.

---

## The Fix

In a post on Threads, Meta's executive vice president of wearables, Alex Himel, announced that an update would ensure "the camera will now stop working if the light is covered during a recording." This ensures the capture LED can reliably alert bystanders when photos or videos are being captured, regardless of the sequence of actions taken.

The update represents Meta's effort to balance the practical functionality of its smart glasses with growing concerns about privacy in an era of ubiquitous recording devices. The glasses remain one of the company's key hardware platforms for ambient computing and AI assistant integration.

---

## Ongoing Privacy Debates

While the update addresses the specific loophole identified, privacy researchers note that other concerns about AI smart glasses remain, including the broader implications of always-on camera devices in public spaces and the potential for AI systems to process faces and other personal data in real time.

The update is being deployed to existing Meta smart glasses owners through a software update.
