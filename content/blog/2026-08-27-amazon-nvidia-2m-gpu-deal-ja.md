# Amazon Triples Nvidia GPU Order to 2 Million Chips Amid Surging AI Demand

Amazon and Nvidia announced a major expansion of their partnership on August 26, 2026. Amazon will add 2 million Nvidia GPU chips to its data centers over the next two years, marking the largest single-customer order in Nvidia's history.

---

## From 1 Million to 2 Million in Five Months

In March 2026, Amazon had committed to deploying over 1 million Nvidia GPUs across AWS infrastructure by the end of that year. Demand for AI model training, however, far exceeded projections, and the order tripled within just five months.

The new chips include Nvidia Blackwell Ultra, Rubin, and Rubin Ultra GPUs, designed to handle the heavy compute demands of training and running AI models.

---

## Beyond Chip Procurement

The announcement, made during Nvidia's quarterly earnings call, extends well beyond purchasing more chips. Nvidia's networking hardware—which connects thousands of GPUs into unified systems—along with CPUs, data processing software, and robotics products are all part of the expanded agreement.

Both companies cited "surging demand from startups, enterprises, AI labs, and even governments" as the driver behind the deeper collaboration.

---

## Amazon's AI Chip Strategy

While expanding its Nvidia partnership, Amazon continues investing in proprietary AI chips. AWS has been ramping up its general-purpose CPU capabilities, building a complementary stack alongside Nvidia GPUs.

Neither company disclosed financial terms, but given GPU unit costs, the deal is estimated to be worth tens of billions of dollars.

---

## Market Implications

The announcement underscores how AI compute infrastructure has become a core competitive investment for major cloud providers and AI labs alike.
