# Nvidia Reportedly Acquires Hugging Face for $12.9 Billion

Nvidia has agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, The Information reported Wednesday night, citing a source familiar with the matter. The deal would unite the dominant force in AI accelerator hardware with the leading open-source AI platform, reshaping the competitive landscape of the AI industry.

---

## A Strategic Move to Protect and Expand Nvidia's AI Empire

The acquisition would allow Nvidia to both protect its chip empire and re-enter the cloud business. Hugging Face, founded in 2016, has become the de facto standard platform for sharing and deploying open-source machine learning models, hosting over 1 million models and thousands of datasets.

For Nvidia, Hugging Face represents both a defensive play and an offensive one. By owning the dominant model hub, Nvidia can ensure continued demand for its GPUs while gaining direct access to the growing ecosystem of AI developers who build on the platform.

---

## Implications for the Open-Source AI Ecosystem

Hugging Face has long positioned itself as a neutral player, supporting models from all vendors. The acquisition raises questions about whether the platform will maintain its vendor-agnostic approach or become more tightly integrated with Nvidia's ecosystem.

Industry observers note that the deal follows a pattern of vertical integration in AI, where hardware vendors seek to control the full stack from chips to deployment platforms.
