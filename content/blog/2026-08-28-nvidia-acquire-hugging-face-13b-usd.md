# Nvidia Reportedly Acquiring Hugging Face for $12.9 Billion

Nvidia is acquiring Hugging Face, the popular AI model repository and community platform, for approximately $12.9 billion, according to a report from The Information confirmed by CNBC. The deal would give Nvidia dominant influence over the open-weight AI model ecosystem.

---

## Background: Hugging Face as AI Infrastructure

Hugging Face operates as a cloud repository for AI models, functioning similarly to how GitHub serves software developers. Researchers and developers search for models matching specific criteria, download and run them, fine-tune them into variants, and upload those variants back to the platform. It has become the de facto standard for storing, downloading, and working with open-weight AI models.

Founded in 2016, Hugging Face has attracted investment from Google, Microsoft, and Nvidia itself. The company is reportedly not yet profitable, but its strategic position as the default hub for open-weight models makes it highly valuable regardless of its current bottom line.

---

## Why Nvidia Wants Hugging Face

Nvidia has publicly supported open-weight models as alternatives to proprietary frontier models from OpenAI and Anthropic. The company has even released its own family of open models called Nemotron. Acquiring Hugging Face would give Nvidia significant influence over the open-weight AI ecosystem and help revive its previously stalled cloud AI business plans.

The acquisition is particularly important as frontier labs including OpenAI and Anthropic invest in their own specialized hardware. Owning the primary model distribution platform would give Nvidia additional leverage in shaping the AI industry's hardware trajectory.

---

## Deal Status and Reactions

The acquisition is reportedly not finalized and could still face obstacles, but both parties appear to be actively pursuing the deal. Salesforce was also reportedly interested in acquiring Hugging Face.

Industry analysts have noted that the deal would face regulatory scrutiny given Nvidia's dominant position in AI training chips and Hugging Face's central role in the open AI ecosystem.

---

## Strategic Implications

If completed, the acquisition would represent one of the largest AI infrastructure deals in history. It would give Nvidia control over the platform where millions of developers download and share AI models, potentially strengthening Nvidia's position against competing chipmakers AMD and custom silicon from Google and Amazon.

The deal also raises questions about Hugging Face's independence as a platform for the broader AI research community, which has valued its vendor-neutral positioning.
