# Calendly Enters Meeting Note-Taker Market with AI Assistant "Callie"

Calendly, the popular scheduling platform known for eliminating the back-and-forth of meeting coordination, is expanding into the meeting productivity space with "Callie," an AI-powered meeting note-taking assistant. The move places Calendly in direct competition with a crowded field of AI meeting tools including Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, and Microsoft's Copilot integration.

---

## The Meeting Productivity Battlefield

The market for AI meeting assistants has exploded as remote and hybrid work normalized distributed meetings. Tools that can automatically transcribe, summarize, and extract action items from meetings provide clear productivity gains for teams drowning in meeting schedules.

Calendly's advantage is its established position in the scheduling workflow. Users already rely on Calendly to book meetings — adding Callie creates a natural extension into capturing and organizing meeting outcomes. The integration with the scheduling layer means Callie can automatically join meetings scheduled through Calendly without requiring additional setup.

---

## Competitive Landscape

Callie enters a competitive market where established players have significant traction. Otter.ai claims millions of users and deep integrations with Zoom and Google Meet. Fireflies.ai has built substantial enterprise adoption. Microsoft's AI Copilot covers meeting notes as part of its broader productivity suite.

Calendly's differentiation may rely on its relationship with professional services firms and sales teams that already use Calendly for client scheduling. Bringing meeting notes into the same workflow could reduce tool sprawl for these users.
