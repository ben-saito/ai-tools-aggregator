# SpaceXSI Bundles X Premium, Grok, and Cursor into One Subscription

---

In a bid to attract more subscribers, SpaceXSI is bundling its X Premium subscription service with Grok AI access and Cursor AI coding tools into a single offering. The move signals a deeper push into the AI-integrated productivity space, combining social media, generative AI assistance, and AI-powered software development tools under one roof. Sarah Perez, TechCrunch

The bundled subscription aims to provide a comprehensive AI-powered workflow for users, blending communication, creative assistance, and developer productivity into a unified experience.

Sarah Perez SpaceXSI bundles X Premium, Grok and Cursor into one subscription In a bid to attract more subscribers, the company now known as SpaceX Super Intelligence is bundling all its subscriptions, including X Premium, Grok, and Cursor, into one plan called simply "The X Subscription." The idea is that the combined offering could potentially attract more subscribers than any single subscription, given the competitive paid AI subscription market, where SpaceXSI faces rivals like OpenAI, Anthropic, and others. With the X Subscription, users can gain access to Grok for AI-powered chat, with support for voice, images, video and the API. Grok's agentic AI Bot is also available, as is Cursor for AI coding and the paid features that come with an X Premium subscription, like the blue verified checkmark, creator tools, and fewer ads. There are five plans available on the new subscription: free, Plus ($8/month), Premium ($30/month), Super ($100/month) and Ultra ($200/month), with AI usage caps increasing as you move up through the tiers. The free plan will only offer limited chat and agent usage, and Premium or higher is required to access the latest Grok models. There's another draw for existing X Premium+ subscribers: the comparable plan on the new X subscription costs the same or less, the company noted. Existing users can either switch from their subscription settings on X to upgrade to the new bundle, or maintain their current plan. The option to upgrade will roll out gradually, so interested customers will need to keep an eye on their billing settings for the offer to upgrade. ## Why This Matters

The integration reflects a broader trend of bundling AI services with existing platform subscriptions. Rather than paying separately for a chatbot, a coding assistant, and a social platform, users get an all-in-one package that could lower the barrier to AI adoption.

Cursor, the AI-powered code editor, has gained significant traction among developers, while Grok provides conversational AI capabilities through the X platform. Combining these creates a unique value proposition for power users.

---

## Competitive Landscape

This bundle puts SpaceXSI in direct competition with other bundled AI offerings from Microsoft (GitHub Copilot + Microsoft 365), Google (Gemini + Workspace), and Apple (Apple Intelligence across devices). The subscription model allows SpaceXSI to create ecosystem lock-in while providing multiple AI capabilities.
