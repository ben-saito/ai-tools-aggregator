# OpenAI Acquires Presentation Startup NextSlide, Team Joins ChatGPT

*Ahmed Beshry, previously co-founder of Caper AI (acquired by Instacart in 2021), leads team into OpenAI*

---

## Acquisition Overview

OpenAI has acquired NextSlide, a presentation AI startup, with the startup's team members now working on ChatGPT. The acquisition, which took place earlier this year according to a LinkedIn note from founder Ahmed Beshry, was announced publicly on August 8, 2026. Financial terms of the deal were not disclosed.

---

## NextSlide's Product and Mission

NextSlide developed an AI product capable of transforming prompts, notes, documents, or research into polished, editable presentations. In a note on the NextSlide website, Beshry described the product's goal as "making visual communication more accessible and helping more people express their ideas clearly."

"We built NextSlide to help more people create better presentations faster," Beshry said. "By joining OpenAI, we'll continue pursuing that same mission: building AI products that help people create, communicate, and turn their ideas into meaningful work."

The announcement comes a few months later than originally planned, Beshry noted in his LinkedIn post, citing the acquisition timeline.

---

## Founder Background

Ahmed Beshry previously co-founded Caper AI, a smart cart and cashier-less checkout startup. Instacart acquired Caper AI in 2021, integrating its computer vision-based checkout technology into Instacart's platform. Beshry's transition from e-commerce infrastructure to AI presentation tools represents a shift toward productivity-focused AI applications.

---

## Strategic Context

The acquisition underscores OpenAI's continued expansion beyond conversational AI into specialized productivity tools. Integrating presentation capabilities directly into ChatGPT would enable users to generate slides from written or spoken prompts without switching between applications.

NextSlide's technology addresses a common workflow gap: while large language models excel at generating text, formatting that text into visually structured presentations typically requires additional tools or manual effort.
