# Sam Altman Promotes ChatGPT Work as Parenting Tool, Draws Mixed Reactions

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has suggested that parents could use the company's new ChatGPT Work product to generate personalized daily podcasts for their children — a use case that quickly drew skepticism from observers who questioned whether AI is the right solution for family communication.

---

## The Pitch: AI-Generated Morning Podcasts for Kids

On Friday, Altman posted about what he called a "cool use case" for ChatGPT Work: connecting a family calendar and explaining children's interests, then generating a daily podcast for the morning drive to school covering each child's soccer game, upcoming birthday, news, and other topics.

Altman's suggestion drew immediate pushback. Alex Hirsch, creator of the animated series "Gravity Falls," responded with a simple post: "What if you just talked to your children?" Hirsch's response went more viral than Altman's original — Altman's post was reposted around 300 times with 9,600 likes, while Hirsch's reply was reposted 9,000 times and received 122,000 likes.

---

## OpenAI's Strategy: Capturing the Family Market

Getting parents on board appears to be a priority for OpenAI. The company recently posted a job listing for a product manager with experience building "trust-sensitive consumer experiences for parents and families." Meanwhile, Meta is testing an AI-powered app that tells bedtime stories.

This is not Altman's first suggestion that ChatGPT can substitute for traditional parenting activities. Last year, he appeared on "The Tonight Show with Jimmy Fallon" and said, "I cannot imagine having gone through figuring out how to raise a newborn without ChatGPT" — while also acknowledging, "Clearly, people did it for a long time, no problem."

---

## Legal and Safety Context

OpenAI faces multiple lawsuits from parents and families alleging that ChatGPT played a role in loved ones' delusions and suicides. The company has said it is "continuously improving how our models respond in sensitive interactions." OpenAI has added safety features for parents in ChatGPT Work.

---

## A Broader Pattern: Tech Leaders and AI-Centered Family Life

Altman's pitch echoes a wider pattern of tech executives promoting AI as a solution to everyday human experiences. Last year, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said he had stopped listening to his favorite podcasts during his morning commute, instead asking an AI chatbot about them.
