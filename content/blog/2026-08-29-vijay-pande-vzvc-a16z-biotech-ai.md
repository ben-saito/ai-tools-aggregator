# Vijay Pande Leaves a16z to Launch AI-Native Studio VZVC

---

Vijay Pande, the prominent biotech investor who managed approximately $4 billion in life sciences assets at Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), left the firm in 2025 to found VZVC, a much smaller AI-native studio. In a TechCrunch interview, Pande stated: "We are not doing 30 bets a year."

## VZVC Investment Philosophy

VZVC operates at a scale that distinguishes it from traditional venture capital. While leading a16z's large biotech practice, Pande is now adopting a more nimble investment approach at the new studio. With biological discovery transitioning from a "discovery science" to an "engineering science," Pande argues that smaller, focused bets are more likely to produce breakthrough results.

## AI and Biology Convergence

According to Pande, the transformation of biology through AI is still in its early stages. While the high cost of clinical trials remains a significant barrier, AI has the potential to dramatically improve the efficiency of candidate compound screening. Pande states that open, shared datasets rather than walled-off ones are the true key to AI-driven transformation in medicine.

## Importance of Data Sharing in Drug Discovery

A key point from Pande's interview is the value of data sharing in drug discovery. As long as companies keep their datasets walled off, the possibilities of AI remain limited. Pande argues that a cooperative approach leads to better patient outcomes. It remains to be seen what startups VZVC will invest in.
