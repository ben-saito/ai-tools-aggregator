# Petlibro's AI-Powered Cat Feeder Brings Smart Monitoring to Multi-Cat Homes

Petlibro has launched its next-generation Granary 2 smart cat feeders, incorporating built-in scales and AI-powered cameras to provide precise tracking of individual cats' eating habits. The new system addresses one of the biggest challenges in multi-cat households: monitoring food intake for each pet individually.

---

## Smart Feeding Technology

The Granary 2 lineup introduces a built-in precision scale beneath each food bowl, allowing the feeder to track exactly how much food a cat removes at each meal. Higher-end models add an AI camera that identifies which cat is eating and when, enabling per-pet consumption analytics without manual logging.

For households with multiple cats, especially those managing weight issues or medical dietary requirements, this level of granular monitoring has historically required significant manual effort or expensive veterinary equipment. Petlibro's approach brings this capability to the consumer market at a fraction of the cost.

The AI camera system can distinguish between different household cats using visual recognition, automatically logging each pet's meal times and portion sizes. The companion app provides daily and weekly consumption reports, alerting owners to changes in eating patterns that might indicate health concerns.

---

## Market Context

Smart pet feeders have gained traction as pet ownership becomes increasingly technology-mediated. The global pet tech market continues to expand as owners seek tools that provide peace of mind and data-driven insights into their animals' wellbeing.

Petlibro's focus on the multi-cat problem reflects a specific gap in the consumer market. While single-cat households have access to various smart feeding solutions, multi-cat homes face unique challenges in tracking individual consumption, particularly when cats share feeding stations.
