# AI Data Startup Micro1 Reaches $500M Gross Run Rate Amid AI Training Boom

The surge in demand for AI training data is driving explosive growth for data-labeling startups, with Micro1 expanding from $100M to $500M gross annual run rate in just eight months.

---

## The Data Labeling Boom

Micro1, a four-year-old startup, has become one of the fastest-growing players in the AI training data market. The company hires domain experts—including doctors, lawyers, and scientists—on a contract basis to label and annotate data used for training AI models. According to a person familiar with the company, Micro1 retains roughly 60% to 70% of its revenue, putting its net annual run rate between $150 million and $200 million.

The broader data-labeling market has seen similar momentum across multiple players, as demand for high-quality training data continues to outstrip supply. Labs and corporations are competing for finite pools of qualified human annotators, particularly for specialized domains like medical imaging, legal documents, and scientific research.

---

## Market Dynamics

Unlike general-purpose data collection, domain-specific annotation requires subject matter expertise that cannot be easily outsourced to low-cost labor markets. This constraint has allowed specialized startups like Micro1 to maintain pricing power while scaling their workforce.

The growth trajectory reflects a wider trend: as AI models become more capable, the marginal value of clean, domain-specific training data increases. Frontier labs are willing to pay premiums for data that can improve model performance on specialized tasks, particularly in regulated industries where accuracy is critical.
