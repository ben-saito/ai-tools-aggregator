# Google Maps Adds Agentic Features Including Food Ordering and Hotel Bookings

Google announced on Thursday that Google Maps' "Ask Maps" feature is gaining new agentic capabilities, including the ability to order food, book hotels, and find event tickets. The tech giant is also bringing Personal Intelligence to Ask Maps, allowing the AI tool to personalize its responses by drawing on information from a user's Gmail and Google Calendar.

The launch of the new features reflects Google's ambitions to transform Google Maps from a navigation tool into an assistant capable of helping users complete real-world tasks. The agentic features go beyond information retrieval to include transactional capabilities: users can complete food orders and hotel reservations without leaving the Maps interface.

---

## From Navigation to Task Completion

The expansion represents a significant shift in Google Maps' product philosophy. Previously, the service helped users find and navigate to destinations. With agentic capabilities, it now aims to complete tasks on behalf of users, drawing on personal context stored across Google's ecosystem of productivity tools.

Personal Intelligence integration allows Ask Maps to access a user's Gmail and Calendar data, enabling contextual recommendations based on upcoming travel, reservations, or preferences expressed in email communications.
