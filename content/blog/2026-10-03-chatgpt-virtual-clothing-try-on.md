# ChatGPT Adds Virtual Clothing Try-On Feature

OpenAI is rolling out new shopping features for ChatGPT that allow users to virtually try on clothing and accessories using their own photos. The feature lets users see how items look on them before purchasing, while saving products to a Favorites library for later reference.

---

## How the Feature Works

The virtual try-on capability uses generative AI to overlay clothing items onto user-provided photos. Unlike traditional AR try-on tools that require specialized hardware, ChatGPT's implementation works with standard photos uploaded to the platform.

The feature represents OpenAI's push into e-commerce integration, positioning ChatGPT not just as a conversational AI but as a shopping assistant. Users can browse compatible products and see them rendered on their own images without leaving the ChatGPT interface.

---

## Consumer AI Shopping Landscape

Virtual try-on technology has gained traction across the retail industry as brands seek to reduce return rates and improve online shopping experiences. Major retailers including Warby Parker, Sephora, and Nike have deployed similar AR-based try-on features for eyewear, cosmetics, and footwear.

ChatGPT's entry into this space reflects the broader trend of AI assistants expanding beyond information tasks into practical consumer applications. The ability to understand and manipulate visual content positions foundation models as increasingly capable of handling real-world purchasing decisions.

---

## Privacy Considerations

The use of personal photos for virtual try-on raises questions about image handling and data privacy. Users uploading photos to ChatGPT for try-on purposes are trusting OpenAI with biometric-adjacent data that could theoretically be used for identification or profiling purposes.

OpenAI's privacy policy governs how uploaded images are processed and stored, but the incident underscores the growing amount of personal data flowing through AI platforms as they expand into consumer applications.
