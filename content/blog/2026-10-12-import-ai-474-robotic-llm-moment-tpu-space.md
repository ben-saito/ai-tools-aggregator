# Import AI 474: Platonic Mindspace, Space Computers, and Zhipu's RSI Loop

---

## Robotics Preparing for Its LLM Moment

Stanford researchers Perry Dong and Chelsea Finn argue robotics needs a **universal algorithm** to enable the kind of transfer learning that LLMs achieved in language. Current robot learning is too task-specific — a robot trained to fold one type of cloth can't generalize to others.

The missing piece: a foundation model architecture that lets robots learn from broad experience and adapt quickly to new situations, similar to how GPT-style pretraining enabled language generalization.

---

## Space Computers: Google TPUs Head to Orbit

Google is advancing **Project Suncatcher** — putting TPU computers in space. The initiative aims to eventually train AI systems beyond Earth's atmosphere, potentially leveraging unique space environments for AI development.

This follows growing interest in space-based computing infrastructure for AI training and inference.

---

## Zhipu's Outer RSI Loop

Chinese LLM developer **Zhipu AI** (behind the GLM-5.3 open-weight model) has begun using its own models to automate internal infrastructure work — effectively running what Import AI calls an **"outer RSI loop"** where AI helps build AI systems.

This marks a concrete step toward recursive self-improvement, though Zhipu's implementation appears narrow in scope.

---

## AI Scientists Running Real Labs

Companies like **Periodic Labs** are hooking AI models directly to physical laboratory equipment. Their Periodic Neon model has been trained to help design and execute experiments — a step toward fully automated scientific discovery pipelines.

The pattern: AI that doesn't just suggest hypotheses but physically runs the experiments to test them.
