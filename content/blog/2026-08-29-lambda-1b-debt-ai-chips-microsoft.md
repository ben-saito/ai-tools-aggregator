# Neocloud Lambda Secures $1B in Private Debt to Purchase More AI Chips

Lambda, an AI cloud infrastructure company that purchases computing chips and leases them to enterprise customers, has raised $1 billion in private short-dated debt to acquire additional Nvidia AI chips for lease to Microsoft, according to Bloomberg.

---

## Deal Structure and Growth Strategy

The debt arrangement, coordinated by JP Morgan Chase, signals Lambda's confidence in its ability to rapidly deploy the chips and begin generating revenue to repay the debt quickly from incoming lease payments. This latest financing comes as Lambda reportedly explores a $3 billion pre-IPO funding round.

The company has been aggressively expanding its GPU infrastructure through a series of financing deals. In May 2026, Lambda closed a $1 billion secured credit facility. Earlier this week, the company announced the closing of a $926 million loan to fund Nvidia GB300 GPUs — one of Nvidia's newest chip models — for a contracted deployment with Nvidia itself.

Lambda's core business model centers on acquiring large quantities of AI computing hardware and leasing it back to customers who lack the capital to purchase such infrastructure directly. This approach has proven attractive to hyperscale customers like Microsoft, which can secure dedicated compute capacity without the upfront capital expenditure of building owned data centers.

---

## AI Infrastructure Debt Boom

Lambda is not alone in turning to debt markets to fund AI infrastructure expansion. According to Bloomberg data, banks and technology companies have collectively raised over $400 billion in AI-related debt globally during 2026 so far. The scale of capital being deployed into AI compute infrastructure reflects the intense competition among cloud providers and AI companies to secure sufficient GPU capacity.

The company's last known valuation was $5.43 billion post-money from a $1.5 billion venture round closed in November 2025, per PitchBook data. Lambda's ability to secure such large debt facilities at relatively quick intervals suggests lenders have confidence in the company's revenue visibility, driven by long-term contracts with creditworthy customers.

---

## Competitive Landscape

Lambda competes with other specialized AI cloud providers including CoreWeave, which has similarly pursued aggressive GPU lease financing strategies. The company's direct relationship with Nvidia for the newest GB300 chips positions it among the first movers in deploying cutting-edge AI hardware, potentially giving it an edge in attracting customers seeking maximum compute performance.
