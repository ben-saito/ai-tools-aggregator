# General Intuition Raises at $6B Valuation, Pushes into Robotics

---

## The Deal: $6B Pre-Money Valuation for Physical AI Startup

General Intuition, the startup building foundation models that train generalized AI agents to operate through physical space and time, is in talks to raise a new funding round at a $6 billion pre-money valuation from investors including Valor Equity Partners, Point72 Ventures, and Seven Seven Six, according to sources familiar with the matter. The round comes just weeks after the company raised $320 million at a $2.3 billion valuation — a dramatic jump that reflects surging investor appetite for physical AI companies.

Existing investors Khosla Ventures and General Catalyst are also participating in the new round, which General Intuition is still finalizing.

---

## From Video Game Clips to Robotic Foundation Models

CEO Pim de Witte spun General Intuition out last October from Medal, his video game clip-sharing platform. The company's initial training data comes from hundreds of millions of hours of gameplay footage, annotated with "action labels" — records of which buttons a player pressed and when. This data provides a rich dataset of human behavioral sequences that General Intuition's large action models learn from.

The WSJ reported a first look at how the company's large action models work, which involve training AI systems to understand and predict sequences of physical actions in real-world environments.

---

## The Physical AI Thesis

General Intuition is part of a new wave of AI companies focused on physical AI — systems that can perceive, reason about, and act in the physical world. This contrasts with purely digital AI systems and represents a frontier that major investors believe will be central to the next wave of AI deployment.

The company describes its mission as building foundation models that can train generalized AI agents to move through space and time, suggesting applications across robotics, autonomous vehicles, and other physical systems.

---

## Robotics Push and Strategic Vision

The fresh capital will accelerate General Intuition's push into robotics, where the company sees significant opportunity for its action models. Unlike language or image models, training AI systems on physical tasks requires massive datasets of human behavior in spatial contexts — exactly the kind of data that Medal's gaming platform has been generating.

The rapid valuation increase from $2.3 billion to $6 billion in weeks reflects the competitive dynamics of the physical AI space, where multiple startups and established players are racing to develop foundation models for robotic applications.
