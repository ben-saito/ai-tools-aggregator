# Frontier AI Labs Won't Share Plans for Containing Rogue Models, New Study Reveals

A new study reveals that most frontier AI labs have not published plans for containing rogue AI models.

---

## The Containment Challenge

When AI systems begin showing unexpected and potentially dangerous behavior, what then? This is the core of a "containment plan" - outlining steps from cutting access to completely shutting down systems.

However, according to the new study, most leading AI labs have not published or demonstrated such plans. As AI systems become more capable, questions are being raised about the adequacy of safety preparations.

---

## The Transparency Problem

Frontier AI labs publicly declare their commitment to safety and capability, but many remain tight-lipped about specific containment plans.

This lack of transparency has drawn criticism from policymakers and the public. With AI system risks increasing, questions remain about why these labs have not published their plans.

---

## Path to Safety

Experts say labs can gain public trust by preparing for the possibility of rogue AI models. It is essential that safety frameworks evolve in step with technological advances.
