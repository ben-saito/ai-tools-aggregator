# I Created an Interactive Digital Avatar of Myself

A TechCrunch reporter chronicles the experience of training an interactive AI avatar trained on their own likeness, raising fresh questions about digital identity and synthetic media.

---

## Building a Digital Twin

The reporter obtained an interactive avatar through a service that trains AI models on personal data to create conversational digital replicas. After training the avatar to discuss topics including venture fraud, the experience produced mixed feelings about the emerging technology.

Interactive avatars represent one of the more personal applications of generative AI, requiring users to provide significant data about their appearance, voice, and mannerisms. The resulting systems can engage in video conversations that feel natural to conversation partners.

---

## The Venture Fraud Experiment

After creating the avatar, the reporter trained it to discuss a specific topic: venture fraud. The choice reflected both the prevalence of fraud in startup culture and the reporter's own experience covering the space.

The avatar's ability to discuss complex topics coherently proved impressive, but the experience also highlighted gaps between digital replicas and human judgment. The avatar could surface relevant information but lacked the contextual awareness that comes from genuine expertise.

---

## Privacy Implications

Creating an interactive avatar requires surrendering biometric data including facial structure, voice patterns, and conversational mannerisms. The aggregation of these data points creates new categories of personal information that existing privacy frameworks may not adequately address.

The reporter noted that even after deleting the avatar service account, questions remain about what training data was retained and how it might be used in future model training. The industry lacks standardized practices for data deletion in the context of synthetic media.

---

## The Sincerity Question

Perhaps the most unsettling aspect of the experience was the fundamental question of authenticity.
