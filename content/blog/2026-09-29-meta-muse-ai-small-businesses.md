# Meta Expands AI Agent Muse to Small Businesses

Meta is expanding its AI agent product, Muse, to small business owners. The company says the agent can help business operators run their day-to-day operations and find new customers — extending Muse beyond its consumer-focused origins into the SMB market.

---

## From Consumer to Business Use

Muse launched earlier in 2026 as a consumer AI agent focused on personal productivity and communication tasks. The expansion to small businesses represents Meta's first serious push into the B2B agent market, positioning Muse as a tool for sole proprietors and small teams who need affordable automation.

Small businesses are a notoriously difficult market for enterprise software. Unlike large enterprises, SMBs have limited IT resources, tighter budgets, and workflows that do not fit standard SaaS templates. Meta's approach with Muse is to offer pre-built agent templates for common small business tasks rather than requiring custom configuration.

---

## Core Capabilities for SMBs

Muse for small businesses includes agent templates for:
- Customer inquiry handling and appointment scheduling
- Inventory management and supplier communication
- Social media posting and review response
- Basic bookkeeping and invoicing through integration with QuickBooks and Stripe

The agents operate within a chat-based interface, allowing business owners to delegate tasks through natural language instructions rather than learning a software interface.

---

## Meta's Agent Strategy

Meta's agent strategy with Muse contrasts with competitors like OpenAI (which has targeted enterprise with ChatGPT Agents) and Google (which offers Gemini for business through Workspace). Meta is leveraging its existing relationship with small businesses that advertise on Facebook and Instagram, making Muse a natural extension of its ads ecosystem.

The company has not disclosed pricing for the SMB tier. Industry observers expect a per-seat or per-agent monthly model similar to other SMB SaaS tools.

---

## Competitive Landscape

The small business AI agent market is becoming crowded. Intuit offers AI agents within QuickBooks and Mailchimp. Salesforce has its Einstein AI for small business editions. Microsoft provides Copilot for SMBs through Microsoft 365. Shopify has rolled out AI tools for merchant operations.

Meta's advantage is its distribution — Muse is pre-integrated with Instagram and Facebook business tools that many small businesses already use. The integration with Meta's ad platform could allow Muse agents to automatically adjust ad campaigns based on inventory or appointment availability.

---

## Outlook

Meta has not disclosed adoption metrics for the small business tier. The move positions Muse as a platform play rather than a standalone product, with Meta betting that embedding AI agents into its existing SMB ecosystem will drive retention and advertising revenue. Competitors will be watching closely to see whether Meta's consumer DNA translates to B2B success.
