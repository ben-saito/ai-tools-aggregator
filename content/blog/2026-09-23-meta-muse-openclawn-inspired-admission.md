# Meta Acknowledges Muse AI Assistant Was Heavily Inspired by OpenClaw

Meta has admitted that its recently launched AI assistant Muse was "heavily inspired" by OpenClaw, acknowledging that the resemblance between the two products extends to workspace filenames and content. The admission comes as Meta positions Muse as a privileged, on-device alternative to ChatGPT.

---

## From Denial to Acknowledgment

When questions first arose about Muse's similarity to OpenClaw, Meta initially stated the assistant was built from scratch. However, following scrutiny, the company revised its stance, acknowledging that the OpenClaw product influenced Muse's design and functionality.

The overlap reportedly extends to some workspace filenames and content, suggesting a closer parallel than Meta originally indicated.

---

## Background

OpenClaw is an AI agent framework that gained traction among developers for its desktop integration capabilities. Meta's Muse, launched as a consumer-facing AI assistant, offers similar on-device and workspace-aware features, raising questions about how much of Muse's design was original versus derived from OpenClaw's architecture.

Meta has not disclosed whether any intellectual property or licensing arrangements were involved.
