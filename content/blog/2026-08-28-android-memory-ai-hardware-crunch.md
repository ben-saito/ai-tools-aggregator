# Google Sets New Memory Limits for Android Apps as AI Data Centers Strain Hardware Supply

Google announced new Android app requirements this week addressing memory usage, citing industry-wide hardware supply constraints driven by AI data center expansion. The changes target developers and could particularly impact lower-cost devices.

---

## The Hardware Crunch

Google explains that the mobile industry is facing significant hardware supply constraints altering device memory availability. AI data center buildouts have increased demand for memory chips, affecting the consumer device market. This could leave budget smartphones with less memory than previous generations.

To address this, Google is introducing new performance thresholds covering dynamic memory usage and bitmap usage. The company is also adding code optimization requirements designed to prevent app slowdowns and crashes related to performance issues.

---

## Developer Tools and Timeline

Google will roll out diagnostic tools to alert developers when their apps exceed new thresholds. A Memory Limiter feature arriving later this year will prevent apps from consuming excessive device memory. Developers have until February 2027 to meet the new requirements.

Additionally, Google will require all Play Store apps to meet Zero Tap Sign-In requirements during device migrations by April 2027. This standard requires apps with user sign-ins to automatically restore the user's sign-in state when moving between Android devices using the Android Restore Credentials API.

---

## Industry Context

The announcement reflects a broader shift in the hardware market. As AI companies consume significant computing resources, the downstream effects are reaching consumer devices. Mobile chips and memory that might have gone into smartphones are instead being allocated to data center infrastructure.

For app developers, the changes mean re-evaluating memory management practices. Apps optimized for previous device generations may need refactoring to meet the new standards.
