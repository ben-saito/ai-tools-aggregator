# WindBorne Wins $50M US Air Force Contract for AI Weather Balloon Data

---

WindBorne Systems has secured a contract worth nearly $50 million from the U.S. Air Force to supply atmospheric data collected by its fleet of autonomous weather balloons. The company operates approximately 700 weather balloons globally, collecting sensor readings that feed into its own AI-powered weather forecasting model.

## The Contract

The Air Force will purchase data from WindBorne's balloon fleet, using it to support weather forecasting and atmospheric monitoring operations. The contract represents one of the larger government contracts for a weather AI startup and signals growing military interest in commercial weather data.

## WindBorne's Technology

WindBorne's balloons collect atmospheric sensor readings at various altitudes, data that is valuable for weather prediction and climate modeling. The company combines this sensor data with AI models to generate weather forecasts. Founded in 2015 and based in Redwood City, California, WindBorne is one of the first organizations to directly sample atmospheric conditions at scale using autonomous platforms.

## AI and Weather Forecasting

AI models trained on large atmospheric datasets have shown increasing accuracy in weather prediction. WindBorne's approach combines direct sensor measurements with machine learning, providing a data source that can improve forecast models.
