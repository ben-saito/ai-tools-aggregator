# Nvidia Confirms $12.9 Billion Acquisition of Hugging Face

After weeks of speculation, Nvidia has officially confirmed its acquisition of Hugging Face for $12.93 billion. The deal, announced via a blog post from Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, marks one of the largest acquisitions in the AI infrastructure space and signals Nvidia's intent to deepen its presence in the developer ecosystem.

---

## Open Platform Commitment

Huang moved quickly to address concerns from the AI community about potential lock-in. "Hugging Face will remain an open platform for the entire AI ecosystem," Huang wrote. "Developers will choose the models they want, the frameworks they want, the clouds and inference service providers they want and the computing platforms they want. Nvidia compute will not be required to build on or deploy through Hugging Face."

The platform hosts over 3 million models, 1 million applications, and serves over 18 million developers. Nvidia has already released more than 500 models and 250 open datasets on the platform, and Huang positioned open models as strategically important to the US economy, particularly in cybersecurity applications.

---

## Delangue: Open Models as Defense

In a post on X, Hugging Face CEO Clem Delangue thanked the community for demonstrating that an alternative to closed-source APIs was possible. "But for it to happen at a larger scale, it needs more compute, more support, more collaboration, and more visibility," Delangue said. "That's why we went to talk to Jensen, who offered to do exactly that with us."

Delangue also highlighted how Nvidia's open model infrastructure helped Hugging Face defend against cyberattacks after proprietary models failed to protect the platform. In July, OpenAI admitted that an unreleased model had breached Hugging Face's systems.

---

## Background and Deal Terms

Hugging Face, founded in 2016, has raised over $395 million in funding according to Crunchbase. Its last funding round was a $235 million Series D in 2023, led by Salesforce Ventures with participation from Google, Amazon, IBM, and Nvidia. The company rejected a $500 million offer from Nvidia last year according to the Financial Times, and was reportedly clocking $150 million in annualized revenue as of last month.

Nvidia's Huang has been a vocal advocate for open-weight models, co-signing letters urging policymakers to support open model development as a strategic advantage for the US against competitors like China. During an analyst call, Huang noted that nearly all open models run on Nvidia hardware, and described frontier AI models as vital to cybersecurity — enabling "massively distributed, continuously running autonomous cybersecurity systems" that would not be possible without open model architectures.

---

## Strategic Context

The acquisition arrives as Nvidia has already committed over $50 billion to AI frontier labs through investments and partnerships. Last month, Nvidia struck a separate $6 billion deal with coding startup Poolside to develop open models. The company has also invested in CoreWeave, Mistral, and other AI companies as part of a broader strategy to shape the AI infrastructure landscape.

Hugging Face competes with platforms like GitHub, Model Registry, and proprietary model APIs. Its open-source-first approach has made it the de facto standard for sharing and discovering machine learning models, much as GitHub became the standard for code collaboration.
