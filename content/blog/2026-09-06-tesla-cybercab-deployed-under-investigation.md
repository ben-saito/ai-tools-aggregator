# Tesla's Cybercab Deployed and Already Under Investigation

Tesla has begun deploying its Cybercab autonomous taxi service in select locations, and the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) has already opened an investigation into the vehicles. The development, reported by Ars Technica on September 5, 2026, marks the first real-world operation of Tesla's dedicated robotaxi, though regulatory scrutiny has followed quickly.

---

## Deployment and Investigation

Tesla's Cybercab, a purpose-built autonomous vehicle without steering wheel or pedals, has launched in a limited geographic area. The deployment represents the culmination of Tesla's shift from developing driver-assistance features for vehicles it sells to consumers, toward operating a fleet-based autonomous ride-hailing service.

Almost immediately after deployment became public, NHTSA announced an investigation into the Cybercab's performance. The agency has not disclosed specific details about what prompted the inquiry, though standard practice involves reviewing incident reports and safety data from early deployments of autonomous systems.

---

## Tesla's Autonomous Driving Strategy

Tesla has pursued a vision-only approach to autonomous driving, relying primarily on cameras and neural networks rather than the lidar and high-definition maps used by competitors like Waymo and Cruise. The company argues that this approach scales more efficiently, as it does not require bespoke mapping of every road the system will operate on.

The Cybercab represents Tesla's first vehicle designed from the ground up for fully autonomous operation. Unlike Tesla's existing vehicles with Full Self-Driving capability, the Cybercab has no provisions for human control in the traditional sense, though it retains remote override capabilities for the operator.

---

## Regulatory Environment

The rapid opening of an investigation after deployment highlights the challenging regulatory environment for autonomous vehicles in the United States. Companies deploying robotaxi services must navigate a patchwork of state regulations while also facing federal safety oversight.

The investigation could result in requirements for design changes, operational restrictions, or additional reporting before Tesla can expand the service more broadly. The outcome will be closely watched by other companies developing autonomous vehicle services.
