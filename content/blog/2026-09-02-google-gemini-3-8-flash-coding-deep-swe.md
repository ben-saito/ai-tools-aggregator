# Google Gemini 3.8 Flash Tops DeepSWE Leaderboard, Targets Coding and Agentic Workflows

Google released Gemini 3.8 Flash on September 2, 2026, marking the third Flash model the company has shipped in six weeks. The new model comes in two variants: a standard "workhorse" Flash version and Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, tuned for vulnerability detection and mitigation. Ars Technica reports that the standard Flash variant has climbed to the top of the DeepSWE leaderboard, which measures a model's ability to solve complex software engineering problems.

---

## Coding Performance and Benchmark Results

Gemini 3.8 Flash shows marginal improvement over its 3.7 Flash predecessor across most tests, with larger gains appearing in coding evaluations. The model now leads the DeepSWE benchmark at a lower cost point during Google's promotional pricing period: $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens through the end of 2026. Standard pricing will be $1.50 / $7.50 per million tokens.

Google reportedly delayed the release of Gemini 3.5 Pro when its coding performance failed to match competing models. The 3.8 Flash results suggest Google is now competing through its Flash family rather than a Pro-tier release.

In the OSWorld-2.0 test of agentic computer use, Gemini 3.8 Flash improved over 3.7 Flash but remains significantly behind the market leader, Claude Opus. GPT models also trail in this benchmark, indicating that computer use remains a challenging frontier for frontier AI models.

---

## Flash Cyber: Cybersecurity Focus

The Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber variant replaces the 3.5 Cyber version and targets vulnerability detection workflows. Google claims substantial improvement over previous models in internal testing. The Chrome security team reportedly saw a 2.6x increase in patch accuracy with the new model, and Google's Cloud team reported that 3.8 Flash Cyber identified a critical vulnerability in two hours of testing.

Partners including Wiz and Palo Alto Networks have provided statements attesting to the model's capabilities. The Cyber variant is currently limited to trusted testers and government users, with broader availability planned.

---

## API Access and Availability

Gemini 3.8 Flash is available across Google's ecosystem starting September 2. The standard Flash model requires a Google One Pro or Ultra subscription for access through the Gemini app, though AI Studio offers free access for experimentation. Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber remains restricted to trusted tester programs.

Google's rapid release cadence with three Flash models in six weeks reflects competitive pressure in the AI market, with other labs recently dropping token pricing to retain business customers.
