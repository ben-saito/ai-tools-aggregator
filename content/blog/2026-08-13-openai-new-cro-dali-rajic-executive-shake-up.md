# OpenAI Hires New CRO as Executive Shake-Up Continues

OpenAI has appointed Dali Rajic, former president and COO of Wiz, as its new Chief Revenue Officer, replacing Denise Dresser after just nine months in the role. The move is the latest in a series of executive departures that have reshaped the company's leadership over the past month.

---

## A Quick Replacement in a Shaking Leadership Structure

Denise Dresser held the CRO position for only nine months before being replaced. Dali Rajic, who was president and chief operating officer at cybersecurity startup Wiz, will now lead OpenAI's commercial efforts as the company seeks to monetize its AI products at scale.

---

## Broader Executive Departures

The CRO appointment follows the departures of COO Brad Lightcap and Fidji Simo, OpenAI's No. 2 executive and CEO of AGI deployment. These exits represent a significant recalibration of the company's executive team as it navigates the transition from research lab to commercial enterprise.

---

## Revenue Focus Amid Competitive AI Landscape

The hiring of a revenue-focused executive signals OpenAI's increasing emphasis on commercial growth as competition in the AI sector intensifies. The company has been expanding its enterprise offerings while managing the substantial costs of running large language models at scale.
