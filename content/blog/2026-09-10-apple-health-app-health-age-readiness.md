# Apple Revamps Health App With 'Health Age' and Readiness Score Powered by Apple Intelligence

Apple's redesigned Health app, unveiled alongside watchOS 11 at the September 9 "Surprise and Shine" event, introduces two AI-driven metrics designed to give users a clearer picture of their overall health status: a "Health Age" score and a daily "readiness score." Both are driven by Apple Intelligence processing data from Apple Watch and iPhone sensors.

---

## Health Age: How Your Body Compares to Your Chronological Age

Health Age uses data from Apple Watch — including VO2 max, sleep quality, heart rate variability, and exercise metrics — to generate an age-equivalent score. Where traditional health metrics report raw numbers, Health Age translates them into an intuitive comparison: how old does your body "act" relative to your actual age?

The feature incorporates additional biomarkers when users opt to import lab results from blood tests via the Health app, giving a more comprehensive picture that extends beyond wearable sensor data.

---

## Readiness Score: One Number for Daily Physical Preparedness

Borrowed conceptually from smart rings like Oura and Whoop, the readiness score distills multiple data streams — recent activity levels, overnight vital signs, sleep quality, and recovery indicators — into a single morning metric. A high readiness score suggests the body is well-recovered and prepared for physical exertion. A low score might suggest the user should take it easy.

Apple Intelligence ties into this by continuously analyzing the incoming health data stream and surfacing patterns. For example, the app might suggest that a user incorporate more movement into their day if their readiness score is being dragged down by prolonged sedentary time.

---

## Longevity Tab: Cross-Factor Health Assessment

Apple also introduced a "Longevity" tab that aggregates sleep, movement, heart health, and other factors into an ongoing assessment. The tab pulls in data from Apple Watch and iPhone motion sensors, offering guidance calibrated to the user's specific health profile rather than generic recommendations.

---

## Health App Integration with Fitness Assessments

The Health app now includes in-depth physical assessment tools designed for users recovering from injury or managing age-related mobility issues. Users can follow along with video demonstrations of exercises, and the app's AI can evaluate movement quality using the phone's camera.

---

## Privacy: On-Device Processing

As with all Apple Intelligence features, the health data processing happens on-device where possible. Apple says it does not access individual health data for AI model training, positioning the Health app as a private AI health coach that stays on the user's side of the cloud divide.
