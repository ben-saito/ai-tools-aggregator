# Microsoft President Brad Smith: UK Is Best Placed to Lead AI Regulation

---

Microsoft president Brad Smith has called for urgent AI regulation, arguing that consumer products like milk and washing machines currently have more oversight than AI systems. In a BBC podcast appearance, Smith described AI as both a "weapon and a tool" that poses cybersecurity risks, and said the time for global safety rules has arrived.

## The Argument for UK Leadership

Smith contends that the UK is uniquely positioned to lead AI regulation globally, ahead of the United States or European Union. His argument centers on the UK's combination of technical expertise, legal infrastructure, and international relationships.

## Global AI Safety

The call for AI regulation comes as frontier AI models become more capable and as governments worldwide grapple with how to manage the risks and benefits of advanced AI systems. Microsoft, as a major AI developer, has a stake in shaping regulatory frameworks.

## Industry and Regulation

Smith's comments reflect a growing trend among major AI companies acknowledging the need for government oversight while simultaneously lobbying for rules that favor their capabilities.
