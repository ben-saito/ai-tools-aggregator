# India Ringg Raises $10M from Peak XV — Voice AI Pivots from Phone Calls to Full Agents

---

## From DesiVocal to Ringg

Indian voice AI startup Ringg secured $10 million in funding from Peak XV Partners as an extension of its Series A round, adding to its prior $5.5 million raise.

Ringg's journey is notable: originally launched as text-to-speech startup DesiVocal, the company pivoted to voice AI after finding self-trained speech model costs unsustainable.

---

## Beyond Phone Calls — New Voice AI Applications

Ringg current focus extends traditional phone-call voice AI into new domains. The company started with high-volume, low-complexity use cases — outbound calling, lead qualification, loan collection — and is rapidly expanding scope.

Backed by Peak XV, Ringg plans to accelerate development of AI voice translation and conversational AI agents.

---

## Indias Growing Voice AI Market

India multilingual environment creates substantial opportunity for voice AI beyond English — Hindi and regional language needs represent a large addressable market. Ringg multilingual capabilities position it well for domestic and international expansion.
