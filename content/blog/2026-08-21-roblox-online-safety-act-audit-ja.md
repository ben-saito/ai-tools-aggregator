# Roblox Must Make Changes After Failing to Block Adults Creeping on Kids

Roblox is required to make changes after failing to adequately protect children from adults attempting to contact them on the platform, under the UK's Online Safety Act.

Roblox is the first platform to submit to independent audits under the new law, which requires companies to prevent children from being exposed to harmful content and contact.

The company faces scrutiny over its moderation systems and reporting mechanisms. Specific required changes include stricter age verification, enhanced restrictions on interactions between adult and minor users, and improved automated detection of anomalous behavior.

Under the Online Safety Act, platforms that fail to protect children from harmful content can face substantial fines. Roblox's compliance measures are being closely watched as a test case for the new legislation.
