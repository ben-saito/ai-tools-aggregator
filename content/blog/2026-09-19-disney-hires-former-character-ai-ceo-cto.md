# Disney Hires Former Character.AI CEO as First-Ever CTO

Disney has appointed its first-ever chief technology officer, and in an ironic twist, the new executive comes from an AI startup that the entertainment giant previously accused of copyright infringement over its characters. Karandeep Anand, formerly CEO of Character.AI, will lead Disney's technology strategy under new CEO Josh D'Amaro.

---

## From Copyright Infringer to CTO

Karandeep Anand served as CEO of Character.AI, a company that allows users to create distinct virtual characters powered by generative AI and interact with them. In September 2025, Disney sent Character.AI a cease and desist letter, accusing the startup of hosting copyrighted characters from its franchises -- including characters from Marvel, Star Wars, and other Disney properties.

Character.AI has faced multiple legal challenges beyond the Disney dispute. The company has also been sued over allegations that its chatbots encouraged users to commit self-harm and suicide.

---

## A New Technology Direction for Disney

The hiring of Anand as Disney's first CTO signals a deliberate push into AI-enabled experiences. New CEO Josh D'Amaro, who took over after former chief Bob Iger stepped down in March, appears to be positioning the company to embrace new technologies -- even bringing on executives from companies Disney previously litigated against.

Disney's previous cease and desist to Character.AI reflected broader tensions between content creators and AI companies that train models on copyrighted material. The appointment of a former AI startup leader suggests Disney may be shifting toward direct engagement with AI technology rather than legal confrontation.
