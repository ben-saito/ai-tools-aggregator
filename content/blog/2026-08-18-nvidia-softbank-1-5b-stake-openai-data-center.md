# Nvidia Invests $1.5B in SoftBank Data Center Developer Behind OpenAI Project

---

## The Investment

Nvidia announced on August 17, 2026, that it will invest $1.5 billion in SB Energy, a data center developer linked to SoftBank and OpenAI. The investment secures Nvidia's position as the sole supplier of compute infrastructure at OpenAI's Ports-Pike data center near Cincinnati, Ohio.

Nvidia will also provide up to $105 billion in credit to help build the facility, which is planned to scale from an initial 4.25 gigawatts to 8 gigawatts.

---

## Site Details

The data center will be built on land owned by the U.S. Department of Energy that previously enriched uranium for the U.S. nuclear arsenal and Navy submarines. SB Energy plans to build a 9.2 gigawatt natural gas power plant on the site.

The power plant alone is expected to cost $33 billion, reflecting the skyrocketing costs of natural gas infrastructure which have risen 66% in the last two years according to BloombergNEF.

---

## Financial Links

SoftBank previously held $5.8 billion worth of Nvidia stock, which it sold in November to fund other AI investments. The new Nvidia investment comes after Elon Musk announced an exclusive arrangement for Nvidia to power SpaceX's data centers, and just days after SpaceX acquired code editor startup Cursor for $60 billion.
