# Airbnb Launches AI-Powered Search with Cautious Approach to Agentic Interfaces

Airbnb has introduced AI-powered search functionality as part of its fall product update, taking a deliberately cautious approach compared to the chatbot-style interfaces many competitors have adopted.

Instead of a conversational AI interface, Airbnb users can now enable a toggle that processes text or voice search queries. The system then presents dynamic filters based on user preferences — for example, typing "baby" automatically surfaces filters for cribs, high chairs, and other infant-related amenities.

CEO Brian Chesky, who has been vocal about his skepticism toward chatbot-first product designs, framed the approach around conversion preservation. "Anyone can vibe-code a search function, but to do something that doesn't kill conversion rate, that's the hard part," he said.

Beyond property search, Airbnb is using AI to generate summaries and comparative insights across a user's wishlisted properties, pulling from reviews and property features. The platform is also expanding into adjacent services including meal delivery, laundry, baby-gear rental, ski and boat rentals in select markets, and enhanced grocery delivery.

On the social side, Airbnb is building map views that display connections' past and upcoming trips, allowing users to see where friends have stayed and what experiences they booked. Privacy controls let users opt out of sharing their travel activity.

The company is also developing neighborhood pages driven by guest reviews to help travelers discover dining and activities.
