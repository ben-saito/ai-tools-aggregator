# Amazon Makes Alexa+ Free on All Fire TV Devices in US

Amazon announced on August 19, 2026 that its AI-powered assistant Alexa+ will be rolled out to all compatible Fire TV devices in the United States at no additional cost, regardless of whether the user has a Prime subscription.

---

## From Paid to Free: Alexa+ Expands Reach

Previously, Alexa+ cost $19.99 per month for non-Prime members and was only available on newer Fire TV devices Amazon announced last fall. Now, Amazon says all users with compatible Fire TV hardware will be upgraded to Alexa+ automatically — no app download or subscription sign-up required.

Compatible devices include all current-generation Amazon Fire TV products sold in the US.

---

## Industry-Wide Push for AI on Consumer Devices

The move reflects a broader industry trend toward embedding AI assistants directly into consumer electronics. Google has similarly pushed its Gemini AI onto Android devices and TVs. These companies argue that broader availability drives user adoption metrics.

Amazon says users with Alexa+ engage in longer sessions and use more voice commands compared to the previous Alexa generation. The company says Alexa+ allows natural conversational queries — users no longer need to phrase requests as precise commands.

---

## What Alexa+ Brings to Fire TV

On Fire TV, Alexa+ enables conversational search across streaming services, smart home device control, and AI-powered content recommendations. Users can ask for show suggestions based on theme, mood, or actor rather than searching by exact title.

The expansion of Alexa+ to all Fire TV users marks Amazon's clearest statement yet that its AI assistant strategy prioritizes broad distribution over premium pricing — a notably different approach from the original $19.99 monthly tier.

---

*Amazon's Alexa+ Fire TV rollout was announced August 19, 2026.*
