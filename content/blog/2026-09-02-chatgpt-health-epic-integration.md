# ChatGPT Health Adds Epic Integration for Clinicians to Access Patient Records

---

## Bridging AI and Electronic Health Records

OpenAI has announced a significant integration between ChatGPT Health and Epic Systems, one of the largest electronic health record (EHR) providers in the United States. The integration allows clinicians to import patient data directly into ChatGPT for summarization and analysis.

With ChatGPT, clinicians can now access information including appointment notes, laboratory results, medications, and specialist documentation -- and summarize them easily. The system can also look at patient histories to provide contextual insights.

---

## New Healthcare Public Data Plugin

OpenAI is also adding a Healthcare Public Data plugin to ChatGPT, which can fetch information from ClinicalTrials.gov, CMS Coverage, RxNorm, DailyMed, and PubMed. This gives clinicians access to authoritative public health data without leaving the ChatGPT interface.

The company said that apart from enabling these new features, it is allowing organizations with a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) to use ChatGPT Work, Codex, apps, and connectors in their workspace for clinical documentation purposes.

---

## Healthcare AI Integration Landscape

The Epic integration represents a significant step in bringing AI into clinical workflows. Epic serves thousands of hospitals and clinics, meaning this integration could make AI-assisted clinical documentation available to a large portion of US healthcare providers.

The read-only access model addresses one of the key concerns around AI in healthcare: data security and patient privacy. By restricting ChatGPT to read-only access, clinicians can leverage AI analysis without risking unauthorized data modification.
