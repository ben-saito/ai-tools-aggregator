# HiddenLayer Raises $100M as Enterprises Rush to Secure AI Deployments

AI security company HiddenLayer has secured $100 million in new funding as enterprises intensify efforts to protect their AI systems from emerging threats. The round values the company at over $1 billion, making it the latest AI security unicorn.

---

## Why AI Security Is Growing

As AI agents proliferate across enterprise environments, security companies are racing to build products that can monitor not just the agents themselves but also the tools, plugins, and add-ons they interact with. Traditional security tools were not designed for AI systems that can autonomously execute tasks and access sensitive data.

HiddenLayer's platform focuses on detecting adversarial attacks against AI models, including prompt injection, model extraction, and data poisoning attempts. The company reports that demand has accelerated as enterprises move from AI pilots to production deployments.

---

## The Agent Security Challenge

AI agents introduce new attack surfaces that security teams are still learning to defend. Unlike traditional software, AI agents can follow natural language instructions, access multiple systems through APIs, and exhibit emergent behaviors that are difficult to predict. This creates unique security challenges that require specialized tooling.

---

## Market Opportunity

The AI security market is projected to grow significantly as AI adoption accelerates. Enterprises are increasingly required to demonstrate AI security compliance as regulators focus on the risks associated with autonomous AI systems. HiddenLayer's position as a pure-play AI security vendor makes it a strategic acquisition target or partner for larger security vendors.
