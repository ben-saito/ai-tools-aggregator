# Brad Lightcap, OpenAI's Longtime COO, Is Leaving to 'Start Something New'

---

## One of OpenAI's Most Senior Executives Departs

Brad Lightcap, one of the longest-serving executives at OpenAI, is leaving the company to embark on a new venture. Lightcap, who joined OpenAI in 2018, spent four years as the company's chief financial officer before ascending to the role of chief operating officer in 2022. Earlier this year, amid a broader shakeup of executive roles at the company, Lightcap transitioned to lead special projects.

In a message to staff, Lightcap reflected on his tenure: "I had the privilege of building the first versions of most of our operations and business teams — from Finance to Legal, People, CorpSec, GTM/Gov, Partnerships, and more. Among the most rewarding parts of this journey for me has been watching each of these teams mature under brilliant leaders."

Lightcap also hinted at future plans while offering few specifics: "I'm excited to help you all advance the mission from a different vantage point."

---

## A Period of Executive Turnover at OpenAI

Lightcap's departure comes as OpenAI prepares for an IPO of industrywide significance. The company has been undergoing a significant shakeup of top-level executives in recent months. In July 2026, Fidji Simo, the company's No. 2 executive who led AGI development, announced she would be stepping down. Other recent departures include Bill Peebles, who formerly headed the company's now-defunct video generator Sora, and Kevin Weil, vice president of the company's Science vertical.

Prior to joining OpenAI, Lightcap worked alongside CEO Sam Altman at venture capital firm Y Combinator, giving him a long-standing relationship with the company's leadership that predates his seven years at the AI lab.

---

## Implications for OpenAI's Corporate Structure

Lightcap's exit represents the loss of one of the architects of OpenAI's operational infrastructure. As CFO and then COO, he built many of the systems and teams that allowed the company to scale from a research organization into one of the most valuable AI companies in the world. His departure raises questions about continuity as OpenAI navigates its transition to a public company.

The timing is notable: OpenAI is widely reported to be preparing for a public listing that would be one of the most significant technology IPOs in recent memory. The departure of key executives during this preparatory period could attract scrutiny from investors and regulators alike.

---

## What's Next for Lightcap

As of this writing, no details have been disclosed about Lightcap's next venture. His note to staff suggested he is planning something new in the AI space, though he declined to provide specifics. Given his deep experience building operational infrastructure at one of the world's leading AI labs, any future venture is likely to attract significant attention from investors and industry observers alike.
