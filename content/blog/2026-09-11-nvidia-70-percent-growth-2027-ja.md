# Nvidia、2027年に70%の成長を予測 -- 黄仁勲CEOがゴールドマン・サックスCEOconferenceで明かす

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang appeared at the Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference on September 4 (US time) to discuss the sustained growth of AI-focused GPU demand. Nvidia projects continued growth through 2027, defying concerns about market saturation.

## Nvidia Defies Competition in Crowded GPU Market

Hyperscalers including Amazon, Microsoft, and Google are each building their own AI chips, while AI labs Anthropic and OpenAI are also developing proprietary silicon. Yet Jensen Huang emphasized that demand for Nvidia GPUs shows no signs of slowing despite this intensifying competition.

"Most people think Nvidia builds a chip. I mean, you need airplanes to ship what we build," Huang said, underscoring that Nvidia's physical manufacturing and shipping capacity continues to scale. He stressed that the company's deals are "not circular," pushing back against perception that its growth is artificially sustained.

## Data Center Business Drives Growth

The vast majority of Nvidia's revenue comes from data center GPUs. Huang told the Goldman Sachs conference that AI infrastructure investment will accelerate for at least the next several years. Cloud service providers continue to add Nvidia GPUs for both model training and inference workloads, with no signs of slowdown in sight.

Nvidia's growth forecast of 70% for next year reflects continued strength across all major customer segments, with no single customer representing more than 10% of total revenue -- a diversification that Huang called a key buffer against any single customer's decision to build their own silicon.

## Competing Chip Startups Face Uphill Battle

Newly public competitor Cerebras and startups like Etched have emerged as potential challengers, but Nvidia's CUDA software ecosystem -- now deeply embedded across the AI industry -- creates a moat that hardware alone cannot easily overcome. "The software ecosystem is everything," Huang noted, explaining why customers continue to choose Nvidia despite lower-priced alternatives.

The 70% growth projection comes despite recent signs that some hyperscalers are diversifying their chip suppliers, a trend analysts had speculated could dent Nvidia's dominance. Huang's confident outlook suggests Nvidia believes its hardware performance lead and software advantages will sustain its market position through at least 2027.
