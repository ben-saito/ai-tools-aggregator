# Destro AI Comes Out of Stealth With $8M Seed, Claims Unique Approach to Human-Robot Collaboration

Destro AI, a logistics robotics startup, emerged from stealth on September 30 with an $8 million seed round and a counter-intuitive pitch: the best way to deploy robots successfully is to stop focusing on building robots.

---

## The Approach

Rather than developing proprietary robot hardware or training dedicated AI models for specific manipulation tasks, Destro has built an intelligence layer that sits between robots and human workers in logistics environments. The system coordinates both robotic and human labor from a single control plane.

"We are not a robotics company," said founder Pawar, who holds a master's degree in robotics from NYU Tandon and has eight years of experience in the U.S. supply chain and robotics industry. "One of the biggest reasons we are winning against robotics companies is because we are not a robotics company."

The Destro system tells human workers what to do in addition to directing robots, creating a unified workflow that Pawar claims is more adaptable to real-world logistics complexity than pure automation approaches.

---

## Customer Traction

Yusen Logistics, a South Korean shipping giant with approximately 30 facilities across the U.S., has deployed Destro's system under the guidance of Richard Brunelle, director of automation for the American logistics group. The company uses fixed automation including conveyors and sorters but has historically struggled with adaptive robotic integration.

Brunelle helped validate the Destro approach in live warehouse environments, where unpredictable conditions and variable package types create challenges that fixed automation cannot address.

---

## Business Model

Pawar claims Destro is on a path to cash flow positive by the end of the year, a bold claim for a hardware-adjacent robotics startup. The company's software-first model, which does not require customers to purchase new robot hardware, is central to this argument.

The $8 million seed will be used to expand deployments and develop additional integrations with existing warehouse management systems.
