# Meta Unveils Muse Charm: A Tamagotchi-Style Wearable for Its AI Agent

Meta has introduced Muse Charm, a palm-sized wearable device that gives users a physical way to interact with its AI assistant Muse. Unveiled at Meta's annual Connect event as a "one more thing" surprise, the keychain-sized device features a tiny screen displaying a digital avatar called Jolly, letting users talk to Muse without unlocking their phone.

---

## A Physical Home for an AI Agent

Muse, Meta's personal AI assistant, now has a dedicated hardware companion. The Muse Charm is a small totem roughly the size of a stack of poker chips that can be attached to a keychain or carried in a pocket. A built-in screen shows Jolly, an animated avatar that represents the AI agent. Users wake the device by tapping a fingerprint sensor in the corner and can immediately start speaking to Muse.

"We packed the whole Muse experience, including the whole real-time voice and avatar stack, into something that fits on a keychain and is always available to talk to," CEO Mark Zuckerberg said during the keynote. "You just go ahead and tap here in the fingerprint sensor in the corner, and you can start talking without having to unlock a phone or open an app."

The device connects to Muse through Bluetooth when paired with a smartphone, serving as a always-available voice interface to the AI agent. Meta did not disclose the processor or other hardware specifications during the announcement.

---

## Holiday 2026 Ship Target

Muse Charm is not yet shipping. Zuckerberg said the team was still "finalizing laying out the components" and promised the devices would be "ready to ship in time for the holidays in December." Pricing was not announced.

The product places Meta in direct competition with other physical AI companions like Friend, a wearable that raised both investment and skepticism for its premise of always-on AI conversation on the body. Meta's advantage is the existingMuse ecosystem and distribution reach through Ray-Ban retail partnerships.

---

## Muse Expands Beyond Smart Glasses

Muse Charm represents a step in Meta's broader strategy to give its AI agent a presence across multiple hardware form factors. Meta's Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses already run Muse, and the new camera-free Ray-Ban Meta Audio glasses announced at the same event also integrate the assistant. Adding a dedicated wearable gives users another option for hands-free AI interaction.
