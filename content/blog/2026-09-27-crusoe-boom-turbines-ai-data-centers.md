# Crusoe abandons $1.25B plan to use Boom turbines at AI data centers

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/crusoe-abandons-1-25b-plan-to-use-boom-turbines-at-ai-data-centers/)

---

Crusoe, the AI-focused data center operator, has abandoned its $1.25 billion plan to use Boom Supersonic's turbine technology for power generation at its AI data centers. The reversal marks a significant shift in how AI companies are thinking about energy infrastructure.

Boom Supersonic CEO Blake Scholl confirmed that the company's new stationary power plants were no longer part of Crusoe's near-term plans. The original partnership, announced as a novel approach to pairing high-speed aircraft turbine technology with compute infrastructure, appears to have been scrapped in favor of more conventional power solutions.

The collapse of the turbine deal highlights the intense pressure on AI infrastructure companies to secure reliable, scalable power as data center demand skyrockets. Crusoe, which has raised billions in venture capital to build out AI factories, appears to be recalibrating its technical approach after concluding that turbine-based power was not viable at the required scale or timeline.

Boom Supersonic has been developing its Renaissance engine for use in commercial aircraft, with the stationary power plant variant representing an attempt to diversify the technology into non-aviation applications. The company will need to find alternative customers for its power generation systems.

For AI companies building massive data centers, the incident underscores that energy infrastructure decisions made in boardrooms and engineering labs can unravel quickly when meets the demands of large-scale compute operations.
