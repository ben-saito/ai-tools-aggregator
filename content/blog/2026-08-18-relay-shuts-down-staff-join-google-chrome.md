# AI Automation Startup Relay Shuts Down, Staff Joins Google's Chrome Team

---

## The Shutdown

Relay, an AI-powered workflow automation tool launched in 2021 with ambitions to become the next Zapier, is shutting down. The company's application will cease operations for paying customers on September 14, 2026. Free tier users lost access on August 15.

---

## Staff Move to Google

Some of Relay's staff, including founder and CEO Jacob Bank, are joining Google's Chrome team. The announcement comes with Bank's comment: "We have some really ambitious plans to help you work with AI in Chrome to get things done, and I'll have more to share soon."

---

## Background

Relay was founded in 2021 with the goal of automating complex workflows using AI. The startup aimed to simplify how businesses connect their tools and automate repetitive tasks through natural language interfaces.

---

## Assessment

The acquisition of Relay's talent by Google signals continued interest in embedding AI-powered automation directly into the browser. Chrome's team may be looking to expand AI-assisted workflow capabilities within the browser environment.
