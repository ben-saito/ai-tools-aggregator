# Ando Aims to Be the Slack for Human and AI Agent Collaboration

Ando has launched a team messaging platform designed from the ground up for collaboration between humans and AI agents, TechCrunch reported on September 24, 2026. The product gives agents their own identities, inboxes, and the ability to participate naturally in team conversations.

---

## Design for Agent Integration

Unlike traditional messaging tools where AI assistants are peripheral integrations, Ando treats AI agents as first-class participants. Each agent gets a user identity, can receive direct messages, and can contribute to group channels. Agents can read context, respond to queries, and take actions within the messaging flow.

---

## Technical Architecture

The platform appears to be built around an agent protocol that defines how agents authenticate, communicate, and execute tasks within channels. This approach mirrors broader industry moves toward agent-to-agent (A2A) communication standards, but Ando's focus is specifically on the human-in-the-loop scenario.

---

## The Team Messaging Market

Slack and Microsoft Teams dominate enterprise messaging, but neither was designed with AI agent participation as a primary use case. Ando's bet is that as agents become common in workplace settings, the existing tools will feel kludgy when trying to accommodate them.

---

## Implications for Workflow Automation

If AI agents are to handle meaningful workplace tasks — scheduling, document retrieval, status updates, task coordination — they need to operate in the same communication channels as humans. Ando's approach suggests a future where team messaging is the operating system for agentic workflows.
