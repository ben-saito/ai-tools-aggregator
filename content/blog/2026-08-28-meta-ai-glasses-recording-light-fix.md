# Meta AI Glasses Fixes Recording Loophole but Privacy Risks Remain

Meta has closed a key loophole in its AI glasses that allowed users to record people without their knowledge by covering up the device's recording indicator light.

---

## The Loophole and Its Fix

Meta had previously deactivated recording if a user tried to start capturing video after placing a sticker over the LED safety light. However, users quickly learned they could bypass this by covering the light after starting the recording.

In a post on Threads, Meta's executive vice president of wearables, Alex Himel, explained that an update would ensure "the camera will now stop working if the light is covered during a recording." The change means "the capture LED can reliably alert bystanders when photos or videos are being captured for someone's gallery," Himel said.

According to Meta, "only a tiny minority of people try to get around these protections, and the fact they have to go to these lengths shows our efforts are working."

---

## Remaining Privacy Concerns

Bad actors may still be able to modify the glasses or tamper with the light without impacting recordings. Semi-transparent stickers that make the light less noticeable could potentially still allow recording under the new safeguard.

EU data regulators are investigating privacy concerns with Meta's glasses, with Germany considering a ban after a nonprofit filed a criminal complaint alleging the devices breach Germany's strict privacy laws. The European Data Protection Board is expected to release a report later this summer on actions to prevent nonconsensual recording.

A proposed class action lawsuit in the US accuses Meta of falsely marketing the glasses with claims of being "Built for your privacy and others' too" despite known privacy risks.

---

## Meta's Marketing Push

Meta has launched a new marketing campaign in Los Angeles featuring billboards that highlight the LED light feature, with the caption: "The camera lets you capture the moment. The light lets everyone around you know when you do."
