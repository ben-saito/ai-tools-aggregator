# OpenAI Launches Dots: Always-On Personal AI Agents for Pro and Business Users

OpenAI announced Dots at its DevDay event on September 29. Powered by GPT-6 Astra, Dots are persistent personal agents designed to operate independently of any specific hardware or interface, pursuing user-defined goals continuously in the background with minimal oversight.

---

## Distinct from Codex and ChatGPT

Unlike Codex, which focuses on software engineering tasks, and ChatGPT, which operates through conversation, Dots are designed to run autonomously over extended periods. Users can name their primary dot and personalize its behavior. Over time, OpenAI envisions teams of dots working together on behalf of a single user.

---

## Multi-Agent Collaboration Vision

OpenAI's announcement describes scenarios where developers deploy dedicated dots for specific domains: one for code documentation, another for email management. The vision extends to coordinated agent teams handling complex workflows without requiring constant user intervention.

---

## Availability and Pricing

Dots launched September 29 in ChatGPT, available to Pro and Business Premium users in eligible markets at no additional cost. General availability for all users remains undetermined.

---

## Competitive Landscape

Dots enters an increasingly crowded agent market alongside Anthropic's Claude Computer Use, Google's Gemini Agent Mode, and Meta's Muse Agent. OpenAI differentiates through ChatGPT's existing user base of 1.2 billion weekly active users, aiming for frictionless deployment through familiar interfaces.

---

## Technical Considerations

Long-running autonomous agents present challenges around stability, resource consumption, and unexpected behavior prevention. OpenAI's approach prioritizes user-defined goal pursuit within configurable oversight parameters, with the primary technical challenge being reliable long-horizon operation without unintended system interactions.
