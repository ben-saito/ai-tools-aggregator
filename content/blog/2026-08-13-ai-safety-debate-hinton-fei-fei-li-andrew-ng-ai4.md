# AI Safety Pioneers Debate Openness: Hinton, Fei-Fei Li, and Andrew Ng at Ai4

At the Ai4 2026 conference, three of the world's most respected AI experts—Geoffrey Hinton, Fei-Fei Li, and Andrew Ng—came together to debate the future of AI regulation, open-source access, and how America can maintain its competitive edge as China advances in AI development across Asia.

---

## The Case for Staying Open

Despite mounting safety concerns across the AI industry, all three pioneers made the case for keeping AI systems open and accessible. The debate centered on a fundamental tension: how to ensure AI safety without stifling innovation or ceding ground to competitors abroad.

---

## Regulation and Competition

The panel addressed the growing calls for AI regulation, with each expert offering a different perspective on how to balance safety with competitiveness. China's rapid advancement in AI was a recurring theme, with the experts debating what policy responses, if any, would best serve America's interests.

---

## Open-Source vs. Closed Systems

A key point of discussion was the open-source versus closed-source debate. Proponents of open AI systems argue that transparency enables better external scrutiny and faster collective improvement. Critics counter that openness makes it easier for bad actors to misuse the technology.

---

## Industry Implications

The Ai4 debate reflects a broader reckoning in the AI community. As AI systems become more powerful and pervasive, the decisions made around openness, regulation, and international competition will shape the technology's trajectory for decades.
