# Former SpaceX Engineers Build Robotic Factory for Steel Parts

Former SpaceX engineers have launched a new venture to build robotic factories for manufacturing steel parts, signaling a new wave of automation in industrial manufacturing.

---

## From Rocketry to Manufacturing

The team, comprised of engineers who worked on SpaceX's rocket development program, is now applying their expertise in precision engineering and autonomous systems to the steel fabrication industry. The venture aims to bring the same level of innovation that disrupted the aerospace launch market to traditional manufacturing.

"We're not necessarily building in a dogmatic fashion towards full autonomy," the founders explained, signaling a pragmatic approach that combines human oversight with robotic precision.

---

## Targeting Manufacturing Inefficiency

The steel parts manufacturing sector has long been characterized by manual-intensive processes and relatively slow adoption of automation compared to other industries. This new entrant aims to change that calculus by deploying advanced robotics and AI-guided fabrication systems that can operate with higher precision and consistency than traditional methods.
