# ChatGPT Health Integrates with Epic EHR for Clinical Data Access

OpenAI has expanded ChatGPT Health's capabilities with a direct integration into Epic's electronic health record system, giving clinicians read-only access to patient data directly within the AI workflow. The integration covers over 325 million patient records across Epic's hospital and clinic network.

---

## Integration Details

The integration allows clinicians to query ChatGPT using patient information from Epic's EHR without switching between applications. In some deployments, ChatGPT will appear directly within Epic's clinical workflow, enabling AI-assisted chart review and documentation support.

OpenAI emphasized that the integration is read-only at the data access level — ChatGPT cannot modify records. The AI processes information to generate summaries, flag relevant clinical findings, and assist with documentation, but all data remains within Epic's infrastructure.

---

## Clinical Use Cases

Early deployments focus on reducing documentation burden and improving information retrieval. Clinicians can ask ChatGPT to synthesize information across multiple visits, identify gaps in care history, or draft preliminary notes based on chart data.

The integration reflects a broader trend of healthcare systems piloting large language models for workflow assistance rather than diagnostic decision-making. By positioning ChatGPT as a clinical information assistant rather than a diagnostic tool, OpenAI navigates regulatory expectations more conservatively.

---

## Epic's AI Ecosystem

Epic has been expanding its AI partnership ecosystem across multiple model providers. The ChatGPT integration follows agreements with other major AI companies and reflects Epic's strategy of offering clinicians choice among AI tools within the EHR environment.

Healthcare systems using Epic can enable or disable specific AI integrations at the organization level, giving IT and clinical leadership control over which tools are available to staff.

---

## Regulatory Considerations

Healthcare AI deployments face heightened scrutiny around data privacy and clinical validity. OpenAI's read-only approach sidesteps some regulatory complexity, but healthcare organizations still need to evaluate ChatGPT's outputs for accuracy and ensure clinicians remain accountable for decisions informed by AI assistance.

The integration's success will likely depend on whether it demonstrably reduces clinician administrative load without introducing new compliance risks.
