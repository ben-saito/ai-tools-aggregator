# Anthropic to Watermark AI-Generated Text Under EU AI Act Transparency Code

Anthropic has announced it will add text watermarking capabilities to its AI models to comply with the EU AI Act's Transparency Code, which took effect on August 2, 2026. The watermarking will embed detectable signals into text generated by Claude and other models.

---

## EU AI Act Transparency Code: A Compliance Mandate

The EU AI Act Transparency Code requires AI companies to label AI-generated or AI-edited content in a way that other systems can identify. This applies to generated content, edited content, and any content modified using AI tools.

Companies are now rushing to watermark AI-generated content to comply with regulations and avoid backlash. Recently, AI music platform Suno announced it would label tracks created on its platform.

---

## How the Watermarking Works

For models released after August 2, Anthropic will automatically add watermarking to both computer-generated text and files. For files, the company uses the C2PA open standard.

The watermark is embedded in the text itself, meaning it persists through copy-paste operations in most cases. Some editing operations may also preserve the watermark signal. However, it remains unclear how much editing is required to remove the watermark — Anthropic has not yet provided specific details.

---

## Strategic and Economic Implications

Complying with the EU AI Act carries significant economic implications for AI companies. The Transparency Code, while technically a compliance regulation, carries substantial enforcement weight. Companies must extend support to models already in production, driving a collective push toward watermarking infrastructure.

Watermarking will apply across Claude platform, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, and Claude Tag products.
