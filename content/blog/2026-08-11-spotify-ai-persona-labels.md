# Spotify Will Label 'AI Persona' Profiles and Exclude Their Music From Recommendations

Spotify announced on August 11, 2026 that it will begin labeling artist profiles representing AI-generated identities with "AI Persona" badges and exclude their music from editorial, algorithmic, and personalized recommendations by default.

---

## AI Persona Labels: What Changes

Starting mid-September, Spotify users will see "AI Persona" badges appear on artist profiles that signal the artist\'s identity is AI-generated, rather than representing a real person. The badges will appear in the profile banner, the About section, in Search, and on track rows across playlists.

Spotify says it will not rely solely on self-disclosure. The company will also actively review artist profiles and identify those where the artist\'s name and imagery appear to represent photorealistic AI-generated identities. The review will prioritize profiles that meet pre-defined audience thresholds, ensuring the most-listened-to artists are covered first.

Once labeled, AI Persona music will be excluded from Spotify\'s editorial and algorithmic recommendations by default. Only users who explicitly follow an AI Persona will receive personalized recommendations from that artist.

---

## Industry Context

This policy expansion builds on Spotify\'s existing AI guidelines, first announced in September 2025, which identify and label AI music using industry-standard techniques and ban unauthorized AI voice clones and deepfakes.

Spotify is attempting to balance AI-driven innovations — including AI-powered Prompted Playlists, AI DJ, AI remixes, and forthcoming AI-generated covers permitted under recent licensing deals with UMG and Merlin — while preventing the proliferation of low-quality AI-generated content.

The move also signals growing consumer backlash against AI-generated art. Spotify stated: "While we believe all artists have creative choice in determining how they present themselves, Spotify\'s programming is focused on elevating music from authentic artists building careers in music."

Artists will be able to self-disclose as AI Personas starting August 11, 2026 through Spotify for Artists. Users will also gain the ability to report unlabeled AI Personas in the coming months.
