# Spotify to Label AI-Generated Artists with "AI Persona" Badges, Exclude from Recommendations

Spotify announced on August 11, 2026 that it will begin labeling AI-generated artist profiles with "AI Persona" tags and exclude their music from editorial and algorithmic recommendations. The new labeling system will roll out starting mid-September.

---

## Labeling AI-Generated Artists

Spotify will attach "AI Persona" badges to artist profiles that represent AI-generated identities. The company says it will not rely solely on self-disclosure by artists—instead, it will proactively review profiles and identify those where the artist's name and imagery appear to represent photorealistic AI-generated identities.

The review will prioritize artists who have met predefined audience thresholds, ensuring that the most-listened-to AI-generated artists are covered first.

---

## Default Exclusion from Recommendations

Under Spotify's new policy, AI Persona music will be excluded by default from:

- **Editorial recommendations** — playlists curated by Spotify's editorial team
- **Algorithmic recommendations** — personalized suggestions based on listening history
- **Personalized recommendations** — suggestions learned from other users' behavior patterns

Users who explicitly follow an AI Persona artist will still receive that artist's music in their recommendations.

---

## Context: AI-Generated Content in Music Streaming

This move highlights the growing challenge of AI-generated content in the music streaming industry. Cases have been reported where AI-generated tracks were presented as human-created works, and songs mimicking existing artists' styles using AI have appeared in playlists.

Spotify's new labeling policy will allow users to identify whether content is AI-generated. The company says it will combine self-declaration with technical analysis to determine which profiles receive the AI Persona tag.

---

## Looking Ahead

After the mid-September rollout, users will be able to check which AI Persona artists they follow. Artists who wish to label their own work as AI-generated can apply to Spotify for the designation.

This initiative is part of a broader industry effort to improve AI ethics and transparency in music streaming platforms.
