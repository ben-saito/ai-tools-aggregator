# Google to Exempt Developers in Sanctioned Nations from Android Developer Verification

Google has confirmed that developers located in sanctioned countries—including Iran, Cuba, North Korea, and occupied areas of Ukraine—will be exempt from upcoming Android developer verification requirements. The exemption means developers in these regions can continue distributing apps globally without going through Google's new identity verification process, while developers in other countries face mandatory verification starting September 30.

---

## The Developer Verification Program

Google is rolling out a new "Advanced Verification" program that requires all Android developers to verify their identity before distributing apps through Google Play. The program is designed to improve app store security and reduce malware distribution.

The verification requirements are scheduled to launch on September 30 in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, and Thailand, with global expansion expected in 2027.

---

## The Sanctions Exemption

For developers in sanctioned nations, Google has taken a different approach. According to a Google spokesperson confirmed to Ars Technica, people living in countries subject to US sanctions will not be able to go through the verification process at all—meaning they are effectively exempt.

The practical outcome is paradoxical: developers in Iran, Cuba, and other sanctioned nations will retain the ability to freely distribute apps globally, including through Google Play, while developers in non-sanctioned countries face new verification requirements.

Currently, developers in sanctioned nations can already distribute apps globally, and the new exemption preserves that status quo. Developers in these regions will not be able to verify their accounts, but they also will not lose the ability to publish.

---

## Why This Matters for Developers

For Android developers in non-sanctioned countries, the new verification requirements represent a significant change. The process includes identity verification, ongoing compliance checks, and potential restrictions for developers who cannot pass verification.

For developers in sanctioned nations, the exemption preserves an important distribution channel. Many developers in countries like Iran and Cuba use Google Play to reach global audiences, and the exemption ensures they can continue doing so without needing to navigate US sanctions compliance.

Google is implementing the verification program in phases, with the sanctions exemption applying specifically to developers physically located in sanctioned jurisdictions.
