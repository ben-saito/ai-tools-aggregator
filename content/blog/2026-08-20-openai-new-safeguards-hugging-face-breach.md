# OpenAI Announces New Security Safeguards Following Hugging Face Breach

OpenAI has announced a new batch of security policies focused on containing security incidents during model development and testing. The new safeguards come in response to a breach at Hugging Face and aim to prevent similar incidents.

---

## Enhanced Monitoring During Development

The new safeguards include more detailed monitoring of models during the development process, as well as greater emphasis on alignment and security during the post-training process. OpenAI acknowledged that as models become more capable, the risks associated with developing and testing them internally also grow.

"Our standards for monitoring, alignment, and security will continue to evolve as capabilities improve," the company said in a blog post.

---

## Learning from the Hugging Face Incident

The timing of the announcement follows a breach at Hugging Face, another major AI platform. The incident underscored the security challenges involved in managing model development infrastructure and the potential risks when security protocols fail.

OpenAI's new policies represent a broader industry shift toward treating AI model security as a first-class concern during the entire development lifecycle, not just at deployment time.
