# Google Tests Gemini AI Shopping Integration with Flipkart in India

Google is testing a feature that allows users in India to purchase products directly through Gemini and AI Mode, using Walmart-owned Flipkart as the initial retail partner. The limited pilot covers select products and users, with a broader rollout planned for later in October ahead of India's festive shopping season.

---

## From Search Queries to Transactions

The test marks a significant step in Google's strategy to move AI-powered services beyond information retrieval and into direct commerce. Rather than simply recommending products in response to shopping queries, Gemini and AI Mode can now initiate and complete purchases within the chat interface.

Google introduced the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) earlier this year, describing it as an open standard designed to let AI agents interact with retailers across the entire shopping journey, including checkout. Flipkart is among the first merchants to integrate with this protocol, enabling what Google calls "agentic" shopping experiences.

---

## Google's Financial Stake in Flipkart

Beyond the technology partnership, Google holds a financial interest in Flipkart. In 2024, Google invested approximately $350 million in Flipkart as part of a funding round led by Walmart, acquiring a minority stake in the e-commerce company. This dual relationship — both as a technology partner and a minority investor — positions Google uniquely in India's competitive e-commerce landscape.

India represents the world's second-largest internet market with more than a billion subscribers. Flipkart and Amazon compete fiercely for these online shoppers, with competition intensifying dramatically during the country's festive season when e-commerce companies roll out their largest sales events.

---

## Industry-Wide Push Toward Agentic Commerce

Google is not alone in this push. Rival OpenAI and others are adding commerce capabilities to their AI offerings, aiming to transition from answering shopping questions and recommending products to playing a more direct role in online transactions.

The October rollout is timed to coincide with India's festive shopping season, one of the most important periods for e-commerce companies in the country.
