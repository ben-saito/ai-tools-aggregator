# Google Shut Down Earth AI Feature One Day After Launch Over Misinformation Concerns

Google has pulled its newly launched Earth AI feature just one day after release, following widespread backlash over concerns that the tool could be used to create and spread AI-generated fake imagery superimposed on real geographic locations. The tool, which allowed users to generate AI-crafted images and layer them onto Google Earth satellite views, quickly drew criticism from researchers, policymakers, and platform integrity advocates who warned it could supercharge geographic misinformation campaigns.

The feature was announced and deployed without prominent safeguards or watermarks indicating AI-generated content, leading observers to raise alarms about its potential use in disinformation, fraud, and harassment. Within 24 hours of launch, Google reversed course and disabled the feature pending further review.

This rapid reversal underscores the growing tension between AI capability deployment and platform safety. Unlike text-based AI systems where output can be audited and filtered, image generation combined with real-world geography creates a new category of risk where fabricated scenes can be anchored to verifiable real locations, making verification substantially harder for average users.

The incident adds to a pattern of AI product launches where safety evaluations lag behind capability demonstrations. Google has not disclosed what review process, if any, was conducted before the feature went live, nor has it provided a timeline for a potential relaunch with enhanced safeguards.
