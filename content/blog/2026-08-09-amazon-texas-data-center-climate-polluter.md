# Amazon Texas Data Center Plan Could Become Largest US Climate Polluter

Amazon plans to build an on-site power plant as part of a Texas data center project that could become the largest source of climate pollution in the United States, according to reports.

---

## Background

Amazon is the largest cloud services provider in the US, rapidly expanding data center investments. AWS handles approximately one-third of the world's cloud infrastructure, requiring substantial electricity for operations.

---

## Environmental Impact

The plan calls for an on-site fossil fuel power plant to be built alongside the data center. Such facilities emit large amounts of carbon dioxide and nitrogen oxides, potentially becoming the largest climate polluter in the US under current regulations.

AI model training requires energy comparable to a data center's entire annual electricity consumption, and major tech companies including Google, Microsoft, and Amazon are rapidly expanding their data centers.

---

## Industry-Wide Issue

Data center power consumption has surged with AI adoption. According to IEA estimates, global data center power consumption could reach up to 3,000 terawatt-hours by 2026.

Amazon is not alone. Microsoft, Google, and Meta have all announced plans for new large-scale data centers, each requiring substantial energy resources.

---

## Response and Future

Amazon has pledged to transition to renewable energy, but the on-site fossil fuel power plant plan has drawn criticism from environmental groups. Concerns about delays in clean energy transition continue to grow.
