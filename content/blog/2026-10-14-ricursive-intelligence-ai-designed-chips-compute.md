# Ricursive Intelligence Uses AI to Design AI Chips, Aiming to Solve the Compute Crisis

---

Ricursive Intelligence, a startup founded by former Google DeepMind and Intel engineers, is developing AI systems that can design chips faster and more efficiently than traditional methods. Founders Anna Goldie and Azalia Mirhoseini took the stage at TechCrunch Disrupt to outline their approach to solving the compute crisis through AI-driven chip design.

## The Compute Crisis

Goldie outlined the scale of the problem: "In the next five years, we are going to have at least 10,000x more demand for compute." Rather than building exponentially more data centers, Ricursive's approach focuses on making chips more efficient — using bandwidth, wafers, and fabrication facilities more effectively. "Even if we make [data centers] in space or something, I don't know, you won't see the sun anymore," Goldie said.

## AI Designing Chips

The core of Ricursive's technology uses AI to explore the chip design space autonomously. The system can design chips, learn from each iteration, and improve subsequent designs. This recursive approach allows for rapid exploration of unconventional layout that human engineers might not discover.

"AI is challenging traditional chip design methods by discovering unconventional layouts that can outperform traditional ones," the company explains. For example, AlphaChip — an AI system the founders previously developed — produced a circular, "donut-shaped" arrangement of chip components that initially surprised engineers but ultimately proved more successful than conventional designs.

## Optimization Approach

The founders explained that they are optimizing entire chips rather than following the traditional approach of partitioning them into dozens of smaller blocks. This approach allows AI to create intricate patterns, including wave-like designs. The pair said they plan to share more about these designs in the future.

## The Human-AI Partnership

Goldie framed the approach as a partnership: "Humans bring their great ideas and creative thoughts, and execution is handled by AI." This paradigm is appearing across chip design, software development, and other fields.

"If it takes six months or a year or two years to design a new chip, then you really have to be extremely selective about the chips that you design," Goldie said. "But if you can design a chip in like a week... you can design custom chips for all sorts of different types of applications in space and AR, VR, and biological computing — it makes sense financially to do that, and we can be more energy efficient."

## Background

Ricursive Intelligence was founded by Anna Goldie and Azalia Mirhoseini, both previously from Google DeepMind and Intel. The company is part of a broader trend of applying machine learning to hardware design, with implications for AI training and inference efficiency.
