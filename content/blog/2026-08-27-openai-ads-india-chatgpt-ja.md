# OpenAI to Start Showing Ads on ChatGPT's Free and Go Tiers in India

OpenAI has announced plans to introduce advertisements on ChatGPT's free tier and the lower-priced Go tier in India, targeting the country's massive and growing user base. India represents one of OpenAI's largest markets, with over 100 million weekly active ChatGPT users.

The decision reflects OpenAI's evolving monetization strategy as the company seeks to diversify revenue beyond API subscriptions. By introducing ads on the free and lower-priced tiers, OpenAI can capture advertising revenue from price-sensitive markets where a full subscription may be out of reach.

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has previously indicated the company would consider advertising as a revenue stream, though the company has been cautious about ad placement to avoid disrupting the user experience.

---

## India's Strategic Importance

India's 100 million-plus weekly active ChatGPT users represent a huge opportunity for ad monetization. The country has a rapidly growing smartphone user base and an advertising market projected to reach billions of dollars annually. For OpenAI, India serves as both a large user base and a testbed for ad-supported AI products.

The move comes as competition in the Indian AI assistant market intensifies. Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, and local competitors are all vying for market share in the country, making advertising an attractive way to keep ChatGPT free for users while generating revenue.

---

## Ad Format and User Experience

OpenAI has not disclosed specific ad formats, but the company has emphasized it plans to keep advertisements unobtrusive. The ads are expected to appear in the ChatGPT interface, likely as sponsored prompts or contextual suggestions.

The company has been hiring advertising specialists in recent months, signaling the seriousness of its ad-supported ambitions. Industry observers will be watching to see how OpenAI balances monetization with user experience.
