# Anthropic Signs $10B Compute Deal with AI Cloud Startup Volta

Anthropic has signed a six-year, $10 billion deal with AI cloud startup Volta to secure compute capacity for its Claude models—backed by crypto-mining company Bitdeer and Nvidia's GPU infrastructure.

---

## Volta, Bitdeer, and Nvidia's Cloud Partner Program

Volta, founded earlier this year, will provide cloud compute to Anthropic over a six-year period. The deal includes Bitdeer, a crypto-mining company that will develop the data center in Norway. The facility will deliver 133 megawatts of capacity, powered by Nvidia's Vera Rubin systems.

Volta is part of Nvidia's Cloud Partner program—a consortium of AI cloud providers using Nvidia GPUs in their data centers.

---

## Anthropic's Compute Expansion Spree

The Volta deal is the latest in a series of compute infrastructure announcements from Anthropic. The company has aggressively expanded its GPU capacity as it competes directly with OpenAI, Google, and Amazon for AI supremacy.

Anthropic has also announced compute partnerships with SpaceX and Amazon. The company is racing to secure enough training infrastructure to support next-generation Claude releases.

*This article is based on information from August 4, 2026.*
