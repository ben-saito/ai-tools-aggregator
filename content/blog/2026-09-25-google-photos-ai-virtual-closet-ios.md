# Google Photos AI Virtual Closet Now Available on iOS After Android Rollout

Google has expanded its AI-powered virtual closet feature, inspired by the iconic outfit-planning scene in the 1995 film "Clueless," to both Android and iOS platforms. The feature, first unveiled in June 2026, uses AI to analyze users' existing wardrobe photos and create a digital closet for mixing and matching outfits.

---

## From Film Inspiration to Real-World Fashion AI

The feature draws direct inspiration from the famous scene in "Clueless" where Cher Horowitz uses her computer to plan outfits with a few clicks. Google's version accomplishes this through computer vision analysis of users' saved photos, identifying clothing items and organizing them into a searchable digital wardrobe.

Users can photograph individual pieces or let Google Photos automatically detect clothing from existing images. The AI then suggests combinations based on color theory, weather conditions, and personal style preferences stored in the app.

---

## Broader AI Features in Google Photos

The virtual closet launch is part of a broader push to embed generative AI throughout Google Photos. Google has been systematically adding AI features including smart editing tools, memory movies, and enhanced search capabilities powered by Gemini models.

Google's strategy positions Photos not just as image storage but as an AI-powered personal styling and memory assistant. The company has been rolling out these features incrementally, with the virtual closet representing one of the most consumer-facing AI applications in the Photos ecosystem.

The feature works without requiring the most recent Pixel hardware, though Pixel 11 owners receive early access to certain premium Gemini-powered features. iOS users can access the full feature set through the updated Google Photos app now available on the App Store.

---

## Technical Implementation

Google Photos uses multimodal AI to recognize clothing items across different photo contexts — from full-length mirror selfies to packed suitcases. The system categorizes items by type, color, pattern, and occasion, building a structured wardrobe database that updates as users add new photos.

Outfit suggestions leverage contextual signals including local weather forecasts, calendar events, and time of day. The AI considers formal versus casual dress codes and can learn user preferences over time through interaction patterns.

Google says the feature was designed with privacy in mind — all clothing analysis happens on-device where possible, with cloud processing used only for more complex outfit recommendations.

---

*Google Photos AI features are available as of September 2026. The virtual closet feature is rolling out globally on both Android and iOS platforms.*
