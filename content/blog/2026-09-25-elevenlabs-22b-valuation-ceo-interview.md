# ElevenLabs Reaches $22B Valuation in Latest Funding Round

ElevenLabs, the AI voice technology company powering customer service calls and audio experiences across the web, has reportedly reached a $22 billion valuation according to a TechCrunch interview with CEO Mateusz Slict. The valuation represents a significant leap from earlier funding rounds and underscores investor enthusiasm for voice AI infrastructure.

---

## The Business of AI Voice

ElevenLabs provides the underlying voice technology behind countless customer service interactions. According to the company, businesses should expect to eventually disclose when they're using AI voice agents — though the norm may shift to where having a human on the line becomes the notable exception.

The company has expanded beyond customer service into music generation, dubbing, and audiobook narration. Its voice synthesis technology is used by major media companies and is increasingly embedded in enterprise communication platforms.

---

## Competitive Landscape

The $22 billion valuation places ElevenLabs among the most valuable AI startups globally. Competitors include companies like Hume AI, Speechify, and WellSaid Labs, but ElevenLabs has differentiated through multilingual capabilities and a developer-friendly API ecosystem.

The company has raised substantial capital to scale its inference infrastructure and expand internationally. The voice AI market is projected to grow significantly as enterprises automate more customer touchpoints.

---

## Technical Approach

ElevenLabs uses proprietary neural voice synthesis models trained on diverse datasets. The system can generate expressive, context-aware voice output and supports real-time streaming for interactive applications. The company offers both SaaS API access and enterprise deployment options.
