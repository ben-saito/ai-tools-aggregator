# Wonderful Raises $550M at $5B Valuation, Doubling in Six Months

Israeli-Dutch AI startup Wonderful has raised $550 million in a Series C funding round that values the company at $5 billion, more than doubling its previous $2 billion valuation from nearly six months ago.

---

## Rapid Growth in Enterprise AI

Insight Partners led the round, returning from the company's previous funding. Existing investors Index Ventures, IVP, Vine Ventures, 9Yards, and Bessemer Venture Partners also participated. Notably, Salesforce invested in Wonderful for the first time.

Founded in early 2025, Wonderful initially gained traction with its customer service AI agent platform, customized for non-English-speaking markets. The company's strategy of deploying forward-deployed engineering teams to work on-premises with customers to integrate AI into their workflows proved highly successful. Wonderful now operates across more than 35 countries.

---

## From Customer Service to AI OS

Wonderful has evolved its core offering over the past several months. Departing from its customer service roots, the startup now offers a platform called "Wonderful AI OS," which coordinates and connects AI agents, workflows, and applications with companies' data, context, and existing integrations. The platform works with any AI model and is compatible with existing tech stacks.

"Customers can adopt whichever parts of the platform make the most sense, integrate them with existing systems, choose the best models for each workload, and retain ownership of everything they build," said Roey Lalazar, Wonderful CTO and co-founder.

The company will use the fresh capital to develop products faster, expand its forward-deployed engineering teams, and meet growing demand.

---

## Investors Bet on AI Infrastructure

The rapid doubling of Wonderful's valuation in under six months reflects continued investor appetite for AI infrastructure plays. The company's model of embedding engineers directly into customer sites to accelerate adoption differentiates it from pure-software AI vendors, particularly in markets where AI literacy remains a barrier to deployment.
