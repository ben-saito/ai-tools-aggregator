# Judge Orders OpenAI to Turn Over Hiring Records in Apple Trade Secrets Case

---

class="wp-block-group template-content is-layout-constrained wp-block-group-is-layout-constrained"> A judge has ruled that Apple will gain expedited access to OpenAI's hiring materials in the trade secrets-focused lawsuit the iPhone maker filed against OpenAI in July. Specifically, the court will allow Apple to view documents associated with OpenAI's interviews with former Apple employees who applied for roles within its consumer hardware and robotics groups ahead of the rumored launch of OpenAI's first hardware device. These documents could include interview checklists, guides, instructions, manuals, and scripts, as well as technical presentations and records showing that interview discussions had included references to Apple technology or components.
