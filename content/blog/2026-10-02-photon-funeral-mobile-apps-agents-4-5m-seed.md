# Photon Holds Funeral for Mobile Apps, Raises $4.5M to Replace Them with AI Agents

Photon, a startup building an AI agent infrastructure layer for messaging platforms, held a literal funeral for mobile apps in San Francisco on September 17, 2026. The event was also a developer day featuring panels from Vercel, Stripe, and OpenAI. The company has now raised $4.5 million in seed funding to expand its vision.

---

## The Thesis: Apps Are Giving Way to Agents

Photon co-founder and CEO Daniel Tian described the event as "somewhat tongue-in-cheek," but the underlying belief is sincere: the era of downloading new apps is ending. "Honestly, it's going to take a while, but I think, directionally, it's inevitable," Tian said.

The company's core product lets developers build AI agents that work over iMessage, SMS/RCS, email, and other messaging platforms. The bet is that consumers will increasingly prefer interacting with agents through apps they already use, rather than installing dedicated applications.

The open source version of Photon, released on GitHub, attracted thousands of developers before the company even launched a commercial product. According to Tian, 98% of Photon's usage still comes from the open source version. In April 2026, the company introduced a managed platform with subscription tiers offering 99.95% uptime SLA, moving beyond the free tier for teams with more than 10 users.

---

## Traction and Customer Base

To date, Photon has attracted over 40,000 developer sign-ups and counts paying customers including Corgi Insurance, social introductions app Boardy, Gen Z dating platform Ditto, business banking platform Rho, finance AI assistant Fliptexts, AI email client Slashy, and Nous Research's Hermes agent, which uses Photon as its default iMessage layer.

The startup's technology integration partners include Vercel (Eve and ChatSDK) and Nous Research. Vercel also participated as an investor in the seed round, which was co-led by Gradient and A* with participation from HongShan, Z Fellows, Llama Ventures, Karman, and other angel investors.

Across both hosted and open source versions, Photon estimates its technology reaches millions of end users.

---

## The Next Frontier: Agent-to-Agent Communication

While Photon currently focuses on agents connecting with humans, Tian sees a larger market emerging for agent-to-agent interactions. "I think, in the future, agents will be able to find you the agent that can do the right things — so it's going to be the A-to-A communication layer," he explained. As an example, a person might one day ask an agent to find another agent capable of booking a specific type of travel.

The timing of Photon's emergence coincides with Meta's Muse app climbing to No. 1 in app stores — a product Tian sees as validating the consumer demand for agent-based experiences, while also demonstrating the competitive threat to traditional app distribution.
