# HiddenLayer Raises $100M as Enterprise AI Security Market Heats Up

HiddenLayer has secured $100 million in new funding as enterprises race to secure their AI deployments against adversarial attacks, vulnerabilities, and malicious code injections. The security company counts the Department of Defense and intelligence community among its customers.

---

## The AI Security Gold Rush

Security companies are scrambling to build products that monitor not just AI agents but also the tools and add-ons they use. As enterprise AI adoption accelerates, attack surfaces expand — creating demand for specialized AI security tooling that traditional cybersecurity vendors cannot provide.

HiddenLayer has positioned itself at the center of this emerging market, offering protection for AI models, agents, and workflows. The company's platform is designed to detect and defend against adversarial attacks that target AI systems specifically.

---

## Enterprise Adoption

Financial services and large technology companies building AI products currently represent HiddenLayer's largest customer verticals. The company has also expanded into government contracts, landing work with the Department of Defense and intelligence agencies actively deploying AI systems.

The funding round signals continued investor confidence in AI security as a distinct and growing market category, separate from traditional endpoint, network, or cloud security.

---

## Market Context

The $100 million raise comes as AI agents move from pilot programs into production environments across enterprises. This shift creates new security challenges: agents interact with external tools, access sensitive data, and make decisions that traditional security monitoring cannot easily audit.

HiddenLayer's growth reflects the broader trend of AI-native security startups attracting significant venture capital as incumbents scramble to add AI-specific capabilities to their platforms.
