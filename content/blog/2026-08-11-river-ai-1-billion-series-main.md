# River AI, 2-Month-Old Startup, Raises $1.1 Billion Led by General Catalyst

River AI, a personal AI agents startup founded just two months ago by xAI co-founder Igor Babuschkin, has closed a $1.1 billion seed round led by General Catalyst, TechCrunch reported August 11, 2026. The round values the company at a significant multiple given its near-zero operating history, signaling continued investor appetite for AI infrastructure plays even at frothy valuations.

---

## Personal AI Agents: The Next Frontier

River AI's thesis centers on personal AI agents — AI systems that act on behalf of individual users to handle scheduling, communications, and real-world tasks across devices and platforms. Babuschkin, who left xAI where he served as co-founder alongside Elon Musk, is positioning River as a vertically integrated agent platform rather than a point solution.

The $1.1 billion seed is unusual both in size and timing. Most startups at the two-month stage raise pre-seed or seed rounds in the $5M-$30M range. The outsized round suggests either exceptional investor conviction or a structural deal that includes substantial compute or infrastructure commitments from partners.

---

## The Agent Platform Race

River AI enters an increasingly crowded field. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Meta are all building agent frameworks. Meanwhile, startups like Browserbase, Kitesurf (Cloudflare's newly launched agent browser), and Cross床上 are racing to provide the underlying infrastructure for AI agents to interact with the web and real-world interfaces.

What differentiates River is Babuschkin's deep background in language model research and his specific focus on personal, rather than enterprise, agents — a market that has proven elusive for AI companies given the complexity of personal data access and the trust required to delegate high-stakes decisions to AI systems.
