# Anthropic Annualized Revenue Surges to $65B — $18B Increase in Two Months

---

## Revenue Growth Continues to Accelerate

Anthropic's revenue shows no signs of slowing down. According to Bloomberg reporting on Monday, August 17, the company's annualized revenue run rate surpassed $65 billion at the end of July — up from $47 billion in May and just $9 billion at the end of last year. The $18 billion jump in just two months represents an extraordinary acceleration that highlights Anthropic's dominant position in the enterprise AI market.
