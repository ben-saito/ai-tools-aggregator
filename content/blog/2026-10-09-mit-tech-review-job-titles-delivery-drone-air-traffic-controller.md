# MIT Tech Review: Job Titles of the Future — Delivery Drone Air Traffic Controller

---

Trevor Wischnewsky heard that drones were delivering pizza and sushi around his Texas neighborhood and immediately wanted to be part of it. He's now training to become a delivery drone air traffic controller — one of a new generation of jobs emerging as autonomous aviation scales up. The article explores how the FAA is beginning to certify this new occupation and what skills these operators need.
