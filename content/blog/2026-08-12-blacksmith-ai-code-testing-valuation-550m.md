# AI Code Testing Startup Blacksmith Valued at $550M, Up 10x in Under a Year

Blacksmith, an AI-powered code testing startup, has reached a $550 million valuation — nearly 10x growth in less than a year. The company says revenue has grown more than tenfold over the past year.

---

## AI-Powered Software Validation

Blacksmith automates bug detection and quality control in software development using AI. Companies increasingly seek tools that combine development speed with code quality, driving rapid adoption of Blacksmith's platform.

---

## Developer Tool Market Boom

Blacksmith's 10x+ annual revenue growth reflects the broader boom in AI-powered developer tools. Investment in AI-assisted code testing, review, and optimization has intensified across the IT industry.
