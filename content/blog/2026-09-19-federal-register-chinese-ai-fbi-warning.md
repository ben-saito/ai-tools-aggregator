# US Government Website Used Chinese AI Model the FBI Called "Malicious"

Ars Technica reported on September 18, 2026 that the Federal Register website briefly integrated an open-source Chinese AI search tool that the FBI had classified as malicious. The incident raised concerns about federal agencies' vetting processes for AI tools.

---

## What Happened

The Federal Register—the official journal of the U.S. government used for publishing government notices and proposed rules—implemented a Chinese-developed AI search capability that allowed users to query regulatory documents using natural language. The tool was live on the site for a period before being identified and removed.

The AI model in question was developed by a Chinese company and had been flagged by the FBI as potentially malicious due to concerns about data collection, backdoor access, or information security risks. The FBI's warning classified the model as a potential national security concern.

---

## Security Implications

The incident highlights several ongoing challenges in federal AI procurement:

**Supply Chain Risk**: Government agencies increasingly want to integrate AI capabilities, but the global nature of AI development means many tools have foreign origins or dependencies that may not be immediately apparent.

**Vetting Gaps**: Standard security reviews for software may not adequately cover AI-specific risks such as model behavior, data exfiltration patterns, or embedded malicious functionality.

**Open Source Complications**: The tool was open-source, which often receives less scrutiny than proprietary commercial products but can still contain security vulnerabilities or intentional backdoors.

---

## Federal Response

Following disclosure of the incident, the General Services Administration (GSA), which manages FederalRegister.gov, removed the tool and initiated an investigation into how it was deployed without adequate security review. Lawmakers called for hearings on federal AI procurement practices.

The White House issued guidance reminding agencies that AI tools—particularly those from foreign sources or with foreign training data—require additional cybersecurity review before deployment on government systems.
