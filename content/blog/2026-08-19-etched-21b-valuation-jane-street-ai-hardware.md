# Etched Valuation Doubles to $21B in One Month on $700M Raise Led by Jane Street

Etched on Tuesday announced it has raised another $700 million at a $21 billion valuation, led by Jane Street after the quant fund tested and bought the startup's AI hardware.

Even by AI standards, this valuation step-up is jaw-droppingly fast. Etched was valued at $5 billion in December, then raised a $300 million Series C at a $10.3 billion valuation in July. Now investors have doubled that to $21 billion in a single month.

Etched makes ASICs optimized specifically for AI inference workloads, competing with NVIDIA's GPU dominance in large-scale AI inference clusters. Jane Street installed Etched's first shipped AI cluster system and was impressed enough to lead this massive round.

The pace of AI hardware valuation increases is unlike traditional software companies. Etched's valuation grew over 4x in seven months, from $5 billion to $21 billion.

---

## AI Inference Hardware Race

Etched's chips are designed to outperform general-purpose GPUs on specific AI workloads, offering higher speed and energy efficiency for inference tasks. As AI models grow more complex, demand for inference compute is expanding rapidly.

The company competes directly with NVIDIA in the AI inference market, where specialized ASICs can offer advantages in cost and performance for specific model architectures.
