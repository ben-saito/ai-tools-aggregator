# Shipt Joins AI Cart Race with "Ask Shipt" Shopping Assistant

Shipt, the same-day delivery platform owned by Target, announced on Wednesday a new AI-powered shopping assistant called "Ask Shipt" — positioning the company as the latest delivery app to embed generative AI directly into the shopping experience.

---

## From List to Cart in Plain Language

Ask Shipt lets users describe their needs in natural language and receive a fully assembled cart. Example prompts include "Create a cart for my Saturday tailgate for 25 people and include some brunch items," or "Build a cart for easy school lunches and after-school snacks." The assistant interprets the request, maps it to Shipt's product catalog, and generates a ready-to-buy cart without manual browsing.

Users can also upload a photo of a dish — from a restaurant plate or online recipe — and Ask Shipt will identify the ingredients and add them to a cart. Budget-based prompts are supported as well, such as "a weeknight meal for a family of five under $35."

---

## Delivery Apps Pivot to AI Shopping Partners

Shipt's launch arrives the same morning Instacart unveiled Clementine, its own AI grocery assistant. Uber Eats and DoorDash have introduced similar tools in recent months. The simultaneous moves signal a broader industry shift: delivery platforms are no longer competing solely on speed or selection, but on how well they can anticipate and fulfill user intent before a purchase is made.

Target has been expanding AI features across its digital properties, including AI-powered photo search and customer review summaries on Target.com. Ask Shipt extends this strategy into the last-mile delivery layer.

---

## Competition Heats Up in Last-Mile AI

The AI cart race reflects a fundamental change in how consumers interact with grocery and delivery services. Rather than searching for products and adding them to a cart one by one, users delegate the task to an AI agent that handles the end-to-end workflow — from intent interpretation to catalog matching to order completion. As these assistants improve, the competitive moat shifts from logistics speed to conversational understanding and personalization.
