# Anthropic Forms Custom Silicon Team for AI Chip Design

Anthropic is building an internal team to design its own custom AI chips, the company confirmed on August 5, 2026. The Claude maker said it would co-design hardware and models to help its technology run faster and more efficiently.

---

## Moving Beyond Off-the-Shelf Hardware

Anthropic has historically relied on partnerships with major cloud providers — the company has deals with AWS, Google, Nvidia, and AMD for compute access. But as demand for Claude has surged, Anthropic has determined that relying exclusively on third-party hardware is insufficient for its scaling roadmap.

The move puts Anthropic in direct competition with other AI labs that have pursued custom silicon. Google DeepMind has long run on Alphabet's in-house TPU chips. Meta has developed MTIA accelerators for AI inference. OpenAI unveiled its Broadcom-built Jalapeño inference chip in June 2026.

---

## Samsung Partnership in Discussion

According to The Information, Anthropic has been scouting Samsung as a potential manufacturing partner for chip fabrication. Samsung has aggressively pursued foundry customers for its advanced process nodes, and an Anthropic partnership would represent a significant win for the Korean semiconductor company.

Anthropic is currently recruiting engineers with chip design experience for its "custom silicon team," per job listings.

---

## Hardware-Software Co-Design Strategy

Anthropic's stated approach is co-design — jointly optimizing hardware architecture and model architecture. This mirrors a broader industry shift toward treating model design and silicon design as coupled problems rather than sequential ones. Custom chips can deliver efficiency gains in inference cost-per-token and in training throughput, particularly for models with specific computational patterns.
