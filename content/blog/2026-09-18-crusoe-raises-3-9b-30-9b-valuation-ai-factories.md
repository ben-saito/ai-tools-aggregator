# Crusoe Raises $3.9B at $30.9B Valuation, Targeting Modular AI Factories

Data center developer Crusoe raised $3.9 billion in a Series F round on September 17, pushing its valuation to $30.9 billion -- up from the $3 billion at $30 billion valuation reported just two weeks earlier. The massive round was co-led by Atreides Management, Mubadala Capital, and Valor Equity Partners, with participation from Founders Fund, GIC, Nvidia, Qatar Investment Authority (QIA), Radical Ventures, and TPG.

---

## New Board Members Signal Strategic Ambitions

Crusoe also announced three new board members: Cloudflare CFO Thomas Seifert; Bill Stein, partner and CIO at Primary Digital Infrastructure; and Redwood Materials founder and CEO JB Straubel, who also sits on Tesla's board. Straubel has existing ties to Crusoe -- he invested personally in 2021, and Crusoe became the first customer of Redwood's energy storage business.

---

## Modular AI Factories: Compute That Moves by Truck

The eight-year-old company's fresh capital will finance existing data center projects, including a large site in Abilene, Texas, used by OpenAI, as well as smaller modular AI factories that can be transported by truck and connected to large power sources almost anywhere.

By manufacturing these modular data centers, called Spark, at its own facilities, Crusoe can deploy compute capacity quickly without large construction workforces. The smaller centers also help Crusoe sidestep community backlash against massive data center complexes -- a growing obstacle for developers in the U.S.

---

## Business Model: Three Revenue Streams

Crusoe generates revenue through three channels: leasing data center space to customers that bring their own GPUs, renting out its own GPUs, and selling inference compute directly. This model has made Crusoe one of the most valuable AI infrastructure companies.

Crusoe recently signed a $13 billion, five-year cloud contract to supply quantitative trading firm Jane Street with GPUs and AI infrastructure. The company has also held discussions with investment bankers including Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley about a potential IPO.

---

## From Crypto Mining to AI Infrastructure

The September 2026 fundraise comes 10 months after Crusoe raised $1.38 billion at a $10 billion valuation in October 2025. Founded in 2018 as a crypto mining operation powered by flared natural gas, Crusoe has pivoted into a major AI infrastructure provider known for developing hyperscale data center campuses for clients including Oracle and OpenAI.
