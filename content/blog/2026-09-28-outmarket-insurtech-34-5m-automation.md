# Insurtech Startup Outmarket Raises $34.5M to Automate Insurance Paperwork

Outmarket, an insurtech startup using AI to automate tedious administrative tasks for insurance agencies and brokers, has raised $34.5 million in a funding round just months after its previous round. The company focuses specifically on commercial insurance, where policy evaluation and brokerage involve deep nuances and extensive customization for each business client.

The funding round was led by Future Ventures, with participation from Hyperplane and Lakestar. Outmarket has now raised $41 million total since its founding. The company plans to use the new capital to expand its customer base and enhance its AI capabilities for insurance workflow automation.

---

## The Complexity of Commercial Insurance

Commercial insurance presents a particularly labor-intensive workflow for brokers. Businesses must navigate over 250 different types of coverages depending on their industry, risk profile, and specific protection needs. For each policy type, brokers must evaluate coverage options, compare terms, and recommend appropriate protection — a process that traditionally involves substantial manual research and paperwork.

Outmarket's AI automates these time-consuming administrative tasks, enabling brokers to quickly evaluate options and recommend optimal policies. This allows insurance professionals to spend more time on client relationships and complex advisory work that requires human judgment, while routine evaluation tasks are handled automatically.

---

## Customer Traction

Since launching its current product 14 months ago, Outmarket has acquired over 300 insurance agencies as customers, including 25% of the top 100 agencies in the United States. The rapid adoption reflects strong demand from brokers seeking to improve efficiency while maintaining service quality.

Insurance distribution has traditionally relied heavily on human agents, with approximately 95% of insurance in the U.S. still sold through agent channels. Outmarket targets this established distribution network rather than attempting to disintermediate agents, positioning its AI as a productivity tool for existing brokerages rather than a replacement for human advisors.

---

## Investor Perspective

Investors see significant opportunity in insurance automation given the industry's reliance on manual processes and the complexity of commercial lines. The combination of AI capabilities with a focus on broker productivity — rather than end-consumer replacement — represents a differentiated approach in the insurtech market.
