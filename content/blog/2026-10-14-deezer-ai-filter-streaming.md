# Deezer Building AI-Generated Music Filter for Users

---

Streaming platform Deezer remains steadfast in its intent to help users avoid listening to AI-generated music: the company on Tuesday said it is working on a new toggle that lets users filter out AI-generated music uploaded to its platform, with the feature expected to be available before the end of the year.

Deezer CEO Alexis Lanternier said in a statement: "We've seen clear user demand for the ability to filter out AI-generated music, and we are now giving everyone the option to remove this content completely from their streaming experience on Deezer, and focus only on human creators and creations, if they wish." The explosion of AI music on the platform was one of the catalysts for the toggle-based features.

## AI Music Now Dominates Daily Uploads

Deezer added AI-detection tooling last year and has been reporting on the number of tracks being uploaded to the platform ever since. At that time, AI-generated tracks represented **10% of daily uploads**. By July, the company said more than **50% of daily uploads** to its platform were AI-generated tracks, with an average of **90,000 tracks per day**.

## Industry Responses to AI Music

Other music streaming services have also created their own policies. Bandcamp has completely banned AI tracks, while Tidal cut off monetization for people uploading AI music. Spotify has taken a leaner stance, only tagging AI tracks and artists more prominently on its platform. In August, Variety reported that Apple Music was looking to label AI tracks by the end of the year.

Deezer's filter toggle represents one of the most user-controlled approaches to date, giving listeners explicit control rather than making platform-wide decisions about AI music's presence.
