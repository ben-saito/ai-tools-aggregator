# Sequoia Backs Cymphony With $30M to Tackle AI Agent Security Risks in Enterprise

Cymphony, a New York- and Tel Aviv-based startup focused on AI agent security, emerged from stealth on September 9, 2026 with $30 million in funding — including a $25 million Series A co-led by Sequoia Capital and SMBC Fin Atlas Beyond Fund. The round values the company at over $100 million post-money.

---

## The Problem: AI Agents Create New Attack Surfaces

As AI agents gain access to corporate email, financial systems, and sensitive data repositories, enterprises face security risks that traditional identity and access management tools were not designed to address. Unlike human employees, AI agents operate at machine speed and can execute commands across multiple systems without the contextual judgment that human workers apply instinctively.

Cymphony's platform gives security teams visibility into both human and non-human identities — AI agents, service accounts, and other programmatic access vectors — along with the data those identities can reach. The goal is to help enterprises audit, govern, and secure the growing population of AI workers operating within their environments.

---

## Why Sequoia Is Betting on AI Workforce Security

Sequoia's investment reflects a broader thesis that AI agent adoption will create new categories of security tooling demand. As enterprises deploy AI agents for tasks ranging from code review to financial analysis, the blast radius of a compromised or misconfigured agent grows significantly.

Cymphony's early focus on the financial and enterprise software sectors aligns with industries that are leading AI agent adoption and have the most stringent compliance requirements. The company's tel Aviv office suggests it is drawing on Israeli cybersecurity expertise — a well-established talent pool for enterprise security startups.
