# MIT Spotlights Next Generation: Under-35 Researchers Transforming Biotech with AI

MIT Technology Review's special report "Under 35" profiles researchers and entrepreneurs under 35 who are pioneering the intersection of AI and biotechnology.

## Notable Researchers

Among those highlighted, MIT Lincoln Laboratory alumni are creating innovations in AI-driven protein engineering. Their work represents a new wave of technological advancement in the biotech sector.

### Key Themes

- **AI-driven drug discovery**: New drug candidates using AlphaFold3-like AI tools for target identification
- **Synthetic biology**: AI-designed microbial factories for biofuel and biodegradable plastic production
- **Precision medicine**: Patient-derived iPSC cell-based personalized medicine becoming commercially available

## Industry Impact

The rise of young researchers is accelerating collaboration between Big Pharma and AI venture capital. With NVIDIA, Google DeepMind, and Microsoft expanding investments in AI biotech startups, the sector is positioning itself as the next major frontier for generative AI.
