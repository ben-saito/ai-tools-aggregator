# Meta's Muse AI Assistant Arrives on Mac, Taking Actions Across Files, Messages, Calendar and Mail

Meta's AI assistant Muse is now available on Mac, extending the agent's capabilities to work directly with a user's files, messages, calendar, notes, and mail within their native applications on the desktop.

---

## Desktop Agent Capabilities

On Mac, Muse can interact with files, messages, calendar, notes, and mail all within their native applications. Users control what Muse has access to on an opt-in basis, and the assistant always asks for approval before performing sensitive actions, Meta says.

Mark Zuckerberg announced the Mac app on X with the message: "The team is shipping fast." The launch follows a pattern of rapid feature deployment from Meta in the consumer AI assistant space.

---

## Competitive AI Agent Landscape

The Mac release comes as multiple players compete for consumer market share in the AI agent space. Instinct, another AI assistant frequently mentioned in tech circles, is said to be raising funds at a $10 billion valuation. Both companies have rolled out voice calling features within the past week, highlighting the pace of competition.

Other notable entrants include Poke, which exited to Cognition in July, and a range of vibe-coding tools from companies including OpenAI, Lovable, and Replit.
