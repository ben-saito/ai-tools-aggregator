# Mistral Announces Mistral Large 4: Europe's Open-Weight Frontier Bid

---

Mistral has announced Mistral Large 4 (ML4), an open-weight model that the company plans to release by the end of October 2026. The model is tuned for cyber tasks while generating fewer refusals than proprietary models from Anthropic and OpenAI, positioning it as a competitive option for security-sensitive deployments.

## Performance and Benchmark Scores

ML4 will be the strongest Western open-weight model upon release, though below Chinese frontier models:

| Benchmark | ML4 | Kimi K3 | GLM-5.3 |
|-----------|-----|---------|---------|
| DeepSWE 1.1 | 62 | 68 | 61 |
| Terminal-Bench 4.0 | 28.3 | N/A | 41.9 |
| Finance Agent V2 | 54.7 | N/A | 55.8 |

## Cyber-Optimized with Fewer Refusals

ML4 has been carefully tuned for cyber tasks while maintaining helpfulness. The emphasis on reduced refusals addresses a common complaint about proprietary frontier models — that they refuse too many legitimate requests.

## European Sovereignty

Mistral is Europe's best hope for a national or continental AI champion. But the benchmark data shows the difficulty of competing with Chinese frontier models that consistently outperform Western alternatives on capability benchmarks.

## Training Infrastructure

ML4 was trained on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's European datacenters, a significant compute investment for a European AI company.
