# Ukraine's Drones Target Yandex AI Data Center in Russia, Damaging Supercomputers for Training

---

Ukraine's drone strikes have damaged an AI data center owned by Yandex—the Russian tech giant often described as "Russia's Google"—in what appears to be a targeted attack on the infrastructure powering Russia's AI capabilities.

Satellite imagery from Planet Labs PBC shows damage to the data center in the Sasovo region of Russia. According to reports, one damaged data center housed supercomputers used for training Yandex's AI models.

The attack represents a significant escalation in the intersection of AI development and modern warfare, with AI training infrastructure becoming a direct target in armed conflicts. Yandex has been developing its own AI models, including the YaLM family of language models, competing with Western AI systems.

This is not the first time AI infrastructure has been caught in cross-border conflicts. Earlier this year, reports emerged of data centers being targeted in various regions, highlighting the strategic importance of AI computing infrastructure.

The incident underscores the geopolitical dimensions of AI development, where access to computing resources and data centers has become a critical factor in AI capabilities. Nations increasingly view AI infrastructure as strategic assets requiring protection.

Military analysts suggest that as AI becomes more central to both military and civilian applications, such infrastructure will face increased risk during geopolitical tensions and armed conflicts.
