# Thrive Holdings Raises $2B to Bring AI to the Enterprise

OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings has closed a $2 billion funding round at a $12 billion valuation, with participation from SoftBank, D1 Capital Partners, and Altimeter Capital. The investment will fuel the company's mission to bring AI capabilities to enterprise customers at scale.

---

## Enterprise AI Push

Thrive Holdings positions itself as a bridge between cutting-edge AI technology and traditional enterprise needs. The company provides AI-powered tools and infrastructure designed for business integration, helping organizations deploy AI without building from scratch.

---

## Backing from Major Investors

The round draws on some of the most prominent names in venture capital. SoftBank brings its track record of backing transformative technology companies, while D1 Capital Partners and Altimeter Capital add deep enterprise technology expertise.

---

## Market Context

The enterprise AI market has seen accelerated investment as companies move beyond AI experimentation toward production deployments. Thrive's positioning as an OpenAI partner gives it access to state-of-the-art models while providing the integration layer enterprises require.
