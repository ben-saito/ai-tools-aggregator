# Crusoe、30億ドル調達で300億ドル評価額 -- AIインフラ企業として急成長

Data center developer Crusoe, which counts Meta, Microsoft, and OpenAI as its customers, has raised a new $3 billion round at a $30 billion valuation, Bloomberg reported.

The deal is being co-led by Atreides Management and Valor Equity Partners, and includes participation from Mubadala Capital, the asset management subsidiary of Abu Dhabi's sovereign wealth fund Mubadala.

The company recently signed a massive $13 billion, five-year cloud contract to supply quantitative trading firm Jane Street with GPUs and AI infrastructure, Bloomberg reported.

---

## From Crypto Mining to AI Infrastructure

The fresh fundraise comes 10 months after Crusoe raised a $1.38 billion round at a $10 billion valuation last October.

Launched in 2018 as a crypto mining operation powered by flared natural gas, Crusoe has since pivoted into a major AI infrastructure and cloud provider that is best known for developing hyperscale data center campuses for clients like Oracle and OpenAI.

The company recently met with investment bankers, including Goldman Sachs, to explore a potential IPO, according to the report.

---

## Growing AI Cloud Competition

Crusoe's rapid ascent reflects the intensifying competition in AI cloud infrastructure. The company operates data centers that house thousands of GPUs for lease to AI companies, competing with established players like CoreWeave, AWS, and Google Cloud.

The $13 billion Jane Street contract alone represents one of the largest AI infrastructure deals this year, highlighting the growing demand for dedicated GPU capacity from both AI labs and financial firms seeking to build proprietary trading models.
