# WindBorne Raises $37M Series B to Scale AI Weather Prediction

WindBorne Systems has raised a $37 million Series B funding round to scale its weather balloon network and AI-powered weather forecasting platform, TechCrunch reported on August 5, 2026.

---

## AI-Enhanced Weather Forecasting

WindBorne combines a constellation of long-duration weather balloons with machine learning models to produce more accurate weather predictions than conventional numerical weather prediction systems. The company's approach ingests data from its balloons along with satellite imagery and atmospheric readings to generate forecasts with greater spatial and temporal resolution.

The company argues that traditional weather models are constrained by sparse data coverage, particularly over oceans and in the developing world. WindBorne's balloon network addresses this gap by maintaining continuous sensor coverage in areas where ground-based weather stations are unavailable.

---

## Commercial Viability of Weather AI

The Series B signals growing investor confidence that AI-enhanced weather prediction can transition from research capability to commercial product. The weather forecasting market is substantial — agricultural, energy, insurance, and logistics companies spend billions annually on weather risk management.

WindBorne's approach reflects a broader trend in applying large-scale machine learning to physical simulation problems, combining the pattern recognition capabilities of neural networks with domain-specific physics constraints.
