# Instinct's Powerful AI Assistant Raises Privacy and Security Concerns

Everyone is buzzing about Instinct, an AI personal assistant still in private access, not only for its incredible capabilities but also for its potential privacy concerns. The agent, described as having sweeping access and the ability to act on users' behalf, is generating both excitement and caution among early testers.

---

## Powerful Capabilities Come with Trade-offs

Early testers are raving about what Instinct can do, but some say the AI assistant's sweeping access, broad terms, and ability to act on users' behalf come with uncomfortable trade-offs. The assistant's capabilities appear to go significantly beyond typical AI assistants, which has led to questions about data handling and security practices.

---

## Growing Attention on AI Assistant Security

As AI assistants become more powerful and gain the ability to take actions on behalf of users, security researchers and privacy advocates are paying closer attention to the access patterns and data practices of these systems. Instinct is the latest example of an AI assistant prompting these discussions.

The company has not yet publicly addressed the specific concerns raised by testers and security researchers, and details about the assistant's technical architecture and data policies remain limited while the product is in private access.

---

## Private Access Phase

Instinct remains in private access, meaning the full scope of its capabilities and limitations is not yet publicly documented. This limited information availability makes it difficult for external security researchers to evaluate the assistant's risk profile.
