# Gamma Acquires Design Startup Lica to Build AI Research Division

Gamma, the presentation and design platform backed by Accel, has acquired Lica, a design startup that specialized in turning screenshots and recordings into project presentations and videos. The acquisition brings Lica's co-founders and team into Gamma's new research division focused on AI-powered communication.

---

## Building the Next Generation of Presentations

Lica was founded by Priyaa Kalyanaraman and Purvanshi Mehta in 2023 and raised funding from Accel the following year. The company built AI models around communication methods including video and design, with a focus on automating the transformation of raw content into polished presentations.

Gamma will continue to focus on its core presentation-building product, but like other design companies, it has begun offering image generation capabilities. The acquisition signals Gamma's ambition to explore more ambitious AI-driven presentation formats.

"Presentations for us are today a core use case, but how do we expand what presentations look and feel like, and how do people engage with them in the future? What visuals are possible? How interactive can they be?" Gamma's CEO Grant Lee said in a statement.

---

## Personalization as the Next Frontier

Lica's Mehta said the new research division would work on customizing the presentation and communication styles of AI models for different audiences and tastes. The goal is building a platform where presentations can be personalized to the point that users get highly tailored output with minimal manual effort.

The deal continues a consolidation trend in the AI design tooling space, as companies seek to acquire specialized AI capabilities rather than build them from scratch. Both Gamma and Lica have backing from Accel, which facilitated the connection.

Financial terms were not disclosed. The Lica team will join Gamma's San Francisco office immediately.
