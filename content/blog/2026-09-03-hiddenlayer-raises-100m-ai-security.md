# HiddenLayer Raises $100M as AI Security Market Explodes to $2.83B

Enterprise spending on AI security tools is surging as companies race to protect AI deployments from emerging threats. HiddenLayer, an AI security startup, announced a $100 million funding round as Gartner projects the AI security market will reach $2.83 billion this year.

---

## The AI Security Market Takes Off

When HiddenLayer raised its $50 million Series A three years ago, the question was whether AI-specific threats would materialize in sufficient quantity to create a real market. At the time, it was difficult to point to high-profile attacks against AI systems at scale.

That calculus has changed dramatically. Security companies are now scrambling to build products that monitor not just AI agents but also the tools and add-ons they use. While there are still few public headlines about agents being exploited, the risk of AI systems behaving unexpectedly in production is very real. Gartner estimates companies will spend $2.83 billion on AI security products this year, up 83% from 2025, with spending expected to reach $4.78 billion next year.

---

## HiddenLayer's Position

Financial services and large tech companies building AI products represent HiddenLayer's largest customer verticals. The company also holds contracts with the Department of Defense, reflecting growing government interest in AI security.

The funding will be used to expand HiddenLayer's product capabilities and meet surging demand for AI security monitoring tools.

---

## Why This Matters for Developers

For developers building AI-powered applications, the emergence of a dedicated AI security market signals a maturation of the enterprise AI landscape. As AI systems become production-critical, the attack surface expands to include model manipulation, prompt injection, and agent orchestration attacks.

HiddenLayer's Series B follows a pattern seen across the AI infrastructure stack: security vendors are following the money as enterprises move from AI experimentation to AI deployment.
