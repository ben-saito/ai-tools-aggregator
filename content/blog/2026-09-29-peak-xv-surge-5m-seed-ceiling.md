# Peak XV Raises Surge Seed Investment Ceiling to $5M as Series A Bar Climbs

Peak XV Partners has increased the maximum investment through its Surge seed-stage program to $5 million per company, up from $3 million previously, as the venture firm unveiled its 12th cohort of 18 startups spanning AI, robotics, space, consumer products, healthcare, music, and fintech.

The new Surge 12 batch represents the first cohort operating under the higher investment ceiling. Peak XV invested more than $50 million across the cohort, which has collectively raised over $90 million in seed funding. At least three companies in the cohort had previously raised outside capital, in some cases from Peak XV itself.

---

## Rising Series A Bar Drives Bigger Seed Bets

"The bar to raise a Series A has gone up pretty significantly," said Rajan Anandan, managing director at Peak XV, in an interview with TechCrunch. Anandan noted that the firm is seeing more capital-intensive companies, particularly in AI and robotics, requiring larger early checks to give startups sufficient runway to reach Series A milestones.

Peak XV has backed more than 180 startups through Surge since its 2019 launch, when the firm operated as Sequoia Capital India and Southeast Asia. The latest cohort spans founders and companies from San Francisco to Sydney, with 13 of the 18 startups targeting global markets and more than half based in India.

The surge in seed check sizes reflects broader trends in venture capital, where accelerating compute costs and longer development cycles for AI companies have compressed the effective runway between seed and Series A, pushing investors to write larger early checks to avoid excessive dilution in follow-on rounds.
