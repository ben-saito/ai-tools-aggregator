# America Gave Up Its Rare Earth Edge. China Took Full Advantage.

Decades of relying on cheaper imports have left the United States dependent on China for crucial rare earth processing, according to a new report raising alarms about Western supply chain vulnerabilities. The analysis comes as geopolitical competition over critical minerals intensifies.

---

## The Rare Earth Dependency Problem

Rare earth elements are essential for manufacturing electric vehicles, wind turbines, smartphones, and defense systems. China controls a dominant share of global processing capacity, giving it significant leverage over downstream manufacturers in the US and Europe.

The report details how the US and allied nations surrendered processing capabilities over decades of offshoring, choosing short-term cost savings over long-term supply chain security.

---

## Strategic Implications for AI and Tech

The dependency has direct implications for AI hardware supply chains. Graphics processing units, custom AI accelerators, and data center components require rare earth materials for magnets, semiconductors, and thermal management. China's position in the processing chain creates structural risk for Western AI development.

The report adds to mounting pressure on Western governments to rebuild domestic processing capacity and diversify supply sources for critical minerals.
