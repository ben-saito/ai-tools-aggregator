# OpenAI Introduces 'Ultrafast' Mode: GPT-5.6 Sol Runs 14x Faster

OpenAI has launched "Ultrafast," a new inference mode that makes GPT-5.6 Sol operate at 14 times the speed of the standard configuration. The preview, designed for enterprise customers, aims to address one of the biggest friction points in AI deployment: latency.

---

## Speed at Scale

GPT-5.6 Sol is OpenAI's most capable model, but raw power has historically come with tradeoffs in response time. Ultrafast mode uses a distilled variant optimized for rapid token generation while retaining the model's full reasoning capabilities. In internal benchmarks, OpenAI reports median latency reductions of over 90% for complex multi-step queries.

The 14x speedup is achieved through a combination of speculative decoding improvements and a new caching layer that reuse intermediate computations across similar queries. Enterprise teams running high-volume applications like customer support automation, code generation pipelines, and real-time document analysis are the primary targets.

---

## Enterprise Pricing and Availability

Ultrafast is available in preview to API customers starting today. OpenAI has not yet published separate pricing, but the company indicated that speed-tiered pricing will arrive with general availability. The standard GPT-5.6 Sol API remains unchanged for customers who need the full model without the speed optimizations.

---

## Competitive Context

The move puts OpenAI in direct competition with Google's Gemini Flash series, which has gained traction in the market specifically on the strength of speed and cost. Google recently announced Gemini 3.7 Flash, just three weeks after 3.6 Flash. The rapid release cadence reflects an accelerating arms race in fast inference among major AI providers.
