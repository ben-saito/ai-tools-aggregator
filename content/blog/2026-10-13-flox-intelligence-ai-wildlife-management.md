# Flox Intelligence Uses AI to Protect Wildlife from Trains, Airports, and Roads

---

Flox Intelligence is an AI startup that detects and deters wildlife from hazardous infrastructure like railways, airports, and roads. The company's system uses a combination of acoustic sensors, computer vision, and predictive analytics to identify animals before they enter dangerous zones, then applies non-lethal deterrents to redirect them safely.

**The core problem:** Wildlife-vehicle collisions cause an estimated $8 billion in damages annually in the United States alone, plus the obvious ecological impact. Traditional methods like fencing are expensive and can trap animals in dangerous areas. Flox's approach uses AI to predict animal movement patterns and deploy targeted interventions only when necessary.

The system processes real-time sensor data through a custom ML model trained on thousands of hours of wildlife behavior footage. When an animal enters a monitored zone, the system can activate acoustic deterrents, visual warnings, or temporarily adjust infrastructure lighting to encourage the animal to move away from danger. Unlike static fencing, the AI continuously learns from each encounter to improve response accuracy over time.

Flox has already been deployed at several European rail operators and is in trials with a major U.S. airport. The company raised its Series A in Q3 2026 and plans to expand into industrial facility protection next. The broader vision is a connected wildlife monitoring network that integrates with smart city infrastructure to reduce human-wildlife conflict at scale.
