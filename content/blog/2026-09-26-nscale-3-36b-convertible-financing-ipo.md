# Nscale Secures $3.36B Convertible Financing Ahead of US IPO

British AI neocloud provider Nscale has secured $3.36 billion in convertible financing from investors including Third Point and Nvidia, fueling the company's massive AI data center buildout ahead of a planned US IPO.

---

## New Funding Bolsters IPO Prospectus

The convertible financing round adds to Nscale's war chest as the company prepares for its US public listing. The funding comes from Third Point, Nvidia, and other institutional investors, signaling continued confidence in AI infrastructure plays despite market volatility.

The financing will support Nscale's ambitious data center expansion, which has already amassed over $103 billion in contracts.

---

## From Cryptocurrency Mining to AI Infrastructure Powerhouse

Nscale was spun out of Australian cryptocurrency mining company Arkon Energy two years ago and has rapidly pivoted to become a major player in AI data center development. The company has positioned itself as a critical supplier for the AI industry's growing compute needs.

Nscale depends heavily on contracts with Microsoft and Anthropic, which account for the majority of its revenue. The company has also struck deals with other major AI players as the race to build AI infrastructure accelerates.

---

## Growing AI Compute Bet

The new financing comes as AI companies increasingly invest in diversified compute infrastructure. While GPUs have dominated AI training workloads, CPUs remain essential for inference and general-purpose computing tasks that AI agents perform.

Nscale's bet on AI infrastructure puts it in direct competition with established cloud providers and other data center developers seeking to capitalize on the AI boom.
