# ChatGPT Mobile App Gets Voice-Based Agentic Features

OpenAI announced on Wednesday that it is bringing voice-based agentic features to mobile, allowing users to trigger complex workflows using voice commands. Plus and Pro users will be able to use the Work tab on their phones to create documents, draft emails, or summarize Slack messages, among other tasks.

---

## Voice Commands for Agentic Workflows

The new feature builds on the conversational model GPT-Live, launched in July 2026. Users can start a voice conversation and seamlessly transition to working on tasks like building sites or drafting documents. The Work tab on mobile now supports voice-initiated agentic workflows that previously required keyboard input.

OpenAI said ChatGPT's voice conversations will include richer text output. Plus and Pro subscribers can switch between text and voice easily and continue working on a conversation started on the go.

---

## Competitor Landscape

Anthropic recently made the handoff between mobile and desktop easier by merging its Cowork and Chat interfaces. OpenAI is keeping chat and workspaces separate, but the new voice agentic features narrow the gap.

The move reflects a broader trend: users are increasingly turning to voice to issue commands to AI assistants for complex tasks. Voice interaction is becoming a first-class interface for AI-powered productivity tools.
