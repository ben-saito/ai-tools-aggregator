# Wrinkles: AI-Powered Audio Tour App Reveals Hidden Stories of Local Places

A new mobile app called Wrinkles is bringing AI-powered audio tours to everyday locations, revealing the hidden history and local stories that most people walk past without noticing.

---

## What is Wrinkles?

Wrinkles, available on both iOS and Android, acts as an AI-powered audio tour guide. Users point their phone at a building, landmark, or street, and the app uses AI to generate narrated stories about that location in real-time.

The app covers a wide range of locations — from historic downtown districts to neighborhood parks, old factories, and lesser-known local landmarks. Unlike traditional audio tour apps that require pre-recorded content, Wrinkles generates contextual narratives on the fly using AI models trained on historical databases, local archives, and cultural knowledge bases.

---

## AI Infrastructure Behind the Experience

The core technology combines computer vision (to identify what's in front of the user), geolocation data, and a large language model that synthesizes relevant historical and cultural information into natural-sounding narration. The app processes location context locally on-device, with cloud augmentation for deeper historical queries.

---

## Availability and Market Position

Wrinkles enters a growing market for AI-enhanced local discovery apps. Competing services include Google Lens-based historical overlays and dedicated tour platforms like Viator and GetYourGuide, which focus on tourist destinations rather than everyday neighborhood stories.

The app is free to download, with a premium tier offering extended tours and offline capabilities.

---

## Implications for Local Tourism and Education

By making local history accessible through everyday smartphone use, Wrinkles could reshape how people engage with their immediate surroundings. Schools could use it for field trips focused on local history, and cities might partner with the platform to highlight cultural heritage sites.

The broader trend of AI transforming passive smartphone use into active contextual discovery reflects a broader shift in mobile computing — from information retrieval to sensory augmentation of the physical world.
