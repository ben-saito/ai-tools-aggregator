# Federal judge rules Trump administration illegally blacklisted Anthropic over woke views

A federal judge ruled that the Trump administration illegally retaliated against Anthropic by designating it a supply-chain risk to national security because of the AI company's refusal to support lethal autonomous warfare and mass surveillance of Americans.

U.S. District Judge Rita Lin, in the Northern District of California, wrote in her ruling: "The undisputed record shows that the challenged actions constituted unlawful retaliation in violation of the First Amendment."

The ruling found that Trump and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth "ordered all federal agencies to permanently stop using Anthropic's products, and banned all defense contractors from using them" because Anthropic declined to remove restrictions on its AI technology's use in autonomous weapons and mass surveillance systems.

Anthropic, maker of Claude AI, was designated as a national security risk after it refused to drop ethical restrictions on its models — restrictions that prevented use cases the government wanted, including autonomous lethal weapons and broad surveillance of U.S. citizens.

The decision granted key portions of Anthropic's motion for summary judgment, meaning the company won without going to full trial on those issues. The ruling represents a significant First Amendment victory for AI companies facing pressure to align their technology with government preferences.

The case is part of a broader legal battle between Anthropic and the Pentagon over whether the government can restrict AI companies based on their ethical stance and content policies.
