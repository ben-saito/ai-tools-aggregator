# Meta Brings Pocket, Its Vibe-Coding Gaming App, to US Users

Meta has launched Pocket, an experimental AI-powered application for creating and sharing interactive games, to users across the United States. The app enables users to build games through natural language instructions ("vibe-coding") and share creations with a community of remixes and adaptations.

---

## From Gizmo to Pocket

Pocket emerges from Meta's acqui-hire of the team at vibe-coded gaming platform Gizmo earlier this year. The resulting application lets users create interactive experiences responding to touch and device tilt, complete with sound effects and music clips. Users can incorporate photos from their camera roll or camera, then share finished games on their profiles for others to save, remix, or repost.

Meta is shutting down the original Gizmo app as Pocket launches.

---

## AI-Driven Development Velocity

Pocket represents Meta's latest addition to a growing portfolio of standalone mobile applications enabled by AI-assisted development. CEO Mark Zuckerberg cited AI-enabled software development as a key driver of the company's increased release pace during the July earnings call.

"In earlier years, we shipped Instagram Instants. We also just launched Forum, a stand-alone Groups app, and Seller, a stand-alone Marketplace app," Zuckerberg told analysts. "I expect it to become a lot easier to ship new apps."

Recent experiments include AI-generated bedtime stories and a dedicated Meta AI application for Mac.

---

## Competitive Landscape

Pocket enters a landscape where AI-powered creation tools are proliferating across platforms. By enabling game creation through conversational interfaces and community remixing, Meta positions Pocket as both a creative tool and a social platform—combining elements of gaming, social sharing, and AI-assisted development.
