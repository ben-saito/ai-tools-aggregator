# Particle Launches Radar: Making 130,000 Podcasts Searchable for AI Agents

Particle, known for its AI-powered news reading app, has pivoted to focus on podcast intelligence with a new product called Radar. The platform transcribes and analyzes more than 130,000 podcasts — making it the largest transcribed podcast service — and provides access through an API and MCP (Model Context Protocol) for AI agents.

The product emerged from one of Particle's most popular features in its news app, which sourced podcast clips via API to include alongside related news stories. The company realized the value of this capability but saw an opportunity to serve the growing AI agent ecosystem instead.

## Business Applications

Radar has already attracted interest from hedge funds looking for audio intelligence their agents cannot access through traditional web crawling. While most AI agents and services focus on text, Particle is providing an audio intelligence layer that opens new data sources for automated research and analysis.

The platform adds 20,000 episodes to its index daily, covering all Apple Top 200 podcasts across 135 verticals. This scale, combined with structured API access, positions Radar as infrastructure for AI systems that need to consume audio content.

## Technical Architecture

Particle's API allows AI agents to search podcast content programmatically, moving beyond keyword matching to semantic understanding of audio discussions. The MCP integration enables direct connection to AI agent frameworks, allowing automated systems to query podcast intelligence as part of larger workflows.

CEO Sara Beykpour described the vision as providing "all new media intelligence and all audio intelligence" through the API, emphasizing that while most agents focus on text, audio represents a significant untapped data source.
