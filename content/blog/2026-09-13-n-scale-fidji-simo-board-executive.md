# Nscale Adds Former OpenAI Exec Fidji Simo to Board

AI infrastructure startup Nscale has appointed Fidji Simo, OpenAI's Head of Product and former Instacart CEO, to its board as the company prepares for a potential IPO.

---

## A Major Board Addition

The No. 2 exec at OpenAI also led Instacart through its IPO in 2023, bringing public company governance experience to Nscale's board. Fidji Simo's appointment signals the company's ambitions to scale from an AI infrastructure startup to a public company.

Nscale is building GPU cluster infrastructure for AI training and inference, competing with established players like CoreWeave and emerging challengers in the AI cloud space.

## IPO Preparations

The addition of Simo comes ahead of Nscale's anticipated IPO, which sources suggest could happen in 2026 or 2027 depending on market conditions. IPO advisors typically seek board members with public company experience to strengthen governance before listing.

Simo's dual role at OpenAI gives her perspective on what AI companies need from their infrastructure providers — knowledge that could help Nscale win larger contracts.

## Infrastructure Wars

The AI infrastructure market has become intensely competitive, with billions flowing into GPU clusters and specialized AI cloud providers. Nscale's ability to attract talent like Simo suggests confidence in its ability to compete.

The company has raised significant venture capital and is expanding its data center footprint to meet demand from AI labs seeking alternatives to the major cloud providers.
