# Anthropic Annualized Revenue Surges to $65B, Up $18B in Just Two Months

Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate reached $65 billion at the end of July 2026, up from $47 billion in May and just $9 billion at the end of 2025. The company added $18 billion in annualized revenue in just two months.

---

## Rapid Growth Trajectory

Anthropic has grown from $9 billion to $65 billion in annualized revenue over just seven months — a 730% increase. Rival OpenAI doubled its revenue from $20 billion to $40 billion over the same period, but Anthropic's growth rate has captivated investors far more.

CEO Dario Amodei has been pushing back against the narrative that his public warnings about AI risks have fueled backlash against AI infrastructure. "The backlash against AI is fundamentally a crisis of trust," Amodei has argued.

Investors expect Anthropic to reach $100-120 billion by the end of 2026, according to the Financial Times.

---

## IPO Outlook

Both Anthropic and OpenAI have filed confidential IPO paperwork. Anthropic is expected to go public ahead of OpenAI — possibly as soon as fall 2026 — seeking a valuation of $2 trillion or more, which would make it the largest market debut on record.

Anthropic was last valued at $965 billion in late May when it raised a $2.5 billion round.
