# Google Launches SynthID Website for AI-Generated Media Detection

---

Google has launched a new public website for **SynthID**, its AI-generated media identification tool. The platform allows anyone to verify whether an image, video, or audio clip was created using AI synthesis, addressing growing concerns around synthetic media and deepfakes.

## What SynthID Does

SynthID uses Google DeepMind's watermarking technology to detect AI-generated content. Users can upload media files directly to the website, which then analyzes the content and reports whether it contains AI-generated elements. The tool covers three modalities: **images, video, and audio**.

## Why This Matters

As generative AI tools proliferate, distinguishing authentic media from synthetic content has become increasingly difficult. SynthID represents a technical solution to this challenge, embedding invisible watermarks during generation that can later be detected even after human-perceptible editing. The public launch marks a shift from research project to consumer-facing tool.
