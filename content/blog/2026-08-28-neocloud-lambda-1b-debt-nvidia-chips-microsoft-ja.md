# Neocloud Lambda Secures $1B in Private Debt to Purchase Nvidia Chips for Microsoft Leasing

Neocloud Lambda has raised $1 billion in private, short-dated debt to purchase Nvidia AI chips that will be leased to Microsoft, according to a Bloomberg report. The deal, arranged by JP Morgan Chase, signals Lambda is betting it can quickly deploy the chips and start generating revenue, allowing it to repay the debt quickly using incoming cash flow.

---

## The Latest in a String of GPU Financings

This is the latest in a series of loans Lambda is using to fund GPU infrastructure for specific customers. In May, the company closed a $1 billion secured credit facility, and this week announced the closing of a $926 million loan to fund Nvidia GB300 GPUs, one of Nvidia's newest chip models, for a deployment it is under contract to provide to Nvidia.

The $1 billion private debt deal comes as Lambda is reportedly in talks for a $3 billion pre-IPO round. The company last November raised $1.5 billion in venture capital at a significant valuation, reflecting investor appetite for AI infrastructure plays.

---

## AI Cloud Computing's Capital Intensity

Lambda operates as an AI cloud company that purchases computing chips and rents them out to businesses. The company's financing strategy highlights the enormous capital requirements of building AI infrastructure. By using debt financing tied to specific customer contracts, Lambda can fund chip acquisitions without diluting equity further.

The arrangement with Microsoft represents a direct relationship between a GPU financier and a major hyperscaler, reflecting how AI cloud capacity is being financed and deployed at scale.
