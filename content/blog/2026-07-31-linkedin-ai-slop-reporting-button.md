# LinkedIn Adds Button to Report AI-Generated Slop

LinkedIn is introducing new features to combat low-quality AI-generated content on its platform, including a dedicated reporting option for posts that appear to be AI-produced spam.

---

## New Reporting Feature

LinkedIn has added a "seems like AI slop" reporting option that allows users to flag content they believe was generated by AI without meaningful human oversight. The feature targets the growing wave of low-effort, AI-generated posts designed to boost engagement metrics rather than provide genuine professional value.

---

## Replacement of LinkedIn's Own AI Writing Feature

In addition to the reporting tool, LinkedIn is replacing its own AI writing assistant with a more focused proofreading tool. The original AI writing feature, which would generate entire posts or messages for users, was criticized for contributing to the very problem it attempted to solve by making it too easy to mass-produce generic content.

---

## Platform Quality Challenges

The move reflects broader challenges faced by professional networks in balancing AI assistance with content authenticity. LinkedIn's user base increasingly demands genuine professional insights over optimized engagement bait, creating pressure to distinguish human-generated quality content from AI-produced filler.

---

## Industry Implications

Other platforms have experimented with similar approaches, though most lack LinkedIn's professional context. The effectiveness of user-driven reporting versus algorithmic detection remains to be seen, but the initiative signals a shift away from AI-content-generation toward AI-content-enhancement on professional platforms.
