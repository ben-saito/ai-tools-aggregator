# Waymo Doubles Lobbying Spending as Robotaxi Battle with Uber Intensifies

Alphabet-owned Waymo spent more than $1 million on federal lobbying between April and June 2026 — more than double its outlay from the same period a year earlier — as competition in the robotaxi market heats up.

The filing puts Waymo's spending close to Uber's and well ahead of rivals including Amazon's Zoox and Tesla, which have also entered the autonomous vehicle race.

---

## Competing Visions for Ride-Hailing's Future

Waymo and Uber are pushing fundamentally different approaches to the robotaxi industry. Waymo advocates for a faster route to fully driverless commercial services, while Uber is campaigning for a staggered rollout in which robotaxis operate alongside human drivers.

These diverging legislative strategies have strained the relationship between the two companies. The Financial Times reported that Waymo is exploring options to exit its Uber partnership in Austin and Atlanta, where Uber operates its traditional ride-hailing services.

---

## The Regulatory Landscape

The lobbying surge reflects intense competition not just for market share, but for the regulatory framework that will govern autonomous vehicles. How the government classifies and regulates robotaxis will determine which company captures the emerging market.
