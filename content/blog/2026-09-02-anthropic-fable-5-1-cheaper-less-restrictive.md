# Anthropic Releases Fable 5.1: Cheaper and Less Restrictive

Anthropic has released Fable 5.1, an updated version of its advanced AI model that includes changes meant to reduce token costs and decrease false-positive restrictions from the model's safeguards.

The release comes as Anthropic continues to refine its approach to AI safety while making the technology more accessible and practical for developers. Fable 5.1 represents the company's latest effort to balance safety considerations with practical usability.

Key improvements in Fable 5.1 include more efficient token usage, which reduces the cost of running applications built on the model. Additionally, the update addresses cases where the model's safety filters were producing excessive restrictions, creating friction for legitimate use cases.

Anthropic has been systematically updating its model lineup, with the Fable and Mythos 5.1 releases representing twin versions of the company's most advanced AI system. These updates reflect the rapidly evolving nature of AI model development, where continuous refinement is essential for meeting user needs.

The company has positioned these releases as part of its broader strategy to provide AI systems that are both capable and responsible. By reducing costs and improving usability, Anthropic aims to expand the range of applications where its models can be effectively deployed.

For developers and enterprises considering Anthropic's models, the Fable 5.1 release offers improved economics and reduced friction compared to previous versions. The changes demonstrate Anthropic's responsiveness to feedback from the developer community.
