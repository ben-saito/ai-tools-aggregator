# Meta Muse Spark Open brings open-weight AI to Mac hardware

---

Meta has launched what it calls the "most capable" open-weight model in the United States. Dubbed Muse Spark Open, the model also has the distinction of having been designed to work with Macs. The new model, which leverages 816 billion parameters and a 1 million token context window, claims to offer superior performance to other popular open-weight models, according to evaluation results shared by the company.

The model represents Meta's latest push into the open-weight AI space, a strategy that has seen the company release successive versions of its Llama family to developers and researchers. Muse Spark Open builds on this approach with optimizations specifically targeting Apple silicon, including Metal Performance Shaders and Apple's Neural Engine.

"We're excited to bring truly capable open-weight AI to Mac users," said a Meta spokesperson. "Muse Spark Open demonstrates what's possible when you optimize for specific hardware rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach."

The model is available now for download, with support for macOS systems running Apple silicon chips. Integration with popular development frameworks is also part of the launch, aiming to lower the barrier for developers looking to experiment with open-weight models on personal hardware.

This release comes as the competition in the open-weight AI space intensifies, with several companies vying to provide the most capable freely available models. The emphasis on Mac optimization reflects a growing trend of hardware-specific AI optimizations that cater to developers and researchers who prefer working on personal workstations.
