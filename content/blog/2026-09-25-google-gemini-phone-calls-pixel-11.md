# Google Gemini Can Now Make Phone Calls on Your Behalf

Google is testing a feature that lets Gemini make phone calls to businesses on behalf of users, initially available to Pixel 11 owners in the US who subscribe to Gemini, TechCrunch reported September 24, 2026.

---

## Feature Overview

The AI-calling feature allows Gemini to dial businesses, interact with receptionists or automated systems, and complete tasks like scheduling appointments or confirming hours of operation. The feature represents an extension of the AI agent paradigm from text and app interaction to real-time voice communication.

---

## Pixel 11 Exclusive Launch

Google is rolling out the capability exclusively to Pixel 11 hardware and Gemini subscribers, creating an incentive for users to upgrade and subscribe. This mirrors Apple's strategy of gating advanced AI features behind hardware generations.

---

## Integration with Android Ecosystem

The feature appears tightly integrated with Android's call handling system, allowing Gemini to place calls, hold place in queues, and communicate results back to the user. The practical applications include restaurant reservations, appointment booking, and customer service interactions.

---

## Privacy Considerations

Phone call interception and AI voice synthesis raise obvious privacy questions. Google will likely need to disclose when calls are AI-assisted, and regulators in some jurisdictions may require explicit consent from called parties.

---

## Competitive Context

Apple Intelligence has explored similar territory with call summarization and voice assistance. Amazon's Alexa has handled voice calls for years. Google's move puts Gemini in direct competition with these established voice assistants for a specific use case: completing real-world tasks through phone calls.
