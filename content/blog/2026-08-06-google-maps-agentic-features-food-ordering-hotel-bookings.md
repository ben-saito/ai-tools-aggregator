# Google Maps Adds Agentic Features, Including Food Ordering and Hotel Bookings

Google is transforming Google Maps from a navigation tool into an AI assistant capable of completing real-world tasks. On August 6, 2026, the company announced a suite of new agentic features for Google Maps, including food ordering, hotel reservations, and event ticketing — directly within the Maps interface.

---

## From Navigation to Task Completion

The centerpiece of the update is **Ask Maps**, a conversational AI feature that allows users to express complex, multi-constraint queries in natural language. Rather than searching manually, users can ask questions like:

- *"Where can I order vegan avocado toast and an oat milk latte near home?"*
- *"For next weekend's conference in downtown Miami, find me a decently priced, top-rated hotel with an artsy vibe within walking distance from a gym and restaurants."*

Ask Maps then surfaces relevant options, handles comparisons, and routes users to partner platforms to complete the transaction.

### Food Ordering via Ask Maps

After selecting a restaurant through Ask Maps, users can tap **"Order online"** to place an order through supported platforms including Square, Toast, or Uber Eats. Ask Maps adds items to the cart, where users can modify quantities before completing payment on the partner platform. The integration avoids Google taking a direct cut of transactions, instead driving traffic to third-party ordering systems.

### Hotel Booking and Event Discovery

Ask Maps also handles hotel search with multi-constraint queries, comparing prices and checking availability across partner booking sites. For entertainment, users can ask about comedy shows or live music near their location and purchase tickets through integrated partner links.

### Personal Intelligence and Memory

With the addition of **Personal Intelligence**, Ask Maps gains access to a user's Gmail inbox and Google Calendar. This enables contextual responses to queries like *"What time will I land in Vancouver on my upcoming flight?"* or *"Recommend where to eat near my hotel."* Ask Maps also now remembers prior conversations, allowing users to resume queries across sessions — for example, asking *"What activities had you suggested for my trip to Seattle?"*

---

## Platform Strategy: Agentic UX Without Owning Transactions

Google's approach differs from building a full ordering and checkout stack. By routing transactions to Square, Toast, Uber Eats, and hotel booking partners, Google positions Maps as an **agentic interface layer** — orchestrating task completion across existing platforms rather than displacing them. This mirrors the broader industry shift toward AI agents that delegate to specialized tools rather than attempting to handle every step independently.

The September 4 sunset of Google Assistant on mobile devices will leave Gemini as the sole voice assistant, and these Maps features represent Google's vision for Gemini-powered task completion embedded in everyday utility software.
