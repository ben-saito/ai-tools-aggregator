# AI Data Center Infrastructure Rush: Meta Tents, AirTrunk 5GW India, MIT Security Warning

In June 2026, AI industry data center construction is accelerating rapidly. Meta adopted Tesla's tent-based approach for cost reduction, Australian AirTrunk announced a 5GW-scale facility in India, and MIT Technology Review published a warning about AI security threats beyond sophisticated attacks.

---

## Meta Adopts Tesla Tent-Style Data Center Construction

Meta announced on June 4, 2026 that it would adopt Tesla's construction method for data centers. Tesla famously used temporary tent structures at Gigafactory sites, and Meta is copying this approach to reduce massive construction costs.

AI model training requires tens of thousands to hundreds of thousands of GPUs, with enormous power consumption and construction costs. Rather than traditional large-scale facility construction, Meta is choosing tent structures for rapid expansion, highlighting how companies are hitting physical infrastructure walls for AI.

---

## AirTrunk Announces 5GW AI Data Center Plan in India

Australian infrastructure company AirTrunk announced on June 5, 2026 its plan to build a 5GW-scale AI data center in India. Total investment reaches 30 billion USD.

A 5GW scale is equivalent to about 5 million households' power consumption, driven by the rapid increase in computing resources needed for AI model training and inference. India is in competition with South Korea, Singapore, and other countries to attract NVIDIA and AMD GPU cluster construction.

---

## MIT Technology Review Warns of AI Security Threats

MIT Technology Review published a new article on June 5, 2026 about AI security. According to the article, the real threats are not sophisticated attacks like "Mythos" but simple, basic attack techniques that require no advanced technology.

Prompt injection and data tampering are examples of basic vulnerabilities that cause serious damage when left unaddressed. While Anthropic and OpenAI advance safety measures, companies are neglecting basic security practices during AI deployment.

---

## What This Means for Developers

As AI model performance improves, the physical infrastructure behind it becomes increasingly important. GPU supply shortages, power constraints, and construction costs are major challenges for the AI industry in 2026, and innovative solutions like Meta's tent-style facilities are in demand.

AI security is also receiving more attention, and developers need to implement prompt injection countermeasures from the early stages of development.
