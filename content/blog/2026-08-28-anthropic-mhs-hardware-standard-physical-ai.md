# Anthropic Releases MHS Hardware Standard for Physical AI Agent Control

Anthropic has announced a new hardware standard called "Model House Sitter (MHS)" that enables AI agents to directly communicate with and control physical devices. The standardized interface aims to eliminate custom integrations and allow AI systems to interact with equipment without bespoke translator programs.

---

## MHS Design Goals

Currently in "research preview," MHS is being sold primarily as a way to help scientists streamline the creation of custom software integrations often needed to get disparate components of an experiment working together. MHS provides a common interface and common format for data sharing between devices, allowing them to communicate across a network without needing a bespoke translator program in between.

Anthropic states that this standardized system could reduce weeks or months of exacting experimental setup down to "hours or minutes."

## Industry Impact

The standardization of driver interfaces could significantly accelerate AI agent deployment in physical world applications. Research experiments that currently require weeks of configuration could be reduced to hours or minutes, potentially revolutionizing laboratory automation and robotics control.
