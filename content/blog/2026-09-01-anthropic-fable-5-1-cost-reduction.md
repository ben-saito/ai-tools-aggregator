# Anthropic Releases Fable 5.1: Lower Costs and Relaxed Safeguards

Anthropic has released Fable 5.1, its latest model family, featuring two major changes: significantly reduced token costs and relaxed false-positive restrictions from the model's safeguards.

---

## Cost Structure Changes

Fable 5.1 reduces cost-per-token by 35% compared to previous versions, targeting long-context applications in legal and academic document analysis where cumulative costs have been a barrier to adoption.

The company stated that user feedback consistently cited long-document processing costs as a pain point, and Fable 5.1 addresses this directly.

---

## Safeguard Relaxation Rationale

False-positive restrictions occur when the model's safety filters incorrectly block or modify responses that contain no harmful content. Fable 5.1 reduces these incidents, improving developer experience for legitimate content creation use cases.

Anthropic says the goal is to create an environment where developers can integrate the model more productively without unnecessary friction from overcautious content filters.

---

## Market Position

With competition intensifying against OpenAI o1 Pro and Claude Sonnet, Anthropic is using price competitiveness as a key differentiator. Fable 5.1 reportedly offers up to 40% better tokens-per-dollar ratio than comparable competitors.
