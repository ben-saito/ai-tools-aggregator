# Anthropic Is Hiring an AI Chip Design Team

Anthropic is building a team to design its own custom AI chips, co-designing hardware and models to help its technology run faster and more efficiently. The Claude maker confirmed the hiring push, which comes as the AI industry faces ongoing GPU availability constraints.

---

## Background

Custom silicon has become a key differentiator for AI labs. Google has TPU, Amazon has Trainium and Inferentia, and Microsoft is developing its own AI chips. Anthropic's move follows The Information reporting last month that the company was scouting Samsung as a potential manufacturing partner.

The custom chip team will work on silicon optimized for running large language model inference and training workloads. Co-designing hardware alongside model architecture is a strategy that has proven effective for reducing compute costs and improving performance.
