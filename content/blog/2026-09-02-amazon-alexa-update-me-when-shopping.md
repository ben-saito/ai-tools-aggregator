# Amazon Alexa Adds AI Shopping Alerts with "Update Me When" Feature

---

## Alexa Gets Smarter About Product Discovery

Amazon is adding a new Alexa-powered feature called "Update Me When" that can send personalized alerts about product launches, tours, books, shows, and events that could trigger a purchase. The feature represents Amazon's push to make Alexa not just a voice assistant but an active shopping companion.

The launch is one of several AI-powered shopping features Amazon highlighted, pointing to a broader shift in how the company is integrating AI into its e-commerce ecosystem.

---

## How "Update Me When" Works

Users can ask Alexa to notify them when specific products, brands, or categories get new releases. For example, users could track when a favorite brand launches a new product line, when a new season of a favorite show is available, or when tickets for an event go on sale.

The feature combines Alexa's voice capabilities with Amazon's product database and recommendation engine to deliver timely alerts that drive purchases.

---

## Amazon's AI Shopping Strategy

The Alexa update is part of Amazon's broader AI integration into its shopping experience. Beyond voice assistants, Amazon is embedding AI features throughout its platform -- from personalized recommendations to automated deal hunting.

The "Update Me When" feature turns Alexa into an active product discovery tool rather than a passive assistant, closing the loop between awareness and purchase.
