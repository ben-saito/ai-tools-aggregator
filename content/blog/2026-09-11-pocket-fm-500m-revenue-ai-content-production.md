# India's Pocket FM Hits $500M Revenue Run Rate as AI Powers 93% of Audio Content

India's Pocket FM has doubled its annualized revenue run rate to $500 million over the past year, the company told TechCrunch, as generative AI now produces 93% of its catalog and 99% of new content. The dramatic shift underscores how AI is reshaping content production economics in emerging markets.

---

## AI-Driven Content at 80x Lower Cost

Pocket FM, founded in 2018 as a serialized audio storytelling platform, now relies on AI to handle the bulk of content creation while human creators focus on ideas and storytelling direction. CEO Rohan Nayak said the technology has reduced content production costs by approximately 80x, with 100 hours of content that previously took a year now completable in a single day.

The company has trained its own AI models for creative writing and text-to-speech, using years of production data and listener engagement signals. AI head Vasu Sharma, a former Meta and Tesla scientist, described building specialized models rather than relying on general-purpose systems.

---

## From 100K to 2.5 Million Hours: The Content Explosion

Two years ago, Pocket FM's entire catalog held roughly 100,000 hours of audio content. Today, its more than 550,000 creators produce about 2.5 million hours of AI-powered content annually, with the total library exceeding 770,000 audio series.

The content expansion has driven measurable retention improvements. Monthly revenue retention rose to 76%, up from 44% two years ago, as the larger library better matches diverse listener preferences.

---

## Financials and Fundraising

Pocket FM's annualized revenue run rate progression: approximately $250 million a year ago, $430 million in April 2026, and $500 million currently. The company calculates this by multiplying monthly revenue by 12 rather than using contracted recurring revenue.

Ninety-six titles have surpassed $1 million in revenue each, with 13 crossing $10 million. The U.S. market, growing approximately 70% year-over-year, now accounts for about 70% of annualized revenue. Ad revenue contributes roughly $85 million annually, with the remaining ~$415 million from episode-level user purchases.

Parent company Pocket Entertainment is in talks to raise $100 million to $120 million at a roughly $2 billion valuation. Nayak said the company is profitable on an adjusted basis but declined to disclose specific figures. A public listing is not planned within the next 24 months, with potential IPO venues in India or the U.S.

---

## Beyond Audio: Pocket Saga and Microdramas

Pocket Entertainment is applying its AI content model to video. Three-month-old microdrama app Pocket Saga has reached an approximately $15 million annualized revenue run rate, with all content produced entirely by AI. The startup converts successful Pocket FM audio stories into AI-generated videos, bypassing traditional production entirely.

The company plans to enter at least two additional entertainment formats over the next five years and is exploring licensing deals to adapt successful stories into books, television, and film.
