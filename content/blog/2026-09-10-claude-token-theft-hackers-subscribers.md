# Hackers Are Stealing Claude Tokens from Subscribers

A Claude user in the United Kingdom discovered unauthorized token consumption on his account in early August 2026, sparking a warning from Anthropic about an active campaign targeting subscriber credentials.

---

## The Incident: Unexplained Token Usage

On August 4, Grant De Swardt, an independent AI consultant based in East Sussex, U.K., noticed his Claude Max 20x account was consuming tokens even though he had not been actively working. The usage spike persisted after he disabled all connected tools and paused scheduled tasks. In what he described as the clearest controlled interval, token consumption rose from 45% to 55% despite no corresponding work being performed.

De Swardt reported the incident to Anthropic, which subsequently issued a warning to users about credential compromise. The attack vector remains unclear, though the pattern suggests attackers gained access to De Swardt's account and used it to run their own prompts — either directly or through connected services like Claude Code.

---

## Growing Threat: AI Account Takeovers

The incident highlights an emerging security risk as AI assistants become central to professional workflows. Unlike traditional credential theft, AI account compromises can result in direct resource consumption — attackers use the victim's compute budget to run their own tasks, effectively stealing API credits and compute time.

Anthropic has not disclosed how many accounts were affected or whether the attack campaign has been fully contained. The company advised users to review connected applications, rotate passwords, and monitor token usage for anomalies.

---

## What Users Should Do

Recommended steps for Claude subscribers:
- Review active sessions and connected integrations
- Rotate passwords and enable additional authentication measures
- Monitor token usage dashboards for unexplained spikes
- Revoke access for unused or unknown third-party integrations
