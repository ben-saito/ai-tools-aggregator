# OpenAI's New AI Smart Speaker: $300-400 Donut-Shaped Device Coming in 2027

*OpenAI reveals more details about its first consumer hardware device, developed in partnership with Jony Ive's LoveFrom*

---

## Overview

OpenAI is developing a premium AI-powered smart speaker with a distinctive donut-shaped design, priced at $300 to $400, and planned for release in 2027, according to Bloomberg reporting on August 6, 2026. The device, being designed in partnership with LoveFrom—the design studio founded by former Apple design chief Jony Ive—features high-quality metal construction and moving parts, marking OpenAI's most ambitious consumer hardware venture to date.

---

## Design Details

The device is described as donut-shaped, allowing users to carry it around the home and place it in different locations—a bedside table or kitchen counter. The design employs high-quality metal with a premium look, but notably includes distinct moving parts, a detail that has puzzled observers given the typical form factor of smart speakers.

The partnership with LoveFrom brings Ive's iconic industrial design sensibility to the project. Ive, who left Apple in 2019 after decades leading design for the iPhone, iPad, and MacBook Air, founded LoveFrom as a design consultancy. The collaboration positions OpenAI's hardware ambitions in the premium segment of the smart home market.

---

## Market Positioning and Competition

At $300 to $400, OpenAI's device would sit significantly above most competing smart speakers. Amazon's Echo lineup ranges from $40 to $240, while Apple's HomePod starts at $299. The premium pricing signals OpenAI's intent to position the device as a high-end lifestyle product rather than a commodity smart assistant.

The smart speaker market has historically been difficult to monetize—Amazon's Alexa has been criticized for thin hardware margins and the ecosystem has seen mixed profitability across vendors. OpenAI's approach of bundling advanced voice AI capabilities with premium industrial design represents a differentiated strategy.

The company is currently engaged in a legal dispute with Apple over trade secrets, adding complexity to OpenAI's hardware ambitions as it competes with the incumbent leader in personal electronics design.

---

## Technical Integration

The device represents the physical manifestation of ChatGPT—a way to extend OpenAI's conversational AI from screens and apps into physical space. The inclusion of moving parts suggests potential capabilities beyond audio output, possibly including physical interaction or adaptive positioning, though specific functionality remains undisclosed.

The 2027 release timeline gives OpenAI roughly a year to refine the hardware and manufacturing process before launch, a notably long runway for a company known for rapid AI development cycles.

---

## Developer Perspective

For developers, OpenAI's hardware expansion signals the continued blurring of AI and physical product categories. The device will likely offer API access for third-party integration, following the pattern established by Amazon Alexa and Google Nest ecosystems.

OpenAI's move into dedicated AI hardware also raises questions about edge inference, on-device model optimization, and the role of specialized AI chips in consumer products. If the device runs a distilled version of GPT-5 or a future model family, it could represent the most powerful personal AI assistant hardware available.

The partnership with LoveFrom also suggests a design-first approach that could set new standards for how AI hardware is experienced—potentially influencing expectations for future AI-native devices.
