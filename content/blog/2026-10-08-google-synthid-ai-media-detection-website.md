# Google Launches SynthID Website for AI-Generated Media Detection

---

Google has launched a new public website for **SynthID**, its watermarking technology that identifies AI-generated images, videos, and audio clips. The tool allows anyone to upload media and receive a confidence score on whether it was produced using AI systems, including those from Google and potentially third parties.

---

## How SynthID Works

SynthID embeds invisible watermarks into AI-generated content at the pixel level for images and equivalent signatures in audio and video. Unlike visible labels or metadata tags, these watermarks are designed to persist through common edits like cropping, color adjustments, or re-compression.

The website provides a simple upload interface returning results within seconds. Google positions this as a step toward transparency in AI-generated media, particularly relevant as detection becomes harder with increasingly capable generative models.

---

## Industry Context

Multiple organizations have developed AI detection tools, but accuracy remains inconsistent — particularly for heavily edited or converted content. Google's approach leverages its own generation infrastructure, meaning detection confidence is highest for content produced by Google's models.

The launch reflects growing pressure on AI companies to address deepfake risks and content authenticity, especially ahead of major elections and as generative media becomes indistinguishable from real footage in many contexts.
