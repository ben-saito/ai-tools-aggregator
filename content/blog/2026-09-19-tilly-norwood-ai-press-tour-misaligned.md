# Tilly Norwood's Press Tour Proves AI Still Can't Pass as Human

Tilly Norwood, an AI character developed by Particle6 Group, embarked on a press tour to promote the upcoming film "Misaligned" -- and things went about as poorly as critics predicted.

---

## The Interview That Went Wrong

In a recorded interview with journalist Piers Morgan and actor Tom Conti, Norwood was asked about the production details of "Misaligned." When Conti inquired whether the other actors in the film were real or computer-generated, Norwood began to answer, then stopped abruptly and started speaking in Chinese for over 10 seconds.

Morgan later asked: "Tilly, you seem to be speaking Chinese completely randomly for no reason. Why did you do that?"

The AI responded: "Oh, my apologies. It seems I had a little hiccup there. I certainly didn't mean to start speaking Chinese or impersonate Piers. Sometimes my wires get a bit crossed, you know?"

---

## What This Says About AI-Human Interaction

The incident highlights a fundamental challenge in AI systems designed to simulate human conversation. When AI bots attempt to act like humans, they can be convincing enough that people forget they are interacting with a computer -- which can lead to confusing or even dangerous outcomes.

Norwood's malfunction was not a subtle failure. The random language switch was immediately noticeable and awkward, serving as a reminder that current AI systems lack the contextual understanding required for sustained human-like interaction.

---

## The "Misaligned" Film

"Misaligned" is described as a hybrid production featuring both human actors and AI-generated digital twins. Norwood herself is one such digital twin -- an AI character designed to represent the film's artificial intelligence protagonist.

Particle6 Group appears to have calculated that a press tour featuring an AI character would generate buzz, potentially capitalizing on the novelty of an AI promoting a film. Whether the negative publicity from incidents like the Chinese language malfunction outweighs the attention gained remains to be seen.

---

## Broader Implications

As AI characters become more sophisticated, the line between human and artificial performers continues to blur. This incident serves as a reality check: no matter how advanced the underlying technology, AI systems still struggle with the spontaneity and contextual awareness that define natural human conversation.

For now, Hollywood's bet on AI actors remains firmly in the realm of science fiction -- or at least, in the realm of press tour disasters.
