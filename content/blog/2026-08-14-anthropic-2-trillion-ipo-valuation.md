# Anthropic IPO could value company at $2 trillion, making it biggest listing ever

Anthropic is planning an autumn float that could value the AI company at $2 trillion, according to investors cited by the Financial Times. The Claude maker's rapid revenue growth has fueled expectations that its IPO would be the largest in history.

---

## Revenue growth drives lofty valuation

Backers of Anthropic say the company's rapidly rising revenue would enable it to more than double its current valuation in the planned autumn listing. Investors expect annualized revenue to reach $100 billion to $120 billion by the end of 2026, up more than 10x over the course of 2026.

"If Anthropic is growing 800 percent a year, you'd think at the incredibly low end they would trade at 30 times [revenue]," said one investor. "That would make them a $3 trillion company."

---

## IPO would test public markets

A listing at that level could unlock billions of dollars in gains for the five-year-old company's early investors. However, it would also test public markets that are growing more nervous about the AI boom.

Anthropic lacks a publicly listed US peer that would provide a benchmark for its valuation. AI beneficiaries such as Palantir and Nebius have traded at roughly 55 times revenue this year.

---

## Challenges mount despite bullish projections

The bullish projections come despite mounting challenges, including rising competition from China and the high costs of developing next-generation AI models. Anthropic has raised billions from investors including Google and Amazon, building compute infrastructure for its frontier AI research.

The company has focused on AI safety and has developed the Claude family of AI assistants. The IPO would mark a significant milestone for the AI industry as it continues to attract massive capital investments.
