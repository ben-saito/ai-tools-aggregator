# Shopify Says AI Search Is Driving More Traffic and Sales, Not Replacing Google

E-commerce software maker Shopify seems to be benefiting handily from people using AI to search. On the company's second-quarter earnings call, Shopify President Harley Finkelstein said AI has become a "complement to search, rather than a substitute for it," and had particularly benefited the long tail of e-commerce, including the smaller merchants that make up the majority of its customer base.

---

## The Numbers: AI Traffic Tripled Year-Over-Year

Shopify credited its earnings beat and soaring revenue to AI search, at least partly. The company noted that AI-driven traffic and orders to Shopify stores had tripled year-over-year in the second quarter. This is quite different from how AI is impacting online publishing, where AI summaries have led to a measurable drop in click-through rates, which lowers traffic and consequently eats into advertising revenues.

---

## AI as a Complement, Not a Threat

Finkelstein told analysts: "In fact, search remains one of our largest sources of buyer traffic to our merchants, and it's still growing. Traditional search sessions on Shopify-powered stores are up double digits year-over-year." The company believes that AI is expanding the overall search pie rather than redistributing it, with AI helping discover products that would not have been found through traditional search queries alone.

Shopify's positioning contrasts sharply with the experience of online publishers, who have seen AI Overviews and similar features cannibalize organic traffic. For e-commerce, AI-powered product recommendations and discovery tools drive purchases directly on merchant sites, whereas AI-generated summaries in publishing replace the need to visit the original source.
