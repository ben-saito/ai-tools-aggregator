# Threads Rolls Out New Podcast Promotion Tools as Meta Positions It Against X

Meta announced on Wednesday that Threads, its social network and X rival, is rolling out new tools designed specifically for podcast creators to promote their shows, grow audiences, and engage listeners directly on the platform.

---

## New Tools for Podcasters

The features include podcast profile cards and banners that appear on creator profiles and in posts. When episode links are posted, they now appear as tappable cards, allowing users to listen without leaving the app. Creators can also post transcripts for episodes, tag featured guests with display cards, and receive reminders when new episodes drop to post on Threads.

Additionally, Threads is providing analytics tools that give creators insights into how their audience engages with posts, including metrics on views from followers, non-followers, and overall reach.

---

## Competing with X for Creator mindshare

The move comes as X has been actively encouraging creators to post podcasts directly on its platform to earn revenue and grow engagement. While Threads is not attempting to host podcasts itself, it aims to be a hub for podcast promotion and discussion.

Threads is now approaching X in terms of user base, having reached 500 million active users in June 2026.

---

## Industry Context

This announcement reflects a broader trend of social platforms competing for creator attention and content. X has been investing heavily in video and audio features, while Meta has taken a different approach by focusing on discovery and sharing tools that integrate with existing podcast workflows.

The podcast promotion feature rollout demonstrates Meta's strategy of building tools that keep users within the Threads ecosystem rather than driving them to external podcast apps.
