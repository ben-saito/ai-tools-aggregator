# Nvidia-Backed Lambda Raises $4B Ahead of Planned 2027 IPO

---

Lambda, an AI computing infrastructure startup backed by **Nvidia**, is raising up to **$4 billion** at a **$14.5 billion pre-money valuation** ahead of a planned 2027 IPO. The round is led by **Coatue** and **Blackstone**, according to TechCrunch.

## Company Background

Lambda provides GPU cloud infrastructure optimized for AI workloads, competing with established players like CoreWeave and traditional cloud providers. The company has grown alongside the AI boom, offering dedicated GPU clusters and inference capacity to enterprises and AI labs.

## IPO Market Context

The planned 2027 IPO would enter a market that has seen mixed results for AI infrastructure companies. CoreWeave went public earlier in 2026 with strong early performance, while other infrastructure plays have faced more skeptical public market valuations. Lambda's $14.5B pre-money valuation represents a significant vote of confidence from institutional investors despite turbulent public markets.

## Strategic Investors

The involvement of Blackstone as a lead investor signals interest from the private equity side in AI infrastructure as an asset class, while Coatue brings technology-focused growth equity expertise. Nvidia's continued backing — through existing equity and likely GPU supply arrangements — anchors the company's competitive position.
