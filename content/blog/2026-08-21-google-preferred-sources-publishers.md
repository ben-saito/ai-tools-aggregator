# Google Rolls Out Preferred Sources Button to Combat AI-Driven Traffic Losses

Google has expanded its "Preferred Sources" feature to all online publishers, enabling websites to embed a button that lets readers designate them as a trusted source. The initiative addresses growing concerns that AI-powered search experiences reduce click-through traffic to original content publishers.

---

## How Preferred Sources Works

Publishers can embed Google's interactive Preferred Sources button on their own websites. Readers who opt in to the feature will preferentially see and click through to content from sources they have designated as favorites when using Google Search, Discover, and Google News.

Google reports that people are twice as likely to click through to a preferred source when the option is available. The feature was initially rolled out to Google's own AI experiences (including AI Mode and AI Overviews) in May 2026, where adoption data informed the expansion to publisher-embedded deployment.

---

## Publisher Traffic Decline

The initiative responds to documented traffic reductions as AI search interfaces summarize content directly rather than linking out. By giving readers a mechanism to signal preference for specific publishers, Google aims to preserve referral traffic relationships that sustain independent journalism and specialized content creation.

Alongside the button launch, Google announced upcoming features allowing readers to customize their Discover feed using natural language instructions to surface more of preferred topics.
