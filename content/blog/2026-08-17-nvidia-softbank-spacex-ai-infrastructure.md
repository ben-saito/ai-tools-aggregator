# Nvidia Invests $1.5B in SoftBank Data Center Developer and Discloses $21B SpaceX Stake

Nvidia made two significant AI infrastructure moves on August 17, 2026: a $1.5 billion investment in a SoftBank-affiliated data center developer powering OpenAI's expansion, and a disclosure that it holds a $21 billion stake in SpaceX — the largest known position in Elon Musk's aerospace company.

---

## $1.5B in SoftBank's Data Center Developer

Nvidia is investing $1.5 billion in a SoftBank Group data center development company that is serving as the primary infrastructure partner for OpenAI's expanding compute footprint. The investment secures Nvidia's position as the exclusive GPU supplier for the project, guaranteeing chip orders as OpenAI scales its training and inference infrastructure globally.

The deal represents a continuation of Nvidia's strategy of strategic investments in companies whose infrastructure decisions directly impact GPU demand. SoftBank has positioned itself as a central broker of AI data center development, leveraging its access to capital and real estate to build facilities purpose-built for AI workloads.

---

## $21B SpaceX Stake Disclosed

In a separate regulatory filing, Nvidia disclosed a $21 billion stake in SpaceX — the space exploration company founded and led by Elon Musk. The disclosure comes after Musk announced an exclusive arrangement for SpaceX to equip its data centers with Nvidia-powered infrastructure.

The stake makes Nvidia one of the largest known outside investors in SpaceX, which has historically kept its cap table private. SpaceX has been expanding its compute infrastructure to support machine learning workloads for Starlink network optimization, autonomous rocket navigation systems, and the development of Starship — the next-generation launch vehicle targeted at Mars missions.

The $21 billion valuation implies SpaceX is valued at approximately $350 billion or more, based on Nvidia's disclosed percentage ownership — consistent with SpaceX's most recent secondary market trading ranges.

---

## Infrastructure Bet Intensifies

Together, the two announcements signal Nvidia's aggressive pursuit of infrastructure partnerships that lock in GPU demand. By investing directly in data center developers and disclosing strategic equity stakes in compute-heavy operators, Nvidia is extending its influence beyond chip supply into the downstream infrastructure ecosystem that determines GPU utilization rates.

The SoftBank deal is explicitly tied to OpenAI's expansion plans, with Nvidia's investment serving as both a financial bet and a supply agreement guarantee. The SpaceX stake represents a longer-term bet on Musk's vision for compute-intensive space and broadband infrastructure.

Both deals underscore the degree to which AI infrastructure investment has become central to Nvidia's corporate strategy — not just as a chip supplier but as an active investor in the companies that consume the most GPU compute.
