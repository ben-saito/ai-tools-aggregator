# Enveda Raises $311M Series E at $2B Valuation to Advance AI-Discovered Drugs into Clinical Trials

Enveda, a biotech startup that uses AI to discover new drugs from natural compounds found in plants and microbes, has raised a $311 million Series E funding round led by Catalio Capital Management, with participation from Iconiq and other investors. The round values the company at $2 billion — double the valuation it achieved just 12 months ago.

---

## From natural compounds to clinical candidates

The central thesis behind Enveda is that nature has already done much of the chemical optimization work: plants and microbes have evolved bioactive molecules over millions of years, and AI can dramatically accelerate the process of identifying which of these compounds might become effective medicines.

Founded in 2019 by Viswa Colluru, a former early employee at Recursion Pharmaceuticals, Enveda uses a combination of AI, metabolomics, and high-throughput screening to search through natural chemical space for drug candidates. Rather than designing molecules from scratch in a lab, Enveda's approach is to find promising starting points in the natural world and then optimize them using AI-guided medicinal chemistry.

The company is currently testing several drug candidates in human clinical trials. One is directed at severe skin conditions, and another is designed to help patients maintain weight loss after discontinuing GLP-1 drugs like Ozempic or Wegovy — a significant unmet need as many patients regain weight once they stop taking the weekly injections.

---

## The AI drug discovery landscape

While no AI-discovered or AI-designed drug has yet received FDA approval, Enveda sits at the leading edge of a wave of companies that are bringing AI-discovered candidates into human testing for the first time. The field has shifted from early-stage research demonstrations to genuine clinical validation trials.

The $311 million round is among the larger biotech financings of 2026 and reflects continued investor appetite for AI drug discovery platforms that have progressed past the proof-of-concept stage. Enveda's approach — starting from natural compounds rather than purely computational molecular design — represents one of several distinct strategies in the broader AI pharma sector.

---

## Clinical validation as the next frontier

The critical question for Enveda and companies like it is whether AI-discovered candidates can demonstrate efficacy and safety in clinical trials at rates comparable to traditional discovery methods. Early human trials will provide the first major data points on whether the AI-guided approach to natural compound optimization produces drug candidates with sufficient potency and selectivity.
