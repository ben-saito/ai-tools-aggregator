# Friend AI Wearable Returns with New Voice and $180 Price Tag

Friend, the AI-powered wearable device that first launched with a controversial premise — an AI companion meant to be worn as a bracelet or pendant — has returned with a significant hardware revision and a substantially higher price. The updated device adds voice interaction capabilities, allowing the AI to speak to users, a feature absent from the original launch.

The new Friend wearable retains the core concept of the original: an always-listening AI device designed to offer conversational company and respond to user queries throughout the day. The addition of a voice response system transforms the product from a text-centric device into something closer to a portable voice assistant with a physical form factor.

Pricing for the revised Friend wearable starts at $180, representing a notable increase from the original launch price. The company reportedly cited improvements in AI model quality, hardware components, and voice synthesis technology as factors behind the price adjustment. The device connects to a companion smartphone app for configuration and extended functionality.

The wearable AI market has seen renewed investor interest following advances in on-device AI model capabilities. Products like Rabbit r1 and Humane Pin attempted similar positioning but faced mixed reviews regarding real-world utility. Friend distinguishes itself by maintaining a dedicated hardware form factor explicitly designed for ambient AI interaction rather than repurposing existing devices.
