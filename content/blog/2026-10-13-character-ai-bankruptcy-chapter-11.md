# Character.AI Files for Chapter 11 Bankruptcy After Five Years of Growth

---

Character.AI, the interactive AI character chatbot platform that let users create and converse with AI-generated personas, has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection. The filing comes after a series of high-profile lawsuits related to the creation of AI characters modeled on real individuals without consent, combined with the company's struggles to monetize its free-to-use platform at scale.

**The core issue:** Character.AI's primary product allowed anyone to create AI characters that could mimic the personality, speech patterns, and likeness of real people — celebrities, fictional characters, and private individuals alike. Multiple lawsuits alleged that the platform facilitated defamation and right of publicity violations at scale. While Character.AI had terms of service prohibiting harmful character creation, enforcement proved challenging with a user base in the tens of millions.

The company attempted several monetization strategies, including a subscription tier with priority access and faster response times, but conversion rates remained low. The average user spent little money despite high engagement metrics. Meanwhile, server costs for running large language model inference at scale ate into the company's limited runway.

Character.AI's bankruptcy filing lists assets and liabilities in the range of $100–500 million, with the company's primary assets being its user base, proprietary model weights, and dataset. The company is seeking a buyer for its core technology, with several AI labs reportedly expressing interest in the character personalization technology even as the consumer business collapses.
