# AEO Startup Profound Hits Unicorn Valuation with $180M Series D, 7 Months After Last Round

Profound, a startup helping brands appear in AI-powered search results, has raised a $180 million Series D at a $1.8 billion valuation — less than seven months after its $96 million Series C, the company announced Tuesday.

---

## The AEO Opportunity: Surfacing in AI Answers

Profound sits at the intersection of search marketing and the AI transition. As consumers increasingly use AI assistants — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini, Claude — to discover products and services, a new category of optimization has emerged: AEO, or Answer Engine Optimization.

Where traditional SEO focuses on ranking in search engine result pages, AEO focuses on having a brand's information selected as the direct answer to user queries. Profound's software helps companies research how AI systems reference their products, identify gaps in AI-generated answers that could be filled with better-structured content, and create marketing strategies optimized for inclusion in AI responses.

The company launched two years ago as an analytics platform and has since expanded into a full-stack AEO solution, helping enterprise clients understand and navigate the shift from traditional search discovery to AI-mediated discovery.

---

## Rapid Revenue Growth and Enterprise Adoption

Profound reports that revenue has grown 3x in the past six months, driven by accelerating enterprise adoption. The company now serves more than 1,000 enterprise customers, including firms in retail, financial services, and software.

The growth trajectory reflects a broader pattern in the market: as AI-native search has grown from experimental curiosity to mainstream behavior, companies that once invested exclusively in Google SEO are now building dedicated AEO teams and budgets.

---

## Backers Include Top-Tier VCs

The Series D was co-led by Sequoia and Kleiner Perkins, with participation from existing investors Lightspeed Venture Partners, Khosla Ventures, and South Park Commons. The combination of Sequoia and KP in a single round — two of Silicon Valley's most prominent firms — signals strong institutional confidence in both the AEO category and Profound's position within it.

The raise comes as the broader AEO/GEO market continues to attract venture capital. Dozens of startups have emerged in the past 18 months offering variations on AI optimization services, but Profound's rapid scaling and enterprise traction have distinguished it from earlier entrants.

---

## Competitive Landscape

Profound competes with a growing roster of AEO-focused startups, including other well-funded companies like Elsewhere and Semflow. Traditional SEO platforms have also begun adding AEO features, and major search engines themselves are increasingly integrating AI-generated answers directly into results pages — a dynamic that creates both opportunity and uncertainty for the AEO category.

The fundamental question is how AI systems will ultimately decide which sources to cite in their answers. If AI assistants become the primary discovery layer for consumer brands, the company that controls what appears in those citations will hold significant influence over online commerce.

---

## What Comes Next

With $180M in new capital and a unicorn valuation, Profound is positioned to expand its enterprise sales motion and deepen its AI system integration capabilities. The company is expected to use the funding to expand its data infrastructure — building proprietary datasets on how different AI systems reference and cite brand information across categories.

The broader AEO category remains early and fragmented. Whether Profound can sustain its growth velocity as the market matures will depend on whether AI-powered search continues to displace traditional search as the primary discovery mechanism for consumers.
