# Nscale、Anyscaleを買収 — AIコンピュートスタックの垂直統合を加速

British AI neocloud provider Nscale has acquired Anyscale, the software startup that helps companies scale AI workloads across distributed infrastructure. The acquisition positions Nscale to own a larger portion of the AI compute stack.

## Background

Anyscale was founded by the creators of Ray, the popular open-source distributed computing framework for AI. Ray is widely used for training and serving large language models, making Anyscale a key infrastructure provider in the AI ecosystem.

Nscale, based in the UK, operates a neocloud focused specifically on AI workloads. By acquiring Anyscale, Nscale gains both the software expertise and customer base needed to compete with larger cloud providers.

## Strategic Implications

The deal highlights the consolidation happening in AI infrastructure. As the market matures, companies are seeking to control more of the stack — from hardware to software to orchestration. Nscale's acquisition of Anyscale follows similar moves by CoreWeave and other AI-focused cloud providers.

For Anyscale customers, the acquisition raises questions about Ray's open-source roadmap. Anyscale has been a major contributor to Ray's development, and the community will be watching closely to see if Nscale maintains that commitment.

## The Bigger Picture

AI infrastructure is becoming a battleground. Hyperscalers like AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure dominate traditional cloud, but AI workloads have different requirements. Specialized providers like Nscale are betting that vertical integration will give them an edge in price and performance.
