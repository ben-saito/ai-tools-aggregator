# Shopify Debuts Canvas, a Chat-Based Site Builder Powered by AI Agent Sidekick

Shopify has launched Canvas, a new way for merchants to build and customize their online stores by chatting with the company's AI agent Sidekick. The feature positions Shopify as a platform where commerce creation itself becomes a conversational interaction with an AI assistant.

---

## From Click-Based to Chat-Based Store Building

Traditionally, building a Shopify store required merchants to navigate themes, editors, and configuration panels. Canvas replaces that workflow with a natural language interface: merchants describe what they want in text, and Sidekick generates the corresponding storefront configuration.

The agent can handle layout decisions, product arrangement, and design customization based on descriptions like "modern minimalist aesthetic" or "vibrant and playful." Shopify says the feature is designed for merchants who want a professional store without learning the platform's technical details.

---

## Integration with Sidekick Ecosystem

Canvas builds on Shopify's existing Sidekick agent infrastructure, which already supports inventory search, order management, and checkout flows. With Canvas, Sidekick becomes an end-to-end commerce creation assistant, handling both the frontend store design and the backend transaction flow.

The feature is rolling out to Shopify merchants starting October 1. Shopify has not disclosed pricing details, indicating Canvas is initially included as part of existing Shopify plans.

---

## Agentic Commerce Platforms

Canvas represents a broader trend: AI agents becoming the primary interface for platform interactions. Rather than clicking through UIs, users describe goals and agents handle the implementation. For e-commerce, this means merchants can focus on what they sell while AI handles how it is presented.

*Information accurate as of October 1, 2026.*
