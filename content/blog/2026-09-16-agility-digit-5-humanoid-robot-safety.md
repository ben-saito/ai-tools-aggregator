# Agility Robotics Unveils Digit 5: Humanoid Robot That Squats When Humans Get Too Close

Agility Robotics has announced Digit 5, the latest generation of its humanoid robot, featuring a new safety system that allows the robot to autonomously detect nearby humans and take protective actions -- including stopping in place or squatting down to avoid collision.

---

## A New Approach to Human-Robot Safety

Previous generations of commercial humanoid robots required physical cages or safety barriers to operate alongside human workers. Digit 5 changes this with a redesigned safe motion system that uses onboard sensors to detect human presence and adjust the robot's behavior in real time.

When Digit 5 detects a person at a distance, it can autonomously take precautions such as moving to avoid the person or standing still to allow the person to pass without getting closer. In situations where a person enters the robot's immediate proximity, Digit 5 can choose to squat and assume a seated position -- reducing its height and footprint to minimize the risk of collision.

"The robot was designed with a complex safe motion system that can take a variety of different mitigations depending on exactly what sort of human presence is detected," said Pras Velagapudi, chief technology officer at Agility, in an interview with Ars Technica.

---

## From Cages to Collaborative Workspaces

The shift from cage-free to collaborative operation represents a significant milestone for humanoid robotics in industrial environments. Warehouses and manufacturing facilities have historically kept robots separated from human workers due to liability and safety concerns. Agility's approach with Digit 5 is designed to enable true human-robot collaboration without the overhead of safety barriers.

Customers can expect early access to Digit 5 within the first half of 2027, with general availability targeted for the end of 2027. Agility has also been retooling its RoboFab manufacturing system to scale production of the new platform.

---

## Technical Context

Agility Robotics is one of several companies competing in the humanoid robot market, alongside Figure AI, Apptronik, and others. The company's earlier SPAC listing valued the company at approximately $2.5 billion. The Digit platform has been deployed in warehouse and logistics operations with partners including GXO Logistics, Nissan, and Amazon.

The Digit 5 safety architecture represents a departure from purely reactive collision avoidance systems. Rather than simply stopping when contact is imminent, Digit 5 can proactively reposition itself based on predicted human trajectories -- a capability that requires both reliable perception and real-time motion planning at the edge.
