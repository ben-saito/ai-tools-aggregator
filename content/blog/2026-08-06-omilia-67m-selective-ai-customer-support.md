# Omilia Raises $67M to Scale Customer Support Platform With Selective AI Deployment

Athens-based Omilia has raised $67 million in Series B funding to scale its customer support automation platform, the company announced Thursday. The round is Omilia's second fundraise since 2020, during which the company increased its annual recurring revenue 10x to $60 million.

Unlike many recent customer support AI startups that apply large language models broadly, Omilia's approach is more selective. CEO Dimitris Vassos argues that deploying LLMs for every query is wasteful, since a large proportion of incoming customer support queries involve basic information such as account balances that do not require the full capability of a large model.

---

## The Case for Selective AI in Customer Support

Omilia's platform uses a routing layer to determine which queries can be handled by simpler automated systems and which require more sophisticated AI. This approach aims to balance cost efficiency with resolution quality, directing high-complexity queries to appropriate resources while automating routine transactions.

The customer support industry has seen significant investment in AI automation, with startups like Sierra, Decagon, and Parloa pursuing aggressive LLM deployment strategies. Omilia's selective approach represents a counterpoint, arguing that not every support query benefits from frontier AI capability.
