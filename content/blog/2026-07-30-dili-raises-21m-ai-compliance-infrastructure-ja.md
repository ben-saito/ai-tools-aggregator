# Dili Raises $21.7M to Bring AI Compliance to the Infrastructure Boom

---

## A New Funding Round for AI-Powered Infrastructure Compliance

Dili, a startup applying AI to compliance workflows in the infrastructure and construction sectors, has announced a $21.7 million Series A funding round. The round was led by Khosla Ventures, with participation from Allianz, Rebel Fund, Brick and Mortar Ventures' Darren Bechtel, and Y Combinator. The company plans to use the funding to expand its AI compliance platform, which targets the complex regulatory landscape surrounding large-scale infrastructure projects.

---

## Why Infrastructure Compliance Is a Growing AI Opportunity

Infrastructure projects -- from bridges and highways to energy facilities -- generate enormous compliance documentation. Environmental reviews, safety inspections, permitting, and regulatory filings create multi-year processes that traditionally require large teams of compliance specialists. Dili's approach uses AI to automate the review and generation of these documents, identifying gaps and flagging issues before they cause delays.

The infrastructure sector has seen a significant uptick in investment following the passage of major federal funding packages for transportation, energy, and digital infrastructure. That funding boom has, in turn, created a surge in demand for compliance services that can scale with the pace of new project starts.

---

## Y Combinator's Growing Interest in Vertical AI

Dili's participation in Y Combinator signals the accelerator's continued interest in vertical AI applications -- startups that apply AI capabilities to specific industries rather than building horizontal platforms. Y Combinator has backed a range of infrastructure and construction tech companies in recent cohorts, reflecting a broader trend toward domain-specific AI deployment.

Khosla Ventures, leading this round, has been active in AI infrastructure investments, previously backing companies in robotics, developer tools, and AI-driven scientific research. The firm's interest in Dili suggests confidence that AI compliance in capital-intensive industries represents a durable market opportunity.
