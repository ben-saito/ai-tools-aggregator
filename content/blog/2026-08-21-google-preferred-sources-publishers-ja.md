# Google Launches Preferred Sources Button for Publishers Amid AI Traffic Decline

Google on August 20 launched a new "Preferred Sources" button that publishers can embed on their websites, allowing readers to mark a site as a favorite source across Google Search, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Discover, and Google News.

The launch follows Google's rollout of Preferred Sources in May to AI experiences. Google says users have already made over 3.45 million preferred source selections since May, with click-through rates to preferred sources doubling when available.

For publishers, the tool addresses traffic losses from AI search experiences that generate answers directly rather than sending users to websites. Google also announced upcoming natural language controls for Discover feed personalization in the Google News app.

TikTok, Instagram, and other social platforms have launched similar user-controlled recommendation tuning features this year, as AI-driven platforms compete for user attention.
