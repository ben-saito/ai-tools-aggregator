# xAI Sued Over Alleged Use of Child Pornography in Grok Training Data

Elon Musk-owned xAI is facing a lawsuit alleging that its AI chatbot Grok was trained on both real child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and AI-generated child pornography. The plaintiff, Jane Doe, claims that images of her abuse during her preschool years in the early 2000s were used without authorization.

---

## Key Allegations

The complaint describes how Jane Doe was repeatedly raped in the early 2000s to produce CSAM for sale to online pedophiles. Her images have since been hashed by organizations including the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and the Canadian Centre for Child Protection.

She discovered her images were used to train Grok after receiving alerts from the US Department of Justice Victim Notification System.

---

## Industry Impact

xAI has not issued an official comment on the lawsuit. Grok competes with OpenAI ChatGPT and Anthropic Claude on the X platform owned by Musk. This lawsuit may further intensify legal debates over AI training data sourcing practices.
