# ByteDance Trains 10 Trillion Parameter AI Model, Aiming to Rival Anthropic

ByteDance—the Chinese tech giant behind TikTok—is training an AI model with as many as 10 trillion parameters, roughly three times larger than Moonshot's Kimi K3, the biggest Chinese model released to date, according to three people with direct knowledge of the project.

---

## The Scale of the Ambition

The ByteDance model is currently in pre-training, a stage that typically takes three to six months, before it would be fine-tuned and released. The exact final model size may be adjusted as training progresses. The scale being attempted places ByteDance among the most ambitious AI labs globally, rivaling the rumored parameter counts of Anthropic's frontier models.

Industry estimates put Anthropic's Mythos 5 at approximately 8 trillion parameters and Fable 5 at about 5 trillion. While parameter count sets the fundamental capacity ceiling for storing information, actual capability also depends on data quality and training methodology—factors where ByteDance is investing heavily.

---

## ByteDance's AI Build-Out

ByteDance has invested in AI more aggressively than any other Chinese tech giant over the past three years, rapidly expanding its data center network and research headcount. Its model development team Seed—led by former Google DeepMind scientist Wu Yonghui—has grown to approximately 2,000 members across China and overseas, including core researchers, infrastructure engineers, and data specialists.

Unlike many Chinese AI labs that rely on "distilling" existing models from other providers, ByteDance's approach emphasizes independent development from the ground up. Founder Zhang Yiming reiterated this stance in a recent internal meeting, telling the Seed team to target "world-leading model capabilities" without being distracted by short-term competitive positioning.

ByteDance's flagship consumer AI product Doubao has become the most widely used AI assistant in China, with 324 million monthly active users. The company also operates Volcano Engine, its cloud division selling AI solutions to enterprise customers, and has ambitions to develop custom AI chips.

---

## The Global Race for Frontier Scale

ByteDance's push for trillion-parameter scale reflects a broader pattern among Chinese labs rushing to close the gap with US frontier labs. In recent weeks, models from Moonshot and Alibaba have demonstrated strong benchmark performance, trailing only Anthropic's Fable 5 in certain evaluations.

Anthropic's Mythos 5 remains available only to approved organizations following a temporary ban in June 2026 due to security concerns. The competitive window this created has accelerated investment decisions across the Chinese AI ecosystem.

The scale of ByteDance's model—potentially 10 trillion parameters—would make it one of the largest AI models ever trained, exceeding even frontier models from US labs. If successful, it would signal that the parameter gap between US and Chinese AI labs has fully closed.
