# Anthropic Could Be Worth $2 Trillion at IPO, Analysts Say

Anthropic, the AI safety company behind Claude, is generating enough rapid revenue growth that some analysts are projecting a post-IPO valuation of $2 trillion, which would make it the largest public listing in history if achieved at that scale.

---

## Revenue Trajectory

Claude has become a dominant force in enterprise AI adoption, particularly in regulated industries where Anthropic's Constitutional AI approach provides governance and safety guarantees that enterprises require. Revenue has reportedly grown several hundred percent year-over-year, though Anthropic has not disclosed figures publicly.

The $2 trillion projection is based on comparable public software companies at similar revenue multiples, applied to Anthropic's current growth curve. No official valuation has been set, and the timeline for any IPO remains unconfirmed.

---

## IPO Landscape for AI Companies

Anthropic would enter public markets at a moment when investor appetite for AI infrastructure is near its peak. OpenAI has also signaled potential future public offering plans, and the two companies would likely define the upper bound of AI valuations for years to come.

If Anthropic reaches the projected valuation, it would exceed the market caps of companies like Amazon, Meta, or Google at their respective IPOs by a significant margin.

---

## Investor Implications

The prospect of an Anthropic IPO is drawing attention from institutional investors seeking exposure to the AI value chain beyond the established hyperscalers. Pre-IPO secondary market activity in Anthropic shares has reportedly been robust, with valuations implied by those trades already placing the company in the hundreds of billions.
