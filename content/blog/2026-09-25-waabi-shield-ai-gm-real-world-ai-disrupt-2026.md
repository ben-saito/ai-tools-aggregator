# Waabi, Shield AI, and General Motors to Discuss Building AI Where Failure Is Not an Option

Leaders from Waabi, Shield AI, and General Motors will appear together at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 on the Real World AI Stage. The panel addresses a critical challenge in AI deployment: building systems for high-stakes environments where errors carry serious consequences.

---

## The High-Stakes AI Panel

The session brings together executives from three distinct but related domains:

- **Waabi** specializes in autonomous driving technology with a focus on safety-first approaches
- **Shield AI** develops AI systems for defense and aerospace applications where reliability is paramount
- **General Motors** represents automotive manufacturing's push toward autonomous vehicles

All three companies operate in sectors where AI failures can result in physical harm, making verification, validation, and fail-safe design core engineering priorities rather than afterthoughts.

---

## Why This Panel Matters

Consumer AI applications like chatbots or image generators operate in relatively forgiving error regimes — a wrong response is inconvenient but rarely dangerous. The panel's focus areas represent a different paradigm: AI systems that must perform correctly in novel situations, often with real-time constraints and physical world consequences.

Key discussion themes likely include:

- Testing and validation methodologies for safety-critical AI
- How military and defense AI development differs from commercial AI
- Sensor fusion and redundancy in autonomous vehicles
- Regulatory frameworks shaping high-stakes AI deployment

---

## TechCrunch Disrupt Context

The session is part of TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, running November 17-19 in San Francisco. The Real World AI Stage specifically focuses on AI applications beyond software — manufacturing, transportation, healthcare, and defense. Early bird pricing ends September 25.
