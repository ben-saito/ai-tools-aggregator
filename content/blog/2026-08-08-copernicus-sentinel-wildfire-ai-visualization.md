# EU Copernicus Browser Adds AI Wildfire Detection Layer via Sentinel Satellites

---

## Sentinel Satellite Data + Automated Visualization

The European Space Agency's Sentinel satellite constellation — part of the EU's Copernicus Earth observation program — has added an AI-powered wildfire visualization layer to the free Copernicus Browser tool. Activated on August 4, 2026, the new layer allows anyone to view real-time wildfire locations across Europe through a color-coded overlay on satellite imagery.

The feature is powered by a JavaScript script written by remote sensing expert Pierre Markuse. The script combines infrared and visible spectrum channels through an AI algorithm to automatically detect wildfire smoke from satellite images and highlight dangerous areas in distinct colors. Users simply toggle the "wildfires" layer to instantly see active fire locations and their estimated scale.

Simon Proud, Mission Scientist for Sentinel-2 at ESA, proposed integrating the script as a default layer in Copernicus Browser. The feature went live on August 4 after his advocacy.

---

## Addressing a Record Wildfire Season

2026 has seen Europe experience a record wildfire season. Combined with existing tools like NASA's FIRMS fire detection system, free satellite data accessible to citizens, municipalities, and media organizations represents a notable example of AI-powered satellite visualization serving public safety.
