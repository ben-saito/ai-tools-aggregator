# Palo Alto Networks Pays $500M for Console, Sealing Serval as AI IT Automation Leader

Palo Alto Networks has acquired Console, a Thrive-backed startup specializing in AI-powered IT service automation, for approximately $500 million, according to sources familiar with the deal. The acquisition positions Sequoia-backed Serval as the de facto startup leader in the increasingly competitive AI IT service automation space.

---

## The Deal Landscape

Console, backed by Thrive Capital, has built an AI platform that automates routine IT operations tasks including incident response, system provisioning, and infrastructure management. The company's technology uses large language models to understand natural language commands and execute complex IT workflows without human intervention.

The acquisition price of $500M represents a significant premium for a company that had raised a substantial Series B, reflecting the strategic importance of AI IT automation capabilities to Palo Alto Networks' security-first portfolio.

---

## Serval Left as the Key Competitor

Following the acquisition, Serval — backed by Sequoia Capital — emerges as the primary independent competitor in the AI IT service automation market. Serval has been building its own AI-native IT operations platform, competing directly with offerings from both legacy vendors like ServiceNow and newer AI-native entrants.

Industry analysts believe Serval will now accelerate its own fundraising efforts in response to the consolidated competitive landscape, with speculation that a new funding round could value the company above $1 billion.

---

## Why IT Automation Matters for AI

The IT service automation market is becoming a key battleground for AI deployment. As enterprises deploy more AI tools across their organizations, the complexity of managing AI-generated workloads, monitoring model performance, and handling AI-specific incidents has outpaced traditional IT service management tools.

Console's technology was designed from the ground up for AI-era IT operations, with built-in capabilities for monitoring model drift, managing API rate limits, and handling the unique failure modes that come with LLM-based systems.

---

## Market Context

The AI IT service automation market is projected to grow from $2.83 billion in 2026 to over $15 billion by 2030, driven by enterprise demand for automated infrastructure management as AI adoption scales. The market has seen significant M&A activity in the past 18 months, with Cisco, IBM, and Splunk all making strategic acquisitions in the space.
