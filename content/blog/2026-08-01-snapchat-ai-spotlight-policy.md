# Snapchat Removes AI-Generated Content from Spotlight Recommendations

Snapchat has adjusted its recommendation systems to ensure that only videos created by real people are eligible for Spotlight recommendations, taking a direct stance against what the industry has come to call "AI slop" — low-quality, mass-produced AI-generated content flooding social platforms.

The policy shift means that content detected as fully AI-generated will no longer receive preferential placement in Snapchat's Spotlight feature, which surfaces trending short-form videos to users. The company is instead prioritizing human-created content that demonstrates genuine creativity and authentic engagement.

The move reflects a broader tension in the social media landscape: platforms have traditionally encouraged heavy AI usage to boost content volume, but an increasing backlash from users and creators has pushed some companies to draw clearer lines. Snapchat's approach distinguishes between AI-assisted human creation and content that is entirely machine-generated without meaningful human input.

The decision also positions Snapchat in contrast to some competitors who continue to favor AI-generated content for its low production cost and high output volume. The company's stance suggests it believes user trust and platform authenticity are long-term competitive advantages, even if it means reduced content volume in the short term.

For developers and creators, the policy raises questions about detection: how Snapchat distinguishes AI-generated content from human-created material will likely involve a combination of metadata analysis, behavioral signals, and possibly watermarking detection. The technical details of their detection approach have not been publicly disclosed.
