# Nscale Adds Former OpenAI Exec Fidji Simo to Board — Potential IPO on the Horizon

AI infrastructure company Nscale announced on September 11 that Fidji Simo, formerly OpenAI's No. 2 executive, has joined its board of directors. Nscale simultaneously suggested it is strengthening its capital strategy with an IPO in view, and the appointment is drawing industry attention as potential preparation for a public listing.

---

## Fidji Simo's Background

Fidji Simo served as Chief Operating Officer at OpenAI, contributing to the company's operational scaling and organizational development. Prior to OpenAI, she was CEO of Instacart, the largest grocery delivery company in the US, and led Instacart through its IPO in 2023.

On Simo's board appointment, Nscale's CEO stated: "Fidji's IPO experience and track record in the AI industry are exactly what we need at this stage of our growth."

Instacart's IPO is known as an example of a successful public listing under challenging post-2023 tech sector conditions, and it is natural that Nscale would seek to leverage that experience.

---

## Nscale's Current Position

Nscale operates a business providing GPU clustering and infrastructure for AI model training. Since 2024, as competition among AI companies has intensified and GPU shortages have become chronic, demand for alternative GPU providers like Nscale has been growing.

This July, Nscale acquired AnyScale to expand its AI compute stack, signaling a clear trend toward business expansion.

---

## Signals Toward an IPO

There are several grounds for the view that Nscale is considering moving up its IPO timeline. First, the hiring of Fidji Simo is being read in the industry as "evidence of IPO preparation" — given Simo's Instacart IPO experience, this is a welcome development for Nscale's shareholders and municipality.
