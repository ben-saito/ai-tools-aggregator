# Meta Goes All-In on Muse: Everything New Coming to the AI Agent

CEO Mark Zuckerberg kicked off Meta's annual Connect event on September 23, 2026 in Menlo Park with a clear message: Meta is going all-in on Muse. The keynote outlined significant expansions to the AI agent platform, including new integrations, capabilities, and hardware form factors.

---

## Muse at the Center of Meta's AI Strategy

"The centerpiece of our vision for what we're building is Muse," Zuckerberg declared, sporting a black T-shirt with the words "Building is My Love Language." The Connect keynote made it evident that Muse has become the focal point of Meta's AI strategy across its family of apps and devices.

Muse, which launched in early September, is designed to handle everyday tasks by connecting to users' apps and services including email and calendars. The agent is powered by Muse Spark, Meta's multimodal AI model built specifically for agentic work.

---

## Expanding Across the Device Ecosystem

Meta announced that Muse will be integrated across its hardware ecosystem, including the company's AI smart glasses. The goal is to provide consistent AI assistance whether users are wearing glasses, holding a phone, or accessing the agent through the new Muse Charm wearable device.

The agent's ability to connect to personal calendars and email accounts enables it to function as an always-available executive assistant, handling scheduling, summarization, and communication tasks proactively.

---

## The Business Model: AI That Pays for Itself

Beyond convenience, Meta is positioning Muse as a tool that can generate value for users. Zuckerberg suggested that the agent could eventually handle commercial tasks — coordinating with services, managing transactions, and optimizing schedules — effectively making the subscription cost worthwhile through time saved and services accessed.

The company did not announce specific pricing or availability dates for new Muse features during the keynote.
