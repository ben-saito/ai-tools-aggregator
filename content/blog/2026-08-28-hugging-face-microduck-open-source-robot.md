# Hugging Face Sells $399 Open-Source Duck Robot, Microduck

Hugging Face has launched the Microduck, a $399 palm-sized open-source robot designed for developers to train reinforcement learning behaviors at home. The 25-centimeter-tall duck can waddle, pick up objects with its beak (up to 800 grams), get back up when it falls, crouch, and even roller skate.

---

## A Robot Designed for Physical AI

Clem Delangue, CEO of Hugging Face, described the Microduck as an "open-source robot you can teach new tricks with reinforcement learning." The robot perceives its environment through a camera, lidar sensors, and two inertial measurement units (IMUs).

The Microduck builds on Hugging Face's acquisition of French startup Pollen Robotics in April 2025, which produced the earlier Reachy Mini desktop robot. The new platform goes further: behaviors can be trained in simulation and directly deployed to the physical robot. Developers can fine-tune, retrain, and redeploy. The full SDK, simulation environment, and RL training stack are available on GitHub.

---

## The Open Source Hardware Play

While open source gives developers auditability and control, it does not guarantee data privacy when third-party applications are installed on the robot. Those apps can access the bot's cameras and microphones depending on how they are built.

The launch comes as Hugging Face reportedly faces acquisition by Nvidia at a $13 billion valuation. Nvidia has provided Hugging Face's infrastructure since 2023, and both companies have aligned publicly on open model advocacy.

---

## Technical Specifications

The Microduck weighs approximately 1.5 kg and features a modular design. Its beak can grip objects up to 800 grams. The robot supports Python-based development through the Hugging Face ecosystem and integrates with existing ML workflows. The full hardware schematics and software stack are open source, enabling developers to modify and extend the platform.
