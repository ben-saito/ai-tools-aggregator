# Scimplify Takes On China's Dominance of Critical Materials With $25M and AI

---

As manufacturers across industries strive to reduce their reliance on Chinese suppliers, Indian startup Scimplify has raised $25 million to expand its AI-powered platform for critical materials sourcing and processing. The funding round, led by Vertex Ventures with participation from Peak XV and Z47, values the company at over $200 million and signals growing investor interest in supply chain resilience beyond semiconductors.

Scimplify's platform uses machine learning to map and optimize the supply chains for materials that are essential to clean energy, defense, and electronics manufacturing — including rare earth elements, lithium, cobalt, and graphite. The company sources these materials from mining and processing operations across India, Australia, Africa, and South America, then applies AI to predict price movements, identify quality variations, and recommend procurement strategies.

---

## The Critical Materials Supply Chain Problem

China dominates global processing of critical materials in a way that mirrors Taiwan's role in semiconductor manufacturing. According to the International Energy Agency, China refines roughly 80% of the world's rare earth elements and processes a majority of the lithium and cobalt used in electric vehicle batteries. This concentration creates significant geopolitical risk for countries seeking to diversify their supply chains.

Scimplify's founding team, which includes former executives from Tata Steel and Vedanta, identified an opportunity to build a technology layer on top of existing mining operations that could reduce the friction of sourcing materials from non-Chinese suppliers. The platform digitizes supplier relationships, applies predictive analytics to material quality and pricing, and integrates with downstream manufacturers' ERP systems to enable just-in-time procurement.

---

## AI as a Supply Chain Differentiator

What sets Scimplify apart from traditional critical materials traders is the depth of AI applied to the procurement process. The company's models analyze satellite imagery of mining operations, news events in producer countries, and macroeconomic indicators to build real-time supply and demand forecasts. These forecasts inform procurement decisions that give Scimplify's customers a pricing advantage and greater supply certainty.
