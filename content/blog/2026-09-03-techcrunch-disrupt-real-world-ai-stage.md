# TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 Launches Real World AI Stage: Nvidia, Robots, and EVs Take Center Stage

TechCrunch has announced a new stage at Disrupt 2026 — the Real World AI Stage — designed to explore the intersection of artificial intelligence with physical world applications including robotics, autonomous vehicles, and electric vehicles.

---

## Bringing AI Down to Earth

While much of the AI conversation has centered on chatbots and software, the Real World AI Stage will focus on how AI is increasingly being deployed in physical systems. The stage name reflects TechCrunch's recognition that the next wave of AI transformation will happen not just in data centers, but in factories, roads, and warehouses.

Nvidia will be a featured presenter, showcasing developments in AI computing for robotics and autonomous vehicles. The chip giant has been aggressively expanding beyond its traditional GPU business into the robotics market, with the Jetson platform for edge AI and the Isaac robotics simulation platform.

---

## Featured Speakers and Topics

The Real World AI Stage will feature discussions on:

- **Robotics**: How large language models are being integrated into industrial robots for more flexible automation
- **Autonomous Vehicles**: Progress in self-driving technology and the role of AI in perception and decision-making
- **Electric Vehicles**: AI optimization for battery management, charging infrastructure, and vehicle-to-grid systems

---

## The Broader Trend: AI Goes Physical

The launch of a dedicated physical AI stage reflects a broader shift in the AI industry. After years of focus on software applications, companies are now tackling the harder problem of deploying AI in real-world physical environments where reliability, safety, and real-time performance are critical.

The robotics market alone is projected to grow from $45 billion in 2026 to over $200 billion by 2030, with AI-native robotics representing the fastest-growing segment.
