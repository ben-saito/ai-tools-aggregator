# ChatGPT Brings Unlimited Text Chats to Free Users, Adds "Think" Button

OpenAI has announced that ChatGPT's free tier now supports unlimited text conversations, ending the conversation cap that previously restricted free users to a limited number of chats. The change takes effect immediately for ChatGPT Free and ChatGPT Go users.

---

## Unlimited Chats: What Changed

Previously, ChatGPT Free users faced conversation limits that would reset periodically. With the new update, free users can now engage in unlimited text-based conversations without hitting a reset wall. The change brings ChatGPT Free closer to the unlimited experience that paid subscribers have enjoyed.

Alongside the unlimited chats, free and Go users are receiving a new "think" button designed for complex queries. This feature allows users to explicitly request step-by-step reasoning for complicated questions, a capability previously reserved for premium tiers. The think button is intended to improve response quality on multi-step mathematical problems, coding challenges, and nuanced analytical questions.

---

## Strategic Move to Expand Reach

The timing of this announcement is notable: it comes as OpenAI faces increasing competition from Anthropic's Claude, Google's Gemini, and open-source alternatives. Removing the free tier conversation cap removes a significant friction point that may have pushed potential users toward competitor products.

Industry analysts note that OpenAI's move to expand free access could increase adoption among users who previously encountered the limits and abandoned the platform. The addition of the think button for free users brings advanced reasoning capabilities to a broader audience.
