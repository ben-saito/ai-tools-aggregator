# Ricursive Intelligence Brings AI-Chip Design Loop to Disrupt 2026

---

## From AlphaChip to Full Automation

Anna Goldie and Azalia Mirhoseini, co-founders of Ricursive Intelligence, are bringing their vision for AI-automated chip design to TechCrunch Disrupt 2026. The startup, founded in late 2025, is building AI systems that can design chips, learn from the design process, and improve the next generation — potentially collapsing a development cycle that currently takes two to three years into a matter of weeks.

Before founding Ricursive, Goldie and Mirhoseini co-led AlphaChip at Google, an AI system that demonstrated machine learning could generate chip layouts in hours rather than the much longer human-driven process. AlphaChip showed the principle was sound. Ricursive is turning that proof of concept into a production pipeline.

---

## The Feedback Loop at the Heart of the Startup

The core thesis behind Ricursive is a feedback loop that compounds across generations of hardware. AI helps design better hardware. Better hardware supports more capable AI systems. More capable AI then accelerates the next hardware design cycle.

Goldie and Mirhoseini have already proven at Google that this loop can work at the layout level. Ricursive is extending it across the full chip design process — from architecture exploration to physical implementation — and building learning mechanisms so that experience designing one chip improves the system's approach to the next.

The implications for AI development are significant. As AI models demand more compute, the bottleneck increasingly shifts to the hardware that runs them. If AI can shorten chip design cycles from years to weeks, it creates a new dimension of acceleration in the broader AI development race.

---

## Backgrounds at Google DeepMind and Stanford

Goldie, Ricursive's founder and CEO, holds a PhD in computer science from Stanford and was named one of MIT Technology Review's 35 Innovators Under 35. Mirhoseini, founder and CTO, is an assistant professor of computer science at Stanford and founder of its Scaling Intelligence Lab. Both were early employees at Anthropic and senior staff research scientists at Google DeepMind.

They launched Ricursive in late 2025 and moved quickly on fundraising. Within four months, the company had raised $335 million at a $4 billion valuation, including a $300 million Series A. Nvidia is among its investors — a signal that established AI hardware players see Ricursive's approach as strategically relevant to the future of compute.
