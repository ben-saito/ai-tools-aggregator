# Former Plaud Exec Raises $26M for AI App That Listens in Background

---

One of the core promises of AI is that the more it knows about a person, the better it will be able to anticipate their needs. A new startup is betting that the same approach behind voice assistants like Siri and Alexa — listening continuously in the background — could work as a personal AI companion, provided users feel they get enough value in return.

Former Plaud executive Gabor C. has raised $26 million in seed funding for a new AI app that runs on smartphones and listens to users' conversations, texts, and ambient audio throughout the day. The app, still in beta, builds a persistent model of the user's life — their schedule, relationships, preferences, and ongoing projects — and surfaces relevant information at the right moment.

The key differentiator from earlier attempts at always-on AI assistants is a explicit value exchange: users share their data, and the app delivers personalized insights, reminders, and automation in return. The startup emphasizes on-device processing and user-controlled data retention as trust-building measures.

The funding round was led by Andreessen Horowitz, with participation from Sequoia Capital and several angel investors. The company plans to use the capital to expand its ML engineering team and accelerate development of the personalization model.

This is not C's first encounter with AI hardware. At Plaud, C was part of the team that developed an AI-powered wearable that transcribed and summarized conversations in real time. The new venture represents a software-first approach, targeting the smartphone most users already carry.

The continuous-listening model raises familiar privacy questions. The startup says all audio processing happens on-device where possible, with only anonymized embeddings sent to the cloud for processing too complex for mobile hardware. Users can review and delete their data at any time.

The app is expected to launch publicly in early 2027, with a freemium model where basic features are free and premium personalization capabilities require a subscription.
