# Google Gemini Spark now manages Google Photos — editing, albums, and calendar integration for AI subscribers

Google's Gemini Spark can now manage your Google Photos library, handling tasks like photo editing, album curation, shared collections, and calendar event creation for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.

---

## Gemini Spark photo capabilities

Gemini Spark can edit and curate photo albums, create shared collections, turn photos into calendar events, and handle other Google Photos tasks for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.

This integration represents an expansion of Gemini Spark's reach across Google's productivity ecosystem, extending beyond search and Maps into personal media management.
