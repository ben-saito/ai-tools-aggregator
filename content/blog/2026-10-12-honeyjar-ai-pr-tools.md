# Honeyjar Offers AI Tools to Improve PR and Comms at Every Company

---

Journalists, entrepreneurs, and even some public relations professionals will tell you that the PR process is broken. But could AI actually make things better?

Honeyjar, which is attending TechCrunch Disrupt as part of the Startup Battlefield 200, wants to use AI to make the expertise and judgment of the best PR pros available to everyone, including founders who have no idea where to start.

## AI-Powered PR Assistance

Honeyjar's platform uses AI to help companies craft press releases, identify relevant journalists and outlets, write pitches, and manage media relationships. The system learns from successful PR campaigns and applies those patterns to each company's specific context.

Key features include press release generation with customizable angles and tones, journalist discovery based on past coverage patterns and beat mapping, pitch personalization that adapts outreach to individual journalist interests, and media monitoring with AI-powered sentiment analysis.

## The PR Industry Opportunity

Public relations has been relatively slow to adopt AI compared to other marketing functions, partly because relationship-building is considered central to the craft and partly because AI-generated outreach has a reputation for being generic and ineffective.

Honeyjar argues that the real bottleneck isn't relationships — it's the operational work that PR professionals spend half their time on. Automating that work frees PR professionals to focus on strategy and relationship-building.

The company is actively raising its seed round and expanding its team ahead of TechCrunch Disrupt.
