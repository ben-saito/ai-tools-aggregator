# Meta's Muse Outpaces ChatGPT's Early Mobile Launch Metrics

---

## Strong Initial User Adoption

Meta's new AI agent Muse has achieved faster download and daily active user growth in the U.S. and Canada compared to ChatGPT's early mobile launch trajectory, according to data cited by Meta. The company presented the comparison during internal discussions and at external presentations.

Muse represents Meta's entry into the personal AI assistant market, positioning itself as a household-oriented AI system designed for family scheduling, information retrieval, and task management. The faster-than-expected adoption reflects Meta's massive existing user base and aggressive integration across its family of applications.

---

## Mobile Launch Comparison

ChatGPT's mobile launch in 2023 set records for consumer app adoption, reaching 1 million users within days of release. Meta claims Muse surpassed these benchmarks, though independent verification of these claims remains limited.

The comparison faces methodological questions. ChatGPT launched as a standalone app into a market with no established consumer AI assistant expectations. Muse entered a more crowded market where users already had established relationships with AI assistants, potentially lowering the barrier to switching or multi-app usage.

Meta has not disclosed specific user numbers, citing competitive sensitivity. Third-party estimate services have not yet published independent Muse adoption tracking.

---

## Strategic Positioning

Muse's rapid adoption aligns with Meta's broader strategy of embedding AI capabilities across its existing platform ecosystem. Rather than requiring users to adopt a new standalone product, Meta integrated Muse functionality into Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp—platforms with billions of combined monthly active users.

This integration approach allows Meta to leverage its distribution advantage in the consumer AI market. Users can access Muse through interfaces they already use daily, rather than downloading and learning a new application.
