# Google AI Mode Adds Flight Price Tracking and Hotel Booking Capabilities

Google is expanding AI Mode, its conversational search experience, to handle trip planning and booking tasks including flight price tracking, hotel reservations, and cost comparisons in airline loyalty points or miles. The update positions AI Mode as an AI-powered travel agent.

---

## New Travel Features

Users can now describe their travel plans to AI Mode in natural language. The system will display options from more than 300 airlines and travel sites with current pricing. Unlike traditional search, users can ask follow-up questions and receive personalized recommendations.

For flight tracking, users can ask AI Mode to monitor prices for specific routes and dates. If prices change, the system will send an email notification. Flight price tracking is now available in more than 180 countries.

Hotel booking functionality allows users to search for accommodations and complete reservations directly within AI Mode. Users can also see hotel costs displayed in points or miles from loyalty programs, making it easier to compare redemption values.

---

## From Information Retrieval to Transaction Handling

The updates represent a significant shift from passive information retrieval to active transaction handling. Rather than simply displaying search results, AI Mode now executes parts of the travel planning workflow including price monitoring and booking facilitation.

Google appears to be competing directly with specialized travel agencies and OTAs by integrating the convenience of AI conversation with real-time travel inventory. The company says it is moving beyond helping users find information to actually handling the booking process itself.

---

## Context: AI Assistants Entering Transaction Flows

Google AI Mode's expansion into booking follows similar moves by Microsoft Copilot and OpenAI's ChatGPT, which have both added capabilities to handle tasks beyond information retrieval. The travel sector represents a natural proving ground for AI transaction capabilities given its high volume of structured data and clear conversion metrics.
