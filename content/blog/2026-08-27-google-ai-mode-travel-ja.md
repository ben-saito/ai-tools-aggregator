# Google AI Mode Adds Flight Tracking and Hotel Booking

Google is adding new ways for users to plan and book trips through AI Mode, its conversational search experience. The updates position AI Mode as an AI travel agent, moving beyond information retrieval to handling trip planning and booking.

---

## From Search to Booking

AI Mode initially launched as a way to get AI-powered answers to complex questions. Now Google is expanding it to handle the full travel workflow: tracking flight prices, comparing hotel options, and helping users book accommodations.

The feature draws on Google's existing flight and hotel data, combined with the reasoning capabilities of large language models. Users can ask follow-up questions and get personalized recommendations based on their preferences and budget.

---

## Why This Matters for AI Assistants

Travel planning is a key proving ground for AI assistants. It requires synthesizing information from multiple sources, handling complex constraints, and completing multi-step tasks. Google's push into travel booking puts AI Mode in direct competition with traditional travel sites and emerging AI-native booking tools.

The expansion reflects a broader trend of AI systems moving from passive responses to active task completion. Rather than just answering questions about flights, AI Mode now executes parts of the booking process on behalf of users.
