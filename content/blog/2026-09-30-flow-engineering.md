# Flow Engineering Raises $50M Series B at $750M Valuation

Flow Engineering, a startup developing AI agents for hardware design workflows, has closed a $50 million Series B round co-led by Valar Equity Partners (Antonio Gracias, known for SpaceX investments) and Atreides Management, reaching a $750 million valuation. Sequoia Capital also participated, with Valar's Antonio Gracias and Sequoia's Roelof Botha joining the board as angel investors.

The company positions its AI agents as a way to automate repetitive design tasks in hardware engineering — synthesis, place-and-route, timing closure — areas where human engineers spend significant manual effort. The agents integrate with existing Electronic Design Automation (EDA) toolchains.

Roelof Botha's involvement signals strong VC interest in AI-assisted hardware design. The timing coincides with TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, where hardware design automation is a featured theme.

---

## Hardware Design AI: From Software to Silicon

Traditional hardware design remains highly manual. Engineers iterate across schematic entry, simulation, and physical layout — a process that can span months for complex SoCs. AI agents that can navigate EDA tool interfaces and handle iterative optimization tasks could significantly compress these cycles.

Flow Engineering's approach treats design rules as an API, allowing agents to query constraints and propose modifications without replacing the engineer's judgment on top-level architecture decisions.
