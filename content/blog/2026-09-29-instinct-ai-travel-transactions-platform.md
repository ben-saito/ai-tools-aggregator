# Instinct AI Agent Platform: Over 50% of Transactions Now Travel-Related

Instinct AI, an emerging AI agent platform, has revealed that more than 50% of transactions processed on its platform are now travel-related. The company says the platform is growing approximately 10% day-by-day, signaling strong traction in the travel vertical.

---

## Growth Trajectory and Market Focus

Instinct's AI agent platform enables developers to deploy autonomous agents capable of executing complex multi-step tasks. The platform's rapid adoption in travel reflects the sector's need for agents that can handle booking workflows, itinerary management, and customer service automation across multiple providers.

The company's recent Series C at a $10B valuation (covered September 28) established it as one of the best-funded independent AI agent startups. Today's disclosure of travel-dominant transaction volume adds a new dimension to the company's positioning — it is emerging as infrastructure for travel automation rather than a horizontal agent platform.

---

## Agent Platform Landscape

The broader AI agent market has seen significant funding activity in 2026. Competitors including Instabase, ServiceNow, and Salesforce have all announced agent-centric products targeting enterprise workflows. Travel represents a natural early vertical given its structured, API-driven data environment and high transaction volume.

Instinct's platform growth rate of ~10% daily suggests it is crossing a threshold where network effects and agent capabilities are reinforcing each other. The company is expected to announce expanded API partnerships with major travel aggregators in the coming weeks.

---

## Technical Architecture

The Instinct platform provides developers with a framework for building agents that can:
- Chain multiple tool calls across external APIs
- Maintain state across long-running task sequences
- Handle errors and retry logic autonomously
- Integrate with enterprise authentication and audit systems

Agents are defined using a manifest format that declares required capabilities, data access requirements, and execution constraints. The platform handles orchestration, scaling, and monitoring.

---

## Investor Interest

The company's $1B Series C (announced September 28) was led by Sequoia with participation from Greenoaks and existing investors. At a $10B valuation, Instinct ranks among the highest-valued independent AI infrastructure companies alongside Modal Labs ($8B) and Hugging Face ($4.5B).

The travel transaction data suggests the company has found a beachhead vertical before pursuing horizontal expansion — a strategy that has worked well for other enterprise software platforms.
