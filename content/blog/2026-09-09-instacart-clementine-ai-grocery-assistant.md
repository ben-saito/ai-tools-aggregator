# Instacart launches AI grocery shopping assistant Clementine

Instacart has unveiled a new AI-powered grocery shopping assistant called Clementine, positioning the platform as more than just a delivery service -- now competing to become the default tool for meal planning and grocery decisions.

Clementine can turn a conversation, grocery list, or recipe into a ready-to-buy cart. Users can ask queries like "I need a week of budget-friendly kids' lunches," "high-protein easy dinners for two," or "order my usuals," and the assistant will compile the relevant items into a cart.

---

## Competition in AI-powered grocery delivery

Instacart is not alone in this race. Uber Eats and DoorDash have both introduced similar AI assistants that can turn natural-language requests and grocery lists into personalized carts. The launch of Clementine indicates that delivery apps no longer want to be just places where people find and buy groceries -- they are now competing to become the default planning tool before a purchase is even made.

The platform is betting that AI-powered conversation will increase basket size and customer retention by making the shopping experience more personalized and frictionless.

---

## Technical approach

Clementine uses a conversational AI model to parse natural-language requests and map them to Instacart's product catalog. The assistant appears to leverage a combination of retrieval-augmented generation and product recommendation models to translate vague requests (like "high-protein dinners") into specific items available in the user's area.

This represents a shift from keyword-based search to intent-based AI conversations for e-commerce grocery.
