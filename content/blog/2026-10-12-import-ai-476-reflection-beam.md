# Reflection AI Releases Beam: A Token-Efficient Model That Plans Open Weight Release

---

Beam promises efficiency, and Reflection plans an open weight release. AI startup Reflection has revealed Beam, a 501 billion parameter model that the company claims achieves state-of-the-art performance on coding and reasoning tasks while using significantly fewer tokens per query than competing models. The efficiency claim is central to Beam's value proposition: by reducing the number of tokens required to produce a given output, the model reduces inference costs and latency.

The open weight release plan positions Reflection within the broader open-source AI ecosystem that has been built around Meta's Llama series and Mistral's models. By releasing the model weights publicly, Reflection is betting that the developer community will build tooling, fine-tunes, and applications that extend Beam's capabilities in ways the company cannot do alone.

The model architecture includes modifications to the attention mechanism that Reflection says are responsible for the token efficiency gains. If the efficiency claims hold up under independent evaluation, Beam could represent a meaningful step forward in the practical economics of running large language models in production environments.
