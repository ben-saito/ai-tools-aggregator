# Anthropic gets its first court win over the Pentagon supply-chain risk label

A federal judge in California ruled on Thursday evening that the Trump administration's designation of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk was illegal.

U.S. District Judge Rita Lin said in her ruling that Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth's labeling of Anthropic as a risk to national security signified "unlawful retaliation" in violation of the First Amendment, and said the decision was "arbitrary and capricious."

Anthropic refused to support lethal autonomous warfare and mass surveillance of Americans, which triggered the Trump administration's designation. The ruling found that the government's actions were motivated by Anthropic's viewpoint rather than legitimate national security concerns.

The ruling granted key portions of Anthropic's motion for summary judgment. The case stems from the Trump administration ordering all federal agencies to permanently stop using Anthropic's products and banned all defense contractors from using them as well.

This marks Anthropic's first court victory in what has become a multi-front legal battle with the Pentagon. The company has also challenged a separate Pentagon designation that blocked it from federal contracting.

The decision represents a significant win for AI companies concerned about government retaliation based on political viewpoints rather than technical security assessments.
