# Neocloud Lambda Raises $1 Billion in Debt to Purchase Nvidia AI Chips

Lambda, an AI cloud infrastructure company that purchases computing hardware and leases it to enterprise customers, has secured $1 billion in private short-dated debt to purchase Nvidia AI chips for lease to Microsoft, according to Bloomberg.

The deal, arranged by JP Morgan Chase, signals Lambda's confidence in its ability to quickly deploy the chips and begin generating revenue, allowing for rapid debt repayment from incoming lease payments. This represents the latest in a series of financing rounds Lambda has used to build out GPU infrastructure for specific customers.

In May, Lambda closed a $1 billion secured credit facility. Earlier this week, the company announced the closing of a $926 million loan to fund Nvidia GB300 GPUs — one of Nvidia's newest chip models — for a deployment contract with Nvidia itself.

---

## Lambda's Growth and Valuation

The $1 billion private debt deal comes as Lambda reportedly explores a $3 billion pre-IPO funding round. The company previously raised $1.5 billion in venture capital last November, valuing it at $5.43 billion post-money according to PitchBook data.

Lambda is not alone in relying on debt financing to fund AI infrastructure buildout. According to data compiled by Bloomberg, banks and tech companies have raised over $400 billion in AI-related debt globally in 2026 so far.

The company's model involves purchasing Nvidia GPUs and leasing them to hyperscalers and enterprise customers who prefer OpEx financing over large capital expenditures for AI compute. This has proven particularly attractive to Microsoft, which has been expanding its AI infrastructure partnerships across multiple vendors.

---

## Market Context

The AI compute boom has created unprecedented demand for Nvidia's chips, with GPU shortages driving companies to explore creative financing arrangements. Lambda's ability to secure debt at scale reflects investor confidence in the ongoing AI infrastructure expansion, even as questions remain about the timeline for return on investment in AI data centers.

The company's relationship with Nvidia extends beyond customer to strategic partner, with Lambda serving as an early deployment partner for new Nvidia GPU generations. This positioning has helped Lambda secure favorable terms on both chip acquisitions and customer contracts.
