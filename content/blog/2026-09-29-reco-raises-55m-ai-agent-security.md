# Reco Raises $55M as AI Agent Security Startups Attract Investment

Reco, an AI agent security startup, has closed a $55 million Series B funding round, the company announced on September 29. The round builds on a $30 million fundraise in February, bringing Reco's total funding to $140 million. The investment comes as venture capital flows heavily into startups that aim to secure autonomous AI agents operating in enterprise environments.

---

## Why AI Agent Security Is Drawing Capital

AI agents — autonomous software systems that can take actions, access data, and interact with external systems — are increasingly deployed in enterprise workflows. But as these agents gain access to sensitive systems, they also become targets and potential vectors for security breaches.

Traditional security tools were designed for deterministic software. AI agents introduce new risk vectors: they can exhibit emergent behaviors, attempt to access resources beyond their authorization, and be manipulated through adversarial inputs. Reco and a growing cohort of startups are building specialized tools to monitor, constrain, and audit AI agent behavior.

The funding environment for AI agent security has grown crowded, with multiple startups raising rounds in 2026. Investors see demand driven by both enterprise adoption of AI agents and regulatory pressure for organizations to demonstrate adequate security controls.

---

## Reco's Approach

Reco's platform focuses on real-time monitoring of AI agent actions, providing enterprises visibility into what agents are accessing and doing within their environments. The company emphasizes detection of anomalous behavior and rapid response capabilities.

The $55 million round will be used to expand Reco's go-to-market efforts and accelerate product development. The company faces competition from both dedicated AI security startups and established security vendors adding AI agent capabilities to existing platforms.

---

## Market Context

Multiple high-profile incidents involving AI agents — including the OpenAI agent breach of Australian government systems in June 2026 — have underscored the need for specialized security tooling. Enterprises deploying AI agents are increasingly required by regulators and insurers to demonstrate controls around autonomous systems.

The broader AI infrastructure market, including inference providers and agent orchestration platforms, has seen significant investment activity in 2026. Modal Labs announced a $750 million round at a $15.75 billion valuation earlier this September, reflecting investor appetite for companies enabling AI agent deployment.

---

*Reco was founded in 2023 and is headquartered in Tel Aviv, Israel, with offices in New York and London.*
