# Accel-backed Keenable Exits Stealth with $26M Seed to Index the Web for AI Agents

Search engines were built for humans who cannot spare the time or attention to scan entire web pages. But as AI chatbots increasingly handle search and task execution, the internet's infrastructure was never designed to cater to AI agents that can process far larger portions of information.

Keenable, founded by former Yandex search and AI chief Andrey Styskin and German AI scientist Matthias Petri, is building a web search index specifically optimized for AI agents. The company emerged from stealth with $26 million in seed funding led by Accel, with participation from Conviction Partners and angel investors.

Unlike traditional search engines optimized for human attention spans, Keenable indexes the full web at a granularity suited for AI processing. The index is designed for AI agents that need comprehensive information rather than ranked snippets.

Styskin led Yandex's search, AI, and cloud division before starting Keenable. The startup's approach recognizes that AI agents require fundamentally different infrastructure than people do — agents can read and synthesize entire pages rather than scanning titles and excerpts.

The $26 million seed round will fuel product development and expansion as Keenable builds out its agent-optimized search infrastructure.
