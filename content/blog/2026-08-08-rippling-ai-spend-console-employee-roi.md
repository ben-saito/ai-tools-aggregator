# Rippling Launches AI Spend Console After Blowing Millions on AI in Months

HR software provider Rippling this week unveiled AI Spend Console, a product designed to help companies track and control their AI spending after the company itself burned through millions of dollars on AI tools in just a few months.

---

## Anti-Tokenmaxxing Product

AI Spend Console is described by Rippling as an "anti-tokenmaxxing" product — a play on the term "tokenmaxxing" used in the AI community to describe maximizing AI usage regardless of cost efficiency. The tool gives companies visibility into which employees, teams, and roles are spending the most on AI services, and whether that spending is translating into genuine productivity gains.

One notable feature: the tool can identify engineers whose AI spending is high while peers frequently ask them to redo work in code reviews — suggesting their AI-assisted output may be generating more rework than value.

---

## Wake-Up Call on AI Costs

The product launch comes after Rippling's own experience with runaway AI costs. Companies across the industry have reported similar surprises as they rolled out AI coding assistants, LLM API calls, and other AI-powered tools without adequate monitoring systems in place.

Rippling's tool also promises to show which teams are achieving genuine productivity gains from AI versus those generating what the company describes as "AI slop" — low-quality output that requires significant rework.

---

## Tracking ROI, Not Just Spending

Unlike simple billing dashboards, AI Spend Console aims to connect AI spending to actual business outcomes. The product analyzes whether high AI spending correlates with improved code quality, faster project completion, or other measurable metrics — or whether it primarily results in inflated API bills.
