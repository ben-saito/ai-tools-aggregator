# Bluesky Expands Attie AI Platform with New Creator Tools for Websites, Games, and Interactive Experiences

---

Bluesky has announced a significant expansion of Attie, its experimental AI-powered platform for building custom social feeds, adding capabilities that allow creators to build websites, quizzes, games, polls, and other interactive experiences without needing software development expertise. The announcement, made at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, positions Attie as a broader creative toolkit that competes with traditional website builders and social media creation tools—while keeping AI-powered customization at its core.

## Attie's Evolution: From Custom Feeds to Full Creative Tools

Attie launched as Bluesky's experimental platform for AI-powered custom social feeds, allowing users to create algorithmically curated timelines tailored to specific interests or communities. The platform initially offered tools for building personalized feed algorithms that could surface content based on user-defined preferences.

The expansion announced at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 represents a substantial broadening of Attie's scope. Creators can now build standalone websites hosted within the Bluesky ecosystem, interactive quizzes and polls, simple games, and other experiences that extend beyond social feed curation. The tools are designed to be accessible to non-technical users, with AI assisting in layout suggestions, content generation, and interactive element configuration.

## The AI Layer in Creator Tools

Bluesky has emphasized that AI remains central to the Attie experience even as the platform expands into general creative tools. The platform uses AI to help with content suggestions, automated layout optimization, and the configuration of interactive elements. For quizzes and polls, AI can assist in generating questions based on topic descriptions provided by creators.

The differentiation from traditional website builders lies in the integration of Bluesky's social graph and recommendation infrastructure. Creators building on Attie can tap into Bluesky's existing user base and social features—follows, likes, comments—as native components of their websites and interactive experiences.

## Market Positioning and Competitive Landscape

Bluesky is positioning Attie as a creator-first alternative to platforms like Squarespace, Wix, and other website builders, with the added differentiation of built-in social features and AI-powered customization. The platform is also competing with Patreon and other creator monetization platforms by offering a broader toolkit for building audience relationships.

The expansion reflects Bluesky's broader strategy of building outward from its core social networking product into a more comprehensive creator platform. Attie serves as the AI-forward face of that expansion, offering tools that leverage Bluesky's machine learning infrastructure for content curation and personalization.

## Availability and Rollout

The new creator tools are available starting October 15, 2026, as an expansion of the existing Attie platform. Creators can access the tools through the Attie interface, with AI-assisted features included in the platform's standard subscription tier. Bluesky has not disclosed specific pricing tiers for advanced features.
