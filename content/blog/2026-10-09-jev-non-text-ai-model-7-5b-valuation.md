# Jev Non-Text AI Model Valued at $7.5B Just Weeks After Launch

---

Jev, the AI startup behind a non-text-based artificial intelligence model, has reached a valuation of $7.5 billion just weeks after its official launch. The rapid valuation milestone positions Jev as one of the fastest AI companies to reach unicorn status in 2026.

Unlike traditional large language models that process and generate text, Jev's model operates on structured data representations that the company describes as "conceptual graphs." This approach allows the model to reason about relationships between entities without the linguistic overhead of natural language processing.

The company's focus on non-text AI addresses a gap in the current AI landscape, where most prominent models are optimized for conversational and written content tasks. Jev's technology is particularly suited for applications in supply chain optimization, fraud detection, and scientific research assistance.

Recent partnerships announced include collaborations with three major pharmaceutical companies to accelerate drug discovery pipelines. In these deployments, Jev's model analyzes patterns across genomic data, clinical trial results, and published research to identify promising therapeutic candidates.

The $7.5 billion valuation comes after a $500 million Series B round led by Sequoia Capital, with participation from existing investors including Andreessen Horowitz and Google's venture arm. The round values Jev at approximately 15 times its annual recurring revenue.

CEO Maria Chen explained that the company's approach to AI differs fundamentally from transformer-based models. "We're not trying to predict the next token," she said in a recent interview. "We're building systems that can reason about complex relationships in ways that are interpretable and controllable."

The company's technical approach has attracted attention from AI safety researchers, who note that non-text representations may offer advantages in certain alignment contexts. However, questions remain about how Jev's model will scale to handle the breadth of tasks that text-based models can address.
