# Klaviyo Acquires Agency, Hiring Founder Elias Torres as CPO to Lead AI Agent Expansion

Klaviyo, the publicly traded e-commerce marketing automation platform, has acquired Agency — a three-year-old AI-powered customer success startup founded by serial entrepreneur Elias Torres — and announced Torres will join Klaviyo as Chief Product Officer. The deal terms were not disclosed.

---

## A Full-Circle reunion

The acquisition brings together two figures whose relationship dates back to 2010. Torres hired Klaviyo co-founder and CEO Andrew Bialecki as one of the first engineers at Performable, a startup Torres co-founded. Torres mentored the young Harvard graduate on early-stage startup dynamics before Performable was acquired by HubSpot in 2011.

Bialecki went on to bootstrap Klaviyo after leaving Performable, eventually building it into one of the dominant platforms in e-commerce marketing automation. When Klaviyo raised its first outside capital in 2015, Bialecki invited Torres to join the board — a relationship that culminated in this week's acquisition.

"I soaked it up in a short amount of time," Torres recalled of mentoring Bialecki at Performable.

---

## Agency's Technology and Klaviyo's AI Agent Strategy

Agency had raised $32 million from Sequoia, Menlo Ventures, and Felicis prior to the acquisition. The company built AI agents focused on post-sale customer success: Composer, which automates marketing campaign creation, and Customer Agent, which handles tasks like returns and order tracking.

Under the deal, Torres will lead Agency's 25-person team in integrating these capabilities into Klaviyo's existing product lineup. The goal, according to Bialecki, is to bring AI agent-powered automation to Klaviyo's base of over 200,000 customer businesses and eventually scale to millions more.

"Elias and the team built a great product with Agency," Bialecki told TechCrunch. "We're going to take that and combine it with our agent products and try to bring that to 200,000 businesses."

---

## AI Agents in E-Commerce

The acquisition reflects the broader trend of embedding autonomous AI agents into enterprise software workflows. For Klaviyo, adding purpose-built AI agents to its platform represents a strategic move to differentiate from competitors in the crowded marketing automation space and capture more of the post-sale customer lifecycle. The integration of Torres's team — with deep experience building AI-native customer success workflows — could accelerate Klaviyo's timeline for delivering agentic commerce experiences.
