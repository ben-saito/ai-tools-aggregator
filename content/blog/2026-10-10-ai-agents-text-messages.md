# The top AI agents that can live in your text messages

---

Rather than downloading another app, a growing number of AI agents can simply be texted like an ordinary person. You text the agent what you need, and it remembers context, connects to the apps and services you already use, and completes tasks on your behalf — scheduling appointments, organizing calendars, researching trips, sending emails, making reservations, shopping online, or setting reminders.

The TechCrunch roundup surveyed the leading AI agents accessible via SMS and messaging platforms, examining their capabilities, pricing, and the underlying AI infrastructure powering them. The piece noted that this model of AI interaction represents a fundamental shift from app-based interfaces toward conversation-based service access.

Among the agents covered, key differentiators include memory persistence across conversations, integration depth with calendar and payment systems, and the ability to complete multi-step tasks without user intervention. Some agents can maintain context for days or weeks; others are session-based.

The article also explored the business model implications of text-message AI agents. Rather than driving engagement within a proprietary app, companies building these agents are effectively embedding themselves in the user's existing communication channel — meeting users where they already are rather than demanding they change behavior.

Security and privacy considerations were raised, as giving an AI agent access to text messages creates new attack surface for prompt injection and social engineering. Several agents have implemented verification mechanisms to distinguish agent interactions from human messages.

The trend reflects a broader industry movement toward agentic AI — systems that can take actions autonomously rather than merely responding to queries. Text-message accessibility dramatically lowers the barrier to using AI agents for everyday tasks.
