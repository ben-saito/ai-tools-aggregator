# Meta's Project OT: When AI Agents Were Meant to Replace Workers

Internal documents and reports reveal that Meta explored plans to replace a significant portion of its workforce with AI agents, codenamed Project OT (Organization Transformation). The plan called for slashing some team headcounts by 60 percent and included two rounds of layoffs, highlighting the scale of AI-driven workforce disruption being considered at major tech companies.

---

## The Plan: 60% Team Reduction Through AI

According to Reuters, Meta confirmed that Project OT explored scenarios in which the company reduced some team headcounts by 60 percent. The plan called for two rounds of layoffs and represented an aggressive attempt to automate knowledge work at scale.

However, the project reportedly resulted in "large-scale, disruptive actions" that forced Meta to abandon the initiative. The failure illustrates the current limitations of AI agents in handling complex organizational tasks that require judgment, context, and nuanced decision-making.

---

## The Reality of AI-Driven Workforce Transformation

The episode reflects a broader tension in the tech industry: while AI agents are increasingly capable of handling specific tasks, replacing entire teams with autonomous agents remains fraught with challenges. Meta's experience suggests that even well-resourced companies with access to cutting-edge AI technology face significant obstacles in automating knowledge work at scale.

---

## Industry Implications

The news comes as companies across industries experiment with AI agents for everything from customer service to software development. Meta's setback serves as a cautionary tale about the gap between AI capability and AI-ready organizational processes.
