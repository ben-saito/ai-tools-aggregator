# Gamma Acquires AI Design Startup Lica to Build Research Division

Gamma, the presentation and design startup known for its AI-powered slide deck tools, announced on August 25, 2026 the acquisition of Lica, an Accel-backed design startup that builds AI models for visual communication. The Lica co-founders will join Gamma's newly formed research team. Financial terms were not disclosed.

---

## Lica's Background and Technology

Lica, founded in 2023 by Priyaa Kalyanaraman and Purvanshi Mehta, started as an app that could turn screenshots and recordings into project presentations and videos. In 2024, the company raised a seed round and began focusing on building AI models around communication methods including video and design. The company developed technology that could analyze visual content and automatically generate structured presentations.

Kalyanaraman said Lica wanted to build AI models around communication methods, including video and design, and the founders were familiar with Gamma's CEO Grant Lee, as both companies are backed by Accel.

---

## Gamma's AI Presentation Strategy

Gamma will continue focusing on helping people build presentations, but like other design companies, it has started offering image generation. The company says it now wants to explore different formats for visual communication. The acquisition gives Gamma access to Lica's research capabilities and talent.

"We have a very similar North Star vision of wanting to make visual communication easy," Kalyanaraman said. "We focused a lot on frontier research and making sure that became accessible to users. But that's just one part of what we want to build."

Mehta said the new research division would work on customizing the presentation and communication styles of AI models for different audiences and tastes. "The point of the presentation is to communicate a large goal. We are thinking about building a platform where these two goals can be personalized to such an extent that you just get a really good output."

---

## AI Presentation Market Landscape

AI presentation startups have attracted significant investor attention. Prezent raised $50 million in two rounds. Gamma itself raised $68 million at a $2.1 billion valuation. The acquisition of Lica positions Gamma to compete more directly with Notion, Canva, and other productivity platforms that are rapidly adding AI features to their design tools.
