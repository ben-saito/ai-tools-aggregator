# HiddenLayer Raises $100M to Secure Enterprise AI Deployments

AI security startup HiddenLayer has raised $100 million in Series B funding as enterprises rapidly deploy AI agents across critical workflows, creating new attack surfaces that traditional security tools do not cover.

---

## The AI Security Market Explodes

Gartner estimates companies will spend $2.83 billion on AI security products this year, an 83% increase from 2025, with spending projected to reach $4.78 billion next year. This explosive growth has created a land grab among security vendors racing to build products for the AI stack.

HiddenLayer's Series B was led by Delta-v Capital, with participation from Ten Eleven Ventures, Morgan Stanley, Microsoft M12, and Booz Allen Hamilton. The round brings the company's total funding to over $150 million.

---

## From Traditional ML to Agentic AI

HiddenLayer initially focused on securing traditional machine learning models. But the rise of generative AI and AI agents has dramatically expanded the attack surface it must defend.

"Inference is still inference. So whether it's on a traditional machine learning model, whether it's GenAI, whether it's an agentic work stream, a lot of our technology still applied," said HiddenLayer CEO Chris Sestito. "We've had to grow our scope from traditional modeling to GenAI to agentic."

The company currently serves financial services and large tech companies as its largest verticals, and has contracts with the Department of Defense and intelligence community. One anchor customer is described as "a leading frontier model provider with more than 700 million weekly users" — widely believed to be OpenAI or Anthropic.

---

## New Threat: Hidden Models Inside Models

HiddenLayer's new products address an emerging attack vector: open source model tampering. The company now parses and scans approximately 50 different AI file frameworks to verify that open-weight models are what they claim to be.

"We're looking at things like models purporting to be one thing, but they're another — hidden models inside of models," Sestito explained.

The company plans to use the new capital to expand sales and distribution, grow engineering and research, and enter the European and EMEA markets. It will also expand horizontally into adjacent areas of cybersecurity that increasingly depend on AI.

---

## Competitive Landscape

HiddenLayer competes with startups like Noma and Zenity, both of which have raised over $100 million. Large cybersecurity companies including Cisco, Palo Alto Networks, and Check Point have shown interest in acquiring AI security capabilities rather than building them in-house.

Sestito acknowledged this risk, noting that AI infrastructure may eventually bundle AI security natively rather than relying on specialized vendors. For now, HiddenLayer is betting that the AI security market will grow faster than the incumbents can respond.

* (Information current as of September 2, 2026)
