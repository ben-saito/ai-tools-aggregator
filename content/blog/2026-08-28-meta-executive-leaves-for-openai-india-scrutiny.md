# Meta Executive Departs for OpenAI as Regulatory Scrutiny Mounts in India

Meta's head of policy for India and South Asia has left the company to join OpenAI, marking a significant executive departure during a period of intensifying regulatory pressure on the social media giant in the region.

Sandhya Devanathan will be based in Singapore and report to OpenAI's Asia-Pacific managing director, Kiran Mani. She will oversee consumer growth, enterprise adoption and partnerships, regulatory engagement and operations across Southeast Asia and Australia, OpenAI said.

The executive is leaving Meta after more than a decade at the company, which has been the subject of growing scrutiny over issues such as online safety and content moderation. Devanathan was involved in the company's decision processes around compliance with India's IT laws and regulations.

---

## Meta Faces Escalating Regulatory Pressure

India has become one of Meta's most important markets, with hundreds of millions of users across Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp. However, the company has faced increasing pressure from Indian regulators over content moderation practices, data privacy concerns, and compliance with the country's IT rules.

The timing of Devanathan's departure coincides with Meta confronting some of its most significant regulatory challenges in India to date. The company has been required to respond to government requests around misinformation, harmful content, and platform accountability.

---

## OpenAI Expands Asia-Pacific Operations

The hiring signals OpenAI's continued expansion in the Asia-Pacific region, where the company is working to build enterprise partnerships and navigate diverse regulatory environments. Devanathan's experience navigating India's complex regulatory landscape is expected to help OpenAI as it seeks to grow its presence in the region.

OpenAI has been actively building out its international operations, with the company establishing partnerships and hiring local expertise across multiple markets. The addition of a senior executive with Devanathan's background suggests the company is taking a proactive approach to regulatory engagement across Southeast Asia.

---

## Industry Impact

The move highlights the intensifying competition for regulatory affairs talent in the AI industry, as companies seek executives who can navigate complex government relationships across multiple jurisdictions. Both Meta and OpenAI are grappling with evolving AI governance frameworks across Asia-Pacific.
