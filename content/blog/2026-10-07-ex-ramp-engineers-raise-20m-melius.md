# Ex-Ramp Engineers Raise $20M for AI Platform Melius After Pivoting from First Product

Former Ramp engineers have secured $20 million in funding for Melius, an AI-powered platform launched after abandoning their initial product approach. The startup reached $1 million in annualized revenue within just two months of its post-pivot launch, demonstrating rapid market traction.

---

## From Ramp to Melius: The Pivot Story

The founding team previously worked at Ramp, the AI-powered finance automation company. After identifying a gap in enterprise AI infrastructure, they pivoted to build Melius—a platform designed to help businesses deploy and manage AI agents at scale.

The quick revenue acceleration suggests strong demand for enterprise-focused AI solutions that go beyond simple chatbots. The Melius platform apparently addresses pain points in AI deployment, monitoring, and optimization that many businesses struggle with when moving from proof-of-concept to production.

---

## What Melius Offers

While details on the exact product are still emerging, the Melius platform appears to focus on:

- **Enterprise AI agent deployment**: Helping companies deploy AI agents that handle complex workflows
- **AI infrastructure management**: Tools for monitoring and maintaining AI systems in production
- **Integration capabilities**: Connecting AI agents with existing enterprise systems and data sources

The two-month path to $1 million annualized revenue indicates the team found strong product-market fit quickly, possibly by targeting underserved mid-market companies that need AI agent infrastructure but lack the resources to build it themselves.

---

## Funding Environment for AI Startups

Melius joins a crowded field of AI infrastructure companies, but its rapid traction—combined with the pedigree of its founding team—likely made it attractive to investors. The $20 million raise is relatively modest for an AI infrastructure startup in 2026, suggesting investors are being more measured after the boom years of 2023-2024.
