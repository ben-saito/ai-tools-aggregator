# Former TikTok Execs Launch Superpose: An AI App That Teaches You How to Pose for Photos

A team of former TikTok executives has launched Superpose, a camera app that uses AI to analyze your selfies and suggest poses that work best for your face shape, body type, and photography style.

The app analyzes selfies or photos users upload and generates four potential poses using AI. The suggestions are tailored based on the user's specific features, including face shape, body proportions, skin tone, and even the lighting conditions in their photos.

"We noticed that most pose suggestions online are generic," said one of the founders. "What makes Superpose different is that it learns your specific geometry and gives you poses that actually work for your body."

Superpose uses a combination of computer vision models and a large dataset of professional photography poses to generate suggestions. The app was built over 18 months with seed funding from Sequoia Capital.

The app integrates directly with the iOS camera and works with any photo from the user's library. A premium tier ($4.99/month) offers unlimited pose generations and access to video pose tutorials.

This launch comes as several former TikTok executives have started new ventures. ByteDance's restructuring following regulatory pressures has led to an exodus of talent into the AI photography and social media spaces.

Superpose is available today on iOS with an Android version expected in Q4 2026.
