# Google Adds New AI Study Tools to Search and Gemini for Students

Google has added new AI-powered study tools to Search and Gemini, aiming to position Gemini as the AI assistant students turn to for learning and studying.

The new Study Deck feature allows students to organize information about specific subjects and topics systematically. Gemini automatically extracts key concepts and generates flashcards and quiz-style study materials.

Search also received AI-enhanced learning support features. Clicking on a specific topic now displays concise explanations and visual summaries directly in results. The launch marks Google's latest effort to compete with ChatGPT for the student demographic.

Google also announced a new Research mode for Gemini, which automatically generates summaries of long papers and technical documents, helping students process large reading assignments more efficiently.

The features are available immediately in the Gemini app and through Google Search integration. Google is positioning these tools as part of a broader push to make Gemini the default AI assistant for academic use.
