# Instinct AI Assistant Gets Its Own Email Address -- Another Step Toward Autonomous Agents

Instinct, the buzzy AI assistant now valued at $2.5 billion, is rolling out a new feature that will allow it to do more on its users' behalf: it is giving everyone Instinct email addresses.

The company's founder, Noah Shinn, announced on Tuesday that the new email addresses let the agent create and run its own accounts without cluttering up users' inboxes.

The idea is to allow Instinct to handle account sign-up and management when completing tasks that require email -- like creating accounts on various services, contacting businesses, and following up on things that need attention.

"For example, Instinct can use its own email to contact a restaurant about a special request, ask a business about availability, or sign up for a service it needs to complete your task," Shinn wrote on X.

---

## Owning Its Own Accounts

The feature is a step toward agents that can operate autonomously on behalf of users without requiring constant intervention. Rather than having to log in or provide credentials, Instinct can now act independently in email-mediated workflows.

Most everyday tasks flow through email: creating accounts, confirming bookings, receiving confirmations, and following up on orders. By giving Instinct its own email address, the company is removing a bottleneck that previously required users to manually forward or share their inbox.

Instinct says it has reserved some email addresses for its earliest users, and others can claim theirs at mail.instinct.com.

The company also recently partnered with 1Password for credential management, suggesting a broader strategy of positioning Instinct as an autonomous agent that can handle cross-service workflows without user mediation.

---

## Implications for Businesses and Consumers

For businesses trying to establish a relationship with their customers, having an AI manage the account could provide a layer of obscurity as to who they are actually dealing with. Customers, however, may see this as a perk -- many are tired of having to hand over emails and phone numbers just to complete simple tasks.

If a user wants Instinct to handle a product return on its behalf, they could forward their order confirmation to Instinct's email address. Instinct could then contact support, provide necessary context, and follow through to resolution -- all without the user being in the loop.

The feature is currently rolling out and represents one of the more concrete examples of an AI agent operating with its own digital identity rather than merely acting as an interface to a user's existing accounts.
