# Open vs. Closed AI: The Debate Shaping Next-Gen Startups

At TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, Nvidia's Nader Khalil and Sydney Sykes took the Builders Stage to discuss one of the most consequential decisions facing AI startups: whether to build on open or closed AI models. The session, titled "The Open vs. Closed AI Debate Is Just Getting Started," explored how this choice affects cost, infrastructure, margins, and competitive differentiation.

---

## The Core Tension

The open versus closed debate centers on a fundamental tradeoff. Closed models like GPT-4 and Claude offer reliability and safety guardrails but come with API costs and dependency on a single provider. Open models like Llama and Mistral provide flexibility and cost savings but require more engineering effort to deploy and secure.

For startups building AI applications, this decision is not merely technical—it shapes the entire business model. Closed model providers capture significant margin, while open model adopters must invest in infrastructure and MLOps expertise.

---

## Nvidia's Perspective

As a hardware provider whose GPUs power both open and closed AI systems, Nvidia occupies a unique position in this debate. Khalil and Sykes discussed how the choice between open and closed affects infrastructure requirements, with closed models often demanding more specialized compute optimized for specific model architectures, while open deployments tend toward more flexible, commodity infrastructure.

The session explored how startups can hedge their bets, using open models for certain tasks while relying on closed systems for others, depending on latency, cost, and customization requirements.

---

## The Road Ahead

The debate remains unresolved, and observers disagree on whether the industry will converge toward open or closed dominance. Some predict that as models commoditize, the differentiation will shift to data and fine-tuning, advantaging open approaches. Others argue that frontier models will remain closed due to safety concerns and the massive compute required.

For founders making this decision today, the consensus is that it must be made deliberately, with clear awareness of how it will affect every aspect of the business.
