# Empirik Emerges from Stealth with $21M to Predict IT Outages Before They Happen

---

## From Reactive to Predictive IT Operations

Two tech veterans who spent years responding to system failures decided there had to be a better approach: predict outages before they happen rather than scramble after the fact. That insight led them to build Empirik, a product that tracks system metrics to identify anomalies that typically precede failures.

Empirik is positioning itself as a new type of observability tool that helps prevent and resolve incidents -- shifting IT operations from reactive firefighting to proactive prevention.

---

## Funding and Spin-Out

Sequoia incubated the startup in 2023, recognizing that Empirik could address a real gap in the observability market. After incubation, Sequoia recruited former Quantum Metric CPO to lead the company as it spun out as an independent entity.

Empirik announced it is spinning out as an independent company after raising $21 million in seed funding from Sequoia, Canapi, and Alumni Ventures.

---

## The Observability Market

Traditional observability tools focus on showing what is happening in systems now -- dashboards, metrics, alerts. Empirik's approach is different: it uses patterns learned from historical failure data to identify conditions that typically precede outages, allowing teams to address issues before users experience them.

The product is aimed at IT infrastructure teams that want to move from reactive incident response to proactive reliability engineering.
