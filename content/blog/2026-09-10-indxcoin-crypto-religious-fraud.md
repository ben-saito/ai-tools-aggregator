# God Told Them to Sell Crypto: Inside the INDXcoin Scandal

A joint investigation by MIT Technology Review and Type Investigations reveals how religious conviction became a tool for cryptocurrency fraud, leaving investors with devastating losses.

---

## The Promise

Eli Regalado, the founder of INDXcoin, told investors that God had instructed him to sell cryptocurrency. For many followers, this religious framing provided the trust needed to invest substantial sums. The promise was simple: divine guidance would lead to financial returns.

---

## The Reality

Instead of the promised returns, investors lost everything they put into INDXcoin. The investigation found that the operation used religious conviction to attract investors who might otherwise be skeptical of cryptocurrency schemes. Messages were framed as divine instructions, creating a sense of urgency and moral obligation.
