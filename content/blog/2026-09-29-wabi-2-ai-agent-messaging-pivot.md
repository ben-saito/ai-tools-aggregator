# Wabi 2.0: Former Replika Founder Pivots App Builder to Agentic Messaging Platform

The AI-powered app builder Wabi, created by Eugenia Kuyda who previously founded the AI companion startup Replika, has pivoted to a messaging-focused experience called Wabi 2.0. Rather than functioning solely as a prompt-based application builder, Wabi 2.0 repositions the platform as a personal AI agent capable of creating interfaces on demand, combining chat, applications, and ongoing tasks within a single messaging environment.

Kuyda described Wabi 2.0 as "the OS for the agentic era" in an announcement post, framing the pivot as a response to the shifting landscape of human-AI interaction. The new platform allows users to engage with AI agents through conversational interfaces while simultaneously accessing dynamically generated application functionalities.

Wabi had previously operated as a tool enabling users to build applications through natural language prompts, generating code and interface elements based on user instructions. The shift to an agentic messaging model reflects a broader industry trend toward AI systems that can autonomously navigate and execute multi-step workflows rather than simply responding to isolated prompts.

The messaging-centric approach distinguishes Wabi 2.0 from other AI agent platforms by embedding task execution directly within a chat interface, enabling users to initiate complex workflows through conversation while maintaining access to generated application components that persist across interactions.

Wabi raised a $20 million Series B in 2024 led by Samsung's venture arm, suggesting the company had previously focused on integrations with Samsung's mobile ecosystem. The strategic pivot to an agentic messaging platform may indicate a broadening of the company's target market beyond hardware-specific deployments.
