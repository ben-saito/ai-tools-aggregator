# OpenAI Safety Researchers Publish Open Letter Denying Misconduct, Warn of Chilling Effect

---

Three fired OpenAI safety researchers—Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak, and Mikita Balesni—have published an open letter denying the company's claims that they mishandled sensitive information, and warning that their dismissals are creating a chilling effect across the company.

The researchers were terminated after allegedly sharing confidential information outside established procedures. In their response, they wrote: "We have become concerned that internal and external communications around our firing have made our former colleagues afraid to speak and operate in ways that, until last week, were an integral part of working at OpenAI."

The letter was addressed to OpenAI's Safety and Security Committee, Safety Advisory Group, and Mission Advisory Council. The researchers dispute the company's narrative, stating they followed proper procedures for external communication.

The incident comes at a time of heightened scrutiny of OpenAI's safety practices, as the company balances commercial development with its stated mission of developing safe AI systems. The open letter highlights growing tensions within the company over how to balance these priorities.

Industry observers note that the departure of safety-focused researchers could impact the company's ability to identify and mitigate risks in its AI systems. The researchers' warning about a "chilling effect" suggests potential long-term damage to the company's safety culture.
