# Ando Emerges from Stealth with $20M to Build AI-First Team Messaging

Ando, a new team messaging platform designed for both humans and AI agents to work together, has emerged from stealth with $20 million in pre-seed and seed funding from investors including Accel, Index Ventures, and Emergence, according to TechCrunch's September 24, 2026 report.

---

## The Problem with AI Agents in Existing Tools

The core insight behind Ando is that existing communication platforms were not designed with AI agents in mind. When AI agents complete work, their outputs often need to be manually transcribed or summarized by humans — what Ando founder Du calls "meat proxies."

"The deeper issue stems from how communication platforms were designed," Du explained. Current tools require people to relay AI agents' work rather than letting agents participate directly in shared conversations.

---

## A Platform Built for Both Humans and Agents

Ando bills itself as a full replacement for Slack or other messaging platforms, but with native AI agent integration. Rather than requiring separate tools for human coordination and AI agent oversight, Ando provides a unified platform where agents can participate in channels, threads, and direct messages alongside human employees.

An ideal system would let agents "participate in the shared conversations where work already gets coordinated," Du said. "They should be able to read, respond, and contribute to the same threads that humans use."

---

## The $20M Bet on Agent-Human Workflows

The $20 million funding round — large for a pre-seed/seed stage company — signals investor confidence that AI agent workflows represent a significant market opportunity. Accel, Index Ventures, and Emergence are all known for backing early-stage enterprise software companies with broad platform ambitions.
