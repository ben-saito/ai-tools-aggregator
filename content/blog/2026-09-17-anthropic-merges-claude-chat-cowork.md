# Anthropic Merges Claude Chat and Cowork in Unified Interface

Anthropic has announced the integration of Claude's chat interface with Cowork, bringing together conversational AI and collaborative features in a single unified experience for Pro and Max plan subscribers.

---

## Unified Claude Experience

The merger combines Claude's natural language conversational capabilities with Cowork's collaborative features, allowing users to access both modalities through a single interface. The initial rollout targets Pro and Max plan subscribers, with broader availability expected to follow.

This integration reflects a broader trend in AI assistant development, where providers are working to create more seamless experiences that combine multiple interaction paradigms rather than maintaining separate products for different use cases.
