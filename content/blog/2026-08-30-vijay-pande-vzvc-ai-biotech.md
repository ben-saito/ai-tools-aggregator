# Former a16z Bio Fund Lead Vijay Pande Establishes New Fund VZVC -- Small Scale, AI-Driven Biotech

Vijay Pande, who led the bio and life sciences division at a16z, one of the world's largest venture capital firms, has established a new fund VZVC earlier this year. Co-founded with longtime investor Zach Werner, VZVC focuses on a handful of concentrated bets per year rather than dozens, has no associates, and relies heavily on AI for day-to-day operations.

---

## Current State of AI Biotech

Pande explains that biology AI is shifting from a "science of discovery" to something that can be engineered, with AI and machine learning driving this transformation.

Unlike text data that can be scraped from the internet, biological data cannot be easily obtained. Almost every company ends up building its own walled-off dataset. This affects the reliability of AI in medicine and who actually gets access to these advances.

---

## Challenges in Drug Development

The cost and time to get to clinical trials has been shrinking with AI, but running a trial can still cost hundreds of millions of dollars. The probability of a drug successfully progressing from first trial to the end of the third trial is just 20 percent. Eight out of 10 drugs fail, typically because animal models used in pre-clinical studies are not very predictive of humans.

AI models are not perfect but are significantly better than animal models. Once they cross that threshold, the implications become exciting.

---

## Precision Medicine and Individualized Treatment

The future lies in determining the right drug for each individual patient from the start, rather than the current approach of trial and error with multiple drugs. Currently, blood test values are compared to population averages when they should be compared to what is actually abnormal for that specific individual.
