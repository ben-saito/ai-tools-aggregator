# AI Chip Startup Etched Valued at $21 Billion After Rapid Scaling

Etched, the AI-specialized chip company, has announced a $700 million funding round at a $21 billion valuation, just one month after its previous valuation. Jane Street led the round after testing and deploying Etched's AI hardware.

---

## Rapid Valuation Growth Signals AI Infrastructure Demand

Etched on Tuesday announced the new funding at a $21 billion valuation, led by Jane Street after the famed quant fund tested and bought the startup's AI hardware. The company's valuation has doubled in just one month, reflecting the intense demand for AI-specific computing infrastructure.

Even by AI standards, this valuation step-up is remarkable. The startup's focus on inference-specific hardware appears to be resonating with major financial and technology firms seeking alternatives to traditional GPU-based computing.

---

## Specialized AI Chips Gain Traction

Etched's hardware is designed specifically for AI inference workloads. "Inference is built in two stages," explained a company representative. "Prefill and decode." The prefill phase, which is mathematically and compute-intensive, is where Etched's chips are designed to excel.

The company's rapid valuation growth highlights the broader trend of specialized AI hardware gaining market share against general-purpose computing solutions.
