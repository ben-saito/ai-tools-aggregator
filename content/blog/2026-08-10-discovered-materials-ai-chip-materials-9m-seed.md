# Discovered Materials Raises $9M to Hunt Better Chip Materials with AI

Discovered Materials, a startup using AI agent swarms to discover new materials for semiconductors, raised a $9 million seed round from Lightspeed India Partners after graduating from Y Combinator, TechCrunch reported on August 10, 2026.

---

## AI-Driven Materials Discovery

The company, founded by Stanford materials science PhD Akash Ramdas and former AI engineer Advaith Sridhar, has built a software pipeline that uses Anthropic models in a custom harness to generate candidate materials, which are then verified using foundational physics models the company has trained.

Ramdas previously conducted around 20 material candidate experiments per day during his PhD. With the AI pipeline, the company now runs thousands of experiments daily, operating agents 24/7 on cloud infrastructure to explore research directions.

---

## The Semiconductor Challenge

Discovered Materials focuses specifically on thermal management in semiconductors — finding materials that can reduce heat generation or improve heat dissipation in chips. The company has released examples of hundreds of new materials alongside its "Material Discovery Bench" benchmark for tracking AI performance on this task.

The challenge is that a useful semiconductor material must satisfy multiple criteria simultaneously: favorable thermal properties, manufacturing feasibility, and appropriate electrical characteristics. As one investor described it, "It's a bit of playing whack-a-mole with atomic structures."

---

## Industry Context

Companies like MatNex, SandboxAQ, and CuspAI are pursuing similar approaches. Discovered Materials believes its focus on thermal problems in semiconductor materials, combined with Ramdas's deep domain expertise, provides a competitive edge. The company plans to patent novel materials it discovers for GPU applications and license them to chipmakers.
