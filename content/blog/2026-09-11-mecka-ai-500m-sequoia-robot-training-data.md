# Mecka AI Nears $500M Funding Round Led by Sequoia Capital

Mecka AI, a startup that collects and analyzes human motion data to train humanoid robots and other robotics systems, is nearing a new round led by Sequoia Capital at a valuation of approximately $500 million, according to two sources with knowledge of the deal.

The new financing comes just three months after Mecka announced a $60 million Series A led by Framework Ventures with participation from Menlo Ventures, SV Angel, and Kindred Ventures. TechCrunch has not learned the precise size of the new round, and terms are not final.

Mecka AI did not respond to requests for comment.

---

## Rush for Robot Training Data

The funding comes as demand for high-quality human motion data for robot training has surged. Mecka's data is critical for training robots to perform complex movements and operate effectively in real-world environments. The company specializes in capturing and annotating motion data that helps robotic systems learn dexterity, balance, and adaptive movement patterns.

This trend is part of a broader shift in AI investment toward robotics and physical AI systems. NVIDIA also announced GR00T TITIAN this week, its humanoid robot training platform, signaling intense competition in this space.

---

## Competitive Robotics AI Landscape

The fundraising environment for robotics AI companies remains highly active. Figure AI, Boston Dynamics, and 1X Technologies have all recorded multi-billion dollar valuations in recent funding rounds. Mecka's approach - focusing specifically on motion data infrastructure rather than building complete robots - positions it as a data provider for the broader robotics ecosystem.

The robotics AI sector has attracted significant capital as investors bet that physical AI systems will become the next frontier beyond language models. Companies building general-purpose robots with integrated perception and motor capabilities are emerging as key players in the next generation of AI competition.
