# Nscale Acquires Anyscale to Own More of the AI Compute Stack

British AI neocloud provider Nscale has acquired software startup Anyscale, which provides tools for scaling AI workloads across data centers and servers. The deal strengthens Nscale's vertical integration in the AI infrastructure stack.

---

## About Anyscale

Anyscale develops software that helps companies manage and scale AI training and inference workloads across distributed computing resources. The company's Ray-based platform is widely used for ML pipeline orchestration and has become a standard tool in the AI infrastructure ecosystem.

---

## Strategic Fit

Nscale positions itself as a full-stack AI cloud provider, offering compute, storage, and networking for AI workloads. By acquiring Anyscale, Nscale gains software layer capabilities that complement its infrastructure business, potentially allowing it to offer a more integrated platform to AI developers and enterprises.

---

## AI Compute Stack Consolidation

The acquisition reflects a broader trend of consolidation in the AI infrastructure space. As AI deployments mature, companies are seeking to simplify their vendor relationships by working with providers that can deliver multiple layers of the stack from hardware to software.
