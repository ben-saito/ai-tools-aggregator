# OpenAI Introduces 'Ultrafast' Mode for GPT-5.6 Sol: 14x Speed via Cerebras Partnership

OpenAI has launched "Ultrafast," a new processing mode for GPT-5.6 Sol that delivers up to 750 output tokens per second — 14 times the speed of standard processing.

---

## Enterprise Focus: Real-Time AI Workloads

The feature is squarely aimed at enterprise customers who need rapid AI responses for high-frequency workflows. OpenAI says Ultrafast is designed for incident response, customer service, financial market analysis, and e-commerce use cases where latency matters.

"Until now, getting real-time speed typically meant choosing a smaller or more specialized model," OpenAI said in its blog post. "Ultrafast points to progress in a new direction: more useful work per second."

---

## Cerebras Partnership Powers the Speed

Ultrafast is being powered by OpenAI's partnership with chipmaker Cerebras, which specializes in large-scale AI compute infrastructure. The preview is currently limited to a small group of customers, with plans to expand as capacity grows.

OpenAI's competitors have also moved into accelerated AI modes. Anthropic offers a fast mode for Claude, though OpenAI claims its 750 tokens-per-second throughput exceeds what competitors currently deliver.

---

## Strategic Implications

The launch reinforces OpenAI's shift toward enterprise deployment as its primary growth vector. Rather than competing solely on benchmark performance, OpenAI is differentiating on real-world responsiveness for production workloads.

For developers and enterprises evaluating AI providers, Ultrafast represents a concrete capability benchmark — particularly for any application where response latency directly impacts user experience or operational efficiency.
