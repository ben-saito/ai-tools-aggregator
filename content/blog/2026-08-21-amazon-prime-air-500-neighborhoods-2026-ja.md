# Amazon Aims for Prime Air Delivery Drones to Reach 500 US Neighborhoods by End of 2026

Amazon plans to expand Prime Air drone delivery to 500 U.S. neighborhoods by the end of 2026, accelerating expansion as regulatory approvals accumulate.

Prime Air is Amazon's proprietary drone delivery system designed to deliver small packages within 30 minutes. The service had already launched in several neighborhoods by mid-2026, with performance data being collected.

Expanding Prime Air requires FAA approval for each new route and operational area. Amazon has secured multiple approvals and is pursuing additional authorizations. The drones are equipped with bad weather auto-return, collision avoidance, and long-range communication links.

Residents in existing service areas have raised concerns about noise and privacy. Amazon is addressing these through flight route optimization and community outreach.

The 500-neighborhood target represents a significant acceleration from current deployment levels, reflecting both regulatory progress and validated technology performance.
