# Lambda raises $1 billion in private debt to purchase Nvidia AI chips

Lambda, an AI cloud company that purchases computing chips and rents them to businesses, has raised $1 billion in private short-dated debt to purchase Nvidia AI chips that will be leased to Microsoft.

---

## Deal Structure

According to Bloomberg, the deal was arranged by JP Morgan Chase. The terms signal that Lambda is betting it can quickly deploy the chips and start generating revenue, allowing it to service the debt efficiently.

This represents the latest in a string of loans Lambda is using to fund GPU infrastructure for specific customers. In May, the company closed a $1 billion secured credit facility and this week announced the closing of an additional facility.

---

## Pre-IPO Fundraising

The $1 billion private debt deal comes as Lambda is reportedly in talks for a $3 billion pre-IPO funding round. The company last November raised $1.5 billion in venture capital at a $5.43 billion post-money valuation.

---

## AI Infrastructure Debt Boom

Lambda is not alone in relying on debt to fund AI infrastructure. According to Bloomberg data, banks and tech companies have raised over $400 billion in AI-related debt globally in 2026 so far.

This surge in AI-related borrowing reflects the massive capital requirements of building and maintaining AI data center infrastructure, particularly for GPU clusters needed to train and serve large language models.
