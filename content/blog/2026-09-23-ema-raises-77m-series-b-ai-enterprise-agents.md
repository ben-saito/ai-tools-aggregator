# Ema raises $77M Series B to build AI agents that eat into enterprise software

Ema, a Bangalore-based AI startup founded in 2023 by former Google and Coinbase executive Surojit Chatterjee and ex-Okta executive Souvik Sen, has raised a $77 million Series B round led by Creaegis, with existing investors Accel, Section 32, and Prosus increasing their stakes. The funding brings Ema's total raised to $140 million, and the company has already signed more than 50 enterprise customers including Google and Microsoft.

---

## The bet: AI agents will replace traditional software

Ema is building what it calls a "universal AI employee" — an agentic AI system designed to automate workflows across enterprise software and IT services. Rather than embedding AI features into a single product, Ema's approach is to deploy AI agents that can navigate and operate across the full stack of enterprise tools a company already uses.

Chatterjee's thesis is that AI will eventually reduce companies' reliance on traditional software products, including software-as-a-service subscriptions. The implication is a fundamental shift in how enterprise IT budgets are allocated — from purchasing software licenses to paying for AI agent hours.

The Series B comes as a wave of enterprise AI agent startups compete for the same market. The difference with Ema is its focus on the Indian enterprise market as a beachhead, alongside expansion into North America.

---

## Enterprise software is facing an AI disruption

The funding round is notable because it signals that venture capital still has appetite for AI infrastructure plays even as the market grows crowded. Ema's approach — automating back-office and IT workflows through agentic AI — targets a different slice of the enterprise software market than copilots embedded in single products.

The company positions itself as an aggregator layer that sits across disparate enterprise systems and orchestrates tasks through natural language commands. Customers describe the workflow they want automated, and Ema's agents execute across systems without requiring API integrations for every step.

---

## Customer traction and expansion plans

With Google and Microsoft as reference customers, Ema has credibility with large technology companies even as it targets mid-market and enterprise clients. The company plans to use the new funding to expand its engineering team and accelerate product development.

The enterprise AI agent market is expected to see continued investment as companies look to automate knowledge work at scale. Ema's focus on the full workflow — rather than a single task — positions it against a broader shift toward agentic AI architectures in the enterprise.
