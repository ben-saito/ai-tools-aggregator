# Meta's Muse AI App Crosses 3.4 Million Downloads, Tops App Store Charts

---

## Meta's Personal AI Agent Reaches Milestone Following Strong Push at Meta Connect

Meta's AI assistant app Muse has surpassed 3.4 million downloads since its September 8 launch, climbing to the top of both the US App Store and Google Play Store charts. The rapid adoption represents a significant early win for Meta's strategy of embedding AI capabilities across its family of applications.

At the start of the week, Muse had 2.5 million downloads. The install numbers have grown substantially following Meta Connect, where the company announced a range of upcoming features for the AI app including enhanced multimodal capabilities and deeper integration with Instagram and WhatsApp.

---

## App Store Traction Across Multiple Metrics

Market intelligence firms show Muse with strong traction across different measurement methodologies. Sensor Tower estimates the app reached 2.8 million downloads in its first two weeks. Apptopia puts total downloads at approximately 2.3 million as of recent estimates, split nearly evenly between iOS and Android platforms.

The variation in estimates reflects the inherent difficulty in tracking mobile application installations across different data sources. However, all metrics point to rapid user adoption that outpaces typical consumer app launches in the AI assistant category.

On September 18, Muse climbed to the top of the US App Store and has retained that position continuously. It reached the top spot on Google Play on September 19 and maintains strong visibility in both major app marketplaces.

---

## Meta's Cross-Platform AI Strategy

Muse represents Meta's flagship personal AI agent, distinct from the Muse Charm wearable device announced at Meta Connect. The app provides conversational AI capabilities accessible directly through mobile devices, positioning Meta against established competitors in the consumer AI assistant market.

The strong download numbers suggest Meta's advantages in distribution through its existing social platforms may translate effectively to AI application adoption. The company's ability to promote Muse across Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp provides built-in marketing reach that independent AI assistant developers cannot match.
