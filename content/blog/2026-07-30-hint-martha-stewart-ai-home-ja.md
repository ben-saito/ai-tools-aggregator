# Hint: Martha Stewart Co-Founds AI Home Management Startup

AI home management startup Hint announced its launch with Martha Stewart as co-founder. Hint combines property records, maintenance schedules, document management, and an AI assistant into a single app, positioning itself as "AI for your home."

According to TechCrunch, Hint aims to provide a system that manages all household information in one place. The product is built on Stewart's practical experience with her own properties, covering areas like appliance warranties, insurance records, and garden documentation.

---

## Smart Home and AI Assistant Integration

Hint's AI assistant connects home smart devices with a smartphone app, giving users easy access to property records and digital content.

For example, the system can manage appliance maintenance schedules, insurance documents, and property registration information in one place. Stewart stated that "home is the most important asset in life," positioning AI as a tool to maximize home management efficiency.

---

## Development Background

Hint positions itself as a practical home management app while also growing as an AI startup. Stewart's brand recognition and hands-on experience provide a distinctive foundation for the product.

Global home automation market size continues to expand, and home record digitization projects represent significant opportunities for AI startups ahead.
