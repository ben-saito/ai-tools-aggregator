# Linkdaze Smart Calendar Targets Household Management

Linkdaze launched its smart digital calendar on August 20, 2026 — a touchscreen tablet built specifically for household organization rather than individual schedule tracking. The product differentiates itself by not placing features behind a paywall and includes an AI-powered meal planner.

---

## Core Features

Linkdaze synchronizes with major calendar services including Google, iCloud, Outlook, Yahoo, and Cozi, enabling families with members on different platforms to consolidate schedules on a single shared display.

The AI meal planner automatically suggests meals based on family preferences, dietary restrictions, and schedule constraints — connecting meal planning to time management in a unified interface.

---

## Target Market

With back-to-school season approaching, Linkdaze targets families managing multiple schedules across work, school, sports, and appointments. The shared display format allows household members to check the calendar without requiring each person to have a personal device.

---

## Business Model

Unlike many smart home devices that require subscriptions or lock features behind paywalls, Linkdaze's core functionality is available without additional cost, pursuing a hardware-plus-services model.
