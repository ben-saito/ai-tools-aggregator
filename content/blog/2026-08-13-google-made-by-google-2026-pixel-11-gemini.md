# Everything from Made by Google 2026: Pixel 11, Pixel Watch 5, Pixel Tag, Gemini Features

Google's hardware event this week delivered a broad refresh of its consumer product lineup, centered on deeper integration between Pixel devices and Gemini AI. The headline hardware includes the Pixel 11 series, Pixel Watch 5, and a new competitor to Apple's AirTag called Pixel Tag.

---

## Pixel 11 and Pixel 11 Pro

The Pixel 11 lineup features the new Tensor G5 chip, which Google says delivers a 40% improvement in on-device AI performance. The Pro model adds a dedicated vision processing unit optimized for real-time translation and camera-based AI features. Pricing starts at $799 for the base Pixel 11, consistent with last year's model.

Camera improvements center on a new 200MP main sensor and an ultra-wide that doubles as a macro lens. Google emphasized computational photography advances rather than hardware alone, with a new "Astrophotography 2.0" mode that handles low-light scenes with reduced noise.

---

## Pixel Tag: AirTag Rival

Google's long-rumored Bluetooth tracker finally arrived. Pixel Tag uses Google's Find My Device network, which now covers over 1 billion Android devices. The tag is smaller than an AirTag and includes a built-in speaker for proximity alerts. Google says it will work across Android and iOS via a cross-platform companion app.

---

## Pixel Watch 5

The Pixel Watch 5 gains a larger display and extended battery life — Google claims up to 72 hours in low-power mode. Health tracking receives upgrades including blood glucose estimation (non-invasive, via the wrist) and a new stress detection algorithm that adjusts recommendations based on heart rate variability.

---

## Gemini Everywhere

Gemini integration is now default across the Pixel lineup. The on-device model handles privacy-sensitive tasks like message drafting and photo editing without cloud connectivity. Gemini Live, Google's conversational AI mode, gains screen awareness on Pixel 11 — it can see what's on your display and answer questions about content in real time.
