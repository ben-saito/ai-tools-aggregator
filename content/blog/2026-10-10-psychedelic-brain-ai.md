# Brain Networks Under Psychedelics: AI Helps Reveal Hidden Order in Apparent Chaos

---

New research using AI analysis of brain scans is challenging the conventional understanding of how psychedelics affect the brain. Rather than causing pure chaos in neural networks, the findings suggest that apparent disorder may contain previously unrecognized patterns of coordination.

## The Established View

For about a decade, the mainstream understanding has been that psychedelics cause chaos in the brain. Brain networks — the stable patterns of communication handling vision, attention, and sense of self — loosen and begin cross-communicating simultaneously. EEG traces become noisier and more complex.

## AI Reveals Hidden Patterns

Researchers had previously interpreted these changes as random noise, but AI-powered analysis of brain imaging data is now revealing what appears to be genuine structure within the apparent chaos. The findings suggest that under psychedelics, the brain may be entering a different organized state rather than simply breaking down its normal coordination.

## Research Implications

The study had participants meditate inside an MRI tube both with and without psilocybin, allowing direct comparison of brain network behavior. Using machine learning to analyze the imaging data, researchers identified patterns that would be impossible to detect through traditional statistical methods alone.

This work represents a convergence of neuroscience, AI, and psychedelic research that could reshape understanding of consciousness-altering substances and their therapeutic potential.
