# Pangram Raises $9M to Detect AI-Generated Content at Scale

AI detection startup Pangram has raised $9 million in funding and launched a new detection model that the company claims achieves over 99% accuracy on text detection, according to TechCrunch. The funding round comes as AI-generated content floods the internet, making it increasingly difficult to distinguish human-written from machine-written text.

Pangram's approach differs from some competitors by training on tens of millions of human documents rather than using AI "synthetic mirrors" — generated text designed to train detection systems. The company argues this produces more reliable detection that is harder to evade.

Along with Pangram 4, the company has released an image detection model and a Chrome browser extension. The subscription pricing starts at $20 per month, making it accessible to individual users and small businesses concerned about AI content authenticity.

---

## Pangram 4 Capabilities

- **Text detection accuracy**: 99%+
- **Training approach**: Human documents vs synthetic mirrors
- **Additional products**: Image detection model, Chrome extension
- **Pricing**: $20/month subscription

---

## Market Context

The AI detection space has become increasingly competitive as concerns about AI-generated misinformation, academic dishonesty, and content authenticity grow. Pangram's human-first training approach represents a different philosophy than systems that rely heavily on AI-generated training data, though the effectiveness of different approaches remains debated in the research community.
