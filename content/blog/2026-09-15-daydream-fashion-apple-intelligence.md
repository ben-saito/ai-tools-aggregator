# Daydream: Apple Intelligence Powers New Fashion Shopping Feature

Apple's iOS 27 has unlocked a new wave of AI-powered mobile apps, and Daydream — a fashion-focused startup — is among the first to take advantage. Daydream's updated app now lets users photograph any outfit in their camera roll and instantly find similar products available for purchase online.

---

## From Camera Roll to Checkout

The feature relies on Apple Intelligence, Apple's suite of on-device AI models branded under the AFM family (AFM 3 Core for on-device processing). When a user selects a photo, Apple's Vision framework identifies individual garments, while a shopping recommendation model cross-references those items against partner retailer inventories.

Unlike previous image-to-shopping tools that required manual tagging of products by the user, Daydream's implementation is designed to work automatically on any saved photo — regardless of whether the original image was taken in-store, from social media, or captured informally.

The company positions this as a move toward "ambient commerce" — surfacing purchase intent at the moment a user is already thinking about a look, rather than requiring an explicit search.

---

## iOS 27 as a Platform for AI-Native Apps

Daydream's launch highlights a broader trend: iOS 27 is increasingly functioning as a distribution layer for third-party AI features. Apple's own Siri AI received a major overhaul in iOS 27, adding natural-language photo search and personal context awareness. Third-party apps are now able to tap into some of those same capabilities through updated APIs.

The timing is deliberate. Apple announced at WWDC 2026 that Apple Intelligence features would be accessible to developers through a new App Intents framework, enabling apps like Daydream to build workflows around photo context without building the underlying vision models from scratch.

---

## Competitive Landscape

Daydream enters a crowded space. Google Lens has offered visual shopping search for years, and Pinterest's Lens feature predates iOS 27 by a similar margin. What distinguishes Daydream is its exclusive integration with Apple Intelligence's privacy-preserving on-device processing, which keeps user photo data local rather than transmitting it to third-party servers for analysis.

The company raised its Series A in early 2026 and has positioned fashion retail as its initial beachhead before expanding to home goods and lifestyle categories.
