# Discovered Materials Raises $9M to Hunt Novel Chip Materials With AI

Discovered Materials raised $9 million to fund the search for novel materials that could enable more efficient chips. The startup uses AI to accelerate the traditionally slow process of discovering and validating new materials for semiconductor applications.

---

## The Chip Efficiency Challenge

As compute demand grows, the semiconductor industry faces fundamental limits on how much performance can be extracted from current materials. Traditional chip design has relied on incremental improvements, but the industry may need breakthrough materials to continue the pace of advancement.

---

## AI-Accelerated Materials Discovery

The traditional materials discovery pipeline takes years. AI enables rapid screening of candidate materials and prediction of their properties, potentially compressing discovery timelines significantly. This approach represents a new front in AI applications beyond software — physical science applications that could reshape manufacturing.
