# Legato Emerges from Stealth with $12M for AI-Powered Hearing Glasses

Legato emerged from stealth with $12 million in funding and a product that integrates hearing assistance technology into eyewear frames. The glasses, called Legato Frames, represent a convergence of audio and visual wearable technology.

---

## Hearing Assistance Meets Eyewear

The startup'sFrames integrate patented hearing-assistance technology into the arms of eyewear frames, creating a discreet form factor for users who need hearing support. The approach combines the form factor of regular glasses with functional hearing technology.

Legato's funding will support continued development and market expansion for its audio-integrated eyewear platform.
