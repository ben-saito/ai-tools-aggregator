# Groq Raises $350M at $3.5B Valuation - Pivot from AI Chips to Neocloud

---

## Funding Overview

On August 17, 2026, AI company Groq announced it raised $350 million in a funding round led by investment firm Disruptive, with planned participation from Nvidia. The company's post-money valuation stands at $3.5 billion.

This is down from the $6.9 billion valuation when Nvidia hired Groq's founding team in September 2025 as part of a $20 billion licensing deal. Groq characterizes this not as a down round, but as a new valuation established for the company after the Nvidia licensing arrangement.

---

## From AI Chips to Cloud

Groq originally built its own processors called LPUs (Language Processing Units) to compete with Nvidia on inference - the compute required to run AI workloads in real time. After losing its star team to Nvidia, Groq pivoted from being a pure AI chipmaker to a cloud and data center provider operating Nvidia systems.

The new capital will primarily be used to expand Groq's data center footprint, adding new GPU clusters alongside its existing greenback infrastructure.

---

## Industry Context

As AI labs increasingly compete for GPU compute resources, the role of cloud infrastructure providers has become critical. Groq's new business model positions it as a service provider to AI companies rather than a competitor - a shift that reflects the realities of GPU supply constraints and the capital intensity of AI infrastructure.
