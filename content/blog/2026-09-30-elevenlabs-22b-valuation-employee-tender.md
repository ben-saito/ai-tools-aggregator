# AI Voice Startup ElevenLabs Doubles Valuation to $22 Billion

ElevenLabs, the New York and London-based company known for ultra-realistic AI-generated voices, has reached a $22 billion valuation after employees were offered a chance to sell shares in a secondary tender.

---

## The Deal

The $300 million employee tender was co-led by Wellington and T. Rowe Price, two large institutional investors. Employees were allowed to cash out a portion of their vested equity at the new valuation, doubling from the $11 billion the company achieved earlier.

This is the second time in four years that ElevenLabs has authorized a secondary transaction for employees. The previous $100 million tender took place at a $6.6 billion valuation in September 2024.

---

## Retention Tool

The offering reflects a growing trend among fast-growing AI startups, using employee liquidity events as a retention tool. Rather than forcing staff to wait for an IPO that may be years away, companies like ElevenLabs let them sell shares to investors and stay onboard.

Founded in 2022, ElevenLabs has positioned itself as a leader in voice AI, generating synthetic speech that is difficult to distinguish from human voices. With the new valuation, it joins Europe's most valuable AI startups.

---

## Market Context

The voice AI space has heated up considerably. OpenAI's Advanced Voice Mode, Meta's audio research, and startups like Hume AI have all pushed the category forward. ElevenLabs has differentiated through multilingual support and low-latency synthesis.

The tender offer signals confidence from institutional investors that ElevenLabs has a sustainable business model, not just impressive demos.
