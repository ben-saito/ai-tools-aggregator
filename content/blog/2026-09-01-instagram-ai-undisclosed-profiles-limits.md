# Instagram Puts New Limits on Undisclosed AI Profiles

As frustration over AI influencers has been growing, Instagram is limiting the reach of undisclosed AI profiles. The platform is renaming its existing "AI creator" label to "AI-generated profile," which Instagram says should make the disclosure easier to understand. The new label is meant to tell users that the person featured in a profile is entirely AI-generated.

---

## The New Policy

Under the new policy, creators who do not properly label an AI-generated profile could see their reach reduced. Creators who use the new label, on the other hand, will not be penalized simply for having an AI-generated persona. The label also is not meant for every use of AI — Instagram clarifies that people who use AI to edit photos, polish captions, create graphics, or make other creative tweaks do not need to use the AI-generated profile label.

---

## Background: The Problem of Fake Human Profiles

Instagram says the change comes partly in response to users who have stumbled across profiles that looked like they belonged to real people, only to find out later that the person was completely AI-generated. As generative AI becomes a bigger part of how people create, the platform recognized that transparency about AI-generated identity is becoming a core trust issue.

---

## Developer Perspective

From a developer standpoint, this policy highlights the challenge of building AI-generated persona platforms that comply with emerging disclosure requirements. The EU AI Act and other regulations are increasingly requiring clear labeling of AI-generated content. Instagram's approach — a reach penalty for non-disclosure combined with a clean label that protects creators who comply — offers a practical model for other platforms building AI influencer products.

The distinction between AI-assisted editing (no label required) and AI-generated personas (label required) also reflects a growing industry consensus: synthetic media where an AI system generates the core identity of a character or person requires explicit disclosure, while AI tools used to enhance or refine human-created content do not.
