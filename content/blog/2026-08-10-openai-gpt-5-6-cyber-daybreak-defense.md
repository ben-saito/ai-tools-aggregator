# OpenAI Launches GPT-5.6-Cyber and Expands Daybreak Defense Platform

OpenAI announced on August 10, 2026 the expansion of Daybreak, its cybersecurity defense service, including the launch of a new specialized cyber model called GPT-5.6-Cyber, TechCrunch reported.

---

## Daybreak Expansion: Blue and Red Tiers

Daybreak now offers two tiers: Blue and Red. Blue provides standard cyber services including incident response, malware analysis, and patch validation. Red offers advanced security testing and vulnerability research capabilities with purpose-trained cybersecurity models.

The Red tier grants access to GPT-5.6-Cyber, a model built on GPT-5.6 Sol and optimized for cybersecurity tasks. Initial trusted partners include Accenture, IBM, CrowdStrike, and Cloudflare.

---

## GPT-5.6-Cyber Capabilities

GPT-5.6-Cyber is designed for defensive cybersecurity operations, offering enhanced capabilities for threat analysis, vulnerability research, and security automation. The model represents OpenAI's response to the increasing sophistication of AI-enabled cyber threats.

---

## Market Context

OpenAI launched Daybreak earlier in 2026 following Anthropic's release of its Mythos cyber-focused model. The expansion reflects intensifying competition among AI labs to provide both offensive and defensive AI security tools to enterprise customers.
