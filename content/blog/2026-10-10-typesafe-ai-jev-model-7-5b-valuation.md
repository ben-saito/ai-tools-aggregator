# TypeSafe AI's Jev Model Hits $7.5B Valuation Weeks After Launch

---

TypeSafe AI, the developer of Jev — a non-text AI model that went viral shortly after its September 15 release — has closed a $870 million funding round at a $7.5 billion valuation, just weeks after launching. The round was led by Andreessen Horowitz, with participation from Sequoia and existing investor DCVC.

Jev is based on a transformer architecture but differs fundamentally from large language models: it outputs probabilities and "calibrated decisions" rather than text. TypeSafe claims Jev works significantly faster and uses far fewer tokens than LLMs, positioning it uniquely for task automation rather than text or code generation.

The startup says a third of Fortune 500 companies are already using the model — an unusually rapid enterprise adoption curve. Co-founder Diogo Almeida, previously a researcher at OpenAI, explained the company's approach: "We have been super good at human language for four years, but it's not useful for automation because computers speak a different language." TypeSafe was co-founded in 2024 by former Meta research engineer Sasha Sheng and Erik Gafni.

---
The rapid valuation jump from launch to $7.5B in weeks reflects intense investor appetite for AI infrastructure plays that move beyond generative text. Jev's approach — calibrated probability outputs optimized for machine-to-machine workflows — suggests the next wave of AI value may accrue not to language models but to models purpose-built for automation pipelines.
