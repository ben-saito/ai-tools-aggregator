# Iran Strikes on Amazon Data Centers Caused Permanent Customer Data Loss

Iranian strikes targeting Amazon Web Services data centers resulted in war damage that exceeded the tolerance levels AWS services were designed to withstand, leading to permanent loss of customer data, according to Ars Technica reporting.

---

## Infrastructure Vulnerability in Conflict Zones

The incident highlights the physical vulnerability of cloud infrastructure in regions experiencing armed conflict. While cloud providers design their systems with redundancy and durability in mind, the report indicates that the specific damage from the Iranian strikes exceeded those engineering tolerances.

AWS maintains data centers globally and provides various levels of data durability guarantees through its service level agreements. However, the extraordinary circumstances of war-related damage appear to have surpassed the assumptions underlying those durability guarantees.

---

## Growing Concerns About Cloud Infrastructure Security

The incident adds to ongoing discussions about the physical security of cloud infrastructure and the need for organizations to consider geographic risk factors when planning data residency and disaster recovery strategies. As more critical infrastructure and data migrate to cloud services, the impact of physical attacks on data centers becomes increasingly significant.
