# Perplexity India Revenue Rises 60% After Airtel Bundle Ends, Retaining Millions of Users

Perplexity on Tuesday shared results from its year-long experiment bundling its premium AI search service with Airtel, India's second-largest telecom operator, showing that 60% revenue growth continued even after the free subscriptions began expiring.

In July 2025, Perplexity partnered with Airtel to offer free 12-month Perplexity Pro subscriptions worth approximately $200 to Airtel's 360 million customers. New redemptions ended January 16, but because subscribers kept access for a year from activation, the earliest users began hitting the paywall in July.

Despite downloads declining, Perplexity's India revenue rose about 60% as most free users converted to paid plans rather than abandoning the service entirely.

---

## Testing the Bundling Model

The experiment tested whether bundling paid AI services with telecom carriers could create lasting users and revenue after giveaways end, in a market where traditional subscription pricing is challenging due to lower average incomes.

India's 1.4 billion population and high smartphone penetration make it a potentially massive market for AI services, though pricing remains a significant barrier.

Perplexity's CEO called the results proof that bundling strategies can deliver sustainable growth models distinct from advertising or free tiers, and said the company is exploring similar programs in other emerging markets.
