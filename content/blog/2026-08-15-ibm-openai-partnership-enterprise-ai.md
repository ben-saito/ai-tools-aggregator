# IBM Partners with OpenAI to Strengthen Enterprise AI Business

---

## Overview

IBM announced a partnership with OpenAI on August 13, 2026. IBM will establish a dedicated OpenAI practice within IBM Consulting and train and certify tens of thousands of consultants on OpenAI technologies. The partnership includes integrating GPT-5.6, Codex, and ChatGPT Work into IBM Consulting Advantage, and jointly developing industry-specific solutions for financial services, government, telecommunications, and retail.

---

## Partnership Details

Under the agreement, IBM will establish a dedicated "OpenAI Practice" within IBM Consulting. According to Mike Healy, Managing Partner at IBM Consulting, the training will primarily retrain existing employees, with tens of thousands of consultants certified on OpenAI technologies over the next several months.

Training will focus on OpenAI's Codex, API, cybersecurity, and consultative solution credentials. IBM will also create a group of specialized "Forward Deployed Experts" trained through OpenAI's Partner Network to build specialized expertise in deploying OpenAI models for enterprise clients.

IBM will integrate OpenAI's latest models including GPT-5.6, Codex, and ChatGPT Work into IBM Consulting Advantage, its AI platform for consultants, to help clients deploy AI across core business operations.

---

## Intensifying Enterprise AI Market Competition

This partnership represents OpenAI's latest move to expand its enterprise business through consulting firms and technology partners, as competition among AI model developers increasingly shifts from building more capable models to winning corporate customers and large-scale deployments.

OpenAI has previously announced partnerships with IT services firms including Infosys and Tata Consultancy Services, underscoring a strategy of working with large global systems integrators to bring its AI products to enterprise customers.

For IBM, the OpenAI agreement expands its range of frontier AI partnerships, pursuing a model-agnostic strategy that combines its own Granite family of AI models with offerings from third parties. This is less than a year after IBM announced a similar alliance with Anthropic in late 2025.

---

## Business Context

The partnership comes as IBM looks to accelerate growth in its AI business after lowering its 2026 revenue forecast last month following weaker-than-expected quarterly results.

With intensifying competition among AI model developers and enterprises racing to deploy AI at scale, the combination of IBM's global consulting infrastructure and OpenAI's frontier models may serve as a counter to the Microsoft-Anthropic alliance.
