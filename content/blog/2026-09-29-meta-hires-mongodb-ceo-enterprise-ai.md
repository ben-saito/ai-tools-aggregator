# Meta Hires Former MongoDB CEO to Lead New Enterprise AI Platform

Meta has hired Devrie Cohen, the former CEO of MongoDB, to lead a new enterprise AI initiative as the company expands its AI technology stack beyond consumer products into business and developer markets.

---

## From Social Media to Enterprise AI

Meta announced the new enterprise AI platform at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, with Cohen as the head of the newly formed division. The company says it will focus on bringing its full technology stack to businesses and developers, including the Muse family of AI products, Meta Business Agent, the Muse API, and Muse Code.

The hire marks a notable shift for Meta, which has historically competed with OpenAI and Anthropic for consumer AI dominance. By bringing in Cohen, who scaled MongoDB from a startup to a public cloud database company, Meta is signaling a serious push into the enterprise market where OpenAI and Anthropic have already established significant traction.

---

## The Enterprise AI Race Heats Up

Meta is not alone in targeting enterprise customers with AI tools. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google all have active enterprise divisions, and competition for large contracts has intensified throughout 2026. The differentiation for Meta lies in its ability to integrate AI capabilities across its existing ecosystem of products used by millions of businesses globally.

Cohen's mandate reportedly includes building out API infrastructure and partnership channels that make Meta's AI tools accessible to enterprise developers, a strategy that mirrors MongoDB's approach to winning developer preference before expanding into enterprise sales.
