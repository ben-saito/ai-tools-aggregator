# MetaのAIエージェント「Muse」がApp Store第2位 -- 8.3万ダウンロードを突破

Meta's new AI app Muse has climbed to the number 2 position on the US App Store Top Charts, with over 83,000 iOS downloads in the United States since its launch on September 9 (US time).

## Strong Debut for Meta AI Agent

Sensor Tower data shows Muse's early performance -- while dwarfed by Meta's other app launches -- signals solid consumer interest in Meta's take on an AI agent. Threads achieved over 4.3 million US downloads on its launch day alone, and the Meta AI app saw over 1 million downloads in its first days. Muse's 83,000 downloads represents a more modest start, but the app is currently US-only with broader international rollout planned.

## Muse Represents Meta's Agentic AI Push

Muse is a standalone AI agent app that lets users generate and edit images using Meta's image generation technology. The app integrates with users' Instagram accounts, pulling from public profiles to enable AI-powered image creation. Unlike Meta AI (which is built into Instagram, Facebook, and Messenger), Muse operates as a separate app focused specifically on AI-powered image generation and editing.

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg described the app as part of Meta's broader shift toward agentic AI -- AI systems that can take actions on behalf of users rather than just responding to queries. The Muse launch follows Meta's release of its multimodal AI model family and represents the company's first major consumer-facing agentic AI product.

## App Store Ranking Reflects Early Momentum

Reaching number 2 on the App Store Top Charts -- even in a limited launch window -- gives Muse visibility that could drive further downloads. Meta will be watching whether Muse can build on this momentum as it expands internationally and adds new features. The strong App Store ranking also signals that consumer demand for dedicated AI agent apps remains high, even as AI features become increasingly embedded in existing platforms.
