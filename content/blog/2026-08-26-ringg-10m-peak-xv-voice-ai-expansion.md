# Ringg Raises $10M from Peak XV to Expand Voice AI Beyond Phone Calls

Indian voice AI startup Ringg has secured $10 million in Series A extension funding from Peak XV, with plans to extend its technology beyond traditional phone call applications.

---

## Voice AI for Enterprise Communication

Ringg has developed voice AI technology focused on enterprise communication tasks, including customer service automation, sales call assistance, and internal business workflows. The company's platform handles voice interactions with context awareness that goes beyond simple voice-to-text transcription.

The funding will support Ringg's expansion into new communication channels and markets, moving beyond phone-based applications into video calls, meetings, and real-time communication platforms.

---

## Market Opportunity in Enterprise Voice

Voice AI for business communication represents a growing market as companies seek to automate routine calls while maintaining quality interactions. Ringg's approach targets the gap between simple IVR systems and full conversational AI.

The Indian market provides a strong testing ground for voice AI given the diversity of languages and accents, and the company has developed capabilities in code-switching and multilingual interactions.
