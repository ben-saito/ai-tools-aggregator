# Meta Developing Camera-Free Smart Glasses "Luna" After "Perv Glasses" Criticism

Meta's camera-equipped smart glasses have proven more successful than other entrants into the market, but they have also deeply disturbed certain consumers who see them as invasive emblems of a dystopian surveillance society. Now, as the company weathers complaints that it's selling "pervert glasses," it has reportedly decided to sell a pair that doesn't come with integrated cameras.

The Information reports that Meta is developing a new smart glasses model, dubbed Luna, that -- sans cameras -- comes equipped with a system to communicate with the company's AI chatbot and Muse, its AI consumer agent. The glasses include six built-in microphones so users can communicate with the chatbot, as well as a button on the side that activates the AI system when pressed.

Luna may be unveiled as soon as Meta Connect, Meta's annual hardware/developer event happening in Menlo Park next week.

The smart glasses industry has grown over the past few years, with Meta positioned near the front of the pack. However, concerns over usability, cost, and privacy have dogged the business, with many consumers still unsure of how, why, or if they should use the devices. Meta's Reality Labs, which develops its smart glasses line, is still losing a gargantuan amount of money, as its earnings report from April revealed.

## Meta's AI Strategy and Smart Glasses Future

Meta's smart glasses products occupy a different position from competitors in terms of AI feature integration. The camera-free Luna model aims to address privacy concerns while expanding AI assistant adoption.

At Meta Connect 2026, the company is expected to present its overall AI strategy, with attention focused on the latest developments in the smart glasses hardware division.
