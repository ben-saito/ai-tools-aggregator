# Cal AI Founder Zach Yadegari to Speak at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 on Viral Growth

Zach Yadegari, co-founder of Cal AI — the nutrition tracking app that reached 15 million downloads and $30 million in annual revenue before being acquired by MyFitnessPal — will join the Builders Stage at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026. Yadegari will share how Cal AI achieved explosive viral growth and how the company turned millions of users into durable business value.

---

## The Cal AI Story

Cal AI lets users photograph meals and uses image recognition combined with the phone's depth sensor to calculate calories, protein, carbs, and fat. The app's core proposition is simplicity: snap a photo, get a nutritional breakdown.

Yadegari co-founded Cal AI while still in high school — he was 17 when he started the company. But this was not his first experience building a large audience online. At age 16, Yadegari had already sold Totally Science, a gaming platform he founded.

The trajectory of Cal AI was unusually fast even by Silicon Valley standards. In less than two years from launch, the app had crossed 15 million downloads and was generating more than $30 million in annual recurring revenue. MyFitnessPal acquired the company in a deal that valued it at a significant premium to typical consumer app acquisitions at that stage.

---

## What Yadegari Will Discuss at Disrupt

At TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, Yadegari will focus on what he and his team learned about building and scaling in a distribution-driven market. The central question: once a product finds a massive audience through viral mechanics, what does it take to convert that attention into durable retention and revenue?

Topics will include:

- **Viral mechanics that actually work**: What drove Cal AI's initial 15 million downloads — and whether those mechanics were repeatable
- **Retention after the spike**: How Cal AI converted one-time downloaders into ongoing active users
- **Monetization in a crowded health app market**: Building $30M ARR from a freemium nutrition tracking product
- **Acquisition as an outcome**: What the MyFitnessPal deal revealed about how buyers in the health and fitness app space think about viral consumer products

Yadegari's story is particularly relevant for founders building consumer apps in the AI era, where new distribution channels (social sharing, agent-driven discovery, API integration) are creating opportunities for rapid growth that did not exist a decade ago.
