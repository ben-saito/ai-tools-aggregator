# OpenAI's Astra Model Solves Ten Open Problems in Mathematics

OpenAI has used an internal version of its Astra model to solve ten open problems in mathematics and computer science, according to Import AI's newsletter #467. The problems span high-dimensional geometry, coding theory, arithmetic circuit complexity, group theory, operator algebras, quantum complexity, lattice cryptography, and extremal combinatorics.

---

## What Astra Solved

The ten problems were drawn from areas where solutions can be rigorously verified, a key constraint for automated theorem proving. OpenAI states all ten problems are of substantial interest to their respective mathematical communities, and several have broad significance across mathematics.

Expert reactions have been notable. Henry Yuen, associate professor of computer science at Columbia University, commented on the variety of breakthroughs: "New circuit lower bounds? A simple, easy-to-describe non-sofic group? Hardness of approximation for CVP without needing a unique games-like conjecture? I didn't just hear about these problems from my friends or from seminars. I feel their importance in my bones."

---

## Engineering vs. Creativity

The result sits in an ambiguous zone between pure engineering and genuine creativity. The Import AI analysis notes that while AI systems appear to lack the intuitive, paradigm-shifting creativity needed to autonomously identify new research directions, they are now solving open problems in domains where the direction is human-defined but the execution requires deep technical reasoning.

This follows a separate study from Princeton and collaborators (also covered in Import AI 467) showing that current AI agents can function as skilled engineers but fail to produce original research at the caliber of top ML conferences — scoring 1-2 out of 10 on peer review scales for novel contributions.

---

## Scientific Tools Within Their Own Right

Whether interpreted as evidence of emerging creative intuition or as sophisticated engineering within well-defined problem spaces, the trajectory is clear: AI systems are becoming powerful scientific instruments. OpenAI has published a technical blog post detailing the ten advances.
