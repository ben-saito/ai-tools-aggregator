# Ars Technica: "Software Is Over" — AI Developer Takes Aim at Adobe With Open Source Clones

---

A bold AI developer has released open source clones of Adobe's flagship creative tools, declaring 'software is over' in a provocatively named project. The clones leverage generative AI to replicate Photoshop and Illustrator workflows entirely in the browser, bypassing desktop software entirely. The project has already attracted thousands of users and sparked debate about the future of creative software business models.
