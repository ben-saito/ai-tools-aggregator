# StrictlyVC Returns to TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 to Examine the Changing Rules of Venture Capital

StrictlyVC, the well-known forum for institutional investors and operators, will hold a dedicated session at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 on October 14 in San Francisco. The program brings together family office investors, limited partners, and venture fund managers to discuss how AI is reshaping the competitive dynamics of startup investing.

---

## The IPO landscape: reopening but with a new playbook

One session examines what has changed in the path to public markets. The IPO window is reopening after a prolonged drought, but the expectations around growth, governance, and credibility have shifted. A conversation with ICR's Ryan Flanagan will cover what makes a company IPO-ready today, the decisions that matter years before a listing, and how to position a business for a successful exit in a more disciplined market.

The session reflects a broader recognition that the 2021-era playbook — growth at any cost with the assumption that public markets would be accommodating — no longer applies. Companies seeking to go public in the current environment face more rigorous scrutiny on unit economics, path to profitability, and the credibility of their AI-driven business models.

---

## Family offices as a growing capital source

Family offices have become one of the fastest-growing sources of startup capital, often moving faster and investing with greater flexibility than traditional institutional investors. A panel will examine how family offices are approaching venture today, where they are placing long-term bets, and why founders increasingly see them as strategic partners rather than purely financial investors.

The discussion recognizes that in an environment where traditional venture capital has become more cautious, family offices with longer time horizons and greater flexibility on structure and terms have become attractive financing partners for Series A and Series B companies.

---

## LP perspectives on AI exposure and manager selection

Venture firms are competing harder than ever for institutional capital, while limited partners are rethinking everything from manager selection to concentrated AI exposure and liquidity expectations. A panel of leading LPs will discuss what they are looking for in today's market, how they are evaluating emerging managers versus established firms, and where they believe the next generation of venture returns will come from.

The conversation is particularly timely given the concentration of capital in AI-focused startups and the questions being asked about whether the current valuation levels in AI are sustainable.
