# Lovable Hits $600M Annualized Revenue as Vibe Coding Trend Accelerates

Lovable, the AI-powered app development platform, has crossed $600 million in annualized revenue, according to figures shared by co-founder Fabian Hedin and reported by TechCrunch on September 24, 2026.

---

## Vibe Coding Goes Mainstream

Lovable's growth is being driven by the "vibe coding" trend — a term describing non-technical users building software using natural language AI tools. Unlike traditional code generation tools that output code snippets, Lovable's platform outputs a complete, deployable product.

"You can use these tools like Codex or Claude code to output code," Hedin said. "The difference is that Lovable does not output code. The output is a product, and that product is immediately usable."

---

## Enterprise Adoption and Traffic Metrics

Hedin said the startup has focused on growing its enterprise business, claiming that two-thirds of Fortune 500 companies are now using its product. Apps created by users on the platform are together attracting nearly a billion views per month — a metric that demonstrates the reach of AI-generated applications.

---

## Rapid Funding Trajectory

The company has raised over $700 million in two rounds just eight months apart. Last December, it raised $300 million from Menlo Ventures and CapitalG at a $1.3 billion valuation. The rapid escalation to $600 million in annualized revenue has likely pushed the valuation significantly higher.
