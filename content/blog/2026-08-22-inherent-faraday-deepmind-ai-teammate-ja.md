# Inherent, Founded by DeepMind Alumni, Claims AI Teammate Faraday Outperforms Anthropic and OpenAI

A British AI lab founded by former DeepMind researchers claims its AI agent Faraday can replicate scientific papers better than Anthropic and OpenAI.

---

## Faraday: An AI Teammate for Scientific Research

Inherent developed Faraday, an AI agent capable of rapidly replicating scientific papers. It is designed as a tool that allows researchers to efficiently verify the results of existing papers when testing new hypotheses.

Built by a team of DeepMind alumni, Faraday aims to be a "stepping stone" for accelerating scientific progress. For researchers, replicating paper results is important, but traditional methods require enormous time and effort.

---

## Accelerating Scientific Research with AI

The reproducibility crisis in scientific research has been a long-standing challenge. Many papers cannot be replicated by other researchers, undermining the reliability of scientific knowledge.

Faraday is attracting attention as a promising solution to this challenge. By leveraging AI, researchers can verify paper results more quickly and systematically, potentially accelerating scientific progress.

---

## Industry Expectations

Inherent's results show AI can complement and accelerate scientific research. The development of AI that serves as a research "teammate" rather than a mere automation tool represents a new direction in the integration of science and AI.
