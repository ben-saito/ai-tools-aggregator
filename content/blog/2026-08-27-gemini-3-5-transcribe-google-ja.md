# Google Announces Gemini 3.5 Transcribe for AI-Powered Speech-to-Text

Google announced Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, a new AI-powered speech-to-text capability integrated into its Gemini platform. The feature represents Google's continued expansion of its AI product suite and builds on the Gemini branding introduced to consolidate its various AI offerings.

The announcement highlights Google's strategy of embedding advanced AI capabilities directly into its existing product ecosystem. Gemini 3.5 Transcribe is designed to provide high-accuracy transcription services for enterprise and consumer use cases.

---

## Technical Approach

Gemini 3.5 Transcribe leverages Google's latest speech recognition models, trained on diverse audio datasets to handle various accents, dialects, and acoustic environments. The integration with the broader Gemini platform allows for seamless combination of speech-to-text with other AI capabilities like summarization and entity extraction.

Enterprise customers can expect API access for integration into existing workflows, while consumer availability through the Gemini app provides accessible transcription for everyday use cases.

---

## Competitive Landscape

The speech-to-text market has seen significant AI-driven improvements, with competing offerings from OpenAI (Whisper), Anthropic, and specialized providers like AssemblyAI. Google's entry with Gemini 3.5 Transcribe brings additional competition to this space, particularly for users already invested in the Google Cloud and Gemini ecosystem.
