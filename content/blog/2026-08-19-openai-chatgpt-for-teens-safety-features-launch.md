# OpenAI Launches ChatGPT for Teens with Safety Measures and Study Mode

OpenAI on Monday announced the launch of ChatGPT for Teens, a version of its chatbot designed with age-appropriate safety measures, parental controls, and learning tools for users under 18.

The new product arrives as OpenAI faces multiple lawsuits alleging AI chatbots lack adequate safety measures, contributing to teen suicides and mental health crises. Schools are also struggling with ChatGPT-assisted cheating.

ChatGPT for Teens introduces a new Study Mode feature designed to encourage curiosity and deeper thinking rather than quick answers. The safety measures include content filtering and guardrails specific to younger users.

OpenAI says the product aims to provide a safer environment for teens while promoting responsible AI use in education. Study Mode guides users through problems step-by-step rather than simply providing answers.

---

## Educational AI and Safety

The launch reflects broader industry efforts to address concerns about AI's impact on young users, from mental health to academic integrity.

OpenAI's move follows regulatory pressure and litigation over chatbot safety for minors, setting a precedent for how AI companies approach younger demographics.
