# Okta Acquires AI Security Startup Permiso for $200M

Okta has acquired Permiso, an AI-powered identity threat detection startup, for approximately $200 million, TechCrunch reported on July 30. The acquisition gives Okta capabilities to detect and respond to threats involving AI agents and other non-human identities operating across cloud environments.

## Why It Matters for AI Developers

As enterprises deploy AI agents at scale, the attack surface expands significantly. AI agents often operate with elevated privileges across multiple systems and cloud environments, creating new identity management challenges that traditional security tools were not designed to handle. Permiso's technology focuses specifically on detecting anomalous behavior from non-human identities — the category that AI agents fall into.

The deal reflects a broader trend in the AI infrastructure market: established enterprise software vendors acquiring specialized AI security startups to fill gaps in their product portfolios. Okta, historically focused on human identity management (single sign-on, multi-factor authentication), is now positioning itself to handle the machine-to-machine and agent-to-agent identity challenges that emerge as AI systems take on more operational roles.

## The Non-Human Identity Problem

AI agents interact with cloud resources, APIs, databases, and other services using credentials and API keys rather than human usernames and passwords. Traditional identity and access management (IAM) tools were built around human users with well-defined access patterns and session lifetimes. AI agents, by contrast, may make thousands of API calls per minute across dozens of services, with patterns that look fundamentally different from human behavior.

Permiso's detection approach uses behavioral analysis to identify when AI agent credentials are being used in ways that deviate from expected patterns — a capability that becomes increasingly important as more enterprises deploy autonomous AI systems in production environments.
