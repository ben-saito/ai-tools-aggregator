# Zuckerberg Details Meta's Personal AI Superintelligence Strategy

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg has published a Wall Street Journal op-ed outlining his vision for bringing AI superintelligence to individuals rather than concentrating it among a few institutions, framing personal AI as the next major computing platform shift.

---

## The Vision

Zuckerberg argues that superintelligent AI must be democratized to reach individual users, not hoarded by a handful of tech giants or governments. His op-ed positions Meta's AI strategy as fundamentally different from competitors who focus on enterprise or cloud-based AI services.

---

## Consumer AI as Platform

Meta has been building AI capabilities into its family of apps including WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook. The company believes AI agents will become the next primary interface for consumer computing, potentially replacing app-based interaction with conversational AI assistants that span social, work, and personal domains.

---

## Technical Approach

Meta has released multiple open-source AI models through its Llama series, and continues to invest in fundamental AI research. The strategy combines open model releases with proprietary integration into Meta's consumer platforms, creating a hybrid approach that aims for broad deployment scale.

---

## Competitive Landscape

The op-ed can be read as a response to OpenAI and Anthropic's focus on enterprise and safety-focused AI development. Zuckerberg positions personal AI as the next battleground where AI companies will compete for user adoption and ecosystem dominance.
