# Mistral Large 4: Europe's Leading AI Developer Releases Its Most Capable Model Yet

---

Mistral has announced Mistral Large 4, its most capable model to date, with a particular focus on cyber and security applications. Cyber refusals become a selling point — the model is explicitly trained to refuse requests that could be weaponized, while maintaining performance on legitimate security research tasks. Europe's leading AI developer is positioning itself as a credible alternative to US-based AI labs for customers with data sovereignty requirements.

The release continues Mistral's pattern of rapid iteration, with each successive model showing meaningful improvements on benchmark evaluations. Large 4 includes extended context support, enabling the model to process and reason over documents of substantial length — a capability that is particularly relevant for enterprise customers processing legal contracts, technical documentation, or research archives.

The open weight release plan for a future version of the model signals Mistral's commitment to the open-source ecosystem, though the full weights of Large 4 are not being released at launch. The company says the open weight version will come with appropriate safety guardrails in place.
