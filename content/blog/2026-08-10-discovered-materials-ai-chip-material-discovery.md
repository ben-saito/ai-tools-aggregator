# Discovered Materials Raises $9M to Find Better Chip Materials with AI

Discovered Materials has raised $9 million to use AI for discovering novel materials that could lead to more efficient and better-performing chips. The startup applies AI-driven materials screening to accelerate the traditionally slow process of semiconductor development.

---

## Why Materials Discovery Matters for AI

The AI industry faces a fundamental challenge: chip performance improvements are critical for training and running larger models. Traditional materials discovery relies on trial-and-error in laboratories, taking years and hundreds of millions of dollars per new material.

Discovered Materials applies machine learning to analyze existing materials databases and predict candidates with desired properties like thermal conductivity and electron mobility. This approach could significantly shorten the path from material discovery to chip deployment.

---

## AI for Science in Practice

The company represents a growing trend of applying AI to industrial challenges. Rather than training on open-ended datasets, their system targets specific performance characteristics needed for next-generation semiconductors.

The $9M raised will be used for experimental validation of AI-predicted candidates and further refinement of the machine learning models.
