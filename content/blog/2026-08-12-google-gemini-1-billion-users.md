# Google Gemini App Surpasses 1 Billion Monthly Active Users

Google's Gemini app has reached 1 billion monthly active users, making it one of the company's fastest-growing products and closing the gap with OpenAI's ChatGPT, which hit the same milestone in June 2026.

---

## Gemini's Rapid Growth Trajectory

CEO Sundar Pichai announced the milestone via X, noting it was the 14th Google product to reach the 1 billion user mark. The growth is particularly notable because Gemini launched well after ChatGPT had already established market dominance. The app's integration across Google's ecosystem — Search, Workspace, Android, and the standalone app — has driven sustained adoption.

Google's AI Mode in Search has separately surpassed 1 billion monthly active users globally, but the Gemini figure refers specifically to the standalone app and excludes users reached through other Google channels.

---

## Voice-First Usage and Image Generation

Google shared detailed usage data revealing how users engage with Gemini. Approximately 63% of Gemini users interact with the assistant using voice features, reflecting a shift toward voice-first AI interactions. Additionally, Gemini now generates more than 150 million images per day, demonstrating strong uptake of the platform's multimedia capabilities.

The company also noted that Gemini has more than 100 million active users on iOS, indicating that growth extends beyond Google's own hardware ecosystem.

---

## New Model Releases

Google continues to iterate on its model lineup. Gemini 3.5 Flash, released recently, is designed for improved coding tasks and autonomous AI-agent operations. The company is expected to announce additional Gemini-powered features at its upcoming Made by Google event, particularly for Pixel devices.

The Q2 2026 earnings call preceded the announcement, with Google reporting over 950 million monthly users at that time and daily active users tripling year-over-year.

---

## Competitive Landscape

Gemini's 1 billion user milestone positions Google alongside OpenAI in the elite tier of AI assistant platforms. ChatGPT reached this threshold in June 2026, and Gemini's rapid catch-up reflects the competitive dynamics in the consumer AI assistant market, where both companies are aggressively expanding functionality to retain and grow their user bases.
