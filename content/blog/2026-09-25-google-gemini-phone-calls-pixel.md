# Google Tests Gemini Phone Call Feature for Pixel Users

Google is piloting a feature that lets Gemini make phone calls on behalf of users, initially targeting Pixel 11 owners in the United States who subscribe to Gemini Advanced. The feature represents an expansion of AI agent capabilities from text and visual understanding into real-time voice communication.

---

## AI Agents Enter the Phone Line

The new capability allows Gemini to call businesses directly, handle reservations, handle customer service inquiries, and complete other phone-based tasks autonomously. This positions Google to compete with Apple's AI initiatives and Microsoft's Copilot integration across platforms.

Early access is limited to Pixel 11 devices, suggesting Google is using its hardware ecosystem as a testing ground before broader rollout. The feature requires an active Gemini subscription, signaling Google's strategy of tying advanced AI features to premium services.

---

## Industry Implications

Phone call automation has been a goal for AI assistants since the early days of Siri and Google Assistant. The challenge has always been maintaining natural conversation flow, handling unexpected responses, and operating reliably in real-world conditions. Gemini's multimodal capabilities give it context awareness that earlier voice assistants lacked.

If successful, the feature could fundamentally change how users interact with businesses, reducing friction in service industries and potentially disrupting call center economics.

---

## Technical Considerations

Gemini's phone call feature leverages speech-to-text, reasoning, and text-to-speech capabilities in a continuous pipeline. The system needs to handle background noise, interruptions, and conversational repair — challenges that have slowed previous attempts at fully autonomous phone agents.
