# Calendly Enters AI Meeting Notes Market with Callie Assistant

Calendly, known for its scheduling and meeting booking platform, is launching an AI-powered note-taking product that joins meetings, records audio/video, transcribes, and generates summaries with action items.

---

## The Competitive Meeting Notes Space

Calendly's new note-taker follows the same pattern as competitors: it joins meetings, records audio and video, transcribes everything, then generates summaries and action items. CEO Tope Awotona emphasizes post-meeting task automation as the key differentiator.

"There's a litany of note-takers, and all of them do a great job of recording and transcribing notes. But a lot of work happens after the meetings. And we think that's really where the opportunity for our note-takers to be efficient," he said.

---

## Callie: AI Assistant Tied to Scheduling Stack

Beyond note-taking, Calendly is launching an AI assistant called "Callie" that leverages the company's existing scheduling infrastructure. Callie checks participants' availability, schedules meetings, and surfaces context from prior meetings - similar to email-based assistants from companies like Read AI.

---

## Privacy-First Approach

Unlike competitors facing privacy allegations (Otter, Granola), Calendly will notify all participants before recording begins via chat. The integration with Calendly's scheduling system allows pre-meeting disclosure that calls will be recorded.

---

## Market Opportunity

The market includes Granola, Fireflies, Read AI, Otter, and Fathom for meeting notes, while Notion, ClickUp, and Wispr have added similar features to their productivity suites. Despite intense competition, Awotona sees opportunity in automating post-meeting workflows.
