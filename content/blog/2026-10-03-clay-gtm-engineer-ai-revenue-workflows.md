# Clay's Kareem Amin on the Rise of the GTM Engineer: AI-Powered Revenue Workflows at Disrupt 2026

Kareem Amin, co-founder and CEO of Clay, will join the AI Stage at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 to discuss the rise of the go-to-market engineer -- a new kind of revenue professional that combines data engineering, AI automation, and traditional sales and marketing execution into a single discipline.

---

## What Is a GTM Engineer?

Clay coined the term "GTM engineer" in 2023 and has published a guide describing the role as practitioners who build automated revenue workflows rather than executing individual GTM tasks by hand. The role sits at the intersection of data engineering, sales operations, and AI agent orchestration.

Traditional go-to-market work involves hours of researching accounts, cleaning data, qualifying leads, preparing outreach, and moving information between separate systems. GTM engineering uses AI, data infrastructure, and workflow automation to compress those tasks into systematic pipelines that run with minimal manual intervention.

The role emerged as AI capabilities reached a point where they could handle the research and synthesis tasks that previously required human judgment about which prospects to target and how to personalize outreach at scale.

---

## Clay's Infrastructure for AI-Powered Revenue Teams

Today, Clay provides infrastructure that gives GTM teams access to data, the ability to run agentic workflows, and the tools to launch campaigns across multiple channels. Its AI agent, Claygent, automates research and outreach for sales and marketing teams, drawing on dozens of data sources to build prospect profiles and generate personalized outreach at scale.

Rather than having sales development representatives spend hours manually researching accounts, Claygent can pull public data about companies, their technology stack, recent funding rounds, leadership changes, and news coverage -- then synthesize that information into a personalized outreach message.

Amin co-founded Clay in 2017 with the founding idea of putting programming capabilities into more people's hands. AI gave that founding vision a new application: enabling GTM teams that previously could not code to build sophisticated automated workflows without writing software.

---

## The Economic Case for GTM Engineering

For founders and GTM leaders deciding where to allocate budget or whether to make their next hire, the GTM engineer model offers a different framework for thinking about growth. Rather than adding headcount to execute the same manual processes at larger volume, GTM engineers build systems that can scale without proportional hiring.

The Disrupt 2026 session will explore how the role has evolved since Clay coined it three years ago, what separates successful GTM engineering implementations from failed experiments, and how the rise of AI agents is changing what GTM engineering looks like in practice.
