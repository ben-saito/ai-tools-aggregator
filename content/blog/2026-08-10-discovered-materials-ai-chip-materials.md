# Discovered Materials Raises $9M to Hunt for Better Chip Materials Using AI

Discovered Materials, a startup using AI to discover novel materials for semiconductor manufacturing, has raised $9 million in seed funding. The company is applying machine learning to the challenge of finding materials that can enable more efficient and powerful chips.

---

## The Problem

Traditional chip materials discovery has relied heavily on experimental approaches, testing candidates one by one in a slow and expensive process. As chip scaling becomes increasingly difficult, the industry needs new materials that can enable performance improvements beyond classical silicon.

---

## The Approach

Discovered Materials uses AI models to predict material properties and identify promising candidates before they are synthesized in a lab. This approach can dramatically accelerate the discovery phase and reduce the cost of exploring the vast chemical space of possible semiconductor materials.

---

## Funding Usage

The $9 million seed round will be used to expand the company's AI models and scale its materials database. The company plans to build partnerships with major semiconductor manufacturers to validate its predictions and accelerate the path from discovery to production.

---

## Industry Interest

The semiconductor industry has shown growing interest in AI-driven materials discovery as physical scaling of silicon approaches its limits. Several major chipmakers have established internal programs, while startups like Discovered Materials offer specialized approaches that complement larger efforts.
