# Import AI 474: Robotics' LLM Moment, Space TPUs, and Zhipu's Infrastructure Automation

---

Import AI 474: Platonic mindspace; TPUs in space; Zhipu starts an outer RSI loop. This week's issue covers robotics preparing for an LLM-style inflection point, Google's plans to deploy TPUs in space, and how Chinese AI lab Zhipu is using GLM-5.3 to automate its own infrastructure.

---

## Robotics Is Preparing for an LLM-Moment

The robotics industry is searching for the equivalent of the large language model breakthrough that transformed AI in 2022-2023. While LLMs gave software AI flexible reasoning and generalization, robotics has yet to find its "universal algorithm" — a foundation model that enables robots to generalize across tasks the way GPT models generalized across language tasks.

Researchers and companies are exploring whether the same transformer architectures that powered language progress could be adapted for physical manipulation, though fundamental differences between discrete tokens and continuous motor control create distinct challenges.

---

## Google DeepMind Prepares to Put TPUs in Space

Google has signaled plans to deploy its custom TPU chips in space-based compute environments — a move that would extend its AI infrastructure beyond terrestrial data centers. The initiative reflects growing interest in edge and space-based computing for AI workloads that require low-latency global coverage.

---

## Zhipu Automates Infrastructure with GLM-5.3

Chinese AI lab Zhipu has begun using its GLM-5.3 model to automate parts of its own infrastructure management — a form of AI-driven DevOps that suggests frontier models may soon play a role in maintaining the systems that train and serve them.
