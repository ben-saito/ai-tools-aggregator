# Figma Config India: Specs Markdown Editor and Payload CMS Integration

---

Design platform Figma announced a new Markdown-based editor called **Figma Specs** at its first Config event in India, aiming to provide a definition that both human teams and AI agents can follow when working within a brand's design system. The feature lets users define design language in a simple text format, serving as a bridge between design intent and code implementation.

During the event, Figma also unveiled integrations with the Payload headless content management system, allowing teams to pull in content libraries directly into the design environment. The company says these features are part of a broader push to make Figma "agent-ready" — enabling AI systems to understand and act on design specifications without manual intervention.

---

## Figma Specs: A Shared Language for Humans and AI

The core innovation behind Figma Specs is the Markdown-based editor. Unlike traditional design specs that exist as static visual annotations, Figma Specs lets designers write out design language in plain text — color tokens, typography rules, spacing conventions — that both designers and AI agents can consume in the same format.

This addresses one of the key friction points in AI-assisted design workflows: the gap between what a designer intends and what an AI system can actually interpret. When AI agents encounter a Figma file, they traditionally have to infer design intent from visual layouts. Figma Specs makes that intent explicit and machine-readable.

---

## Payload CMS Integration

The partnership with Payload headless CMS brings another dimension to the announcement. Development teams using Payload can now connect their content repositories directly to Figma, automating the process of importing real data into design mockups.

This integration is particularly relevant for teams building design systems at scale, where the gap between design and development has historically required constant synchronization work. With Payload and Figma Specs working together, design tokens and content structures can be defined once and propagated across both environments.

---

## Agent-Ready Design Systems

Figma has been increasingly positioning itself as a platform that can serve both human designers and AI agents. The company's push toward "agent-ready" design systems reflects a broader industry trend of building infrastructure that can accommodate both human collaboration and automated execution.

The Config India event marked the company's first major product announcement in the region, signaling Figma's intent to capture more of the Asian market for design tools. The features announced position Figma as a platform that bridges the gap between creative design and AI-driven development workflows.

---

## Technical Details

Figma Specs is rolling out now for Figma organization and enterprise customers. The Payload integration is available immediately through Figma's plugin marketplace. The company has not disclosed pricing specifics for the new features beyond existing Figma subscription tiers.
