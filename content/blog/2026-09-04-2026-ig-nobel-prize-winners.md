# 2026 Ig Nobel Prize Winners Announced: From Kissing Definitions to Space Mining

The 2026 Ig Nobel Prize ceremony will be held in Zurich, Switzerland — marking the first time the awards have left Boston since their founding in 1991. The move follows safety concerns that made hosting the event in the United States impractical, according to Marc Abrahams, editor of Annals of Improbable Research and master of ceremonies.

---

## Why Zurich?

"During the past year, it has become unsafe for our guests to visit the country," Abrahams told The Associated Press. The ceremony will rotate through European cities, with Antwerp hosting in 2027 and Zurich returning in 2028 for even-numbered years.

The Ig Nobels, a parody of the Nobel Prizes, honor achievements that "first make people laugh and then make them think." Acceptance speeches are limited to 60 seconds. While the research may seem absurd on its surface, many recipients have produced genuinely influential scientific work.

---

## This Year's Winners

One of the most relatable winners went to **Matilda Brindle, Catherine Talbot, and Stuart West** for their scientific definition of kissing: "non-agonistic interactions involving directed, intraspecific, oral-oral contact with some mouth-to-mouth action."

The researchers argued that prior definitions — including one requiring "protruded lips and suction movement" — were insufficient. Their analysis found that most large apes engage in some form of kissing, though data on frequency remains limited.

Other winners span chemistry, physics, biology, medicine, peace, and transportation. The full list reflects the Ig Nobel tradition of celebrating research that illuminates everyday phenomena through unconventional methods.

---

## Space Mining Research

Among this year's honorees is research exploring the intersection of space resource extraction and terrestrial economic systems. As commercial space ventures increasingly target asteroid mining and lunar resource extraction, the Ig Nobel committee recognized work examining the legal and economic frameworks that would govern such activities.

This reflects a broader trend: serious academic attention to questions raised by emerging space commercialization, once the domain of pure science fiction.

---

## The Ceremony's Significance

The Ig Nobels have long served as a counterpoint to the gravitas of mainstream scientific awards. By celebrating research that addresses genuinely puzzling aspects of human behavior, biology, and physical phenomena through humor, the prizes underscore that scientific discovery often begins with seemingly ridiculous questions.

Next year's ceremony returns to the United States, pending resolution of current safety concerns.
