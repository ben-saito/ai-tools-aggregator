# Data Centers Become Killer App for New Power Transformer Tech

Data centers are emerging as the primary market for solid-state transformer technology, which could also benefit EV charging and eventually household power delivery.

The technology promises more efficient power conversion and could help address the massive electricity demands of modern AI data centers.
