# Meta AI Releases New Mac App for Business Owners

Meta released a new Mac application for Meta AI on August 20, 2026, featuring built-in system-wide dictation and screen context awareness powered by its Muse Spark model. The release is part of a broader Meta AI update targeting business owners.

---

## Key Features

The app enables voice-based interaction across all applications on the Mac, similar to tools like Wispr Flow, Superwhisper, and Monologue. Users can dictate text in any app without switching windows.

The app can also analyze the user's current screen and answer questions based on context using the Muse Spark model.

---

## Business Owner Integration

Merchants can connect Instagram and Facebook accounts, Meta ad campaigns, and Google Workspace (Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides) to Meta AI. Once connected, Meta AI can provide insights about campaign performance, audience engagement metrics, and competitor benchmarking.

---

## Competitive Context

Last month, Google updated its Gemini app for Mac with similar system-wide dictation capabilities. Both companies are competing to establish AI assistant presence on desktop operating systems.
