# Shopify Says AI Search Is Driving More Traffic, Not Cannibalizing It

Shopify reports that AI-driven search on its platform is generating more traffic and sales for merchants, contradicting fears that AI search would cannibalize organic traffic the way it has for publishers. In Q2, AI-driven traffic and orders to Shopify stores tripled year over year.

---

## The finding

Shopify's data shows that the introduction of AI search functionality has actually expanded overall traffic rather than replacing existing user traffic. Unlike news publishers whose content is used as source material for AI-generated answers, Shopify merchants benefit directly from AI search recommendations because consumers are typically at a later stage in the purchase funnel with clear buying intent.

---

## Implications for merchants

The finding suggests Shopify merchants should optimize for AI search visibility, similar to traditional SEO but with AI-specific considerations. Product data quality and structured information become even more critical when AI systems are selecting which products to surface.
