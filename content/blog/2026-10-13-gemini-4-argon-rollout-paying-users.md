# Gemini 4 Argon Begins Wide Rollout — But Only for Paying Users

---

Google has begun the wide rollout of Gemini 4 Argon, its latest flagship AI model, but the company is restricting access to paying customers only. The model is rolling out today in the Gemini app for users on AI Pro ($20/mo) and AI Ultra (starting at $99/mo) plans, with additional restrictions for coding-focused products.

## Model Capabilities

Nothing about Gemini 4 Argon's capabilities has changed since its initial announcement. Google still points to the benchmarks it published late last month as evidence of the model's advanced reasoning and agentic coding capabilities. The model is trained to refuse malicious instructions, and an automated misalignment monitor can stop processing if it detects an unintended workflow.

Google claims Argon needs those safety measures because it is so capable. The company has shared new examples of how Argon has helped inside Google, where it has been used by thousands of employees. Argon has reportedly been instrumental in making YouTube's comment system more efficient — identifying redundant emoji rendering that, when eliminated, dropped server load by 45 percent.

## Internal Applications

Engineers turned Gemini 4 loose on the proprietary drone physics simulator used in Project Wing, and the AI found critical bugs that had been in the codebase since 2018. Google also notes that Argon has advanced its robotics program, showing a measurable step increase in embodied reasoning — apparently better at solving spatial problems in 3D environments.

## Access Restrictions

For general AI queries, Gemini 4 Argon is available in the Gemini app for paying users. However, there are additional restrictions on Argon for coding-focused products. In Antigravity and AI Studio, the new model is only available for those on AI Ultra plans. Google says it is keeping Gemini 3.1 Pro available for those who need something more capable than Flash models.

For developers accessing Google's models via the paid API, pricing is temporarily set at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with a 95 percent discount for cached tokens. This promotional rate is only good through November 30, when the rate will jump to $4 per 1M input tokens and $20 per 1M output tokens.
