# Deezer Adds AI-Generated Music Filter as AI-Uploaded Tracks Surpass 50% of Daily Uploads

---

Streaming platform Deezer is rolling out a new feature that lets users filter out AI-generated music from the platform entirely. The company says the toggle, expected to be available before the end of 2026, will remove AI-generated tracks, albums, and related artists from search results. The announcement highlights the rapid growth of AI music on streaming platforms, with AI-generated content now representing the majority of daily uploads.

---

## The Scale of the AI Music Problem

Deezer first added AI-detection tooling in 2025 and has been tracking uploads ever since. At that time, AI-generated tracks accounted for approximately 10% of daily uploads. By July 2026, that figure had shifted dramatically — more than 50% of daily uploads to Deezer were AI-generated tracks, averaging around 90,000 tracks per day.

The company cited a survey from 2025 in which roughly 45% of Deezer customers indicated they wanted a way to filter out AI music from their feeds. CEO Alexis Lanternier said the explosion of AI music on the platform was a primary catalyst for developing the toggle-based feature.

---

## Platform Responses to AI Music

Deezer is not alone in grappling with AI-generated content. Other major streaming services have taken varying approaches:

- **Bandcamp** has completely banned AI-generated tracks from its platform
- **Tidal** cut off monetization for users uploading AI music
- **Spotify** has taken a lighter approach, only tagging AI tracks and artists more prominently
- **Apple Music** was reported by Variety in August 2026 to be exploring labeling AI tracks by the end of the year

Deezer's approach — giving users the choice to opt out entirely — represents a middle ground between outright bans and passive tagging.

---

## Technical Detection and User Control

The new filter toggle will allow users to remove AI-generated content completely from their streaming experience, focusing only on human creators and creations. Deezer has not disclosed the specific details of its AI-detection technology, but the company says the detection system has been refined over the past year to handle the volume of uploads.

Beyond platform-level solutions, some users are also turning to independent tracking systems to identify AI-generated music, suggesting that third-party tools may become a growing part of the ecosystem.

---

## Industry Implications

The rapid rise of AI-generated content on music platforms raises questions about content discovery, artist compensation, and platform governance. With AI tools becoming increasingly accessible, the volume of AI-generated music is likely to continue growing, putting pressure on platforms to develop sustainable policies that balance user preferences with the realities of generative content.

Deezer's filter feature, rolling out to all users before the end of 2026, will serve as a test case for whether user-control features can adequately address the challenge.
