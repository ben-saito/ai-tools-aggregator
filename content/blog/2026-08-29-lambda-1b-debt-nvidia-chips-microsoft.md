# Lambda Raises $1B in Private Debt to Buy Nvidia Chips for Microsoft Lease

Lambda, an AI cloud company that acquires computing chips and rents them to businesses, has raised $1 billion in private short-dated debt to purchase Nvidia AI chips that will be leased to Microsoft, Bloomberg reports. The deal signals Lambda is betting it can quickly deploy the chips and start generating revenue to repay the debt.

## Funding Details

The debt facility, arranged by JP Morgan Chase, is Lambda's latest in a series of loans funding GPU infrastructure for specific customers. In May 2026, Lambda closed a $1 billion secured credit facility, and this week announced a similar facility for the new Microsoft deal. The company is reportedly in talks for a $3 billion pre-IPO funding round. Lambda last November raised $1.5 billion in venture capital at a $5.43 billion post-money valuation.

## AI Infrastructure Debt Boom

Lambda is not alone in using debt to fund the AI boom. According to Bloomberg data, banks and tech companies have raised over $400 billion in AI-related debt globally in 2026. This reflects the massive capital requirements of AI data center construction, where GPU procurement is a primary expense.

The company's business model centers on purchasing Nvidia chips and leasing compute capacity to enterprise customers who want AI capabilities without building their own infrastructure. This neocloud approach has attracted significant investor interest as demand for AI training and inference compute continues to outpace supply.
