# OpenAI Stuck Fighting Musk Antitrust Suit After Apple Finds a Way Out

Elon Musk has stopped pursuing his antitrust lawsuit against Apple over ChatGPT integration, but his legal battle with OpenAI continues unabated. The development marks a significant shift in the tech industry's legal landscape as Musk escalates his campaign against OpenAI while dropping claims against the iPhone maker.

---

## Apple Exits the Suit, OpenAI Remains

Musk's legal team filed to dismiss Apple from the lawsuit, which alleged that Apple's integration of ChatGPT into its operating system constituted anticompetitive behavior. The dismissal came after Apple argued it had merely partnered with OpenAI to offer ChatGPT features and had not engaged in the alleged market manipulation Musk claimed.

OpenAI, however, remains the primary defendant in Musk's ongoing antitrust action. The Tesla and SpaceX CEO has alleged that OpenAI has abandoned its original nonprofit mission and operates as a de facto monopoly in the AI industry.

---

## The Broader Campaign Against OpenAI

This latest legal development is part of Musk's broader campaign against OpenAI. In recent weeks, AI industry leaders including Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and OpenAI CEO Sam Altman have publicly advocated for slowing the pace of frontier AI development, citing safety concerns and the potential for catastrophic risks from unaligned AI systems.

Musk himself endorsed Amodei's position, posting "Dario is right" on social media in response to Amodei's essay arguing that the industry must slow down to avoid dangerous outcomes.

---

## Implications for the AI Industry

The lawsuit highlights growing tensions in the AI industry as companies navigate questions of market dominance, safety, and regulatory oversight. OpenAI's position as the target of both antitrust claims and safety advocacy reflects the complex challenges facing frontier AI labs as they balance commercial growth with responsible development practices.
