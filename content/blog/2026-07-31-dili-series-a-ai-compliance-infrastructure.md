# Dili Raises $21.7M Series A to Bring AI Compliance to the Infrastructure Boom

---

## The Compliance Gap in AI-Powered Construction

As AI systems become integral to critical infrastructure — power grids, water treatment, transportation networks — the compliance landscape has not kept pace. Dili, a startup applying AI to infrastructure compliance, raised a $21.7M Series A to close this gap.

The round was led by Khosla Ventures, with participation from Allianz, Rebel Fund, Brick and Mortar Ventures' Darren Bechtel, and Y Combinator's Garry Tan.

---

## What Dili Does

Dili's platform uses AI to automatically check infrastructure projects against regulatory requirements. Construction and engineering teams submit project documentation; Dili's models identify compliance gaps, missing permits, and code violations before they become costly problems.

The target market is large: infrastructure spending globally exceeds trillions of dollars annually, and regulatory complexity is growing as AI systems introduce new safety and audit requirements that existing frameworks do not fully address.

---

## Investor Logic

Khosla Ventures cited Dili's position at the intersection of two fast-growing markets: AI-powered software and infrastructure modernization. With governments worldwide investing heavily in infrastructure upgrades, the demand for automated compliance tools is likely to grow.
