# Google Adds AI Study Tools to Search and Gemini

Google on Wednesday announced a suite of new AI-powered study tools for students, integrated directly into Google Search and Gemini. The features include AI-generated interactive visuals, 3D simulations, a dedicated student hub, and customized practice quizzes — the company's latest move to position Gemini as the default AI assistant for learners.

---

## New Study Features in Search

Students can now generate custom interactive tools and simulations directly from Search queries. For example, searching for "pH scale" returns an AI Overview with an interactive visual that makes the concept easier to grasp. A follow-up question like "plot citrus fruits on the pH scale" generates a tailored interactive experience through AI Mode in Search.

Custom practice quizzes are now available directly in Search across subjects including science, math, humanities, and foreign languages. Students can enter prompts like "Create a quiz with the most commonly tested vocabulary words from Chapter 3" and receive a customized test.

---

## Gemini Integration

The new tools extend to Gemini, where students can access the same quiz generation and interactive visualization capabilities. Google is competing directly with OpenAI, which has been expanding ChatGPT's educational features, and with independent study platforms like Knowt and Gauth.

The launch underscores the convergence of AI assistants and educational technology — a battleground where companies believe student habits formed early could translate into long-term platform loyalty.

---

## Competition in Educational AI

The education market has become a key arena for AI companies. Beyond Google's new features, multiple startups offer AI-powered study and practice tools, creating pressure on established platforms to match or exceed the functionality of specialized competitors. Google's advantage is the integration with Search, which gives these features reach that standalone apps cannot easily match.
