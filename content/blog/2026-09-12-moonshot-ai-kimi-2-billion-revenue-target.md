# Moonshot AI maker of Kimi targets $2 billion annual revenue

Moonshot AI, the Chinese laboratory behind the popular Kimi AI assistant and its K3 open-weight model, is targeting $2 billion in annual revenue, according to Bloomberg. The ambitious revenue goal comes as the company seeks to convert its significant user base into a sustainable business.

K3, Moonshot's open-weight model, has achieved substantial usage through platforms like OpenRouter, where data shows approximately 300 billion tokens being generated daily by K3 models on the system. However, usage figures for K3 have declined slightly in recent months.

Despite the scale, Moonshot's projected revenue remains far below American AI giants. OpenAI is estimated at $40 billion annually, with Anthropic at $65 billion. The gap reflects both the challenge of monetizing open-weight models and the intense competition in the AI market.

Moonshot's model development practices have drawn significant controversy. Anthropic this week accused the company of conducting a long-running model distillation campaign that routed proprietary knowledge from Claude into K3 -- practices that Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei described as potentially illegal.

The company also faces questions about its revenue model for Kimi, which has been one of the most downloaded AI applications in China. Converting free users to paying customers at the scale required to hit $2 billion annual revenue will require significant product development.
