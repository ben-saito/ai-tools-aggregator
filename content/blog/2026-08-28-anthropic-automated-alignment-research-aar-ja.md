# Anthropic publishes paper on automated AI alignment researchers

Anthropic has published a new paper titled "Automated Researchers Can Reliably Mitigate Alignment Failures," detailing how AI systems can reliably improve a model's performance on a set of alignment benchmarks without degrading overall performance.

---

## Automated Alignment Research

The research, led by Anthropic fellow Chen Yueh-Han, describes a system called Automated Alignment Researcher (AAR) that replicates much of the traditional approach to alignment research. Each automated system searches available literature, proposes a method, and trains the model accordingly.

The paper shows that given 10 benchmarks for specific misaligned behaviors, automated systems were able to improve performance on every single benchmark. The best AAR method outperformed proposals from experienced human researchers on average.

---

## Cost and Scalability

The paper includes a striking cost comparison: "An AAR costs roughly $4 per hour in API inference against the $150 per hour we pay our human researchers." This represents a significant cost advantage for automated alignment research.

The system only works insofar as the benchmarks accurately reflect actual alignment goals, and the paper acknowledges significant limitations in this area.

---

## Implications for AI Development

The research represents a step toward recursive self-improvement, which many researchers see as the next significant milestone in AI progress. If models can improve their own alignment training, the implications for AI development cycles could be substantial.

"Overall, these results provide early evidence that automated alignment post-training could become practical in the near term," the paper reads.
