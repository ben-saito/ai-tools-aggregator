# Hello Robot Stretch 4 to Demo at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026

Hello Robot CEO and co-founder Aaron Edsinger will bring Stretch 4 for a live demo on the Real World AI Stage at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026. Stretch 4 is a home-assistance robot designed for people with severe mobility impairments, offering greater independence in everyday tasks.

---

## Stretch 4: Design Philosophy

Stretch 4 does not need legs to get around. It rolls. It does not need two arms to pick things up. Its single telescoping arm reaches out to grab and manipulate objects. The robot is compact enough to operate in home environments.

Hello Robot says the new generation was shaped by six years of customer feedback and comes with a full-stack, open source software platform for developers.

---

## Aaron Edsinger's Robotics Background

Aaron Edsinger has spent his career building robots designed to work with people. Before co-founding Hello Robot, he founded Meka Robotics and Redwood Robotics, two robotics companies acquired by Google.

His expertise covers the entire robot product design stack, from human-robot interaction and UX to mechanical and industrial design, manufacturing, software architecture, real-time control, and more.
