# SpaceX Officially Closes Cursor Acquisition: AI Coding Startup Joins Musk's Portfolio

Elon Musk's SpaceX has officially completed its acquisition of AI coding startup Cursor, marking a significant consolidation of AI development tools under the SpaceX umbrella. The deal, announced in April and valued at approximately $60 billion, gives SpaceX control of one of the most prominent AI-assisted software development platforms.

---

## From Collaboration to Acquisition

Cursor and SpaceX initially announced a partnership in April, with plans to jointly develop technology. The agreement also granted SpaceX the option to acquire Cursor for $60 billion. When SpaceX went public earlier this year, both companies confirmed the acquisition talks were ongoing.

Cursor has built a reputation as an AI-powered coding environment that integrates large language models directly into the software development workflow. The startup's platform helps developers write, review, and debug code with AI assistance.

---

## Access to Massive GPU Infrastructure

In its announcement confirming the deal's closure, Cursor emphasized the strategic advantage of SpaceX's computing infrastructure. The company stated that by joining SpaceX, it would gain access to "the largest fleet of GPUs in the world."

"SpaceX is building the computing capacity needed to scale intelligence far beyond what exists today," Cursor said in a statement. "Cursor will be one place where that intelligence becomes useful."

SpaceX has been renting out its GPU compute capacity to customers including Anthropic and Google, building a commercial cloud infrastructure around its internal AI development needs.

---

## Integration with xAI and Legal Controversies

The Cursor acquisition adds to Musk's growing AI portfolio, which includes xAI (also acquired by SpaceX earlier this year). Cursor's technology is expected to complement xAI's Grok chatbot and related AI services.

However, the acquisition comes amid legal challenges. SpaceX faces a lawsuit over pollution created by its data center gas turbines, adding to the regulatory scrutiny surrounding the company's rapid expansion into AI infrastructure.

---

## Industry Implications

The deal signals continued consolidation in the AI development tools market, with major players seeking to control both the infrastructure and the application layers of AI computing. Cursor's integration into SpaceX positions it as a key component of what Musk has described as his vision for scaling intelligence across multiple domains.
