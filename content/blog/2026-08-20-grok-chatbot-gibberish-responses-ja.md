# Grok Chatbot Reportedly Sends Gibberish Responses to Users

xAI's Grok chatbot has been sending gibberish responses to a subset of users, according to reports from affected users and TechCrunch. The issue appears to be limited to Grok Lite, with affected users noticing problems as early as Wednesday morning.

One user reported that after asking the model to generate a PDF, they received nonsensical text: "match it without and your they and two for planets can practical and often cheese..." with similar gibberish continuing for several paragraphs. Another user found a string of links to reinforcement learning research sites when expecting coherent responses.

The Grok subreddit has been flooded with complaints from confused users. Refreshing the session often restores normal function, though some users reported the gibberish persisted even after multiple refreshes. The bug appears limited to direct queries on Grok.com, while the Grok account on X.com has been unaffected.

TechCrunch was unable to reproduce the issue in its own testing, suggesting it affects only a small subset of users. xAI did not respond to a request for comment.

This incident adds to a growing list of LLM reliability issues, highlighting the challenges AI companies face in maintaining consistent model behavior at scale. For developers building applications on top of LLM APIs, such edge cases underscore the importance of robust error handling and fallback strategies.
