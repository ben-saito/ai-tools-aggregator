# Google Plans to Kill Assistant on Your Phone on September 4

Google is set to retire its Google Assistant on mobile devices, with the AI-powered voice assistant being replaced by Gemini. The change takes effect September 4, when Assistant will disappear from Android phones and iOS devices, leaving only Gemini for voice control interactions.

---

## The End of an Era

Google Assistant launched in 2016 as the company's flagship voice AI product, competing with Apple's Siri and Amazon's Alexa. Over nearly a decade, it became the default voice interface for Android devices, handling tasks ranging from setting reminders to controlling smart home devices. The assistant was built into billions of devices worldwide.

The transition away from Assistant represents a significant shift in Google's mobile AI strategy. Rather than maintaining a separate voice assistant product, Google is consolidating its AI capabilities under the Gemini brand, which offers more advanced reasoning and multimodal capabilities.

---

## What Replaces Assistant

Gemini, Google's latest AI assistant built on its most powerful language models, has been progressively replacing Assistant functionality across devices. Gemini can handle the full range of tasks Assistant performed, including voice commands, smart home control, and information queries, while adding capabilities like advanced reasoning, image analysis, and integration with Google's broader AI ecosystem.

The move reflects a broader industry trend toward consolidating AI assistants under more powerful, reasoning-capable AI systems. Apple's Siri has undergone similar transformations, and Amazon has integrated Alexa with its own AI models.
