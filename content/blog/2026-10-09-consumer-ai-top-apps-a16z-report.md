# A16z's Olivia Moore Analyzes State of Consumer AI: Top 100 Apps Report

---

Andreessen Horowitz partner Olivia Moore has released a comprehensive report on the top 100 consumer AI applications, painting a picture of a market still searching for sustainable business models beyond subscriptions and API charges.

Despite the proliferation of AI applications, Moore sees significant untapped potential. "I wrote this week about the bleak economics of consumer AI," she notes, "but where some see a crisis, others see an opportunity."

The report examines the top consumer AI apps by user engagement, revenue, and growth trajectories. ChatGPT remains the dominant player, but the landscape is diversifying with applications spanning productivity, entertainment, health, and education.

Moore identifies several key trends: AI applications that integrate deeply into existing workflows show stronger retention than standalone AI tools, and applications that can tap into recurring revenue streams beyond one-time subscriptions are more likely to achieve sustainable unit economics.

The report also highlights geographic diversification, with significant consumer AI adoption in markets outside the United States. This includes strong growth in Asia and Europe, where local language models and culturally adapted applications are gaining traction.

For developers and investors, the report suggests focusing on applications where AI can provide clear, demonstrable value rather than novelty, and where the integration with existing behaviors and platforms can drive organic adoption.
