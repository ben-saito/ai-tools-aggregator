# Amazon launches Alexa+ in India with Hindi support

Amazon has announced that Alexa+, its next-generation conversational AI assistant, is now available in India with full Hindi language support. The rollout marks a significant expansion for Amazon's voice assistant platform in one of its largest international markets.

---

## Early Access for All Indian Customers

The upgraded assistant is now available to all customers in India through Early Access, offering both Hindi and English language options. Users can switch between the two languages mid-conversation, enabling more natural bilingual interactions.

After the early testing period concludes, Amazon plans to make Alexa+ free for Prime members. Non-Prime customers will pay ₹2,000 per month (approximately $20.85) for access to the full assistant capabilities.

---

## More Expressive Voice and Enhanced Context Handling

Amazon says the new Alexa+ features a more expressive voice and improved ability to maintain context across longer conversations. The assistant can handle complex multi-step tasks, including ordering groceries through Amazon's quick commerce service Amazon Now and controlling smart home devices.

---

## Integration with India-Based Services

Alexa+ is integrated with popular India-based services including food delivery platforms Swiggy and Zomato's ticket booking service District. Additional integrations cover travel websites TripAdvisor and MakeMyTrip, as well as restaurant reservation app EazyDiner.
