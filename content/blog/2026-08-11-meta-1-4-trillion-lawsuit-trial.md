# Meta Faces $1.4 Trillion Lawsuit Trial as Appeals Court Clears Path for States' Case

A US appeals court has cleared the way for a landmark lawsuit against Meta to proceed to trial, rejecting the company's arguments that it should be shielded from state-level litigation over its platform's alleged harms. The case, brought by the California attorney general and other state AGs, seeks damages that Meta estimates could exceed $1.4 trillion.

---

## What This Case Is About

The lawsuit centers on allegations that Meta's platforms — primarily Facebook and Instagram — have caused harms to users, particularly young people. The states argue that Meta engaged in deceptive practices regarding the mental health impacts of its products. Meta has denied the allegations and argued that it should benefit from federal legal protections that would shield it from state-level damages claims.

A trial is scheduled to begin August 19, with jury selection starting August 12. Yesterday's 3-0 ruling from a panel of the US Court of Appeals for the 9th Circuit rejected Meta's and TikTok's claims that they have legal immunity to the lawsuit.

---

## The Scale of Potential Damages

The $1.4 trillion figure represents Meta's own calculation of the potential exposure if the states prevail on all claims. While the actual damages awarded — if any — would likely be far lower and subject to appeal, the number underscores the seriousness with which Meta views this litigation. The case could set precedent for how state attorneys general pursue consumer protection claims against major technology platforms.

---

## Broader Regulatory Landscape

This case is separate from federal regulatory actions but reflects the intensifying legal pressure on Meta. The company is also facing ongoing scrutiny from the EU AI Act, content moderation debates, and a Wall Street Journal op-ed from Mark Zuckerberg this week outlining his vision for "personal superintelligence" — a framing that critics argue sidesteps the regulatory and social questions the company faces.
