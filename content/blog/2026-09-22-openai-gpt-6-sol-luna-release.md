# OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Sol and Luna with Half-Price API and Improved Accuracy

OpenAI is expanding its GPT-6 model family with two new variants — GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna — featuring significant price cuts and measurable improvements in factual accuracy and coding performance.

---

## Extending the GPT-6 Generation

Earlier this month, OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra, which the company called its most powerful model to date and the "world's best model" for tasks including computer work and coding. The new Sol and Luna variants extend the same generation while targeting different workload profiles.

Sol is optimized for complex tasks like coding, while Luna is positioned for high-volume clerical work — document summarization, information extraction, and quick-question answering.

"GPT-6 Astra introduced a new generation of intelligence; these models extend its benefits by making that intelligence more efficient and accessible," OpenAI said in its announcement.

---

## Half the Cost of the Previous Generation

The most concrete improvement for developers is the price drop. The GPT-6 Sol and Luna models are available at exactly half the API cost of the 5.6 series, a reduction OpenAI attributes to advances in output caching and inference efficiency.

This puts the 6 series in direct competition with lower-priced alternatives, a notable strategic shift as Anthropic and Google have been aggressively pricing their latest models.

---

## Better Accuracy, Fewer Mistakes

OpenAI is also claiming meaningful improvements in output quality. According to the company's internal factuality evaluation — based on de-identified real-world conversations where users flagged model errors — GPT-6 Sol makes approximately half as many mistakes as its predecessor, reaching Astra-level reliability at substantially lower cost.

The company also highlighted a lower error rate on coding tasks, a domain where previous GPT models frequently generated syntactically plausible but incorrect code.

---

## Anthropic Release Timing Noteworthy

Anthropic released Opus 5.5 just 90 minutes before OpenAI's announcement, a timing that underscores the competitive cadence between the two AI labs. Both companies appear to be accelerating their release schedules in response to each other's moves — a dynamic that has become a defining characteristic of the current frontier model race.

The new Sol and Luna models are available now in ChatGPT Work and Codex for most paid accounts, in the ChatGPT API, and Luna will additionally be available in the desktop app and for Free and Go users.
