# Okta、AIセキュリティスタートアップPermisoを約2億ドルで買収

Identity management giant Okta has acquired Permiso, an AI security startup, for approximately $200 million. The deal strengthens Okta's identity threat detection capabilities as enterprises increasingly need to secure AI agents and non-human identities.

## Why This Matters

As AI agents proliferate in enterprise environments, traditional identity management faces new challenges. AI agents operate autonomously, access multiple systems, and often use credentials that were never designed for machine-to-machine authentication. Permiso was built specifically to address these gaps with AI-native identity threat detection.

The acquisition signals a broader trend: security vendors are racing to add AI-specific identity capabilities. Microsoft, CrowdStrike, and Palo Alto Networks have all made similar moves in recent months.

## Technical Details

Permiso's platform focuses on detecting anomalous behavior from AI agents and non-human identities. Unlike traditional SIEM tools that rely on known attack signatures, Permiso uses behavioral analysis to identify suspicious patterns unique to AI workloads.

Okta plans to integrate Permiso's detection engine directly into its existing identity cloud. This would give Okta customers real-time visibility into AI agent activity across their organization.

## Market Context

The $200M price tag reflects the growing importance of AI security. As enterprises deploy more AI agents, the attack surface expands significantly. Each AI agent is a potential entry point for attackers. Security firms that can help enterprises manage this risk will be in high demand.
