# Trump Proposes Rebranding AI as a New Name — Also Announces Formation of an "AI Force"

---

## From "Artificial Intelligence" to What?

On September 19, 2026, former President Donald Trump published a post on Truth Social suggesting that the term "Artificial Intelligence" should be rebranded, calling it "inaccurate" and "very ineloquent" relative to what the technology actually does. The post marked the beginning of a weekend of extensive musings on AI policy and regulation.

The suggestion tapped into a broader undercurrent in American political discourse: skepticism about data centers, AI environmental impact, and concerns about foreign influence over the technology — concerns that cut across traditional partisan lines.

---

## Calling AI Backlash a "Democratic Hoax"

A few hours after his initial post, Trump followed up with a claim that efforts to restrict or regulate AI represent a Democratic hoax — similar, he argued, to past controversies labeled as Russian interference, Ukraine-related issues, and other political narratives. "The decimation, or destruction, of AI," he wrote, is one of "many" Democratic hoaxes, he claimed without providing evidence.

Both Republican and Democratic politicians have raised concerns about data center energy consumption and AI's environmental footprint. Industry analysts noted that Trump's framing elided genuine policy questions with partisan framing.

---

## Forming an "AI Force" — Similar to Space Force

Trump also announced plans to create an "AI Force" — drawing a direct parallel to the Space Force he established during his first term. "To that end, I will be announcing, in the near future, the AI 'Czar' — Only High I.Q. individuals need apply!" he added.

The position of AI czar would follow the recent departure of venture capitalist David Sacks, who stepped down as Trump's AI and crypto czar earlier in 2026 to co-chair the President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology (PCAST).

---

## No Details on AI Force Duties

The announcement notably lacks specifics: Trump did not outline what the AI Force's responsibilities would be, what authorities it would have, or how it would interact with existing agencies like the National Telecommunications and Information Administration (NTIA) or the Department of Commerce. Whether the AI Force would be a military body, a civilian agency, or a hybrid remains unstated.

The proposal raises questions about the future of US AI governance — particularly how AI safety, competitiveness, and national security mandates would be divided among competing bureaucratic entities.
