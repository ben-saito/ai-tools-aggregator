# Floqer Open-Sources AI-Powered Customer Knowledge Platform

---

Floqer is an autonomous customer knowledge platform that provides business-to-business sales and marketing teams with an AI-powered database designed to continuously collect, organize, and update the information used to understand their customers. The startup is pitching Wednesday on the Startup Battlefield stage at **TechCrunch Disrupt 2026**, with plans to open-source its technology in early 2027 pending security reviews.

Floqer says it has surpassed **$1.2 million in annual recurring revenue (ARR)**, with overall revenue growing roughly tenfold over the past year.

## From Chrome Extension to Enterprise Platform

The startup was founded by Shivam Mahajan, the CEO, and Zaaheda Islam, who met at university. Before Floqer, Mahajan worked as a software engineer at Kyndryl, and Islam worked as a senior data scientist at Axis Capital.

Floqer's first product looked nothing like its current one. "We started with a Chrome extension, which is called 'Super Chat,'" Mahajan told TechCrunch. "Anybody can go and log in with their ChatGPT account and then understand information and take notes."

The founders soon noticed that enterprise companies were using the extension to clean up data in their customer relationship management (CRM) systems. Conversations with those companies revealed that keeping CRM data clean was mostly manual work, with few established processes for keeping customer records up to date.

"When we spoke with them, they told us that it's super manual for them to clean their CRM data, and there were no established processes around that," Mahajan said. "So that was insightful for us, and then we pivoted from there to build Floqer."

## Solving the CRM Data Problem

Floqer aims to solve a familiar problem for sales and revenue operations teams: finding potential customers takes hours of searching Google, LinkedIn, company websites, and other sources. The AI-powered platform automates this process, continuously researching and updating customer profiles so teams can focus on selling rather than data entry.
