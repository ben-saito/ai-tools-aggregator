# ScrollEd Wants to Turn Textbooks Into TikTok

ScrollEd, a Palo Alto-based startup founded by student co-founders, is pitching a novel idea at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026: if students won't open textbooks voluntarily, make the learning experience feel like scrolling through social media.

---

## The Product: Textbooks as Short-Form Video

ScrollEd has built an app that transforms any text file — including PDFs and textbooks — into a scrollable, Instagram Reels- or TikTok-style feed. As users scroll, content appears as AI-generated video, audio, text, or interactive elements. The feed is vertical, short-form, and designed to feel native to how younger users already consume media.

"The younger generation is increasingly going short form, vertical feed only (especially the 18 year olds we've spoken to!), and this poses challenges for any kind of media that requires depth," Utsav Gupta, ScrollEd's co-founder, told TechCrunch.

"A short video can make you curious; following that curiosity can mean searching for another explanation or leaving the app," Gupta continued. "We wanted going deeper to be as easy as moving to the next video."

---

## Founders and Origin Story

Gupta bootstrapped ScrollEd this year alongside his co-founder and wife Rebecca Neff. Both are students — Gupta studies AI and human purpose at Stanford, and Neff is also a student. The idea emerged after both noticed feeling dissatisfied after doomscrolling before bed.

The startup is targeting educational institutions, corporate training programs, and individuals who "just want something better to scroll on," according to Gupta.

---

## Business Model: Freemium With Institutional Licensing

ScrollEd is following a freemium model. The free tier provides a consumer feed, while ScrollEd Pro is a paid subscription. Institutions pay for annual licenses to deliver educational material through the platform, in exchange for engagement reports and analytics.

The company will launch its consumer business at Disrupt. ("We're hoping to 'disrupt' social media and short form!" Gupta joked.)

---

## Future Roadmap

In the coming months, ScrollEd intends to focus on its consumer launch, building its lesson library and source-checking workflow, and developing pilots for institutions. The startup is also building infrastructure that will one day allow educators to upload and transform their own content.

"The ambition is a social network that's better for you," Gupta said.

ScrollEd is pitching in the TechCrunch Disrupt Startup Battlefield 200 competition.
