# YouTube Studio Gets New AI Features to Help Creators Analyze and Grow Their Audiences

At its annual Made On YouTube event, YouTube announced new AI-powered tools for YouTube Studio, the app creators use to manage their channels. The updates include expanding Ask Studio, its AI-powered Q&A feature, to iOS and Android, as well as new features that provide feedback on drafts and create dynamic thumbnails.

---

## AI Draft Feedback and Performance Insights

The draft-feedback feature uses AI to analyze a creator's unpublished video and provide suggestions about pacing, structure, and storytelling. YouTube is also debuting a new research feed in the Studio app that shows creators what kind of content is performing well on the platform, so they can apply their own take on emerging trends.

Creators are typically picky about the preview images and titles they use for their videos. The Studio app will now let them generate thumbnails and titles based on a video's content and matched to their personal style. YouTube is also introducing a new dynamic thumbnail feature that generates three options and shows each one to a different segment of their audience.

---

## A/B Testing and Automatic Optimization

YouTube said creators have used its A/B testing feature to run more than 40 million tests on titles and thumbnails. Soon, users will be able to test up to three video cuts to see which opening hook works best. Later this year, YouTube Studio will automatically monitor thumbnail performance and change one proactively if it underperforms.

YouTube is also updating its analytics to go beyond raw numbers, explaining why videos perform the way they do and providing actionable advice about how to make content more engaging.
