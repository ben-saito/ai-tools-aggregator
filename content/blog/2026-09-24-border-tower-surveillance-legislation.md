# US Representative Proposes Bill to Terminate Border Surveillance Tower Program

Delia Ramirez, a Democratic US representative from Illinois, has announced a plan to introduce new legislation to terminate the surveillance tower program along the US southern border. The legislation, called the Reimagining Safety Act, was developed in consultation with community groups and advocacy organizations focused on immigrant rights and racial justice.

---

## The Reimagining Safety Act

The legislation was developed in coordination with Just Futures Law and Mijente, organizations that describe the border tower program as part of a broader system of surveillance and control targeting migrants and communities of color. The organizations published a joint report earlier this year examining the technology used by US Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE).

The bill is part of a broader proposal that includes replacing the Department of Homeland Security with a new Department of Community Safety, while moving CISA, FEMA, TSA, and customs functions to other existing federal agencies.

---

## Background: The Virtual Border Wall

The US has spent billions building a "virtual wall" of surveillance towers along its southern border over the past 25 years. The towers were promised to help detect and apprehend border crossers and save lives, but investigative reporting has documented multiple failures and deaths near tower locations.

Representative Ramirez's proposal comes as Congress continues to debate immigration and border security policy, with the surveillance tower program facing increased scrutiny over its effectiveness and human cost.
