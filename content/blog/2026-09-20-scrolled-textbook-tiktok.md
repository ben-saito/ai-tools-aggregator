# ScrollEd Wants to Turn Textbooks into TikTok

ScrollEd, a Palo Alto-based startup founded by student co-founders Utsav Gupta and Rebecca Neff (who are also spouses), is building a platform that converts textbooks into a scrollable, Instagram-like feed enhanced with video, audio, and quizzes. The company is pitching at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026.

---

## Summary

ScrollEd's pitch is straightforward: educational content in textbook format is difficult to consume in an era of short-form video and social media feeds. The company's solution transforms static textbook content into a dynamic, social-media-style experience that students can scroll through on their phones.

The startup targets students who are accustomed to consuming content in short, engaging bursts through platforms like TikTok and Instagram. By restructuring textbook content into this format—adding video explanations, audio narration, and interactive quizzes—ScrollEd aims to make academic content more accessible to digital-native learners.

---

## Educational Technology Market Context

The edtech space has seen significant investment as the gap between traditional textbook publishing and student learning preferences continues to widen. Several companies have attempted variations of this concept, including platforms that break down academic papers into summaries, convert textbooks to audiobooks, or gamify learning through quizzes and streaks.

ScrollEd differentiates by focusing specifically on the visual and interactive transformation of content, rather than just repackaging text in a different medium. The inclusion of video and audio elements aims to accommodate different learning styles within the same content piece.

The company is competing in the Startup Battlefield 200 at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, where it will present to investors and industry observers alongside other early-stage companies in the space.
