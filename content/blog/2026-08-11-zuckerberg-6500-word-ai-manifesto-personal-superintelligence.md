# Mark Zuckerberg's 6,500-Word AI Manifesto: "Personal Superintelligence" Vision

On Monday, Mark Zuckerberg published a 6,500-word manifesto detailing his vision for "personal superintelligence" systems through Meta AI, representing his most detailed articulation of the company's AI strategy to date.

The essay, which also ran in the Wall Street Journal two weeks prior, outlines Zuckerberg's thinking on AI systems that are "personal" — tailored to individual users and accessible on their own terms. A version of Meta's open-weight Muse Glimmer model, also released this week, serves as a concrete embodiment of parts of this vision.

---

## A Reboot of Meta's AI Strategy

The manifesto arrives as Meta has been trailing competitors in the AI race. Zuckerberg appears to be attempting a course correction, positioning Meta's approach around open-weight models and personal AI as a differentiating factor against closed, API-only competitors.

---

## Why the Manifesto Resonates Poorly

Critics have noted the timing and framing of the essay itself as part of why public sentiment toward AI remains ambivalent. The document frames AI primarily through the lens of commercial opportunity and individual empowerment, without addressing broader societal concerns about automation, labor displacement, or the concentration of AI capabilities.

The ideas in the post are not entirely new — versions have appeared in earnings calls and prior public statements — but this represents the most comprehensive written statement of Zuckerberg's AI philosophy to date.
