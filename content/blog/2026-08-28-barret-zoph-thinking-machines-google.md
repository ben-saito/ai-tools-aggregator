# Barret Zoph, Thinking Machines Lab Co-Founder, Returns to Google After Brief OpenAI Stint

Barret Zoph, who co-founded Thinking Machines Lab alongside Mira Murati and served as the startup's CTO, has joined Google. Zoph had a brief stint at OpenAI following his departure from Thinking Machines Lab, and is now returning to Google, where he previously worked before co-founding the AI startup.

---

## Background: From Google to Thinking Machines to OpenAI and Back

Zoph was among the founding team of Thinking Machines Lab, the AI startup launched by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati. The company positioned itself as a safety-conscious AI lab focused on developing AI systems that are both powerful and responsible. Zoph served as the company's CTO during its early growth phase.

After leaving Thinking Machines Lab, Zoph joined OpenAI -- a move that surprised industry observers given his safety-focused background at Thinking Machines and OpenAI's own safety initiatives. His tenure at OpenAI was brief, and he has now transitioned to Google.

The return to Google marks another chapter in Zoph's career trajectory, which has included stops at some of the most prominent AI research institutions in the industry.
