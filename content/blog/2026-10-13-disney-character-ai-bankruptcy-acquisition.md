# Disney Offers $20M for Character.AI Tech as Bankruptcy Proceedings Begin

---

Disney has offered to purchase "a non-exclusive perpetual license" of certain Character Technologies intellectual property as the chatbot startup navigates bankruptcy proceedings that began this week. The $20 million offer marks a striking reversal from Disney's position in September 2025, when the company sent a cease-and-desist letter accusing Character.AI of weaponizing Disney characters in ways that could damage its brand. The deal may face competition from other bidders, and Disney has not indicated whether it plans to use Character.AI technology in products accessible to children.

The bankruptcy filing and subsequent offer come after Character.AI became one of the first chatbot platforms to face serious regulatory scrutiny over mental health impacts, particularly among younger users. The company's chatbots have been linked in public discourse to high-profile incidents that raised concerns about the intersection of conversational AI and child safety.

Disney's move to acquire technology from a company it once criticized reflects the growing competition among major tech firms to secure AI capabilities. The proposed acquisition would give Disney access to Character.AI's conversational AI infrastructure, potentially for use in theme park experiences, customer service applications, and entertainment personalization.
