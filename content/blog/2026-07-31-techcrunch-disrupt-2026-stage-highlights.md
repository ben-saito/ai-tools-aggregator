# TechCrunch Disrupt 2026's Biggest Stage Features Leaders from Amazon, Replit, Tether

TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 has announced the headline speakers for its main stage, with leaders from Amazon, Replit, and Tether among those confirmed for the three-day event focused on the future of technology and startups.

The Disrupt Stage has historically been where major technology announcements break, with a track record that includes early appearances by companies that went on to become industry-defining. This year's agenda centers heavily on artificial intelligence, with sessions exploring the gap between AI hype and commercial reality, the security challenges of agentic systems, and the infrastructure buildout required to support the next generation of AI applications.

---

## Key Sessions and Speakers

Amazon's technology leadership will discuss the company's AI strategy across its cloud and consumer businesses, including how it is integrating AI into logistics and fulfillment. Replit's CEO will speak on the evolving role of coding in an AI-assisted world, a topic the company has been vocal about as AI code generation tools mature.

Tether, the stablecoin company, will appear on a stage dedicated to the intersection of decentralized systems and AI infrastructure. The company has been expanding beyond its original stablecoin product into AI-related ventures, a direction that will likely face scrutiny during the session.

---

## The AI Stage at Disrupt

The AI Stage at Disrupt has been expanded this year, reflecting the centrality of AI to the current technology landscape. Sessions will cover topics including AI safety and alignment in commercial products, the economics of inference at scale, and the talent pipeline for AI engineering roles.

A dedicated pitch session will feature early-stage AI startups presenting to a panel of investors, following the format that has launched several notable companies in previous Disrupt events.

---

## Event Logistics

TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 takes place in San Francisco from October 27-29. Early-bird registration is available through the TechCrunch website, with general admission tickets opening for sale in August. The event will also be livestreamed for registered remote attendees.
