# Crusoe Raises $3B at $30B Valuation, Eyes IPO

Data center developer Crusoe has raised a new $3 billion round at a $30 billion valuation, Bloomberg reported. The deal was co-led by Atreides Management and Valor Equity Partners, with participation from Mubadala Capital, the asset management subsidiary of Abu Dhabi's sovereign wealth fund.

---

## From Crypto Mining to AI Infrastructure

Launched in 2018 as a cryptocurrency mining operation powered by flared natural gas, Crusoe has pivoted into a major AI infrastructure and cloud provider. The company is best known for developing hyperscale data centers that serve clients including Meta, Microsoft, and OpenAI.

The fresh fundraise comes just 10 months after Crusoe raised a $1.38 billion round at a $10 billion valuation last October -- representing a significant valuation step-up in a short period.

The latest round comes together after Crusoe secured a massive $13 billion, five-year cloud contract to supply quantitative trading firm Jane Street with GPUs and AI infrastructure. This contract represents one of the largest single AI infrastructure deals signed this year.

---

## IPO Preparations

Crusoe has recently met with investment bankers including Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley to discuss a potential near-term IPO, Axios reported last month. The company's path to a public listing appears to be accelerating as it builds scale to compete with established cloud and AI infrastructure providers.

The startup's transformation from a crypto mining company to a key AI infrastructure player reflects the broader shift in compute demand driven by large language model training and deployment. Crusoe's ability to secure contracts with major AI labs and trading firms demonstrates the diversity of its customer base beyond traditional tech companies.
