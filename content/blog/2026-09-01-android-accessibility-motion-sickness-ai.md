# Google's Android Update Tackles Motion Sickness, Accessibility, and More

On Tuesday, Google announced it is rolling out five new updates designed to make Android phones more accessible, useful, and personalized. The features include those aimed at reducing motion sickness, helping blind and low-vision users navigate their surroundings, and new personalization options powered by Gemini AI.

---

## AI-Powered Accessibility Features

The most notable accessibility feature uses AI to help users who experience motion sickness when looking at their phone while passengers in moving vehicles. The system detects when a user is in a moving vehicle and adjusts display settings to reduce the visual dissonance that causes nausea.

Google also introduced improvements for blind and low-vision users, leveraging Gemini to provide more contextual awareness about the surrounding environment through the phone camera.

---

## Personalized Experiences with Gemini

Among the updates is a feature that remembers where users leave important items, using on-device AI to track frequently misplaced objects. Another update adds new ways to personalize the home screen and app recommendations.

Some features see Google playing catch-up to Apple, which already offers similar functionality for iPhone users. However, several updates specifically leverage Gemini to provide improvements unique to Android.

---

## Availability

The features began rolling out on Tuesday. Users with Android devices supporting Gemini will see the updates automatically over the coming weeks.
