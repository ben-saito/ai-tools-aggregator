# Modal Labs Closing $750M Round at $15.75B Valuation

Modal Labs, the New York-based AI inference infrastructure startup founded by former Spotify data team lead Erik Bernhardsson, is closing a $750 million funding round that values the company at $15.75 billion — more than triple the $4.65 billion valuation it reached just four months earlier when it announced a $355 million raise.

---

## Soaring Demand for AI Inference

The fundraise comes amid explosive demand for inference services, the compute-intensive process of running already-trained AI models to generate outputs. Modal's customers rely heavily on open-source models, and the company has emerged as a key infrastructure provider for developers who want to run AI workloads without managing their own servers.

Despite rapidly growing revenue, margins remain thin across the inference sector because compute acquisition and leasing costs remain high. Fireworks AI, a competitor, disclosed in July that its margins were under pressure from the same dynamic.

---

## From Spotify to AI Infrastructure

Bernhardsson, Modal's Swedish CEO, spent more than 15 years building data teams at companies including Spotify before founding Modal in 2021 alongside CTO Akshat Bubna. The company is estimated to employ roughly 150 people.

Modal's platform allows developers to train AI models and run compute-heavy workloads without managing infrastructure directly. As of May 2026, the company had surpassed $300 million in annualized revenue, it told Reuters at the time.

---

## A Tumultuous Summer

The fundraising talks come two months after Modal was pulled into one of the AI industry's most closely watched security incidents. In late July, Modal disclosed that a customer's data had been compromised, highlighting the security challenges facing inference providers who handle sensitive training and deployment workloads for enterprise clients.

The new funding is expected to accelerate Modal's expansion of compute capacity and continued development of its developer platform.
