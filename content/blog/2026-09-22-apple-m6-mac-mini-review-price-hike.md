# Apple M6 Mac Mini Review: The New M6 Impresses, But the $300 Price Hike Is Rough

Ars Technica published a detailed review of the M6 Mac mini on September 21, 2026, finding that while the new chip delivers meaningful performance improvements, the $300 increase in starting price undermines what has traditionally been one of Apple's best-value computing products.

---

## Performance: The M6 Delivers Meaningful Gains

The M6 chip brings Apple's custom silicon architecture to a new generation of the compact desktop computer. The review notes that the M6 Mac mini offers improvements in both compute and graphics performance over the M4 generation, continuing Apple's pattern of year-over-year advancement in its Apple Silicon lineup.

The M4 Mac mini, released in 2024, was notable for offering what reviewers characterized as one of the best values in Apple's computing history — a capable desktop at a $599 starting price. The M6 generation maintains the compact design and general-purpose capability of its predecessor while delivering the expected performance gains from the new chip.

---

## The Price Problem

The central criticism in the Ars Technica review is the $300 price increase on the base configuration. The M6 Mac mini starts at $899 for a model with 16GB of RAM and 256GB of storage — the same memory and storage configuration that cost $599 in the M4 generation.

This increase means the entry point for the Mac mini has risen substantially, moving from an accessible price point to a premium positioning. The review suggests that while the performance improvements are real, they may not be sufficient to justify the premium for users whose needs are adequately served by the previous generation.

---

## Design and Connectivity

The M6 Mac mini maintains the compact redesign introduced with the M4 generation, which represented the first major physical redesign of the Mac mini since the Intel era. The small form factor and selection of front-facing ports remain positive attributes, though the core design is unchanged from the prior generation.

Connectivity includes two 10 Gbps USB-C ports and a headphone jack on the front, with additional ports on the back including Ethernet and HDMI. The review covers the full selection of ports and their capabilities for different use cases.
