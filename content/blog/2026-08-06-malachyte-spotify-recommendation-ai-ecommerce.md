# Malachyte: Ex-Spotify Employees Raise $10M to Bring Recommendation AI to E-Commerce

Three former Spotify employees have launched Malachyte, a startup applying the music platform's recommendation AI to online retail. The company announced on August 6, 2026 that it has raised $10 million in seed funding to scale distribution and hire product and commercial leadership.

---

## The Problem with Current E-Commerce Personalization

According to Malachyte CEO Ravi Motwani, most online stores treat shoppers identically: personalization relies on historical purchases, demographic segmentation, or logged-in profiles. First-time visitors see the same generic storefront as everyone else, while returning shoppers receive recommendations based primarily on past purchases rather than current intent.

Malachyte's approach models each shopper's evolving preferences in real time, using what the company calls a "two-headed Vector AI" architecture. The system predicts what a product a shopper wants next, learns their general taste, and continuously fine-tunes based on real-time behavior — without requiring an account or purchase history.

---

## How It Works: Real-Time Intent Modeling

The key insight is treating every browsing signal — hover, click, scroll, search refinement, add-to-cart — as an intent signal. A search for "heavy-duty boot" followed by two clicks on steel-toed boots is sufficient to elevate work pants and gloves in recommendations while demoting dress shoes, Motwani explains. Each subsequent action sharpens the profile, making recommendations more relevant the longer a session continues and persisting into future visits.

This differs from Spotify's recommendation system primarily in the domain — e-commerce has different signal density and purchase frequency than music streaming — but the underlying philosophy of continuous, behavior-driven personalization carries over.
