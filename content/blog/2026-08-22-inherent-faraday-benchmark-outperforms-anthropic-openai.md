# Inherent's Faraday Outperforms Anthropic and OpenAI at Scientific Paper Replication

London AI lab Inherent released benchmark results showing its Faraday AI agent outperformed larger models from Anthropic and OpenAI at replicating scientific papers, demonstrating that focused training on research tasks can surpass frontier-scale models on specific benchmarks.

---

## The Benchmark Results

Just weeks after emerging from stealth with a $50 million seed round, Inherent reports that its Faraday agent significantly outperformed Anthropic's and OpenAI's models at paper replication tasks. The benchmark tests AI systems' ability to reconstruct key findings from published scientific papers -- a task that requires both understanding of research methodology and attention to experimental details.

Paper replication is a standard test in AI-for-science research, but Inherent claims Faraday's performance suggests a new level of research capability. The company argues this represents a potential stepping stone toward AI systems that can contribute to, rather than just summarize, scientific discovery.

---

## Why This Matters

Inherent was founded by Google DeepMind alumni who have quietly built one of the more technically sophisticated AI research operations outside the major US labs. While better-funded rivals have focused on general capabilities, Inherent has pursued a more specialized approach to scientific AI.

The benchmark results add to growing evidence that model size is not the only determinant of task performance. Focused training regimes and task-specific architectures can outperform much larger general-purpose models on specific problems. This has implications for how smaller labs compete with well-resourced incumbents.

---

## Scientific Research Automation

Beyond the benchmark itself, Inherent's approach points toward a future where AI agents play a more direct role in the research process. Rather than simply summarizing existing papers, AI systems that can replicate and extend published research could accelerate scientific discovery in fields ranging from drug development to materials science.

Inherent has been relatively quiet since its founding, but the Faraday benchmark results suggest the lab is building toward ambitious goals in AI-for-science. The company is positioning paper replication not as a party trick but as a foundation for AI systems that can make genuine contributions to human knowledge.
