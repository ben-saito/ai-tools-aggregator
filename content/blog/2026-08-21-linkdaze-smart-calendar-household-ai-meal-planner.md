# Linkdaze's Smart Calendar Wants to Run Your Household, Not Just Track Your Schedule

Linkdaze, a new startup, is launching a smart digital calendar that goes beyond scheduling: it includes an AI meal planner, integrates with household task management, and is not placing its features behind a paywall.

The product is notable because most consumer AI tools — from smart home assistants to calendar apps — have adopted tiered pricing that reserves advanced AI features for paying subscribers. Linkdaze is betting that a free, feature-rich product can gain enough user traction to make the freemium model work through upsells or data-driven monetization.

The calendar's AI meal planner is its differentiator: it can propose meals based on household preferences, dietary restrictions, and ingredients already on hand. The system is designed to coordinate across family members, learning routines and suggesting optimizations — for example, proposing to batch-cook on Sunday for the week ahead.

Whether Linkdaze can sustain a free model in a market where AI compute costs are substantial remains to be seen. The company's specific monetization strategy is not yet public. Competitors like Calendly and Notion have both moved toward AI features as premium upsells; Linkdaze's approach inverts that logic.

For now, the product is attracting attention for its breadth of features and its approach to pricing. If it can retain users and demonstrate genuine utility, it may carve out a niche in the crowded productivity market.
