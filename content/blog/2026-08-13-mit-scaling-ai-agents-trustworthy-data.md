# MIT Tech Review: Scaling AI Agents with Trustworthy Data

MIT Technology Review published an analysis on the critical role of data quality in scaling AI agents. As organizations rush to deploy agentic AI systems, the article argues that trustworthy data infrastructure may matter more than model architecture for determining real-world performance.

---

## The Agentic AI Challenge

Business and technology leaders have embraced agentic AI as the next frontier. AI agents—systems capable of autonomous multi-step reasoning and action—are rapidly moving from research labs into production environments. But organizations are discovering that technical capability alone does not guarantee business value.

---

## Data as the Differentiator

The analysis emphasizes that the gap between pilot success and scaled deployment often comes down to data. Agents require reliable, consistent, and well-governed data sources to make accurate decisions. Poor data quality compounds across agent workflows, leading to failures that are difficult to diagnose and correct.

---

## Practical Implications

For engineering teams, the article reinforces the importance of investing in data infrastructure before scaling agent deployments. Clean data pipelines, robust validation frameworks, and clear data ownership become critical enablers of agent performance at scale.
