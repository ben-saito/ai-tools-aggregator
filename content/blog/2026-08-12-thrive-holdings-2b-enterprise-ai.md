# OpenAI-Backed Thrive Holdings Raises $2 Billion to Bring AI to the Enterprise

Thrive Holdings, a company building AI solutions for enterprise customers, has raised $2 billion in new funding at a $12 billion valuation. The round was led by SoftBank, D1 Capital Partners, and Altimeter Capital. The company is backed by OpenAI, reflecting the ChatGPT maker's strategy to expand AI adoption in business settings.

---

## Enterprise AI Push

Thrive Holdings positions itself as a bridge between frontier AI capabilities and enterprise requirements. The company provides AI-powered tools designed for business workflows, including document processing, customer service automation, and decision support systems.

The $2 billion raise is one of the largest enterprise AI funding rounds of 2026. SoftBank's participation signals the Japanese conglomerate's continued aggressive investment in AI infrastructure and applications.

---

## Investor Backing

The round included participation from:
- **SoftBank** — The investment arm of Masayoshi Son, which has committed billions to AI companies globally
- **D1 Capital Partners** — A growth equity firm with a focus on technology
- **Altimeter Capital** — Known for investments in SaaS and cloud platforms

OpenAI's continued backing of Thrive Holdings demonstrates the strategic importance of enterprise distribution for AI companies. By supporting channel partners and integrators, OpenAI extends its reach beyond direct sales.

---

## Enterprise AI Market Context

The enterprise AI market has seen significant activity in 2026, with major players including Microsoft, Google, and Amazon all launching competing products. Thrive Holdings differentiates through its focus on specific industry verticals and its relationship with OpenAI's model family.

The funding will likely be used to expand engineering teams, build out customer success capabilities, and pursue strategic acquisitions in the enterprise AI space.

---

## OpenAI's Ecosystem Strategy

OpenAI has been building a network of partners and investors to scale AI deployment. Thrive Holdings represents one of the more significant bets in the enterprise segment, reflecting belief that businesses need specialized implementation support beyond what direct API access provides.

This approach mirrors those of earlier enterprise software winners, who built ecosystems of value-added resellers and system integrators to reach customers at scale.
