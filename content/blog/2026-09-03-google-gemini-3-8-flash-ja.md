# Google Gemini 3.8 Flash: Third Flash Model in Six Weeks

Google has released Gemini 3.8 Flash, its third Flash-tier model in six weeks, continuing the company's aggressive release cadence for the Flash family. The new model comes in two variations and targets developers with an introductory API pricing through the end of the year.

---

## Two Flash Variants

Gemini 3.8 Flash ships in two configurations. The standard Flash model is described by Google as a "workhorse" suitable for agentic tasks, software development, and general-purpose workloads. The second variant targets specific use cases with optimized performance characteristics.

Both models are available via API at introductory rates through the end of the year, continuing Google's pattern of aggressive early pricing to drive developer adoption.

---

## Release Cadence Strategy

The six-week gap between Flash releases reflects Google's strategy of maintaining mindshare against competing models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and other providers. Frequent updates allow Google to incorporate feedback quickly and capture developer attention amid a crowded model marketplace.

Google's Pro model updates have apparently paused, with the company focusing on the Flash tier for near-term releases.

---

## Context

The Flash series targets the cost-sensitive developer market, offering competitive capabilities at lower price points than premium models. Google's aggressive release schedule for Flash variants suggests the company sees the mid-tier market as critical for driving platform adoption.
