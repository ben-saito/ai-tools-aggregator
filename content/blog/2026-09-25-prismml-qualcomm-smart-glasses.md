# PrismML Brings Tiny LLMs to Qualcomm-Powered Smart Glasses

PrismML, an AI lab founded by Caltech researchers and advised by UC Berkeley's Ion Stoica, has partnered with Qualcomm to bring its ultra-compact language models to smart glasses. At Qualcomm's Snapdragon Summit, the company showcased its 1-bit Bonsai LLM running locally on smart glasses built on the Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1 Platform.

---

## From Bonsai 2 to Smart Glasses

Last week, PrismML released Bonsai 2 27B, a compressed version of Alibaba's Qwen3.8 27B that shrinks the model down to just 5.9 GB — a 9x to 10x reduction in memory footprint. Now the startup is demonstrating a version of Bonsai optimized for Qualcomm's AR1 chip, bringing on-device AI to a new class of wearable hardware.

PrismML's larger goal is open-weight AI that runs on devices, making better use of the computing power already embedded in consumer hardware. The startup positions this as an alternative to depending on proprietary AI labs and their increasing demand for compute infrastructure.

---

## Why This Matters for the Smart Glasses Race

The partnership represents a step toward practical on-device AI in wearables. Unlike cloud-based AI assistants, local models offer potential privacy benefits — voice queries and processing stay on the device. Qualcomm's AR1 platform is designed specifically for AI-powered smart glasses, and PrismML's models are engineered to run within the chip's constraints.

No smart glasses running PrismML have been announced yet, but the Snapdragon Summit demonstration suggests hardware partners could integrate Bonsai in future products. The chipmaker's willingness to showcase PrismML at its flagship event signals the strategic importance of compact, efficient AI models for edge devices.
