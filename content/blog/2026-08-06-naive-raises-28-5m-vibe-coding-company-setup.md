# Naïve Raises $28.5M to Automate Company Setup via Vibe Coding

Startup Naïve has closed a $28.5 million funding round to build infrastructure that automates the grunt work of setting up and running a company — taking the concept of vibe coding to its logical extreme.

---

## Vibe Coding Goes Corporate

Naïve's platform allows users to describe business workflows in natural language, with an AI agent handling the implementation. The company claims its infrastructure can automate most of the work involved in launching and operating a business, from setting up legal entities to configuring payroll systems.

The startup's approach goes beyond code generation: it aims to handle the administrative, legal, and operational tasks that traditionally require human judgment and manual execution. By combining large language models with workflow automation, Naïve intends to reduce the barrier to entry for starting a company.

---

## The Vibe Coding Spectrum

The Naïve announcement highlights the expanding scope of vibe coding tools. Where initial tools focused on helping developers write code faster, newer platforms aim to handle entire business processes — from legal compliance to financial reporting — through natural language interfaces.

The $28.5M round signals investor appetite for AI platforms that move beyond assisting developers and toward automating business operations end-to-end.
