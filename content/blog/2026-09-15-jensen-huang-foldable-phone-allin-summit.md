# Jensen Huang's Foldable Phone Moment at All-In Summit Steals the Show

When Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang took a live call from President Donald Trump during the All-In Summit in Los Angeles, attendees were watching not just the conversation about AI safety — but the device Huang used to answer it.

---

## The Foldable Phone Hand-off

Huang, never one to break from his signature alligator leather jacket, was handed a foldable phone to take the presidential call. Once Trump was on speaker, the conversation shifted to AI safety and the "AI slowdown" debate, with both agreeing that mounting fears about artificial intelligence equate to what Trump called a "hoax."

The moment drew attention because Huang has long been associated with Apple's iPhone, making the choice of device a subtle but notable signal. TechCrunch's original report incorrectly identified the device as Apple's iPhone Duo, but a correction noted the device appears to be a different foldable phone.

---

## The AI Safety Debate

During the call, Trump argued that "the real winners of the AI panic are China and political people," echoing talking points from AI industry leaders who have pushed back against calls to slow AI development. Huang, whose Nvidia GPU empire depends heavily on AI labs purchasing more chips, aligned with the president's position.

Whether Huang genuinely shares this view or is aligned by business necessity remains unclear. Nvidia's stock remains up 33% for the year, driven by insatiable demand for H100 and B100 GPU clusters from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Microsoft.

---

## A PR Decision Worth Noticing

The episode illustrates how personal electronics choices by tech executives can become de facto marketing moments. The brief handoff of a foldable phone — and the subsequent correction — generated nearly as much coverage as the substance of Huang's policy positions.
