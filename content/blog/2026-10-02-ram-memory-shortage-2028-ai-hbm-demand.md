# Memory Executives Expect RAM Shortage to Continue Through 2028

Leading memory industry executives have projected that the shortage of random access memory (RAM) will persist through 2028, driven by surging demand from artificial intelligence applications and constrained manufacturing capacity. The shortage has pushed prices higher and created bottlenecks for AI hardware development.

---

## Supply-Demand Dynamics

The AI boom has dramatically increased demand for high-bandwidth memory (HBM) chips, which are essential for training and running large language models. Memory manufacturers have struggled to increase production fast enough to meet demand, leading to extended lead times and price increases.

Executives from major memory companies indicated that pricing for memory allocated to 2027 deliveries is "much higher than 2026 prices." This has created cost pressures for AI developers seeking to scale their infrastructure.

## Impact on AI Development

The memory shortage is becoming a limiting factor in AI development. Cloud providers and AI labs are competing for available memory supplies, with some companies securing long-term supply agreements at premium prices. The constraint has also accelerated interest in memory efficiency improvements and alternative architectures.

## Industry Outlook

Memory manufacturers are investing in new fabrication facilities, but these expansions will not come online for several years. Until additional capacity arrives, the supply crunch is expected to continue, potentially affecting the pace of AI infrastructure deployment across the industry.
