# Meta Unveils Muse Charm: A Tamagotchi-Style Wearable for Its AI Agent

At Meta's annual Connect event held in Menlo Park on September 23, 2026, CEO Mark Zuckerberg revealed Muse Charm — a palm-sized, Tamagotchi-like wearable device that serves as a physical home for Meta's AI agent Muse. The device represents Meta's latest push to make its AI assistant ubiquitous across everyday life.

---

## A Physical Avatar for the Digital Agent

Muse, which launched in early September, is Meta's AI agent designed to handle everyday tasks by connecting to users' apps and services including email and calendars. The Muse agent is embodied by a cute digital avatar named Jolly inside the Charm device.

The physical totem fits in a user's pocket or attaches to a keychain, providing always-on access to the AI agent. "We packed the whole Muse experience, including the whole real-time voice and avatar stack, into something that fits on a keychain and is always available to talk to," Zuckerberg said during the keynote.

---

## Beyond Glasses: Faster Access to Muse

While Meta's AI smart glasses already provide a hands-free way to interact with Muse, Zuckerberg positioned Charm as a complementary device. "So if you're not wearing glasses, this is going to be by far the fastest way to talk to your Muse and to show what's going on around you," he explained.

The Charm device features a small digital display showing Jolly's animated reactions, giving the AI agent a visible personality that responds to conversations and surroundings.

---

## Revenue Ambitions: Making Money for Users

Zuckerberg also outlined ambitious plans for Muse to generate revenue for users. The agent's deep integration with calendars, email, and other personal data positions it as a potential digital assistant that could handle commercial transactions and coordination on behalf of users.

The device is not yet ready to ship. Zuckerberg noted that his team is still finalizing the industrial design and pricing details. A release date has not been announced.
