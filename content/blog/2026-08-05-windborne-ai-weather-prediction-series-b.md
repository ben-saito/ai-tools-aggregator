# WindBorne Raises $37M Series B to Scale AI-Powered Weather Prediction

*Weather balloon data combined with AI models could make weather forecasting significantly more accurate — and the startup is betting it can build a business around that capability*

---

## Overview

WindBorne Systems has raised a $37 million Series B round to scale its weather balloon network and AI-driven forecasting platform. The startup uses a constellation of weather balloons equipped with advanced sensors to collect atmospheric data, then applies machine learning models to generate weather predictions that the company claims are more accurate than conventional approaches.

---

## How WindBorne Works

Traditional weather forecasting relies on a combination of satellite imagery, ground-based weather stations, and radiosonde balloons launched twice daily from around 1,000 locations worldwide. WindBorne's approach augments this infrastructure by deploying a larger, continuously-updated network of smart weather balloons that can be repositioned to focus on specific weather events as they develop.

The AI component comes into play during data analysis and prediction generation. By feeding the balloon-derived atmospheric data into machine learning models, WindBorne says it can produce forecasts with improved accuracy, particularly for severe weather events where granular local data matters most.

---

## The Business Model Question

Weather forecasting has historically been a public good, with governments and international bodies funding the infrastructure. WindBorne is betting that commercial weather forecasting — particularly for industries like agriculture, energy, insurance, and logistics — can support a sustainable business.

The $37M Series B will go toward expanding the balloon network, hiring ML engineers and meteorologists, and developing commercial products for enterprise customers willing to pay a premium for more accurate local forecasts.

---

## AI + Climate Infrastructure

WindBorne represents a growing category of AI-for-climate startups that combine hardware (sensors, balloons, drones) with machine learning to address gaps in environmental monitoring. The intersection of AI and climate infrastructure has attracted increasing investor attention as climate change drives more extreme and unpredictable weather patterns.

The company is not the only player in AI weather prediction — companies like Tomorrow.io, ClimaSens, and Google DeepMind's weather team are all working in this space — but WindBorne's hardware-first approach with integrated AI processing differentiates it from purely software-based competitors.
