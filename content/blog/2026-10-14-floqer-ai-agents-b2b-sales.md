# Floqer Pitches AI Agent Platform That Autonomously Researches B2B Sales Prospects

---

Floqer, an autonomous customer knowledge platform for B2B sales and marketing teams, presented at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026's Startup Battlefield. The startup has built an AI-powered database that continuously collects, organizes, and updates customer information — aiming to eliminate the manual research work that typically consumes sales teams. Floqer also announced plans to open-source its core technology in early 2027, pending security reviews.

---

## From Chrome Extension to Autonomous Agent Platform

Floqer was founded by Shivam Mahajan (CEO) and Zaahera Islam, who met at university. Before Floqer, Mahajan worked as a software engineer at Kyndryl, and Islam was a senior data scientist at Axis Capital.

The company's first product was a Chrome extension called "Super Chat," which allowed users to log in with their ChatGPT account to understand information and take notes. Enterprise companies began using the extension to clean up data in their CRM systems, revealing a significant pain point: keeping CRM data current was largely manual work with few established processes.

"We started with a Chrome extension," Mahajan told TechCrunch. "Anybody can go and log in with their ChatGPT account and then understand information and take notes." The founders noticed enterprise customers were using it for CRM data cleaning, which led them to pivot toward building Floqer.

---

## How Floqer's AI Agents Work

Floqer's core product is a fleet of AI agents that continuously research companies, people, and custom data targets. Users describe what they are looking for, and the system searches across multiple sources to build lists of relevant companies and contacts.

The platform enriches accounts — adding detail about target companies — using more than 100 third-party data sources. When a verified email address or phone number is needed, Floqer uses a "waterfall" approach, checking data providers sequentially until it finds usable information.

A key differentiator is continuous monitoring. If a prospect changes jobs, gets promoted, or their company raises new funding, those updates are reflected in the user's customer records automatically.

---

## Traction and Open-Source Plans

Floqer says it has surpassed $1.2 million in annual recurring revenue (ARR), with overall revenue growing roughly tenfold over the past year. The startup plans to open-source its core technology in early 2027, a move that could accelerate adoption among companies that want to inspect and customize the underlying agent systems.

The company is positioning the open-source release as a commitment to transparency and customer trust, particularly important in an era where AI systems are increasingly asked to handle sensitive business data.

---

## The B2B Sales Intelligence Landscape

Floqer enters a competitive market for sales intelligence tools, which includes established players like ZoomInfo, Clearbit, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator. The company's differentiated bet is on autonomous, continuous research — as opposed to periodic data refreshes — and an agent architecture that can scale research tasks without human intervention.

Whether the open-source announcement signals a shift toward community-driven development or a response to competitive pressure remains to be seen, but the startup's ARR traction suggests there is genuine demand for its approach.
