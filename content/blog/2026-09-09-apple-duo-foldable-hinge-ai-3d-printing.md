# Apple Duo Foldable Hinge Built with AI and 3D Printing

---

Apple has revealed that the hinge in its first foldable phone, the **iPhone Duo**, was designed and manufactured using AI algorithms and 3D printing technology — a precision manufacturing approach the company says is key to the device's durability.

---

## AI-Matched Hinge Assembly

During the manufacturing process, Apple uses AI algorithms to precisely match each individual hinge with its best-fit housing, ensuring perfect alignment for every unit, according to Johny Srouji, Apple's chief hardware officer. A confocal laser then progressively scans the topology of every single device, and up to 25 micro layers of a custom photopolymer are 3D printed to eliminate residual waviness in the hinge mechanism.

---

## Durability Focus for Foldable Form Factor

Foldable phones typically undergo twice as much mechanical stress as traditional smartphones, making durability a key engineering challenge. Apple claims it has integrated protective measures into the Duo to address this — including the AI-optimized hinge system, a custom nano-texture finish that reduces glare and reflections, and a multilayer lamination strategy designed to increase overall device longevity.

---

## Apple Enters the Foldable Market

The iPhone Duo represents Apple's first entry into the foldable phone category, a segment that has seen significant consumer interest but inconsistent product reliability. Apple's approach — applying its on-device AI and chip design expertise to precision manufacturing — positions the Duo as a durability-focused alternative in a market where mechanical failure has been a recurring issue.
