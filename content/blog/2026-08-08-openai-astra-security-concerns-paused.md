# OpenAI Pauses Astra Model Development Over Cybersecurity Concerns

OpenAI said on August 7, 2026 that it has suspended work on some aspects of its upcoming Astra model after an internal review found the model had made significant advancements in agentic coding and cybersecurity capabilities.

---

## Critical Cybersecurity Threshold Exceeded

In a blog post on August 7, OpenAI explained that the model, still in development, reached its "critical cybersecurity threshold" — meaning it could independently identify and carry out cyberattacks against traditionally well-protected real-world systems. Under the company's 2023 Preparedness Framework, crossing this threshold automatically triggers additional safeguards.

OpenAI stated that the advancement in agentic coding and cybersecurity was substantial enough to warrant concern, prompting the suspension of certain development activities.

---

## Agentic Coding and Offensive Cybersecurity Capabilities

The Astra model had achieved progress in two key areas: agentic coding (AI systems that autonomously write and execute code) and offensive cybersecurity (the ability to identify and exploit vulnerabilities). These capabilities were among the most advanced seen in any OpenAI model to date, according to the company's internal metrics.

The company did not specify which aspects of the model were suspended, only that work on "some aspects" had been paused pending further safety evaluation.

---

## Separate from Astra Math Problems Achievement

The security-related pause is distinct from the Astra model milestone reported on August 4 (Import AI #467), in which an internal version solved ten open problems in mathematics. That version appears to be a different build or development track focused on mathematical reasoning, while the suspended work relates specifically to agentic coding and cybersecurity capabilities.

OpenAI has not disclosed a timeline for resuming development.
