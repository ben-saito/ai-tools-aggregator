# Meta Launches an Open-Weight AI Model That Runs on Macs

---

Meta has launched what it calls the "most capable" open-weight model in the United States. Dubbed Muse Spark Open, the model also has the distinction of having been designed to work with Macs. The new model, which leverages 816 billion parameters and a 1 million token context window, claims to offer superior performance to other popular open-weight models, according to evaluation results released by Meta on Thursday.

Muse Spark Open is natively multimodal and built for complex coding tasks and agentic labor. It has also been designed from the ground up to run on Apple's silicon, with specific optimizations for the Neural Engine and unified memory architecture of recent Mac chips.

The model represents Meta's latest move in the open-weight AI space, positioning itself against proprietary models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google while offering the flexibility of open-weight deployment. Developers can run the model locally on Mac hardware without relying on cloud-based inference services.

The 816 billion parameter count puts Muse Spark Open in the frontier-class category, though the actual performance characteristics depend heavily on the hardware and inference optimization. Apple's Neural Engine provides dedicated acceleration for matrix operations common in transformer models, potentially offering efficient inference for local deployment scenarios.
