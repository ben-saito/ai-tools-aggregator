# Instinct and Meta's Muse AI Agents Both Gain Phone Calling Capabilities

Rival AI agents Instinct and Meta's Muse have both added the ability to place phone calls, enabling their assistants to act as concierge services for tasks like restaurant reservations and canceling subscriptions.

---

## Instinct Concierge Launches

Instinct, the AI assistant valued at $2.5 billion, has launched Instinct Concierge -- a new calling feature that allows the agent to place phone calls on behalf of users. Founder Noah Shinn announced the feature as "a white glove service meant to handle high-touch cases, such as making phone calls."

The calling capability follows Instinct's recent rollout of email addresses that let the agent handle account sign-up and management. These features are part of Instinct's broader push toward fully autonomous agents that can complete real-world tasks without human intervention.

---

## Meta Muse Catches Up

Meta's Muse AI assistant is keeping pace, having also gained the ability to place calls to U.S. businesses. The company expanded its beta for outbound calls to U.S. businesses, prioritizing users who had previously requested the feature.

The timing is significant: a number of rivals had been promoting their calling capabilities as a competitive advantage over Instinct. With Instinct's Concierge launch and Muse's simultaneous rollout, the two agents are now on equal footing for phone-based task completion.

---

## Race Toward Autonomous Agents

The addition of calling capabilities represents a key milestone in the race toward truly autonomous AI agents. By enabling agents to interact via phone -- one of the most ubiquitous real-world communication channels -- companies like Instinct and Meta are demonstrating that AI assistants can now handle tasks that traditionally required human phone etiquette and negotiation skills.

For developers, these calling features open new possibilities for building AI-powered customer service, scheduling, and concierge applications that can operate independently of human oversight.

---

## Background

Instinct previously made headlines for receiving its own email addresses, allowing the agent to create and manage accounts without filling users' inboxes. The company has been rapidly expanding its capabilities, positioning itself as a leading platform for AI agent development.
