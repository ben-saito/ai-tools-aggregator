# DOJ Investigation of a16z: What It Means for Venture Capital

On August 21, 2026, TechCrunch's Equity podcast discussed the US Department of Justice's antitrust investigation into Andreessen Horowitz (a16z). The probe centers on a conflict of interest: a16z partners Ben Horowitz (Databricks) and Martin Casado (Fivetran) sit on boards of competing companies. The DOJ has been investigating the arrangement for nearly a year, potentially invoking a 112-year-old antitrust law rarely used against VCs.

The Clayton Act Section 8 prohibits individuals from serving on boards of competing companies simultaneously. As portfolio companies expand into each other's markets, the DOJ's scrutiny raises questions about how VCs manage board seats when boundaries between investments blur.

The podcast also addressed the broader AI startup investment landscape, noting that companies outside Anthropic, OpenAI, and Nvidia face a tightening funding environment as VCs navigate regulatory scrutiny.
