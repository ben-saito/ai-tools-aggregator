# Amazon to Train AI on Twitch Streamers' Content by Default—Unless They Opt Out

Amazon will begin training its AI models on Twitch content by default, requiring streamers to manually opt out if they do not want their content used. Twitch Chief Product Officer Mike Minton acknowledged the decision on a live stream, saying "If this was opt-in, nobody would opt in. That's honestly the answer."

---

## Why Amazon Chose Opt-Out

Twitch's parent company Amazon has access to thousands of hours of audio and video recordings from the streaming platform. For Amazon, this represents a massive dataset for training generative AI models. The opt-out model ensures maximum data collection with minimal user action.

On a Twitch livestream, Minton and Head of Community Mary Kish addressed nearly 3,000 streamers who had expressed frustration. The response revealed Amazon's calculus: an opt-in system would result in near-zero participation, while opt-out shifts the friction to users who must actively protect their rights.

---

## Streamers' Concerns

The Twitch community has shown strong opposition to generative AI training on user content. The most prevalent AI products—trained on books, images, videos, and other creative works—have faced legal challenges from creators. Streamers worry that their content, often featuring copyrighted music, gameplay, and personal performances, could be used without compensation or consent.

Some streamers expressed confusion about whether their past content had already been used for AI training. Twitch did not clarify the timeline of when Amazon's AI training on Twitch content began or would begin.

---

## Industry Context

Twitch is not unique in using public user content for AI training. Meta, for example, uses public content from Facebook and Instagram to train its own AI systems. The practice has sparked debates about intellectual property, creator rights, and the ethics of AI training data.

Amazon's move signals the continued expansion of large-scale AI data collection into live streaming platforms, where content is generated continuously and spans a wide range of topics, languages, and formats.

---

## What Streamers Can Do

Streamers who wish to opt out must manually adjust their account settings. Amazon has not disclosed the specific steps required, but Twitch has committed to providing an opt-out mechanism. Creators are advised to review their account settings and monitor Twitch's official announcements for detailed instructions.
