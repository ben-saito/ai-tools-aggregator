# OpenAI Head of Product Discusses Agents, UX, and ChatGPT Work

Thibault Sottiaux, OpenAI's head of product for Codex and ChatGPT Work, discussed the company's vision for AI agents in a recent TechCrunch interview. Users of OpenAI's software engineering tool Codex know Sottiaux as the person who resets token limits when the product hits growth milestones.

OpenAI is now extending that approach to a broader audience through ChatGPT Work, a platform designed to bring AI agent capabilities to white-collar workers beyond developers.

Sottiaux discussed several key themes in the interview:

**Discovery as product philosophy**: Rather than requiring users to know exactly what they need, ChatGPT Work is designed around discovery — surfacing capabilities users did not know they wanted. This mirrors how Codex grew through engineers stumbling onto use cases.

**Cost of intelligence**: The interview addressed the economics of running AI agents at scale, including token consumption and the balance between capability and cost for enterprise deployments.

**Winning over skeptics**: Sottiaux outlined OpenAI's approach to convincing skeptical enterprise buyers who have heard AI promises before and been disappointed.

The conversation provided insight into how OpenAI thinks about the transition from experimental AI tools to enterprise-grade agent platforms that workers actually use daily.
