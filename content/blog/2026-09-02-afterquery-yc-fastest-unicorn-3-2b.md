# AfterQuery Reportedly Becomes Y Combinator's Fastest-Ever Unicorn at $3.2B Valuation

AI model-training startup AfterQuery has reportedly raised a round that valued it at $3.2 billion, just five months after announcing its $30 million Series A at a $300 million valuation in April.

That's more than a 10x increase in less than half a year and, according to Y Combinator partner Gustaf Alstromer, the fastest that any startup has gone from launch to unicorn status in the accelerator's history. AfterQuery's founders, today 22 and 23 years old, attended Y Combinator's Winter 2025 cohort.

The startup focuses on AI model training infrastructure, an area that has seen significant investor interest as enterprises race to build custom AI systems. AfterQuery's rapid ascent reflects the intense demand for infrastructure that can handle large-scale AI model training and deployment.

Y Combinator's track record includes supporting early-stage companies that have become major players in the AI ecosystem. AfterQuery's achievement marks a new milestone for the accelerator, demonstrating how AI infrastructure companies can achieve rapid growth in the current market environment.

The valuation jump from $300 million to $3.2 billion in five months underscores the premium investors are placing on companies that provide critical infrastructure for AI development. As organizations increasingly build proprietary AI systems, demand for specialized training infrastructure continues to grow substantially.
