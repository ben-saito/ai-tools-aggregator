# YouTube Music launches new labels as AI-generated music floods streaming platforms

---

YouTube Music announced Wednesday the rollout of a new labeling system aimed at improving transparency around songs created with the help of generative AI. The new labels will appear on track pages and in search results, distinguishing between human-created content and AI-assisted works.

The move comes as streaming platforms face increasing pressure from artists, labels, and legislators to address the proliferation of AI-generated music. Unlike traditional covers or remixes, AI-generated tracks can mimic specific artists' voices and styles with minimal human input, raising thorny questions about copyright and fair compensation.

YouTube Music's approach focuses on disclosure rather than outright blocking. Tracks that use any AI generation in their creation process — from vocal synthesis to instrumental composition — must be labeled accordingly. The company says it developed the system in consultation with major labels and artist advocacy groups.

The labeling system builds on YouTube's existing Content ID infrastructure, which already handles copyright claims for sampled material. However, AI-generated music that doesn't directly sample existing copyrighted works falls outside Content ID's scope, necessitating a separate disclosure mechanism.

Deezer announced a similar but more aggressive approach: a filter that lets users exclude AI-generated content entirely from their listening experience. Together, these initiatives represent the industry's first systematic attempts to create norms around AI music disclosure.
