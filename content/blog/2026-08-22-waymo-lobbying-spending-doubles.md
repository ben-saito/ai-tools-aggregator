# Waymo Doubles Lobbying Spending in Robotaxi Battle Against Uber

On August 21, 2026, Ars Technica reported that Alphabet-owned Waymo spent more than $1 million on federal lobbying between April and June 2026, more than double its outlay from the previous year. The spending puts Waymo close to Uber's lobbying budget and well ahead of rivals including Amazon's Zoox and Tesla.

The increase comes as Waymo and Uber push competing visions for ride-hailing's future. Waymo advocates for a faster route to fully driverless commercial services, while Uber wants a staggered rollout where robotaxis operate alongside human drivers.

Waymo has called for a federal framework to ease robotaxi rollout. In May, it hired law firm Greenberg Traurig to lobby on its behalf, adding to several other Washington firms. The company has explored exiting its Uber partnership in Austin and Atlanta.

In the first six months of 2026, Waymo increased its lobbying spending by 93 percent to slightly more than $2 million.
