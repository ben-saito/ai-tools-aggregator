# Grok Keeps Sending Gibberish Responses to Users

Grok, xAI's conversational AI assistant, has been producing nonsensical or incoherent responses for a subset of users — and affected users say the problem has persisted for days.

Users reporting the issue told TechCrunch they were using Grok Lite and noticed the degraded responses as early as Wednesday morning. The gibberish responses varied: some produced grammatically structured but semantically meaningless text, others produced text that appeared to be a mix of unrelated phrases with no coherent thread.

xAI has not publicly acknowledged the issue, and the cause remains unclear. Possible explanations include a model regression, a data pipeline issue affecting training or inference, or a prompt injection attack exploiting Grok's more open design philosophy compared to competitors.

Grok has positioned itself as the "anti-woke" alternative AI — more willing to discuss sensitive topics and less filtered than comparable models from OpenAI or Anthropic. That openness has also made it more susceptible to adversarial inputs that some researchers believe could cause model confusion or degraded output quality.

The incident highlights a persistent challenge for AI companies: model quality can degrade unexpectedly as systems are updated, retrained, or exposed to novel inputs. Unlike a software bug that causes a crash, a model producing plausible-sounding but incoherent text can be harder to detect — and more damaging to user trust.

xAI is reportedly investigating the issue.
