# Meta Launches Muse Spark Open: Open-Weight AI Optimized for Mac Hardware

---

Meta has launched Muse Spark Open, an open-weight AI model specifically designed to run natively on Apple silicon Macs. The model represents a significant push by Meta into the open-weight AI space, with optimizations targeting Metal Performance Shaders and Apple's Neural Engine.

## Key Capabilities

Muse Spark Open features 816 billion parameters with a 1 million token context window, making it one of the most capable open-weight models available in the United States. The model is natively multimodal and built for complex coding tasks and agentic workflows.

According to Meta, the model pairs with Muse Code, its AI coding terminal, and has been deployed in various agentic workflows. One demonstration showed the model handling coding fixes for a Mastodon client, including "bug fixes, new features, [and] full build." Another deployment revealed the model's capability to review and crop footage in DaVinci Resolve, high-end video editing software.

## Mac Hardware Requirements

Running Muse Spark Open locally requires substantial hardware resources: a minimum of 1 terabyte of RAM is needed. The model performs best on Mac Studio equipped with the M5 Ultra chip, where the Metal Performance Shaders and Neural Engine optimizations deliver full performance.

## Open-Weight Model Advantages

Open-weight models like Muse Spark Open offer distinct advantages over closed, proprietary systems. They can be significantly more cost-efficient for organizations running their own inference infrastructure. They also provide transparency and flexibility that closed-source models don't allow, enabling researchers and developers to inspect, modify, and fine-tune model weights.

Meta is making Muse Spark Open available through multiple API platforms, with the model positioning itself as a capable alternative for developers seeking to run powerful AI on personal workstations.
