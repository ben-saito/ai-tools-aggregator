# AI Hedge Fund Situational Awareness Winds Down Public Portfolio, Keeps Anthropic Stakes

OpenAI former researcher-founded AI-focused hedge fund Situational Awareness has been forced to unwind its public equity positions after leveraged bets plummeted. However, the fund continues to hold its Anthropic shares, according to sources familiar with the matter.

---

## Forced Liquidation of Public Equities

Situational Awareness pursued a strategy centered entirely on AI-related investments. The fund used significant leverage in its public market positions, a strategy that backfired as AI sector volatility increased in recent months. The forced liquidation affected only public equities; private holdings remain intact.

---

## Anthropic Stakes Preserved

Unlike public stocks, the fund's Anthropic holdings were not included in the liquidation. Sources indicate the stake represents a small portion of the fund's overall portfolio but carries significant value given Anthropic's positioning as a leading AI safety company with an estimated valuation exceeding $47 billion following its latest funding round.

---

## Risk Management in AI Investing

The situation highlights the distinct risk profiles of public versus private AI investments. Public AI stocks offer liquidity but come with higher volatility and leverage opportunities. Private AI companies, particularly frontier labs, provide less liquidity but potentially greater long-term value creation. The divergence in outcomes for public and private positions underscores the importance of differentiated risk management approaches in AI-focused investing.

---

## Market Context

Situational Awareness was founded by a former OpenAI researcher with the goal of applying rigorous technical analysis to AI sector investments. The fund's approach combined deep ML expertise with traditional quantitative methods, attracting capital from investors seeking exposure to the AI transformation through a research-driven vehicle.
