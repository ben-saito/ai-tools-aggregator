# Adobe Acquires Indian Market Intelligence Startup Rilo

Adobe has acquired Rilo, an Indian market intelligence startup, marking the company's second acquisition out of India following its purchase of AI video generation startup Rephrase.ai in 2023. Financial terms of the deal were not disclosed.

Rilo specialized in providing AI-powered market intelligence and competitive analysis tools, enabling enterprises to aggregate and analyze market data from multiple sources including public records, news feeds, and industry databases. The startup's technology complements Adobe's existing AI capabilities in content creation and customer experience management.

The acquisition reflects Adobe's continued push to expand its AI portfolio through strategic acquisitions, particularly in markets outside the United States where local expertise and data sources provide competitive advantages that global platforms cannot easily replicate.

---

## Adobe's India Strategy

India has become an important market for Adobe's acquisition strategy, with both Rephrase.ai and Rilo bringing capabilities in AI video generation and market intelligence respectively. Rephrase.ai, acquired in 2023, provided Adobe with AI video generation technology that has since been integrated into Adobe's Creative Cloud suite, enabling users to generate and edit video content using text prompts.

Rilo's market intelligence capabilities are expected to enhance Adobe's Experience Cloud platform by providing enterprises with better tools for understanding competitive landscapes, customer sentiment, and market trends. The combination of content creation tools with market intelligence creates a more comprehensive platform for enterprise marketing and customer experience teams.

---

## Market Context

The acquisition comes as Adobe faces increasing competition from AI-native content creation tools that challenge its traditional market leadership. OpenAI's Sora, Runway's video generation platform, and other AI video tools have created new competition in a market where Adobe has historically dominated through professional creative software.

By acquiring specialized AI startups in key markets, Adobe is pursuing a strategy of targeted acquisitions to fill capability gaps rather than attempting to build all AI functionality internally. The company's acquisition pipeline has been particularly active in India, where the combination of strong engineering talent and relatively lower acquisition valuations makes strategic acquisitions attractive.

---

## Competitive Landscape

Adobe competes with Salesforce, Oracle, and other enterprise software companies in the customer experience management market, where AI-powered personalization and content generation are increasingly table stakes. The integration of Rilo's market intelligence into Adobe's Experience Cloud could provide customers with more comprehensive tools for data-driven content strategy.
