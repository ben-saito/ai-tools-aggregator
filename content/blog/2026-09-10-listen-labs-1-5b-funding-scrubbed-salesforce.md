# Listen Labs Scrubs $1.5B Funding Round as Salesforce Acquisition Talks Heat Up

AI research startup Listen Labs has walked away from a signed Series C term sheet with Menlo Ventures worth $1.5 billion, sources tell TechCrunch. The financing collapse is believed to be tied to concurrent acquisition discussions with Salesforce, which has been in talks to buy Listen Labs for approximately $2 billion.

---

## A Rare Walkaway from a Signed Term Sheet

In the venture capital world, walking away from a signed term sheet is rare and generally frowned upon. Sources say Listen Labs made the decision to abandon the Menlo Ventures round after acquisition talks with Salesforce accelerated. The move underscores how AI startups are increasingly caught between the pressures of staying independent and being acquired by larger tech giants.

---

## Salesforce's AI Acquisition Strategy

The CRM giant has been actively expanding its AI capabilities through acquisitions. Reports indicate Salesforce views Listen Labs as a strategic addition that could enhance its customer data platform with AI-powered predictive analytics. The $2 billion price tag represents a significant premium, but Salesforce appears willing to pay to accelerate its AI roadmap.

---

## Listen Labs' Market Position

Listen Labs is one of the leading startups in automating customer research with AI. Founded in 2023, the three-year-old company has grown to approximately $30 million in annualized revenue — roughly three times the industry average for companies at a similar stage. The company was co-founded by Florian Jüngermann, a former German national champion in competitive computer programming, and Alfred Wahlforss, who previously founded a staffing startup.

---

## What Happens Next

If the Salesforce acquisition talks collapse, several venture capitalists expect Listen Labs to return to market and target a valuation of $2 billion or higher. The company has demonstrated strong revenue growth and a differentiated technology stack in the competitive AI customer research space. Both Listen Labs and Salesforce declined to comment on the ongoing discussions.
