# Anthropic and OpenAI to Appear Together at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026

Anthropic and OpenAI have both confirmed appearances at the AI Stage during TechCrunch Disrupt 2026. Presented by Google for Startups, the stage will focus on the hottest topics in the tech community, bringing together leading industry players.

---

## Industry Leaders on One Stage

The TechCrunch Disrupt AI Stage has become one of the most-watched discussions in the generative AI era. This year, both Anthropic and OpenAI will appear together, discussing the future of AI through panel discussions and Q&A sessions.

CEOs and key executives from both companies are expected to address AI safety, regulation, research direction, and more.

---

## Significance of Industry Collaboration

The two rival companies sharing a stage signals a push for broader consensus on AI safety and responsible development across the industry. Direct dialogue between leaders may also send strong messages to policymakers.
