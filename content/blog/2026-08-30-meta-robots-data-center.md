# Meta Inserts Robots into Data Centers -- Human Work Being Replaced by Robots

Meta is pursuing the introduction of robots inside data centers. The company is acquiring robots from multiple vendors including Watney Robotics, Kinova, and ABB, and is working to automate data center operations.

In current experiments, Meta is using Kinova Gen3 robot arms to test power cycling of servers and also testing different robots to swap networking cables. One data center worker estimates that if successful, robots could replace up to 80 percent of some people's workloads.

---

## Background of Robot Introduction

In the data center industry, robots have historically been unsuitable due to expensive hardware costs. Earlier industry trials sometimes crushed servers while attempting simple tasks. However, AI models powering robots have become far more capable, and multiple startups are now working on commercial robots capable of assembling servers, swapping parts, and replacing wires.

---

## Impact on Workers

Among data center workers, the prospect of robots replacing at least some jobs has become a realistic concern. In Altoona, Iowa, where Meta operates its largest data center campus, worker group chats have taken on a demoralized tone, with discussions about being replaced by robots.

Meta states it is seeking workers capable of executing AI instructions rather than those who can think independently and perform complex troubleshooting. Workers report that Meta is developing AI software that may allow hiring lower-skilled, lower-paid workers and centralizing some roles in more financially advantageous areas.

Meta contends its need for skilled workers remains robust, having launched a program to train thousands of people annually in electrical, mechanical, and plumbing work at no cost, with guaranteed employment in states including Louisiana, Ohio, Indiana, and Texas.

---

## Global Trend

Microsoft, Google, and Amazon are also investing in data center robots. Amazon is exploring using robots to help recycle parts at its facilities. In valuable data centers, underwater, in space, or in environments potentially inhospitable to humans are being considered as future deployment locations.
