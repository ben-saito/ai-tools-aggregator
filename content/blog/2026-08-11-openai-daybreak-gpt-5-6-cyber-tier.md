# OpenAI Expands Daybreak Cyber Defense: GPT-5.6-Cyber Now Available Under Red Tier

OpenAI has expanded its cybersecurity defense program Daybreak, launching a new tiered structure that includes access to a dedicated cyber-trained model, GPT-5.6-Cyber.

Daybreak, initially released earlier this year, now consists of two tiers: Blue and Red. Both provide approved customers with access to OpenAI's limited-access frontier cyber models, which have previously been subject to strict usage guardrails.

Blue serves as the entry-level tier, offering incident response, malware analysis, and patch validation services. OpenAI describes it as "the recommended starting point for most defenders."

Red is the higher tier, granting access to purpose-trained cybersecurity models designed for security testing and vulnerability research. This tier also includes GPT-5.6-Cyber, built on GPT-5.6 Sol, which offers enhanced capabilities for specialized cybersecurity tasks. Initial trusted partners include Accenture, IBM, Crowdstrike, and Cloudflare.

---

## AI-Powered Attacks on the Rise

The expansion comes as AI agents are increasingly being used to conduct cyberattacks. Recent incidents include AI systems compromising Hugging Face infrastructure and autonomously exploiting vulnerabilities at scale. Critics note that such incidents simultaneously serve as marketing opportunities for AI labs offering defensive products.
