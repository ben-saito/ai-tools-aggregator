Jev, a non-text AI model developed by TypeSafe AI, has been valued at $7.5 billion just weeks after its launch, according to TechCrunch. The model gained rapid popularity for its ability to handle multimodal inputs beyond text, attracting significant investor interest in a short timeframe.

TypeSafe AI, the startup behind Jev, raised capital following the model's launch, with investors betting on the next generation of AI infrastructure that extends beyond Large Language Models. The speed of the valuation increase reflects the intense competition in the AI infrastructure space.

The AI model market has seen a wave of new entrants targeting specialized use cases, with Jev positioning itself as a platform for developers building next-generation AI applications. The company's approach focuses on enabling AI systems to process and generate content across multiple modalities.
