# AI Hardware Crunch: Memory Execs Expect RAM Shortage Through 2028

Micron and Samsung executives this week indicated that the memory shortage will persist for at least two more years, with implications for both AI development and consumer devices. Micron CEO Sanjay Mehrotra told investors that demand for the company's memory products will exceed available supply through at least 2028.

---

## HBM and AI Compute

Mehrotra's statements refer specifically to Micron's business-to-business sales of high-bandwidth memory (HBM) for AI accelerators and DRAM for server applications. HBM has become a critical component in training large language models, as the high bandwidth enables faster data movement between AI chips and memory.

The constrained supply of HBM is a bottleneck for AI compute expansion. Major AI labs and cloud providers are competing for available HBM allocation, driving up costs for AI infrastructure.

Manufacturing capacity is being prioritized for memory destined for AI and server applications, limiting supply of memory manufactured for consumer devices. This reallocation reflects the premium pricing that AI customers can pay compared to consumer markets.

---

## Impact Beyond AI

The memory shortage extends to consumer devices, where DRAM supply is tightening. Consumer RAM prices have already begun rising as manufacturers shift production toward higher-margin AI-oriented products.

For AI developers, the shortage means continued pressure on compute costs. HBM scarcity constrains the number of AI accelerators that can be deployed in parallel for training runs, potentially slowing the pace of frontier model development.

---

## Supply Chain Dynamics

Samsung and SK Hynix are also struggling to meet demand, creating a three-way constraint across the major memory manufacturers. The concentration of HBM production among a small number of players makes the supply chain vulnerable to disruption.

Industry analysts expect memory contract prices for 2027 to be significantly higher than 2026 levels, reflecting the persistent supply-demand imbalance. AI Hyperscalers are securing long-term supply agreements to guarantee allocation, potentially locking out smaller players.

---

## Investment Implications

The memory shortage is driving increased capital expenditure among memory manufacturers. Micron is accelerating its HBM expansion plans, while Samsung is investing in new fabrication lines. However, meaningful capacity additions are 18-24 months away, meaning tight supply will persist through 2027.
