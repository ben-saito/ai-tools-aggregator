# Former Plaud Exec Raises $26M for AI App That Listens to You in the Background

---

One of the core promises of AI is that the more it knows about a person, the better it will be able to assist them with tasks and make appropriate suggestions — which, if you'll believe the tech companies, requires apps and devices to listen, watch, or record users' activity all the time and gather all possible context.

Former Plaud executive has raised $26 million in seed funding for a new AI app that runs continuously in the background of users' devices, capturing audio and generating summaries, insights, and task reminders based on what it hears.

## The Always-Listening AI Assistant

The app is designed to be always-on — capturing ambient audio from the user's environment and using that data to build a persistent model of the user's life, meetings, and conversations.

The pitch is essentially: an AI that was at every meeting you forgot to take notes at, heard every phone call where a commitment was made, and was present for every conversation where important information was shared but not recorded anywhere else.

## Privacy Architecture

The company claims end-to-end encryption and on-device processing for sensitive audio, with only anonymized insights sent to the cloud for processing. Users own their data and can delete it at any time.

The $26 million seed round signals investor appetite for AI apps that push the boundary of what's considered acceptable in terms of data collection, betting that a segment of users will trade privacy for a genuinely useful AI that knows their full context.
