# General Catalyst Leads $1.1 Billion Round into Two-Month-Old River AI

---

## xAI Co-Founder's New Venture Secures Massive Early Funding

River AI, an AI startup founded by Igor Babuschkin — co-founder of xAI — has secured $1.1 billion in funding in a seed/Series A round led by General Catalyst and AMP PBC, with participation from Nvidia, AMD Ventures, Y Combinator, and Temasek. The round values the company at a substantial multiple of its two-month age, underscoring investor enthusiasm for the next generation of AI infrastructure companies.

The speed and size of the raise is notable: River AI has existed for only two months, yet has already attracted some of the most prominent names in venture capital and strategic AI hardware suppliers.

---

## A Vision for Personal AI Agents

River AI's founding vision centers on the development of personal AI agents that function as what Babuschkin describes as "guardian angels" — quietly present, on your side, helping with what actually matters to you. The company's thesis is that capable AI agents will become a normal part of everyday life, fundamentally changing how individuals interact with technology.

In a launch blog post, Babuschkin articulated the company's core philosophy: "To get there, we believe the stack has to be rebuilt end to end: training, models, the product layer, and new hardware that lets personal AI live close to you."

---

## River's First Product: Fine-Tuning API for Enterprise

River has already launched an API product, billed per 1 million tokens with rates dependent on the open model used. The API allows developers to leverage both reinforcement learning (RL) and low-rank adaptation (LoRA) fine-tuning on open models. This approach is designed as what the company calls an "antidote to prompt engineering," with the philosophy that "prompting steers a model you don't own and can't improve."

The product enables enterprises to take open-weight models and train them into instances that are "truly yours — and serve them like any other endpoint," according to River's product literature. This positions River as a post-training infrastructure provider for organizations seeking to customize open-source AI models for their specific needs.

---

## Timing and Market Context

While the $1.1 billion raise may seem excessive for a two-month-old company, it reflects the current overheated atmosphere around AI infrastructure investments. Enterprises are increasingly seeking to control their AI model destinies by using a mix of models, including open-weight options. River is positioned to address the post-training expertise gap with its neocloud offering.

AMP PBC, one of the lead investors, was founded in 2026 by Anjney Midha, a former Andreessen Horowitz general partner who was instrumental in backing companies including Black Forest Labs, Mistral AI, LMArena, and OpenRouter. The involvement of such prominent AI-focused investors signals strong institutional confidence in River's approach.
