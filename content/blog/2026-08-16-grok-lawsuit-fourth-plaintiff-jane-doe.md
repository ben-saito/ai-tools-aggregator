# Fourth Plaintiff Joins Lawsuit Against xAI Over Grok-Facilitated CSAM

*A woman identified as Jane Doe 4 has joined a lawsuit against Elon Musk's xAI, alleging the company's chatbot Grok was used to generate more than 7,000 explicit images of her from a childhood photograph. She is the fourth plaintiff in a case that includes three Tennessee teenagers.*

---

## Fourth Plaintiff Alleges 7,000 Images Generated From Childhood Photo

A woman identified as Jane Doe 4 has joined an existing lawsuit against xAI, the AI company owned by Elon Musk, according to a report by The Washington Post. The woman alleges that her stepfather used Grok to manipulate a photo taken when she was 11 years old, generating more than 7,000 explicit images of her.

The stepfather was found dead of suicide two days after the images were discovered, the lawsuit states.

The woman's claim is the latest development in a case originally filed by three Tennessee teenagers against xAI, which is now part of SpaceX following the company's merger with Musk's aerospace conglomerate. The teenagers allege that xAI failed to take basic precautions to prevent Grok from being used to create explicit images of real people, including minors.

"Limitless access to these tools is spreading so quickly," the woman said in a statement. "It is taking everyday life and turning it into child sexual abuse."

---

## xAI's Spicy Mode and Safety Bypass Concerns

xAI launched Grok with a "spicy" mode that offered fewer safety restrictions than standard AI assistants, a feature that has drawn sustained criticism as users repeatedly found ways to bypass the chatbot's safeguards.

The lawsuit follows a pattern of enforcement actions against Grok users, including criminal arrests and civil cases. xAI has pursued an aggressive legal strategy, filing lawsuits against alleged users who circumvented Grok's protections, attempting to establish legal precedent that the company cannot be held liable for user-generated content that bypasses its safeguards.

---

## SpaceX Merger Complicates Liability Questions

The case takes on added complexity as xAI is now formally part of SpaceX following the merger completed earlier this year. Legal experts have questioned whether the SpaceX merger affects the liability exposure of the combined entity and whether existing lawsuits against xAI can proceed against its new parent company.

The case is being watched closely as the first major test of whether AI companies can be held structurally responsible for tools that facilitate the generation of CSAM, rather than merely the content itself.
