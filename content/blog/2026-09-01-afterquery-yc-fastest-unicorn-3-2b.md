# AfterQuery Becomes Y Combinator's Fastest-Ever Unicorn at $3.2B Valuation

AI model-training startup AfterQuery has reportedly closed a funding round valuing the company at $3.2 billion, just five months after its previous announcement of a $30 million Series A. The valuation makes AfterQuery the fastest company in Y Combinator history to reach unicorn status.

---

## Background of Rapid Growth

AfterQuery develops infrastructure for efficient machine learning model training. The company addresses key challenges in distributed learning research: compute resource waste and checkpointing overhead. Its proprietary optimization techniques have attracted significant attention from enterprise customers.

The latest funding round included multi-year contracts with major U.S. cloud providers. Y Combinator's demo day historically produces fast-growing companies, but AfterQuery's trajectory from demo day to unicorn in five months sets a new record.

---

## Industry Impact

AI infrastructure has become a focal point for investors as the generative AI boom continues to evolve. While application-layer companies face pressure on unit economics, infrastructure-layer companies like AfterQuery benefit from consistent demand across both research and production environments.
