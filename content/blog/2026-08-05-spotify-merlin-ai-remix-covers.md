# Spotify Expands AI Remix and Covers with Merlin Partnership

Spotify is significantly expanding its AI-powered music remix and covers initiative by bringing Merlin, the independent music distribution powerhouse representing more than 30,000 independent labels and distributors, into the fold alongside Universal Music Group.

---

## The Product: AI-Generated Covers and Remixes

Spotify's upcoming paid tool will allow fans to create AI-generated covers and remixes of participating artists' music. The system is designed with artist protections at its core: artists must opt in before their music can be remixed, they receive proper credit, and they are compensated for any AI-generated uses of their work.

This represents a more structured approach to AI music than many previous efforts, which often faced backlash from artists and rights holders for unauthorized use of copyrighted material to train AI systems.

---

## Why Merlin Matters

Merlin is no small player. The organization represents more than 30,000 independent labels and distributors globally — making it one of the most significant independent music distribution networks in the world. Its involvement signals that the independent music community, not just major labels, is willing to engage with AI music tools under the right contractual framework.

The partnership positions Spotify's AI tool as a multi-rights-holder platform from the start, rather than a tool that artists must opt out of after the fact.

---

## Industry Context

The intersection of AI and music has been contentious. AI-generated covers have proliferated across platforms, often without artist consent or compensation. Spotify's approach — requiring opt-in, credit, and payment — stands in contrast to the more permissive stances that have drawn criticism from musicians and recording artists worldwide.
