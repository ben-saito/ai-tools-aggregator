# OpenAI Calls for Stronger California AI Safety Bill SB 53

OpenAI is now calling for California to strengthen its AI safety bill SB 53, marking a shift from its previous stance.

---

## A Change in Position

OpenAI previously opposed SB 53. However, the company now says California should implement stronger safety measures.

This shift reflects growing concern about safety in AI development. As risks from advanced AI systems become more apparent, even industry leaders are calling for stricter regulations.

---

## Overview of SB 53

SB 53 is a bill that establishes safety standards for AI systems in California. It considers requiring advanced AI labs to conduct risk assessments and safety tests.

OpenAI's change indicates that industry leaders are beginning to recognize that self-regulation alone is insufficient. The company aims to work with policymakers to build more effective safety regulations.

---

## Industry Impact

OpenAI's statement could influence other AI labs. As concern about the safety of advanced AI systems grows, policymakers will likely consider introducing further regulations.

Dialogue between industry and policymakers is essential for the safe and responsible development of AI. OpenAI's shift may serve as a catalyst for this conversation.
