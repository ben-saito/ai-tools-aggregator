# Anthropic Expands Claude for Startups Program With Free Year of Claude Team

Anthropic announced Tuesday an expansion of its Claude for Startups program, offering qualifying early-stage companies a free year of Claude Team access plus $1,000 in token credits. The program, launched as part of Anthropic's SF Tech Week event, is designed to lower the barrier for startups building on AI by subsidizing access to the company's most capable models.

---

## Who qualifies and what is offered

The new program tier provides a free year of Claude Team, Anthropic's paid group plan with up to five premium seats, along with $1,000 in API credits that can be used across Anthropic's model family including Claude 3.5 Sonnet and the latest Claude 3 Opus. Companies are eligible if they were founded in the last five years or have received funding in the last two years.

Claude Team is Anthropic's collaborative AI product designed for organizations, offering higher rate limits and features geared toward team use cases compared to the individual Claude.ai subscription. The program makes this available at no cost to qualifying startups.

---

## The strategic rationale

Anthropic's framing of the program reflects a conviction that AI's broad societal benefits will flow primarily through companies that build on top of foundation models rather than through the models alone. "We created this program because we believe the benefits of AI will reach most people through the companies that build on top of models, rather than through the models alone," Anthropic said in its announcement.

The program is not entirely new, but the expanded tier significantly increases the value proposition for early-stage companies. Previous tiers offered discounted access rather than free access, making the new free year a meaningful shift in how Anthropic is approaching startup ecosystem development.

For startups evaluating which AI provider to build on, subsidized access reduces one variable in the decision matrix. Rate limits, model quality, context window, and developer experience still dominate those conversations, but cost certainty during the critical early product phase carries real weight.

---

## Context for the AI developer ecosystem

The Anthropic announcement comes as the landscape for developer AI programs has grown increasingly competitive. OpenAI, Google, and smaller providers like Mistral have all launched startup-focused programs offering API credits or discounted access in an effort to capture developer mindshare early in company formation. Anthropic's approach with a direct Claude Team seat rather than just credits differentiates the offering somewhat.

The credits can be used across Anthropic's API, which supports both synchronous inference and streaming responses. The company's model family includes models optimized for different tradeoffs between capability and cost, allowing startups to use more capable models for complex tasks while routing simpler requests to less expensive models.

Interested companies can apply through the Claude for Startups program page on Anthropic's website.
