# Instinct Launches Group Chat AI Agent Accessible Without an Account

Instinct has released an AI agent feature that works in group chats, allowing friends to collaborate on tasks like trip planning, carpool coordination, and event organization without requiring everyone to have an Instinct account. The agent acts as a shared assistant accessible through existing messaging interfaces.

---

## Removing the Account Barrier

Traditional AI agents require each user to create an account and log in to access features. Instinct's approach removes this barrier by operating at the group level rather than the individual level — the group chat itself becomes the context for the AI agent, and any member can interact with it regardless of whether they have an Instinct account.

This design decision makes the technology accessible to groups where some members might be reluctant to sign up for another AI service but are willing to participate in a group chat that has AI capabilities.

---

## Use Cases: Trips, Carpools, Events

The primary use cases Instinct highlights include planning trips collaboratively, organizing carpools for commuting or events, and coordinating schedules for group activities. The AI agent can maintain context across the conversation, remember preferences and constraints expressed by different members, and help the group reach consensus on plans.

The company emphasizes that personal accounts remain separate — the AI operates on group-level information rather than accessing individual account data from members.

---

## Implications for AI Accessibility

The group-level approach represents a different model for AI agent deployment. Rather than requiring each user to adopt a new tool, it embeds AI assistance into existing communication patterns. This could lower the barrier for AI adoption among users who are resistant to creating yet another account or learning a new interface.
