# a16z's Olivia Moore on the state of consumer AI

---

a16z partner Olivia Moore presented her annual state of consumer AI report at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, painting a picture of a market at an inflection point: consumer AI has escaped the chatbot phase and is now embedding itself into daily life through a proliferation of specialized agents, AI-first hardware, and ambient computing interfaces.

Key findings from the report:

---

## Mobile AI Agents Reaching Critical Mass

Moore cited data showing that over 60% of US smartphone users now interact with at least one AI agent daily, up from 23% in 2025. The defining characteristic of 2026's winning agents is task completion — not chat — with the most-used agents handling appointment scheduling, travel planning, and financial tracking autonomously.

---

## AI Hardware: The iPhone Moment Passed, the Wearable Moment Arriving

The Rabbit R1 and Humane Pin were declared disappointments, but Moore argued the category itself is not dead — it is being rebuilt around a clearer use case: ambient, always-on AI that augments memory, attention, and communication without requiring a screen. The next generation of AI wearables targets professionals who need hands-free assistance in meetings and field work.

---

## Consumer Trust Shifts by Category

AI trust varies sharply by domain. Users are comfortable with AI managing calendar and email, skeptical of AI health advice, and actively hostile toward AI financial management without human review. Moore noted that the companies winning in consumer AI are those that know where to stop — delivering the full benefit of AI automation in low-stakes domains while maintaining human-in-the-loop for consequential decisions.

---

## The Chinese Consumer AI Market

Moore highlighted China's consumer AI adoption as outpacing the US in several dimensions: AI-native social apps, AI tutoring at scale, and multimodal AI assistants built into payment and delivery super-apps. She predicted that by 2028, Chinese consumer AI companies will begin expanding aggressively into Southeast Asia and Europe, creating the first true global consumer AI competition.
