# Cerebras CEO says we're nearing a future where everyone has a personal AI assistant at home

---

Cerebras Systems CEO and co-founder Andrew Feldman believes we're nearing a future where everyone will have an AI appliance in their own home. Speaking on stage at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, Feldman argued that just as the PC moved from enterprise to consumer in the 1980s, AI compute will follow the same trajectory.

"The refrigerator didn't used to be in every home," Feldman said. "Now it is. The air conditioner used to be a luxury. Now it's in every apartment in Mumbai. I think the AI appliance will follow exactly that curve."

Cerebras makes wafer-scale AI accelerators — chips that are the size of an entire silicon wafer rather than切成 individual dies. This approach eliminates the interconnect bottleneck that limits conventional GPU clusters, delivering memory bandwidth and compute density that GPUs can't match for certain workloads.

Feldman was quick to distance Cerebras from the "AI doomer" narrative that has dominated much of the tech press coverage of AI safety. "The people who are most worried about AI are the ones who've never shipped a product," he said. "If you talk to actual engineers building these systems, the concerns are much more nuanced."

The company is currently shipping its third-generation wafer-scale systems to government customers and is working on a consumer-targeted product that Feldman says will be announced in 2027.
