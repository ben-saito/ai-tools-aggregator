# FAA、空交通管理に8億7500万ドルのAIプログラム「SMART」を導入

---

## Background: US Air Traffic Controller Shortage

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) has been struggling with a nationwide air traffic controller shortage. This problem has persisted for years, making it urgent to secure enough personnel to maintain aviation safety.

Earlier this year, the FAA announced a "bold, new" hiring plan to "erase the longstanding staffing shortage." However, this has not led to a fundamental solution.

---

## Solution: AIR SPACE INTELLIGENCE's "SMART"

One solution is **SMART** (Strategic Management of Airspace, Routes, and Trajectories). Developed by Air Space Intelligence, this AI software is designed as a "cloud-based platform system" that extends the existing FAA air traffic management systems.

According to the Wall Street Journal, SMART will be deployed to the government over 12 years at a total cost of approximately $875 million. The FAA is expected to formally launch the program soon.

---

## How AI Is Used

SMART specifically supports air traffic controllers' workflow management. By extending existing systems with AI, controllers can manage aircraft operations more efficiently.

FAA officials stated that the goal is to reduce the workload of air traffic controllers -- described as the "crossing guards of the skies" -- who manage intersections of aircraft paths.

---

## Key Technical Details

According to Air Space Intelligence's documentation:

- **Cloud-based**: Integrates with existing FAA systems
- **12-year contract**: Total cost $875 million
- **Improved air traffic management**: Route management and trajectory optimization

---

## Industry Reaction

The aviation industry broadly welcomes the FAA's improved air traffic management capabilities. However, concerns about AI dependency remain. In the safety-first aviation industry, AI deployment requires careful evaluation.
