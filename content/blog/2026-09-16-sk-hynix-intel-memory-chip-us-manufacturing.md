# SK Hynix Reportedly in Talks with Intel to Build Memory Chips in US

SK Hynix, the South Korean memory chip giant, is in discussions with Intel to manufacture RAM chips in the US for the first time, according to a Reuters report citing anonymous sources.

---

## Background

SK Hynix is one of the world's two leading memory chip manufacturers, known for its HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) technology used in NVIDIA's AI accelerators.

The discussions reportedly involve SK Hynix leasing space at Intel's planned factory in Ohio to manufacture chips, including HBM. The companies are also said to be considering a joint venture that could include cloud service providers.

---

## Industry Impact

If this partnership materializes, it would significantly alter the US domestic supply chain for AI-relevant memory chips. Currently, nearly all memory chips used in the US are imported, with Samsung, SK Hynix, and TSMC dominating supply.

The explosive growth in AI demand has created persistent HBM shortages. Major players including Microsoft, Google Cloud, AWS, and NVIDIA are engaged in intense competition to secure HBM supply, with SK Hynix's products at the center of this battle.

---

## Differentiation from TSMC and Samsung

Intel's OSAT (Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test) business focuses on packaging and testing rather than wafer fabrication like TSMC. The proposed partnership would leverage Intel's Ohio facility to establish US-based manufacturing capacity.
