# Meta Expands AI-Powered Algorithm Control to Facebook Users

---

Facebook is the latest social media platform to give users greater control over the algorithm that determines what appears in their feeds. Meta announced the launch of its new AI-powered feature, "Your Algorithm," on Tuesday, a feature it first launched on Instagram. The new tool allows Facebook users to influence the content they see in Feed and Reels by describing their preferences in their own words using natural language.

Rather than relying entirely on automated recommendations, users can now communicate directly with the platform about the topics and content they want to see more or less frequently. According to Meta, the feature is designed to make content recommendations more relevant. Users can adjust their preferences and interact with the algorithms responsible for ranking and recommending posts, with changes reflected in their recommendations in real time.

## How It Works

The system, internally called "Feed Shaping," uses AI to let users specify what types of content they want to see more or less of. Instead of relying solely on engagement-based ranking, users can now tell the AI things like "show me more posts from local businesses" or "fewer political posts." The model interprets these preferences and adjusts the ranking model in real time.

Meta claims the system respects user privacy because it runs locally on Meta's servers without storing individual preference data long-term. The company has also updated Facebook's three-dot menu, making it easier for users to manage their content preferences.

## Industry Context

The feature is part of a broader Meta strategy to address criticism that algorithmic curation creates filter bubbles and amplifies divisive content. By giving users explicit control over the algorithm's weighting, Meta hopes to reduce the perception that it is secretly manipulating what people see.

Early tests show that users who actively adjust their Feed Shaping settings report higher satisfaction with their feed quality. However, critics argue that giving users the illusion of control over a system designed to maximize engagement is insufficient if the underlying ranking still prioritizes viral content.

## Rollout

The feature is rolling out to U.S. users first with global expansion planned for Q1 2027. Meta has indicated that similar controls will eventually be available across all its platforms, including WhatsApp and Messenger.
