# TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 Adds Real World AI Stage Featuring Nvidia, Robots, and Extinct Animals

TechCrunch has announced a new stage at its Disrupt 2026 conference focused on the intersection of AI and physical world applications, featuring speakers from Nvidia, Shield AI, Colossal Biosciences, and other companies working on autonomous systems, robotics, and de-extinction technology.

---

## A New Stage for Physical AI

TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, scheduled for October 13-15 at San Francisco's Moscone West, will feature a new "Real World AI" stage dedicated to AI systems that interact with the physical world. The stage will focus on the convergence of digital and physical AI, covering autonomous vehicles, defense technologies, home robotics, and even efforts to bring extinct species back to Earth.

The announcement highlights a fundamental challenge in physical AI: while large language models have benefited from vast internet datasets, robots lack equivalent training data. This "data gap" is cited as the primary reason experts believe general-purpose robotic intelligence remains years away, despite rapid progress in other AI domains.

---

## Speaker Lineup and Sessions

The Real World AI Stage will feature speakers from several notable companies:

**Shield AI** focuses on autonomous aerospace systems, developing AI pilots for aircraft that can operate in contested environments without human control.

**Colossal Biosciences** is working on de-extinction technology, attempting to bring back species like the woolly mammoth and thylacine. CEO Ben Lamm has positioned the company as turning science fiction into a billion-dollar business.

**FieldAI** and **Foxglove** represent the broader ecosystem of startups building infrastructure for physical AI systems, including data pipelines, simulation environments, and foundation models for robotics.

A key session will address the question of when autonomous systems are "ready" for safe deployment—a critical issue when AI failures can result in grounded aircraft, vehicle crashes, or compromised military missions. The conversation will cover safety culture, validation testing, regulatory navigation, and building public trust in high-stakes AI applications.

---

## The Data Challenge in Physical AI

A central theme of the new stage reflects a broader debate in the AI industry: the data gap between language models and physical systems. LLMs trained on internet text have achieved remarkable capabilities, but replicating this success in robotics requires massive amounts of real-world interaction data that simply does not exist in digital form.

Startups are racing to build simulation environments and data pipelines that could trigger a "ChatGPT moment" for physical AI—a point where robotic capabilities suddenly accelerate dramatically. The Real World AI Stage will host discussions on what this inflection point actually requires and how close the industry truly is to reaching it.

## Conference Context

TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 continues the company's tradition of bringing together startups, investors, and technologists. The addition of the Real World AI Stage reflects growing industry interest in AI applications beyond software, particularly as autonomous systems begin moving from laboratories into public spaces, battlefields, and homes.

Registration for TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 is open, with the Real World AI Stage sessions scheduled throughout the three-day event.
