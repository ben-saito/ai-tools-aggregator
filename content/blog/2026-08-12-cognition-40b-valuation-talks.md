# AI Coding Startup Cognition Reportedly in Talks for $40B Valuation Raise

---

## Devin Maker Seeks Mega Round Months After $26B Raise, Bloomberg Reports

Cognition, the AI startup behind the coding agent Devin, is reportedly in discussions with investors to raise another funding round that could value the company at **$40 billion** — roughly 54% above its $26 billion valuation from May 2026. The report comes from Bloomberg sources cited by TechCrunch.

The May raise was a $1 billion round, during which CEO Scott Wu told TechCrunch that Cognition had achieved a $492 million annualized revenue run rate and was growing enterprise Devin usage by 50% month-over-month for six consecutive months.

---

## Revenue Milestone Driving Valuation Leap

The reported $40B target is anchored on Cognition reaching a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate. At its current growth trajectory, the company appears to be approaching or exceeding that milestone just months after the May announcement.

Unlike purely compute-intensive AI infrastructure plays, Cognition's valuation reflects a product-led SaaS model where enterprises pay for AI agents that automate long-tail software maintenance tasks — updating legacy systems, migrating applications between platforms, and handling grunt work that developers dislike.
