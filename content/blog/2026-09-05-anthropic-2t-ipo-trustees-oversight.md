# Anthropic's $2 Trillion IPO Puts External Trustees Under the Spotlight

Anthropic's planned public market debut, potentially valuing the company at $2 trillion, will expose its unusual governance structure to unprecedented scrutiny. The Long-Term Benefit Trust controls the majority of the board and includes notable figures like Ben Bernanke and Reed Hastings.

---

## The Long-Term Benefit Trust Structure

Anthropic's prospective public-market investors must contend with an external group of trustees that control the majority of the AI company's board. The Long-Term Benefit Trust (LTBT) — a small group of advisers created to safeguard the lab's mission of developing AI for long-term human benefit — holds no equity but wields significant influence over the Claude maker's governance.

The trust has the right to appoint or dismiss the majority of Anthropic's board and has selected four of the company's seven directors, including Netflix co-founder Reed Hastings and Novartis CEO Vas Narasimhan. Currently, the LTBT has three members out of a potential maximum of five: chair Neil Buddy Shah (CEO of the Clinton Health Access Initiative), former Federal Reserve chair Ben Bernanke, and Richard Fontaine (CEO of the Center for a New American Security).

Mariano-Florentino "Tino" Cuéllar, a former California Supreme Court justice, left the trust after a few months to become Anthropic's chief global affairs officer.

## Weekly Oversight and Model Launch Notifications

Trustees are required to receive advance notice of major company actions, including new AI model launches. They meet weekly among themselves and with Anthropic's leadership as frequently as every other week. Their oversight has already shaped significant decisions — including a limited rollout of the Mythos cybersecurity model through the Glasswing Project, which trustees encouraged after review.

Bernanke's appointment in July signals Anthropic's ambition to build a more institutionalized governance body, sources close to the company said. The goal is to establish the LTBT as an industry blueprint, similar to how GAAP accounting standards initially emerged as voluntary corporate proposals.

## IPO Timeline and Valuation Context

Anthropic is planning a stock market debut that could value the company at up to $2 trillion, making it one of the largest public offerings in technology history. The structure aims to balance commercial pressures with the company's stated mission of developing beneficial AI — though public-market scrutiny will test whether that balance holds under quarterly earnings expectations.

## Governance Implications for AI Industry

The IPO represents the first major test of whether hybrid profit-mission governance structures can withstand public market pressures. If successful, the Anthropic model could become a template for other AI labs considering public funding while maintaining non-profit-aligned oversight.
