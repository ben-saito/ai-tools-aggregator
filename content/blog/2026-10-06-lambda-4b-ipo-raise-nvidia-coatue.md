# Lambda、AIコンピューティング企業として40億ドル調達を計画——2027年IPO間近

---

Nvidia-backed Lambda is raising up to $4 billion in a Series round led by Coatue and Blackstone at a $14.5 billion pre-money valuation, targeting a 2027 IPO. The AI cloud company purchases Nvidia chips and leases computing power to enterprises, positioning itself as a key player in the AI infrastructure race.

---

## The Capital Race in AI Infrastructure

The fundraising reflects the intensifying capital competition in AI computing. With CoreWeave, Oracle, and AWS pouring billions into AI infrastructure, Lambda is stacking war chests ahead of its public debut to stay competitive. Nvidia GPU supply remains constrained, making partnerships with Nvidia a significant moat against rivals.

Lambda also holds a lease agreement with Microsoft, providing a recurring revenue stream that likely boosted investor confidence. The combination of Nvidia backing, enterprise contracts, and an IPO timeline signals that AI infrastructure plays remain highly attractive to institutional investors despite market volatility.

---

## IPO Pipeline for AI Companies

Lambda joins a growing list of AI infrastructure companies exploring public markets. The 2027 target suggests management believes market conditions will be favorable for tech IPOs, and the current funding round provides a runway to demonstrate commercial traction before listing.

The involvement of Blackstone's credit arm alongside Coatue indicates a mix of growth equity and debt financing, a structure that gives Lambda flexibility as it scales capacity ahead of the offering.
