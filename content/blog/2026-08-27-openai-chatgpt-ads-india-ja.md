# OpenAI Starts Showing Ads on ChatGPT Free and Go Tiers in India

OpenAI has begun displaying advertisements on its ChatGPT Free and Go subscription tiers in India, the company announced on Thursday. The move marks a significant expansion of OpenAI's advertising business, which launched in the United States earlier this year and extended to Europe just this month.

---

## India's 100 Million ChatGPT Users Become Ad Audience

OpenAI disclosed in February that India accounts for more than 100 million weekly active ChatGPT users, a substantial portion of whom use the free or lower-priced Go tiers. The company has invested heavily in building its Indian user base, launching a sub-$5 ChatGPT Go plan in August 2025 and running promotions that offered the tier free for a full year. OpenAI has also advertised extensively during major sporting events, including the Women's Premier League and Indian Premier League cricket tournaments.

The advertising launch will start with approximately 50 brand partners. OpenAI has established partnerships with major advertising agencies WPP and Omnicom for this initiative. The company plans to introduce an ad manager tool next month, enabling marketers to create and manage campaigns with a minimum daily budget of 725 rupees (approximately $7.60).

"With ChatGPT Ads, businesses of every size can introduce themselves at relevant, high-context moments when decisions are beginning to take shape," said Dave Dugan, head of global ads solutions at OpenAI, in a company statement.

---

## Revenue Growth Ahead of Potential IPO

The expansion into India's massive user base comes as OpenAI works to scale its revenue ahead of a potential initial public offering, expected sometime in 2026 or 2027. According to The Wall Street Journal, OpenAI generated $6.7 billion in revenue during the second quarter of 2026, up from $5.7 billion the previous quarter.

The company has been actively diversifying its income sources beyond API access and subscription plans. Advertising represents a new revenue vector that leverages the enormous user engagement ChatGPT has generated globally, particularly in markets like India where free and low-cost tiers have driven rapid adoption.

OpenAI's push into advertising also reflects a broader trend in the AI industry, where companies balance free access to AI tools with monetization strategies that may include sponsored content and targeted advertisements.
