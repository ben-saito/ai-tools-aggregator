# Spotify expands AI remix tool with Merlin partnership

Spotify announced that Merlin, representing over 30,000 independent labels and distributors, has joined its AI-powered music remix and covers product, expanding the catalog of artists who have consented to fan-generated AI creations.

---

## How Spotify's AI music product works

Unlike controversial AI music startups that generate fully artificial songs, Spotify's approach requires artist consent. Artists must actively agree to have their work included in the AI remix catalog before fans can create covers or remixes using the tool.

Co-CEO Gustav Söderström described the distinction as "real artists, not fake artists." The product is positioned as a fan engagement tool rather than a replacement for official releases.

Co-CEO Alex Norström emphasized consent and attribution: "We want artists to be consenting to add their work into this catalog, so people can play around with covers and remixes based on their art. We also obviously want to give them credit."

---

## The licensing landscape

Merlin's addition brings significant independent label coverage to the product. Previously, Universal Music Group had signed on as the first major label partner. With Merlin, Spotify gains access to a network of independent labels and distributors that collectively represent a substantial portion of global music releases.

The deals suggest a potential model for how AI music licensing might work at scale — major label + independent network coverage with explicit artist opt-in.
