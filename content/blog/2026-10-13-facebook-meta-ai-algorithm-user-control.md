# Meta Rolls Out AI-Powered User Control Over Facebook's Recommendation Algorithm

---

Meta announced a new feature allowing Facebook users to exert greater control over the algorithm that curates their News Feed. The system, internally called "Feed Shaping," uses AI to let users specify what types of content they want to see more or less of, with natural language instructions interpreted by a large language model fine-tuned on engagement data.

**How it works:** Instead of relying solely on engagement-based ranking, users can now tell the AI things like "show me more posts from local businesses" or "fewer political posts." The model interprets these preferences and adjusts the ranking model in real time. Meta claims the system respects user privacy because it runs locally on Meta's servers without storing individual preference data long-term.

The feature is part of a broader Meta strategy to address criticism that algorithmic curation creates filter bubbles and amplifies divisive content. By giving users explicit control over the algorithm's weighting, Meta hopes to reduce the perception that it is secretly manipulating what people see.

Early tests show that users who actively adjust their Feed Shaping settings report higher satisfaction with their feed quality. However, critics argue that giving users the illusion of control over a system designed to maximize engagement is insufficient if the underlying ranking still prioritizes viral content. The feature is rolling out to U.S. users first with global expansion planned for Q1 2027.
