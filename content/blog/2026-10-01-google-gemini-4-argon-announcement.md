# Google Announces Gemini 4 Argon: Limited Testing, API Pricing Revealed

Google has announced Gemini 4 Argon, its latest flagship AI model, currently in limited testing with no public access yet available. Despite the restricted rollout, Google has already published API pricing for the model.

---

## Benchmarks and Capabilities

Gemini 4 Argon enters with a focus on software engineering tasks. On the DeepSWE v1.1 benchmark for code generation, the model reportedly achieves a 77% score. Google has presented additional benchmark results across multiple domains, though external verification remains pending as the model is not yet publicly accessible.

The company says Argon represents a significant advance over previous Gemini iterations, though detailed technical papers and independent evaluations have not yet been published.

---

## Cyberdefense Focus

Unlike previous Gemini releases focused on consumer and enterprise productivity, Gemini 4 Argon is being positioned primarily as a **cyberdefense tool**. Google says models of this capability scale require phased release strategies, beginning with a controlled group of testers before broader availability.

This approach reflects growing industry consensus that frontier AI models with significant capabilities should undergo staged deployment, a position that has gained traction following multiple high-profile AI security incidents over the summer of 2026.

---

## API Pricing

Google has published pricing despite the limited testing status. For a limited introductory period, Argon offers rates of **$2 per million input tokens**. No output token pricing has been disclosed.

The pricing positions Argon in the premium tier of AI API offerings, comparable to other frontier models from OpenAI and Anthropic for equivalent capability levels.

---

## Safety and Misalignment Concerns

The announcement comes amid ongoing industry-wide discussions about AI safety following several summer incidents involving frontier AI systems. Google claims it designed Argon with specific alignment properties intended to reduce misalignment risks, though specifics remain limited pending the broader technical paper release.

Industry observers note that the cyberdefense positioning represents a notable shift in how Google frames its frontier model releases, emphasizing responsible deployment over raw capability benchmarks.

---

## Availability

As of October 1, 2026, Gemini 4 Argon remains in limited testing. No public availability date has been announced. Developers interested in access should monitor Google's official channels for updates on the testing program expansion.

---

* Gemini 4 Argon information is based on Google's announcement of September 30, 2026.
