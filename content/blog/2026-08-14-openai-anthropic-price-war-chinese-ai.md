# OpenAI and Anthropic in Price War as Chinese AI Rivals Gain Ground

US AI labs are locked in an intensifying price war as Chinese developers like Moonshot and DeepSeek offer increasingly capable models at a fraction of the cost. The dynamic is forcing OpenAI and Anthropic to cut prices significantly while planning for massive IPO valuations.

---

## The Price War Accelerates

OpenAI recently slashed prices for GPT-5.6 Luna, its "fastest and most affordable model," by 80 percent. Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5, marketing it as "frontier intelligence at half the price" of GPT-5. The cuts have pushed what customers pay for leading US lab models down by nearly a quarter since mid-July, according to Silicon Data's token price index.

The competitive pressure comes from Chinese labs releasing increasingly capable open-weight models that can be freely downloaded and modified. DeepSeek and Moonshot have made significant inroads with developers across Silicon Valley and Europe.

---

## Enterprise Cost Pressures

Corporate AI users face mounting bills as Anthropic and OpenAI shift some enterprise customers away from flat-rate subscriptions toward usage-based billing. Companies including DoorDash and Airbnb have begun testing Chinese-made models to control costs. Some businesses have imposed caps on AI usage entirely.

Both companies are planning IPOs at trillion-dollar valuations, and investors are demanding evidence that the industry's massive AI spending can generate sustainable returns.
