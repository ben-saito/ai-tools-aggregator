# AI Coding Startup Cognition Reportedly in Talks for $40B Valuation

Cognition, the AI coding startup behind the Devin autonomous coding agent, is reportedly already in discussions with investors about a new funding round that could value the company at $40 billion, according to sources cited by Bloomberg.

---

## Rapid Valuation Jump

Just months after raising $1 billion at a $26 billion valuation in May 2026, Cognition is said to be targeting a valuation of at least $40 billion. The increased valuation would be based on reaching a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate, according to people familiar with the matter.

The talks are reportedly in early stages and the terms could change.

---

## Devin's Market Impact

Cognition's Devin positioning itself as an autonomous coding agent capable of handling complete software development tasks. The rapid valuation appreciation reflects growing investor appetite for AI coding tools that can automate software development workflows.

The coding AI market has heated up considerably, with multiple startups competing to build AI systems that can write, review, and debug code with minimal human supervision.
