# Nvidia Leads Formation of Open Secure AI Alliance—120 Companies Join in First Week

One week after Nvidia announced the Open Secure AI Alliance, the consortium has grown to over 120 member companies and already has concrete proposals out for defending AI agents from compromise.

---

## A Defense-First Approach to AI Security

The Open Secure AI Alliance was formed to address a specific threat: AI agents are vulnerable to attack vectors that do not exist for traditional software. Unlike a static application, an AI agent can be manipulated through its prompts, memory, and tool-use chains.

Nvidia moved quickly to build the consortium, leveraging its position as the dominant GPU supplier to bring in cloud providers, model developers, and security firms.

---

## Defending Agent Infrastructure

The alliance's first proposals focus on three areas: agent authentication (verifying that an agent is who it claims to be), prompt injection defense (blocking malicious instructions embedded in agent inputs), and memory integrity (securing the context that agents rely on for long-horizon tasks).

The proposals are open for comment and expected to become foundational standards for enterprise AI deployments.
