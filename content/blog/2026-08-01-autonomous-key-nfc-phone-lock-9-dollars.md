# Autonomous Key: A $9 NFC Device That Physically Locks Your Phone Apps

Screen-time management apps fail because they rely on willpower — users can simply ignore notifications or delete the app. Autonomous Key takes the opposite approach: a physical NFC key that requires you to physically locate and scan it with your phone to unlock distracting apps, turning a mindless scroll impulse into a deliberate effort.

---

## The Physical Barrier to Phone Addiction

The concept is simple but effective. The key pairs with a companion app over NFC and lets you lock away apps like Instagram, TikTok, and other time-wasting applications. To unlock them, you must physically scan the key with your phone — no tapping a button to bypass the block. Each unlock session lasts up to 60 minutes before the apps automatically lock again.

The physical requirement means users can leave the key in another room, at the office, or in a gym bag, making it considerably harder to casually bypass your own restrictions. The friction is intentional: transforming an impulsive unlock into a deliberate decision that requires extra effort.

## Competitive Pricing and Features

At $9, Autonomous Key undercuts its competitors significantly:

- **Blok**: $29
- **Unpluq**: $26.50

While Brick offers advanced features like Sleep Mode and in-app purchase blocking, Autonomous Key covers the core functionality at a fraction of the price.

## Technical Specifications

The key measures about 3 inches long and works with:
- Android 8.0 or later
- iOS 15 or later

The companion app also provides AI-powered insights, tracking unlock frequency, session duration, and peak usage times. The AI summarizes habits with a "sarcastic" personality — for example, if you repeatedly unlock apps immediately after locking them, it might suggest putting the key somewhere less convenient.
