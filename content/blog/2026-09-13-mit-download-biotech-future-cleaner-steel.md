# MIT Technology Review: AI-Powered Biotech Future and Cleaner Steel Manufacturing

The latest edition of MIT Technology Review's newsletter "The Download" covers the future of biotech accelerated by AI and cleaner steel manufacturing technology.

## Key Topics

The issue discusses advances in AI-driven drug discovery and cellular agriculture. NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang's vision of a "chemical universe" for data-driven drug discovery and DeepMind's AlphaFold3 for protein design are highlighted as revolutionary developments.

## Steel Manufacturing CO2 Reduction

Another key topic is CO2 reduction through Direct Reduced Iron (DRI) methods. Commercial viability of hydrogen-based steel production is improving, positioning the iron and steel industry for decarbonization through innovative technologies.
