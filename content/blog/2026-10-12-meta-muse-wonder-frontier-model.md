# Meta Releases Its First Frontier Model, Muse Wonder

---

Meta's Superintelligence Lab has released Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, the company's first frontier-class models. Unlike the lightweight Muse Spark, the Wonder models are larger and more capable, with higher benchmark scores and better performance on complex tasks like coding. According to Meta's testing, Wonder and Wonder Full achieve comparable results to leading models on key benchmarks, marking Meta's entry into the frontier AI race.

## Capabilities and Benchmarks

Muse Wonder Full is the larger variant, designed to handle complex reasoning and coding tasks. The models were evaluated across standard AI benchmarks including MMLU, coding tasks, and multi-step reasoning problems. Meta's model card shows Wonder performing competitively with GPT-4 class models on several key tasks.

## Meta's AI Strategy

Meta has been rapidly expanding its AI model portfolio. Muse Spark was positioned as a lightweight, low-latency model for consumer applications. Muse Wonder represents a shift toward benchmark-leading capability, signaling Meta's ambition to compete directly with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind at the frontier.

## What This Means for Developers

The release provides developers with a new open-weight frontier option. Meta has published model cards and technical documentation. Developers can access the models through Meta's API or by downloading weights for self-hosting.
