# OpenAI Launches Astra, Its Most Powerful -- and Controversial -- Model Yet

OpenAI has launched Astra, a new flagship model that the company calls its most intelligent and aligned to date, positioning it as a leap forward in computer and browser use. The model is rolling out to OpenAI Daybreak cybersecurity program customers immediately, with broader availability across paid plans expected within the week.

---

## Capabilities and Controversy

OpenAI claims Astra represents a new frontier on computer and browser use, handling tasks with unmatched speed, accuracy, and safety. In a call with journalists, OpenAI president Greg Brockman described Astra as the company's most intelligent and, "very importantly, our most aligned model yet."

The model has drawn scrutiny over its use of a technique called opaque recurrence, which obscures certain aspects of model decision-making. Critics argue this approach undermines the transparency that safety-focused AI development requires. Chief scientist Jakub Pachocki defended the approach, framing some level of opacity as a natural outgrowth of how more capable models process information.

OpenAI has also claimed Astra is the best model for software engineering to date, citing benchmark results across a variety of coding tasks.

---

## Alignment and the Hugging Face Breach

The company's focus on alignment appears partly shaped by the recent Hugging Face breach, in which external actors accessed internal systems. OpenAI's published blog post discussing Astra's capabilities also outlined new safeguards the company has instituted, suggesting the incident accelerated internal reviews of model deployment practices.

---

## Pricing and Availability

Astra is being made available initially through OpenAI's Daybreak cybersecurity program, with wider rollout to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, and Enterprise subscribers planned over the following week. Pricing details have not been publicly disclosed, though OpenAI has indicated the model will be included in existing subscription tiers.
