# OpenAI, California Governor Call for Stronger AI Safety Bill SB 53

OpenAI is calling for California to strengthen SB 53, an AI safety bill that the company previously opposed, in a significant reversal that highlights the shifting landscape of AI governance in the United States.

---

## A Reversal on AI Safety Regulation

In a LinkedIn post from OpenAI's global affairs team, the company said California's SB 53 "should be amended to expand safeguards." Specifically, OpenAI proposed requiring monitoring of frontier models during training or evaluation for potential serious incidents, and strengthening cybersecurity protocols.

SB 53 was passed last year as a landmark AI safety bill. OpenAI's change in position comes as the company seeks to position itself as a responsible actor in the AI safety debate, following criticism from policymakers and researchers about the pace of AI development.

---

## What the Bill Would Require

SB 53 establishes baseline safety requirements for frontier AI developers operating in California. The bill mandates incident reporting, compute thresholds for mandatory safety evaluations, and certain cybersecurity practices. OpenAI's proposed amendments would expand monitoring requirements and add provisions for real-time surveillance of frontier model training runs.

The company noted that existing provisions are "a good starting point" but insufficient given the rapid advancement of AI capabilities. Industry analysts view this as an attempt to shape the bill's final form before it takes effect.

---

## Industry Implications

OpenAI's reversal reflects growing recognition among major AI labs that some form of government oversight is inevitable. Rather than fight regulation, companies are now seeking to influence its design. The proposal to expand monitoring requirements could set a precedent for federal AI safety frameworks currently under development.

California remains the center of gravity for AI development in the United States, making state-level regulations particularly consequential for the industry. How SB 53 is shaped in the coming months could define the operating environment for frontier AI labs for years to come.
