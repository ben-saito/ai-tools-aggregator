# Superhuman Acquires Fathom, Adding AI Meeting Notes to Its Premium Email Client

Superhuman, the premium email client known for its speed-focused interface, has acquired Fathom, a Y Combinator-backed AI notetaker for video meetings. The acquisition price was not disclosed.

Fathom offers a free AI notetaker that integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. The service automatically transcribes meetings, summarizes key points, and highlights action items. Over 400,000 monthly active users rely on Fathom for meeting productivity.

The acquisition gives Superhuman a complementary AI product that fits its "inbox zero" philosophy extended to meetings. Superhuman has long marketed itself to professionals who want to process email and tasks at maximum speed. Fathom brings similar efficiency to the meeting lifecycle.

"Fathom is the meeting tool for people who hate meetings but have to attend them," said Superhuman CEO Rahul Varma. "Adding Fathom to Superhuman lets our users go from email to meeting to task completion without switching context."

Fathom will continue operating as a standalone product while being integrated into Superhuman's premium tier. Existing Fathom users will retain access to free features. A combined Superhuman+Fathom offering is expected to launch by end of 2026.

Superhuman has raised over $100 million in venture capital and counts Andreessen Horowitz and Sequoia among its investors. The company reported over 50,000 paying subscribers as of its last disclosed metrics.

The deal is Superhuman's first acquisition and signals the company's intent to expand beyond email into broader productivity workflows. The company has previously hinted at plans to add calendar, task management, and meeting coordination features.
