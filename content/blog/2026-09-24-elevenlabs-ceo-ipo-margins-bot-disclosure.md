# ElevenLabs CEO Discusses IPO Timeline, Margins, and Bot Disclosure Requirements

ElevenLabs CEO Mati Staniszewski sat down with TechCrunch for an extensive interview published September 24, 2026, covering the company's path to profitability, potential IPO timeline, and the ethics of disclosing AI voice interactions to customers.

---

## The Business of AI Voice

ElevenLabs powers the AI voice on the other end of a large percentage of customer service calls, and Staniszewski said businesses should probably tell customers when they are talking to a machine — at least until getting a machine to sound human becomes fully normalized.

The company is reportedly valued at $22 billion following its latest funding round, making it one of the highest-valued AI voice startups globally.

---

## Competition and Market Dynamics

Staniszewski acknowledged that ElevenLabs is not alone in what it does. The company is increasingly bumping into competitors including Decagon, a conversational AI platform that trained its voice AI on content it should not have accessed — a controversy that has raised industry-wide questions about training data practices.

Despite the competition, ElevenLabs maintains a lead in voice quality and is expanding into new markets including real-time translation and content creation.

---

## IPO Timing and Unit Economics

On IPO timing, Staniszewski was measured: "There is still a lot of work to be done, and the quality delta you can achieve just on the model level is still significant. If we think longer term, [going public] is a consideration, but we are focused on building."

The CEO emphasized that the company is working toward sustainable unit economics rather than chasing growth at all costs — a notable contrast to some AI peers burning cash at scale.
