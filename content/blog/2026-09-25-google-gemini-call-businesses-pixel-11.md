# Google Tests Gemini Calling Businesses on Behalf of Users

Google is testing a new "Call for Me" feature in Gemini that lets the AI call businesses directly on behalf of users. The feature will initially roll out to Pixel 11 owners in the United States who pay for a Gemini subscription, requiring the beta version of Google's Phone app for Android. Users will be able to follow conversations in real time and take over at any point.

---

## From Duplex to "Call for Me"

Google first demonstrated the capability at its I/O developer conference years ago, when Google Assistant called a salon to make a reservation, complete with realistic "umm" and "ahh" pauses. That technology evolved into Duplex, which handled tasks like restaurant reservations. Last year, Google launched "Ask for Me," allowing Gemini to call businesses to inquire about pricing or services.

The new "Call for Me" goes further by letting Gemini actually complete transactions on users' behalf, using the user's personal phone number to place the calls. The AI will dial businesses and handle tasks like scheduling appointments or confirming availability, with full transparency into each step of the conversation.

---

## Gradual Rollout and Privacy Considerations

Google says it is starting this experiment at a small scale because "real-world conversations are nuanced" and the company needs time to refine the feature before broader deployment. The initial limitation to Pixel 11 and Gemini subscribers reflects Google's strategy of testing AI agents in controlled environments before scaling.

The feature requires the beta version of Google's Phone app on Android, indicating that Google is treating the technology as still experimental. Users who opt in will be able to monitor and interrupt calls in progress, maintaining human oversight of AI-mediated communications.
