# Keenable Builds Web Search Index for AI Agents - 100 Billion Documents

---

## Yandex and Amazon Veterans Address AI Search Infrastructure Gap

As AI chatbots increasingly handle search tasks on behalf of users, a new startup aims to provide search infrastructure built specifically for AI agents rather than humans.

Keenable emerged from stealth on August 25, 2026, with $26 million in seed funding led by Accel, with participation from Conviction Partners and angel investors. The company has built a web search index covering more than 100 billion documents and already provides API access to several AI labs and inference providers for both training and runtime use.

---

## Founders with Search Industry Experience

CEO Andrey Styskin previously led the search, AI, and cloud division at Russian search giant Yandex. Co-founder Matthias Petri is a German AI scientist. Both founders previously worked on web search infrastructure for AI applications at Amazon, including Alexa.

Styskin explained that AI chatbots perform significantly better when they can ground their responses with source documents. However, current search infrastructure was not designed with AI in mind, making large-scale indexing costly and complex.

---

## Growing Share of AI Crawlers in Search Volume

Keenable drew inspiration from Cloudflare data showing AI crawlers account for a growing share of search volume. With Google and Microsoft gradually shutting down existing search APIs to avoid self-cannibalization, AI companies have few options for web-scale search infrastructure.

The company is building proprietary retrieval capabilities, including an upcoming product called Web Query Language, designed to help AI systems answer questions by combining information from multiple web sources.

---

## Plans to Double Team by End of 2026

Keenable currently has 15 engineering staff across the US and Europe. The company plans to use the fresh capital to double its headcount by the end of the year and build out its go-to-market motion.

The startup estimates building a giant search index is "painfully expensive" but is working to keep costs in check. According to Styskin, while it is extremely difficult to convince people to move away from Google for search, the innovators dilemma means Google is potentially beatable on agentic queries, where a smaller company can innovate more quickly.

---

## Search Market Outlook

Other players have entered the AI search space, including Brave and Exa. Google itself is overhauling its search experience for the AI era. Whether for humans or agents, the era of ten blue links may be giving way to a new paradigm built for machine consumption.
