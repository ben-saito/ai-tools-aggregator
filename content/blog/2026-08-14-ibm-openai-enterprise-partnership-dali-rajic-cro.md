# IBM Partners with OpenAI on Enterprise AI Push; OpenAI Hires New CRO as Exec Shake-up Continues

Two separate but related developments signal intensifying competition for enterprise AI customers: IBM announced a partnership to train tens of thousands of consultants on OpenAI's technologies, while OpenAI itself brought in Dali Rajic as Chief Revenue Officer as its executive team continues to restructure.

---

## IBM to Train Tens of Thousands of Consultants on OpenAI Stack

IBM has partnered with OpenAI to train and certify a large workforce of its consultants on OpenAI's enterprise AI technologies. The partnership is part of OpenAI's broader strategy to expand enterprise reach through global systems integrators — firms like IBM, Infosys, and Tata Consultancy Services that already have deep relationships with corporate IT departments.

Rather than selling directly to every enterprise customer, OpenAI is building a channel of certified partners who can deploy and customize its AI stack on behalf of clients. IBM's global consulting presence makes it a significant addition to that channel.

---

## Dali Rajic Joins as OpenAI CRO

Separately, OpenAI has hired Dali Rajic as its new Chief Revenue Officer, replacing Denise Recchio, who had led the revenue organization through the company's formative growth period.

The hire comes amid a broader executive shake-up at OpenAI. Recent departures include COO Brad Lightcap and Fidji Simo, CEO of AGI deployment. Rajic will be responsible for translating OpenAI's technical capabilities into repeatable enterprise sales execution as the company scales its commercial business.

Sam Altman has explicitly prioritized enterprise deployment in recent months, cutting back on experimental projects to focus the company on winning large-scale corporate deployments.

---

## Enterprise AI Channel Wars Intensify

Both moves reflect a shared reality: AI model providers are increasingly competing not just on model capability, but on go-to-market reach through consulting partners and enterprise sales capacity. IBM brings a vast consulting workforce that can deploy OpenAI-based solutions globally, while OpenAI's hiring of a revenue chief signals it intends to manage that channel and its direct enterprise sales with greater coordination.
