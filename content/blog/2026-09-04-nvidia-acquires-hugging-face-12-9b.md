# Nvidia to Acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 Billion

Nvidia has confirmed its acquisition of Hugging Face for $12.93 billion, marking the largest AI industry acquisition to date. The deal, announced on September 3, 2026, will see the chip giant bring the popular machine learning platform under its umbrella while maintaining Hugging Face's open-source philosophy.

---

## The Deal

After weeks of swirling rumors, Nvidia confirmed the acquisition in a blog post. Hugging Face's platform hosts over 3 million models, 1 million applications used by more than 18 million developers, and half a million datasets. The company was founded in 2016 and had raised over $395 million in funding, with its last round in 2023 bringing in $235 million from Salesforce Ventures, Google, Amazon, IBM, and Nvidia.

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang stated that Hugging Face will continue to support open source and open-weight models and will work on expanding developer access. "Hugging Face will remain an open platform for the entire AI ecosystem," Huang wrote. "Developers will choose the models they want, the frameworks they want, the clouds and inference service providers they want, and the computing platforms they want. Nvidia compute will not be required to build on or deploy through Hugging Face."

---

## Open Source Commitment

Huang highlighted Nvidia's existing contributions to the platform, noting the company has released over 500 models and 250 open datasets on Hugging Face. He argued that the company builds open models to get developers across the world to use them.

Hugging Face CEO Clem Delangue expressed gratitude to the community on X, saying the acquisition will enable the company to scale its mission. "For it to happen at a larger scale, it needs more compute, more support, more collaboration, and more visibility," Delangue said. "That's why we went to talk to Jensen, who offered to do exactly that with us."

Last year, Hugging Face rejected a $500 million deal from Nvidia, according to Financial Times. The company is reportedly clocking $150 million in annualized revenue.

---

## Strategic Implications

Nvidia's Huang has been a strong proponent of open models. He previously co-signed a letter advocating for open-weight models to strengthen the U.S. position against rivals like China in the AI sector. Nvidia is also investing heavily in model development, with The Wall Street Journal reporting an estimated $6 billion investment in AI infrastructure.

The acquisition raises questions about the future of Hugging Face's platform neutrality. While Nvidia has committed to keeping the platform open, competitors may seek alternatives given Nvidia's dominant position in AI chips.
