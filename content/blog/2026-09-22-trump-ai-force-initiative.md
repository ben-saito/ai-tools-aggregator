Trump rejects AI slowdown calls, launches "AI Force" instead

---

## President Announces New AI Initiative

President Trump has rejected calls from industry leaders to slow AI development, instead announcing a new "AI Force" initiative. The announcement comes amid ongoing debates about AI safety and the pace of AI advancement in the United States.

The president offered few concrete details on what his proposed AI Force would do, leaving the technical and policy communities to speculate about the initiative's scope and objectives.

---

## Rejection of Deceleration Calls

The announcement directly counters calls from prominent AI researchers and executives who have urged a measured approach to AI development. The AI safety community has increasingly advocated for slowing the pace of frontier model development to better understand and mitigate potential risks.

Trump's administration has instead signaled an intent to accelerate AI development within the United States, positioning the AI Force as a competitive response to developments in other nations.

---

## Industry and Policy Reactions

The announcement has sparked mixed reactions across the technology sector:

- **Accelerationists** welcome the government's commitment to AI leadership
- **Safety advocates** express concern about the rejection of deceleration approaches
- **Policy experts** await clarification on the initiative's specific objectives and funding

The initiative's structure, budget, and technical scope remain unclear, with further announcements expected in the coming weeks.
