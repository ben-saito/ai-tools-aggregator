# TechCrunch Disrupt 2026: Where the Next Wave of AI Startups Meets the Mainstream

---

TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 kicks off October 13 at Moscone West in San Francisco, and this year's edition places artificial intelligence at the center of nearly every conversation. With over 300 startups exhibiting, 200+ sessions, and 250+ tech leaders speaking, Disrupt has become the premier venue for discovering which AI bets are ready to break through — and which are running out of time.

---

## Startup Battlefield 200: The AI Race

For more than a decade, Startup Battlefield has been the launchpad for companies that later become household names. This year's 200 selected startups span multiple sectors, but AI-related ventures dominate the roster. Twenty finalists will take the Disrupt Stage competing for the $100,000 prize and the coveted Disrupt Cup.

The Battlefield format gives early-stage companies unprecedented access to investors, media, and potential customers in a single venue. For AI startups specifically, the combination of demo floor presence and structured competition creates a forcing function for teams to articulate their differentiation clearly.

---

## Sessions Focus on Practical AI Deployment

Beyond the exhibition floor, the session schedule covers AI across multiple tracks: AI product development, go-to-market strategies for AI companies, fundraising in the current environment, and infrastructure scaling. The breadth of AI coverage reflects how the technology has moved from niche to mainstream in the enterprise.

Sessions dedicated to AI topics run across all three days, with content spanning from technical deep-dives to strategic discussions about AI governance and responsible deployment. Roundtable formats allow for more interactive discussions than typical conference panels.

---

## The State of AI Investment Heading Into Disrupt

The $7.5 billion valuation of TypeSafe AI's Jev model (announced just days before Disrupt) underscores the continued appetite for infrastructure-level AI bets. Meanwhile, the TechCrunch AI news feed has documented a wave of enterprise AI adoption, with companies moving from proof-of-concept to production deployment.

Against this backdrop, Disrupt 2026 serves as both a progress report on the current AI boom and a window into the next chapter — whatever that turns out to be.
