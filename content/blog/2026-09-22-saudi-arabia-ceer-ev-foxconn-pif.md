# Saudi Arabia Launches Ceer EV Brand with Foxconn as Manufacturing Partner

---

## Background

Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF) has officially launched Ceer, a new electric vehicle brand aimed at establishing the kingdom as a major player in the global automotive industry. The joint venture between PIF and Foxconn marks Saudi Arabia's most significant step yet toward diversifying its economy away from oil dependence.

---

## Strategic Context

The launch of Ceer represents Saudi Arabia's broader Vision 2030 initiative to develop non-oil industries and create jobs for young Saudis. The automotive sector is seen as a key component of this diversification strategy, with the kingdom positioning itself as a regional manufacturing hub.

Foxconn, best known as Apple's primary manufacturing partner, brings significant expertise in electronics manufacturing and supply chain management. The company has been aggressively expanding into electric vehicle manufacturing, viewing it as a natural extension of its existing business.

---

## Market Implications

Ceer plans to produce multiple EV models targeting both domestic Saudi and export markets. The timing aligns with broader Middle East EV adoption trends, though the brand will face significant competition from established Chinese and Korean EV manufacturers already operating in the region.
