# ShieldFont: A Font That Fights Back Against AI Scrapers

The web has a new weapon against AI scraping: a font designed to poison training data without making pages unreadable for humans.

---

## What Is ShieldFont?

ShieldFont is a specially designed typeface that, when deployed on a website, causes problems for AI scrapers that crawl web pages to collect training data. The font makes pages look normal in browsers while introducing subtle artifacts that corrupt how AI systems process the text.

The key innovation: the font appears completely normal to human readers but contains Unicode characters that look identical to human eyes yet cause AI models to misprocess the underlying text data.

---

## Why This Matters for AI Training

AI companies have faced increasing legal and ethical challenges over their data collection practices. ShieldFont represents a technical response from website operators who want to opt out of AI training without blocking human visitors.

Unlike traditional robots.txt directives that AI scrapers frequently ignore, ShieldFont operates at the rendering layer, making it harder for automated systems to detect and avoid.

---

## The Arms Race Dynamic

The emergence of ShieldFont highlights the ongoing tension between AI companies that want large datasets and content creators who want compensation or control. As AI systems become more capable, the question of who controls training data has become increasingly contentious.

Website operators deploying ShieldFont hope it tips the economic calculus toward permission-based data collection rather than silent scraping.
