# Nscale IPO Filing Reveals $103B in Contracts, $35B Valuation Target

British AI data center developer Nscale has filed for an IPO on the New York Stock Exchange, revealing detailed financials that paint a picture of rapid growth paired with massive customer concentration risk. The filing, first reported by the Financial Times, shows Nscale targeting a $35 billion valuation while seeking to raise $3 billion in the offering.

---

## From Crypto Mining to AI Infrastructure

Nscale was spun out of Australian cryptocurrency mining company Arkon Energy just two years ago, and has since accumulated $103 billion worth of contracts according to its IPO filing. The company depends heavily on tech giants Microsoft and Anthropic for the majority of its revenue -- a concentration risk that the filing itself acknowledges as a material vulnerability.

The company's relationship with Anthropic is particularly noteworthy. The AI lab's agreement with Nscale is contingent on Nscale obtaining financing, and Anthropic retains the right to walk away from or cancel the deal if Nscale fails to hit specified milestones detailed in the filing.

---

## Financials: Rapid Revenue Growth, Expanding Losses

For the six months ended June 30, Nscale reported revenue of $140.6 million, a significant jump from $10.4 million in the same period the prior year. However, net losses tell a different story: $1.02 billion in losses compared to $369 million in the prior year period, reflecting the capital-intensive nature of AI data center buildout.

Earlier this month, Nvidia -- one of Nscale's major investors -- agreed to provide $1 billion in convertible debt as part of a larger $3.1 billion financing deal. The startup was previously valued at $5.6 billion in its Series B round.

---

## Customer Concentration as Industry-Wide Signal

Nscale's customer concentration is not unique in the AI infrastructure space. A recent paper by credit hedge fund Sona Asset Management, featured in the Financial Times, highlighted the deep interconnectedness of the AI industry. While Sona noted this is not necessarily problematic, a single setback or strategic shift by a major player can cascade through the entire ecosystem.

The company plans to list on the NYSE under the ticker NSCL, according to reports.

---

## Market Context

The IPO will serve as a bellwether for investor appetite for concentrated AI infrastructure bets. Nscale's dependency on Microsoft and Anthropic -- both of which are also Nvidia's most important AI compute customers -- means the offering will test whether public markets are comfortable with single-customer risk at the infrastructure layer.
