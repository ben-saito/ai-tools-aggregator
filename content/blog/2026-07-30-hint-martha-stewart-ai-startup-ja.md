# Martha Stewart Co-Founds AI Home Management Startup "Hint"

Martha Stewart, the lifestyle mogul known for her expertise in home management, has co-founded Hint -- a new AI startup that aims to bring artificial intelligence to everyday home management tasks.

---

## What is Hint?

Hint is positioned as an "AI for your home." The startup combines property records, maintenance schedules, home documents, and an AI assistant into a single app. The goal is to help homeowners manage the complex information ecosystem of a modern home -- from appliance manuals and warranty documents to seasonal maintenance reminders and contractor contacts.

The company was founded by Martha Stewart alongside experienced technologists, bringing her decades of home management expertise together with modern AI capabilities.

---

## The Vision

Stewart described the product as a way to organize and make searchable all the information that accumulates around homeownership. Rather than digging through drawers for appliance manuals or forgetting when the last HVAC service was performed, homeowners can ask the AI assistant questions and get instant answers from their accumulated home data.

The AI assistant can presumably answer questions like "When was my roof last replaced?" or "What is the model number of my refrigerator?" by pulling from organized home records.

---

## Market Context

AI-powered personal assistants have made significant strides in productivity and business applications. The home management space represents a less explored but potentially large market, as households contain substantial undocumented information that could benefit from AI organization.

---

## Key Details

- **Co-founders**: Martha Stewart and technologists
- **Product**: AI home management assistant app
- **Features**: Property records, maintenance schedules, document storage, AI Q&A
- **Target**: Homeowners managing complex property information
