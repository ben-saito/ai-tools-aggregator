# Wispr Raises $280M at $2B Valuation, Expands Beyond Dictation

Wispr, a startup known for its AI-powered voice dictation tool, announced on Monday that it raised $280 million in Series B funding led by Menlo Ventures, reaching a $2 billion valuation. This brings the company's total funding to over $361 million, with the last round occurring less than 10 months ago.

---

## From Dictation to Meetings: Wispr's Expansion

Wispr initially gained traction with its AI dictation tool that accurately transcribes spoken words into text. The new capital will fund expansion into the meetings space, where Wispr has already launched a note-taker tool. This positions Wispr to compete with established players like Otter.ai and Grain in the AI-powered meeting notes market.

The company's voice AI technology differentiates itself through context awareness and the ability to understand natural speech patterns, making it suitable for professionals who conduct many meetings daily.

---

## Growing Interest in Voice AI

Wispr's successful fundraise reflects continued investor appetite for AI companies applying large language models to specific workflows. The voice AI market has seen particular growth as enterprises seek to reduce manual documentation overhead and capture meeting insights automatically.
