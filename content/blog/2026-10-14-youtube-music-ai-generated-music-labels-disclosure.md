# YouTube Music Rolls Out AI-Generated Music Labels as Flood of AI-Uploaded Content Grows

---

YouTube Music announced the rollout of a new labeling system to help listeners identify tracks created with the help of generative AI. The new labels appear on the player page and provide additional context about the specific role AI played in the track's creation, including whether AI was used in composition, vocals, or both.

## Platform-Wide Labeling

The labeling system extends beyond YouTube Music to the main YouTube app as well. When users click on the AI label, they are shown additional information about how AI was used in creating the track. YouTube says the feature is designed to improve transparency as AI-generated music proliferates across streaming platforms.

## Industry Context

The move comes as AI-generated music has surged across streaming platforms. Major labels have raised concerns about the growing volume of AI-generated uploads, with some studios pushing for mandatory disclosure of AI involvement in music creation. YouTube Music's labeling approach represents a proactive step toward transparency rather than outright exclusion.

## Related Developments

Deezer has announced a similar feature that lets users filter out AI-generated music entirely, reflecting divergent approaches to handling AI content — YouTube Music focuses on disclosure while Deezer offers exclusion as an option.
