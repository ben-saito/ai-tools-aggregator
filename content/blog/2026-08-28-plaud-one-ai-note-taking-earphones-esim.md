# Plaud One: AI Note-Taking Earphones with eSIM Case Hit Market

Plaud, a hardware startup with over 2.5 million users across its hardware and software products, has launched a new AI note-taking device in an earphone form factor. Called Plaud One, the device resembles Apple's AirPods in a bare-bones design and is capable of recording calls, transcribing conversations, and invoking an AI agent to perform follow-up tasks.

---

## From AirPods to AI Agent: The Hardware Pivot

Hardware companies have identified note-taking as one of the easiest AI use cases to build for. Plaud is now applying that insight to a wearable format with Plaud One. The earphones can record calls and in-person conversations, while the charging case doubles as a recording device for capturing notes during meetings or presentations.

Once a conversation is transcribed, Plaud's AI agent can connect to productivity tools including Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, and Slack. The agent can then perform tasks such as writing follow-up emails, creating documents, or generating presentations based on the contents of the recorded conversation.

Buyers receive 300 minutes of free transcription per month. Additional transcription time is available from $8.33 per month on an annual plan.

---

## Competing in a Crowded Market

Plaud is not the first company to pursue this form factor. Rivals Viaim and Anker already sell similar note-taking earbuds. Plaud acknowledges this competition but argues that its differentiation lies in its software and AI capabilities rather than the hardware itself.

The company cites an "always-ready interface" as a key advantage. CEO Nathan Xu stated: "For AI, we need a wearable and an always-ready interface -- one that people love to wear, is socially acceptable, and private by design."

The challenge Plaud faces is convincing users to adopt a dedicated device when existing wireless earbuds from Apple, Google, Samsung, and other manufacturers can perform the same recording and transcription tasks when paired with established note-taking applications such as Granola, Fathom, or Read AI.

---

## Availability

Plaud One is available for pre-order now at $399. The device positions itself as an open-source robot platform, though the AI agent capabilities rely on Plaud's proprietary software stack for task execution across connected applications.
