# Okta Acquires AI Security Startup Permiso for Approximately $200M

Identity management company Okta has acquired AI security startup Permiso for approximately $200 million, strengthening its capabilities in detecting and responding to threats involving AI agents and non-human identities in cloud environments.

---

## Strategic Rationale

Permiso specializes in identity threat detection for AI agents and machine identities, an increasingly critical capability as enterprises deploy AI systems that operate autonomously across cloud infrastructure. The acquisition addresses a gap in traditional identity and access management (IAM) tools, which were designed primarily for human users rather than AI agents.

---

## Non-Human Identity Security

The deal comes as enterprises rapidly adopt AI agents that require persistent access to cloud resources, APIs, and sensitive data. These non-human identities present new security challenges: they often operate with elevated privileges, lack traditional authentication factors, and can be difficult to monitor using conventional security tools.

---

## Market Context

The AI agent security market is emerging rapidly. Traditional security vendors are acquiring AI-native startups to close gaps in their offerings. Okta's move follows a pattern of established enterprise software companies acquiring younger AI security firms to stay competitive in an evolving threat landscape.
