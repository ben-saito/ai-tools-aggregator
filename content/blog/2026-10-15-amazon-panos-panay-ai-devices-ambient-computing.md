# Amazon's Panos Panay: AI Devices Will Replace the App Paradigm

---

Amazon is placing its bets on **ambient computing**, not apps, as the future of personal AI. At TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 on Thursday, Panos Panay, Amazon's SVP of Devices, Alexa & Leo, took the stage to articulate a vision where AI assistants handle tasks across domains without users needing to manage individual applications.

"A lot of times, what you're doing right now — what we all do right now — is we have to remember what we're asking our tool to do for us," Panay explained. "It's like point, shoot, return," he continued, describing the current smartphone interaction model as fundamentally broken. The promise of AI, in Panay's framing, is an end to this fragmented experience.

The event coincided with Amazon's launch of new AI wearables from Bee, the company's personal AI device subsidiary acquired last year. The **Bee Frontier** wearable (wrist or pinned configuration) and the **Bee Eclipse** (credit-card-sized, magnetically attaches to smartphones) represent Amazon's hardware bet on always-present AI assistance.

---

## From Apps to Agents: The Ambient Computing Vision

Panay's keynote positioned ambient computing as the logical evolution beyond today's app-centric model. Rather than opening separate apps for different tasks, users would interact with a persistent AI layer that orchestrates across services. The AI would maintain context across interactions, remembering preferences and completing multi-step tasks without explicit user instruction.

This vision aligns with broader industry movement toward **agentic AI** — systems that autonomously navigate digital and physical environments on behalf of users. Amazon's strategy combines proprietary AI models with integrations across retail, home automation, and wearable hardware.

The Gemini app connection in Universal Cart exemplifies this pattern: AI as a persistent shopping assistant that maintains cart state across sessions and retailers, surfacing relevant products based on demonstrated preferences rather than search queries.

---

## Amazon's AI Hardware Strategy

Amazon's AI device portfolio under the Bee brand represents a deliberate push into wearable AI, competing against devices like the Plaud Note and Pocket. The hardware-software integration approach — combining custom silicon with Alexa and Leo AI services — positions Amazon to own more of the AI interaction surface rather than cede it to smartphone platforms.

The company declined to specify timelines for broader AI agent deployments, pointing instead to its December "AI & Autonomy Day" event. But the building blocks — ambient computing APIs, wearable hardware, and deep assistant integration — are clearly being assembled for a coordinated launch.

---

## Competitive Context

Amazon joins Google, Apple, and Meta in racing to define the next computing paradigm. Each company is betting that AI-first devices will eventually replace or supplement the smartphone as the primary AI interaction point. The outcome will determine which ecosystem controls the emerging AI assistant layer.
