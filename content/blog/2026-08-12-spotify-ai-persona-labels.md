# Spotify to Label AI-Generated Artists with "AI Persona" Badges

Spotify announced on August 11, 2026, that it will begin labeling artist profiles representing AI-generated identities with "AI Persona" badges and exclude their music from editorial and algorithmic recommendations by default.

---

## New AI Persona Label Policy

Starting in mid-September, Spotify users will see "AI Persona" badges appear on artist profiles where the identity is confirmed or suspected to be AI-generated. While Spotify will accept self-disclosure from artists, the company says it will not rely solely on voluntary identification.

Spotify will proactively review profiles that meet predefined audience thresholds, prioritizing the most-listened-to artists to ensure broad coverage. Once labeled, badges will appear in the artist banner, About section, Search results, and track rows across playlists.

---

## Exclusion from Recommendations by Default

AI Persona artists will be excluded from Spotify's editorial playlists, algorithmic Discover Weekly and Release Radar recommendations, and other personalized suggestions. Users who explicitly follow an AI Persona artist will still receive their new releases, since following represents an intentional choice to engage with that creator.

Spotify first announced its AI music policies in September 2025, initially focusing on identifying and labeling AI-generated music through industry-standard techniques. The new Persona label expands this framework to cover the artists themselves, not just the music.

---

## Broader Context: AI Music on Streaming Platforms

AI-generated music has proliferated on streaming platforms, with some creators generating thousands of tracks using AI tools. Spotify's move mirrors broader industry efforts to distinguish human-created from AI-generated content as AI-generated music becomes increasingly indistinguishable from recordings made by human artists.

The policy comes as the music industry grapples with questions about royalty distribution, copyright, and the authenticity of artist identities on streaming platforms.
