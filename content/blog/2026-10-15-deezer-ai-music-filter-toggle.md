# Deezer AI Music Filter Toggle: Blocking AI-Generated Content

---

Streaming platform Deezer remains steadfast in its intent to help users avoid listening to AI-generated music: the company on Tuesday said it is working on a new toggle that lets users filter out AI-generated music uploaded to its platform, marking a significant step in the ongoing debate over transparency in the age of generative AI.

The toggle, expected to be available before the end of 2025, will allow listeners to completely remove AI-generated tracks from their recommendations and searches. Deezer says the feature responds to growing listener demand for transparency around how music is made — and who (or what) made it.

---

## The AI Music Flood

Deezer's announcement comes as AI-generated music floods streaming platforms at an unprecedented rate. The company says AI-uploaded tracks now represent more than 50% of daily uploads to the platform, a figure that has alarmed both human musicians and music industry executives.

The new filter is not just a recommendation tweak — it is a fundamental option for users who want to ensure their listening experience remains entirely human-made. Deezer says the toggle will work at the account level, affecting recommendations across all playlists and discovery features.

---

## How the Filter Works

Unlike simple genre or mood filters, Deezer's AI detection system analyzes multiple signals to identify AI-generated content: metadata patterns, audio characteristics, and upload behavior. The company has been developing the detection model in collaboration with music industry partners and rights holders.

Tracks that are identified as AI-generated will be labeled accordingly, and users who enable the filter will never see those tracks in their recommendations or search results. Deezer says the filter will initially focus on fully AI-generated tracks, with hybrid human-AI compositions to be addressed in a future update.

---

## Industry Implications

Deezer's move puts pressure on other streaming platforms to follow suit. Spotify and Apple Music have both faced criticism for their handling of AI-generated music, with neither platform offering a comparable opt-out feature. The music industry has been calling for greater transparency around AI content, and Deezer's filter represents one of the most concrete responses from a major streaming platform to date.

The debate over AI music is likely to intensify as generative models become more sophisticated. Deezer says the filter is part of a broader commitment to supporting human artists and ensuring that listeners have genuine choice in what they consume.

---

## Looking Ahead

Deezer says the AI filter will roll out in phases, starting with a beta release in the coming weeks. The company is also exploring ways to help human artists highlight the authenticity of their work, potentially through a "Verified Human" badge system.

The toggle will be free for all Deezer users, reflecting the company's position that transparency should not come at an additional cost. As AI-generated content continues to proliferate, Deezer's filter could become a significant differentiator in the streaming market.
