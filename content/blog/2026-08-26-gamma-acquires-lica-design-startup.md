# Gamma Acquires Design Startup Lica to Build AI Research Division

Gamma, the presentation and design platform, has acquired Lica, an Accel-backed design startup, with Lica's co-founders joining Gamma's newly formed research team.

---

## Strategic Rationale

The acquisition signals Gamma's intent to expand beyond presentation software into broader AI-powered design tools. Lica's team brings expertise in design systems and collaborative design workflows that complement Gamma's existing product suite.

Lica co-founders will lead Gamma's new research division, focusing on next-generation AI-assisted design capabilities. The move reflects a broader trend of design platforms investing in AI research to stay competitive against rapid AI-enabled alternatives.

---

## Design AI Landscape

Design tools are experiencing significant AI disruption, from automatic layout generation to intelligent asset creation. Gamma's acquisition of Lica positions the company to build proprietary AI capabilities rather than relying solely on third-party model integrations.

The design platform market has seen multiple AI-focused acquisitions recently, as companies seek to combine traditional design expertise with modern machine learning approaches.
