# Gamma Acquires Design Startup Lica to Build AI Research Team

Gamma, the presentation and document design platform backed by Accel, has acquired Lica, a design startup that had raised funding from the same venture firm. The Lica co-founders will join Gamma to work on the company's newly forming research team, according to a TechCrunch report.

---

## Building Research Capability

Lica's focus was on building AI models optimized for communication — including video and other multimedia formats. The acquisition gives Gamma not just technology but talent: the Lica founding team brings deep expertise in AI-native product design that Gamma hopes to leverage as it expands beyond presentations into broader AI-augmented creative tools.

"Lica wanted to build AI models around communication methods, including video and design," said Lica's CEO in a statement. "Joining Gamma gives us the platform to pursue that vision at scale."

The terms of the acquisition were not disclosed. Lica had raised a seed round from Accel, making this at least a partial exit for the VC firm.

---

## Gamma's AI Evolution

Gamma has positioned itself as an AI-first design platform, using generative AI to help users create presentations, documents, and web pages. The acquisition of Lica suggests the company is moving beyond applying AI to existing design tasks and is instead investing in foundational AI research capabilities.

Building an in-house research team is a significant strategic move for a company of Gamma's size, suggesting ambitions that extend beyond incremental product improvements. The company appears to be positioning itself as a serious AI developer, not just an AI user.

---

## Design AI Landscape

The acquisition reflects ongoing consolidation in the AI design tools space. As foundation models become more capable, companies are competing not just on UI and distribution but on the quality of their AI models. Startups with novel approaches to AI-assisted design have become attractive acquisition targets for larger platforms looking to accelerate their AI roadmaps.

Accel's dual investment in both Gamma and Lica creates interesting dynamics — the venture firm now has a stake in both the acquirer and the acquired, aligning interests in the combined company's success.

---

## What Lica Brings

Beyond the co-founders, Lica brings expertise in multimodal AI — systems that can understand and generate across text, images, video, and other media. This aligns with Gamma's vision of an AI design assistant that can work across all the formats teams use to communicate ideas.

The design AI space has grown crowded, with incumbits like Canva, Notion, and Microsoft all investing heavily in AI features. Gamma's acquisition of Lica suggests the company is betting that differentiated AI research capabilities will be key to standing out in a competitive market.
