# Apple Reportedly Building Enterprise AI Server with M8 Ultra Chips for 2029

Apple is reportedly planning to launch an enterprise AI server using its M8 Ultra chips in 2029, according to The Information. If realized, this would mark Apple's first enterprise server product in decades.

---

## Background: Mac Products Popular with AI Developers

Apple's Mac mini and Mac Studio have become popular choices for AI developers and companies running AI workloads. AI companies including OpenAI and Anthropic have purchased "tens of thousands" of Mac minis and Mac Studios for training AI agents through trial-and-error reinforcement learning, according to the report.

---

## M8 Ultra Configuration and Nvidia Partnership

The enterprise server would come in two configurations featuring either two or four of Apple's M8 Ultra chips. Apple is also considering connecting its M8 chips using data center networking equipment from Nvidia, potentially incorporating Nvidia's NVLink Fusion technology.

The Information cautioned that the project could still be canceled or proceed without Nvidia's technology.

---

## New CEO John Ternus's Legacy

Apple officially transitioned from Tim Cook to John Ternus as CEO on September 1, 2026. If Apple's AI server plans come to fruition, it could become a notable part of Ternus's legacy.

---

## Challenge: Memory Chip Shortage

Both Apple's server plans and the growing popularity of Mac computers during the AI boom face a significant supply chain challenge: the ongoing memory chip shortage. This shortage has led to price increases for gaming devices, flagship smartphones, and a wide range of Apple products.
