# General Intuition Raises $6B as AI Robotics Pivot Accelerates

General Intuition, the AI startup building foundation models for generalized AI agents, is in talks to raise a new round at a $6 billion pre-money valuation backed by Valor Ventures, Point72 Ventures, and Seven Seven Six. The round comes just weeks after the company raised $320 million at a $2.3 billion valuation.

---

## From Video Game Clips to AI Agents

CEO Pim de Witte spun General Intuition out of Medal, a video game clip-sharing platform, last October. The pivot leveraged an unexpected asset: hundreds of millions of hours of gameplay footage with action labels recording which buttons players pressed and when. This dataset became the foundation for training AI agents to understand spatial movement and temporal sequences.

The company's core technology focuses on giving AI agents the ability to navigate through physical and digital spaces over extended time periods. Unlike narrow task-specific models, General Intuition's approach aims to create agents that can maintain context and continuity across complex, multi-step operations.

---

## Robotics Ambitions Drive New Valuation

The fresh capital will be used to expand into robotics, building on the company's foundation model technology. Existing investors Khosla Ventures and General Catalyst are also participating in the round. A source close to the deal described the round as oversubscribed, with the startup continuing to field additional investor interest.

General Intuition's approach differs from pure robotics companies by starting with a generalized foundation model that can be adapted to various embodiments and use cases. The company believes that data from video game environments provides a unique training signal for understanding physical world dynamics and human behavior patterns.

---

## Industry Context

The $6 billion valuation places General Intuition among the most valuable AI startups outside the established frontier lab tier. The company's focus on embodied AI agents and robotics applications positions it in a space that has attracted significant venture capital attention as AI capabilities advance toward real-world deployment.
