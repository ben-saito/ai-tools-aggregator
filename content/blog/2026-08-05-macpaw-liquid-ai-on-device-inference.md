# MacPaw Partners with Liquid AI to Bring On-Device AI Inference to App Developers

Ukraine-based app developer MacPaw has partnered with Liquid AI to power its products with locally hosted AI models, with plans to eventually make the technology stack available to third-party developers.

MacPaw is best known for itsCleanup utility and SetApp, a subscription-based app store. The company unveiled its AI assistant Eney last year and is now building a locally hosted version using Liquid AI's on-device inference system called Elix.

The partnership addresses growing demand for privacy-preserving AI features that run entirely on-device rather than relying on cloud APIs. By using Liquid AI's models, MacPaw aims to offer AI capabilities without sending user data to external servers.

MacPaw is also preparing SetApp for AI applications, introducing credit-based pricing plans that allow users to pay for AI inference usage without a flat subscription fee. The move positions SetApp as a distribution platform for AI-native Mac applications.

Liquid AI has been positioning its efficient on-device models as an alternative to cloud-based AI APIs, particularly for applications where latency, privacy, or offline functionality are priorities.

The collaboration is notable as one of the first instances of a major Mac app developer bundling on-device AI inference as a platform-level offering for other developers.
