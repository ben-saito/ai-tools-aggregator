# Trump Administration Secures Voluntary AI Safety Commitments from Tech Firms

The Trump administration has secured voluntary safety commitments from approximately two dozen AI companies, in an agreement announced September 30, 2026. The companies agreed to implement safety controls and undergo independent audits, though no new legal requirements were established.

---

## The Agreement

Under the agreement, participating firms committed to:

- Implementing safety controls recommended by the White House
- Submitting to independent external audits of AI systems
- Regularly meeting to discuss best practices
- Setting common AI safety standards and benchmarks

Signatories included major AI players including Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI, among others. Several of these companies had already committed to external audits prior to the agreement, making this announcement more of a formalization than a new obligation.

---

## Trump's "Constitution" Comparison

Trump characterized the agreement as foundational. "It's almost like a constitution, in a way," he told reporters at the White House following a lunch with tech leaders. He described it as a framework for AI safety that could eventually become law.

The administration indicated the agreement may serve as a precursor to formal legislation. "Eventually, it may make sense to codify these recommendations," the agreement stated near its conclusion.

---

## Limited Enforcement

Critics note the agreement relies entirely on voluntary compliance. No penalties or enforcement mechanisms were established. The administration stressed the importance of industry self-regulation while leaving open the possibility of future legal requirements.

AI safety researchers have questioned whether voluntary commitments from companies with strong financial incentives to deploy rapidly can be effective. The history of voluntary AI safety agreements is mixed, with compliance verification remaining a significant challenge.

---

## What's Missing

Very few details have been released about how the new round of safety audits would work in practice. Trump posted only a summary of the accord on Truth Social, with full details not publicly available as of the announcement date.

Independent AI safety researchers noted the announcement raises questions about audit scope, methodology, and what happens when companies identify problems during audits.

---

* The Trump AI safety agreement was announced September 30, 2026, based on reporting by Ars Technica.
