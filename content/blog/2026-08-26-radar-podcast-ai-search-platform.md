# Radar Launches Platform Making 130,000 Podcasts Searchable by AI Agents

AI startup Radar has unveiled its Podcast Intelligence Platform, which transcribes and indexes over 130,000 podcasts, making their conversations searchable via web and accessible to AI agents through an API and MCP (Model Context Protocol) integration.

---

## Solving Podcast Discovery

Podcasts represent a vast but largely inaccessible knowledge base, with hours of audio content that cannot be easily searched or referenced. Radar's platform solves this by providing full transcription, segment-level indexing, speaker identification, and keyword extraction across a massive podcast catalog.

The MCP integration allows AI agents to access podcast content directly within their context windows, enabling new use cases such as detailed analysis of podcast discussions, cross-source comparisons, and targeted research across episodes.

---

## Enterprise Applications

Beyond search, Radar offers enterprise customers the ability to generate custom training datasets from domain-specific podcasts. This service is particularly valuable for organizations in finance, law, and healthcare, where podcasts contain specialized knowledge that general-purpose LLMs often lack.

The platform's API-first design means developers can build podcast-aware applications without managing audio processing infrastructure themselves.
