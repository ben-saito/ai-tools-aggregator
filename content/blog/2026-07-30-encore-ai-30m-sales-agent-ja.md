# Encore AI Raises $30M to Train AI Agents from Sales Call Analysis

AI startup Encore AI has developed a system that identifies effective sales techniques by analyzing sales call data and converts them into AI agent playbooks. The company secured $30 million in funding with this approach.

According to TechCrunch, Encore AI analyzes customer calls, messages, and CRM data to rapidly identify the most effective sales techniques and structure them into actionable playbooks.

---

## AI Agents That Learn from Call Data

Encore AI's system trains AI agents using actual call data, enabling them to replicate human sales expertise.

By analyzing transcripts of successful sales calls, the system identifies patterns and generates playbooks that AI agents can follow. This allows human sales representatives to extend their learned techniques to AI agents, scaling sales expertise across teams.

---

## Sales Operations and AI Agents Today

Global sales operations face challenges including talent shortages and rising costs. Encore AI's approach aims to address these through automated training from call data.

The $30 million funding represents a significant investment in this space, reflecting accelerating AI adoption in Sales Tech.
