# Two Federal Agencies Hacked in One Month, Millions of Personnel Records Exposed

The United States has experienced a significant cybersecurity breach with two federal agencies compromised within a single month, resulting in the exposure of millions of personnel records. The attacks have raised serious concerns about the federal government's ability to protect sensitive data.

---

## The Breaches

The first breach targeted an agency managing federal employee records, exposing personal information including names, Social Security numbers, and employment details of current and former federal workers. The second breach involved a separate agency, with attackers gaining access to sensitive internal communications and personnel files.

Security researchers have identified the attacks as part of a coordinated campaign, with attackers exploiting vulnerabilities in legacy systems that lack modern security protections. The scale of the breaches has been described as a "bonanza" of sensitive data by cybersecurity experts.

## Government Response

Federal cybersecurity officials have acknowledged the breaches and launched investigations into the incidents. The affected agencies have begun notifying potentially impacted individuals and offering credit monitoring services. Congressional leaders have called for briefings on the state of federal cybersecurity infrastructure.

## Security Implications

The breaches highlight persistent gaps in federal cybersecurity defenses. Despite years of warnings from security researchers, many federal agencies continue to rely on outdated systems that are difficult to secure. The incidents have renewed calls for increased funding for cybersecurity modernization across the federal government.
