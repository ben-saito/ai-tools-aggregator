# OpenAI Gains Ground on Anthropic Among Enterprise Users, Ramp Data Shows

OpenAI is regaining ground on Anthropic among enterprise users, according to new data from Ramp, a corporate credit card and expense management company.

Anthropic overtook OpenAI in May with 41% market share versus OpenAI's 39% among Ramp's paying business users. As of July, Anthropic holds nearly 44% while OpenAI has recovered to nearly 40%, according to Ramp's data covering more than 70,000 U.S. businesses.

Ramp economist Ara Kharazian noted that OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol is "really good, increasingly the choice for developers," while Anthropic's Fable 5 "disappointed both in adoption and real-world application given price + data retention requirements imposed by regulators."

The data suggests both companies should be growing enterprise revenue even as they compete for market share. The percentage of companies paying for AI among Ramp customers climbed to nearly 56% by July, up from 50% in March.

The findings highlight concerns about how "sticky" enterprise AI spending really is. Businesses appear willing to switch providers as each lab releases new models, volatility that should give both companies' investors pause.
