# Meta's AI Agent Muse Reaches No. 2 App in US

Meta's dedicated AI app Muse has climbed to become the second-most-popular app in the US App Store, demonstrating strong early traction for the company's standalone AI assistant.

---

## Muse's Rapid Rise

Meta's newest app Muse is off to a slower start than the company's other apps, like Meta AI or Threads — but relative to other AI agents, the performance is notable. Muse reached the No. 2 position in the US App Store rankings, placing it behind only ChatGPT in the AI category.

Muse differs from Meta AI by being a dedicated standalone application rather than a feature embedded in Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp. The app is centered on voice-based AI interactions and has been positioned as a productivity and creative tool.

## Meta's AI Strategy

The launch of Muse represents Meta's clearest statement yet that it intends to compete directly with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google in the consumer AI market. Meta AI, embedded across the company's social platforms, reaches billions of users — but Muse targets a different use case: focused, distraction-free AI assistance.

The app includes features for summarization, writing assistance, image analysis, and voice conversations. Unlike ChatGPT's voice mode, Muse's voice interactions are designed to feel more like speaking to an intelligent assistant than using a voice interface.

## Market Reception

The strong App Store performance comes despite limited marketing. Meta appears to have relied on cross-promotion from its existing user base rather than large-scale advertising campaigns.

Downloads alone don't tell the full story — retention and daily active usage will determine whether Muse sustains its ranking. Early reviews highlight the voice quality and conversational depth as strengths, while some users note limitations compared to ChatGPT's reasoning capabilities.

## Competitive Landscape

Muse enters a market where ChatGPT dominates, but user appetite for multiple AI assistants appears genuine. Google's Gemini app, Anthropic's Claude, and various other AI products all claim significant user bases.

Meta's advantages include its massive distribution network, the ability to cross-promote across Instagram and Facebook, and significant compute resources from its data center investments.
