# Trump Purchased SpaceX Shares Worth $50,000 Two Weeks After IPO

President Donald Trump purchased as much as $50,000 worth of SpaceX shares on June 23, according to a financial disclosure first reported by Reuters, two weeks after the record-setting IPO of Elon Musk's company.

The purchase came while SpaceX shares were trading in the mid-$150 range, well below their post-IPO highs of over $200. At the end of trading on Monday, shares closed at SpaceX's IPO price of $135.

---

## Context

The timing of the purchase is notable. Trump has been a frequent ally of Musk, and SpaceX's IPO was one of the most anticipated public offerings of 2026.
