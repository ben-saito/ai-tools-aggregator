# Waymo Ordered to Stop Overnight Charging in Santa Monica — Judge Grants Preliminary Injunction

---

## Court Rules Against Waymo in Santa Monica Nuisance Lawsuit

A Los Angeles Superior Court judge has granted a preliminary injunction requiring Waymo to stop overnight charging operations at its Santa Monica facilities. The ruling marks a significant escalation in the ongoing legal battle between the autonomous vehicle company and the City of Santa Monica, which filed a public nuisance lawsuit against Waymo over disturbances caused by its fleet of vehicles.

Judge Bradley S. Phillips ruled this week in favor of the city's motion for preliminary injunction, finding that the constant stream of vehicles at Waymo's charging stations creates light and sound pollution and causes undue traffic congestion on local streets and alleys. The ruling effectively bars Waymo from operating its charging facilities on Broadway between 11 pm and 6 am.

---

## Resident Complaints: "Beep Beep Beep" at All Hours

The city's lawsuit centered on complaints from residents living near Waymo's charging stations at Broadway and 12th and Euclid Streets. Neighbors described persistent disturbances from the autonomous vehicles, including backup beeping sounds and the acceleration noise characteristic of electric vehicles.

Christopher Potter, who lives directly adjacent to the charging lots, described the situation in court filings: "The constant 'beep beep beep' sound as the autonomous vehicles back out of their spaces is an incessant disturbance that hinders both our tranquility during the day and our peace during the night."

Another neighbor, who created the website WaymoProblems.org, noted that the situation had significantly improved since the preliminary injunction took effect. "You can hear the crickets, you can hear your thoughts — it's not a fleet of locusts descending on the neighborhood, it's incredible," he told Ars Technica.

---

## Waymo's Operations Disrupted

Under the new court order, Waymo must cease all overnight charging operations at the affected facilities. The charging stations sit directly across the street from a private school and are surrounded by apartment buildings and other residences. The company had been operating the lots around the clock, with particularly intensive usage between 2 am and 6 am, coinciding with bar closing hours when demand for autonomous vehicle pickups surges.

Waymo has stated that it will comply with the court order while evaluating its legal options. The company has argued that its operations are essential for providing transportation services to the Santa Monica area and that the charging schedule is necessary to maintain fleet availability during peak demand hours.

---

## Broader Implications for AV Operations

The ruling represents a test case for how autonomous vehicle companies manage the operational footprint of their charging infrastructure in dense residential areas. As AV fleets scale up in urban environments, the noise and light pollution from large-scale charging operations may become an increasingly common point of contention with local communities.

Santa Monica Mayor Caroline Torosis has emphasized that the city's action is about protecting residents' quality of life. "Our residents' ability to sleep peacefully in their own homes is not negotiable," she said in a statement following the ruling.
