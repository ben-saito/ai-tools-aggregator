# Gamma Acquires Design Startup Lica to Build Research Division

Presentation startup Gamma has acquired Lica, an Accel-backed design startup that builds tools for turning screenshots and recordings into project presentations and videos. Lica's co-founders will lead Gamma's new research division focused on AI-augmented design.

---

## Lica's Background

Founded in 2023 by Priyaa Kalyanaraman and Purvanshi Mehta, Lica raised $4 million from Accel, South Park Commons, and Village Global. The company originally focused on video presentation tools that could automatically generate slides from screen recordings.

Lica's technology aligns with Gamma's vision of expanding what presentations can look and feel like. The company has been exploring image generation features alongside its core presentation tools.

---

## Research Division Plans

Lica's co-founders will lead Gamma's new research team, working on customizing AI models for different audience communication styles and presentation preferences. The goal is to build a platform where AI can personalize both the content and delivery format of presentations.

Gamma will continue focusing on its core presentation business while expanding into AI-augmented design research — a notable move as design software companies increasingly invest in proprietary AI research capabilities.
