# Robot Brain Builders Push Beyond the GPT-2 Era

Physical AI is emerging as a new frontier. Researchers building AI brains for robots are moving beyond the so-called "GPT-2 era" toward more integrated systems that combine physical embodiment with cognitive capabilities.

---

##  The Traditional Gap: AI and Robotics

Previous robot AI systems were highly specialized for specific tasks but lacked general-purpose intelligence. Researchers have long struggled with how physical embodiment affects the acquisition of broad knowledge in AI systems.

---

##  New Approach: Integrating Body and AI

The Physical AI concept unifies modeling of a robot's physical constraints with AI cognitive capabilities. This approach enables robots to evolve from simple automation tools into autonomous systems capable of reasoning and acting in complex environments.

---

##  Industry Applications

Practical applications in manufacturing, logistics, and healthcare are approaching. Unlike traditional industrial robots limited to pre-programmed movements, new AI-enabled robots possess adaptability to handle unknown situations.
