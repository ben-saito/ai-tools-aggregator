# Etched Valued at $21B After $700M Raise — Jane Street Leads After Piloting AI Cluster

AI chip startup Etched announced on August 18, 2026, that it raised $700 million in a new funding round, led by Jane Street, pushing its valuation to $21 billion. The leap from $10.3 billion just one month prior marks one of the fastest valuation jumps in AI hardware history.

---

## Valuation Trajectory

- December 2025: $5 billion
- July 2026: $10.3 billion (Series C, $300M)
- August 2026: $21 billion (Series D, $700M)

The $11 billion increase in a single month represents an extraordinary acceleration, even by AI standards. From December to August, Etched's valuation grew more than 4x in roughly eight months.

---

## Jane Street as Lead Investor

The standout detail of this round is that Jane Street — the renowned quantitative trading firm — became the lead investor only after piloting Etched's AI cluster system in production. After testing the system, Jane Street concluded it was "deeply impressed" and found "no factors that would impede future growth," leading the $700M Series D round.

Etched is developing the Sohu chip, an AI inference accelerator designed as a lower-cost, lower-power alternative to NVIDIA GPUs for inference workloads. The chip is purpose-built for running large language model inference at scale.
