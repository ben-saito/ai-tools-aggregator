# Snorkel AI Raises $350M Series E at $3.5B Valuation

Snorkel AI announced on September 22nd a $350 million Series E funding round led by Insight Partners and S32, valuing the seven-year-old startup at $3.5 billion -- nearly triple the $1.3 billion valuation from its Series D round 17 months ago.

---

## Shift to Data-as-a-Service Model

While Snorkel originally provided software for data labeling automation, the company shifted last year to providing completed datasets to customers under a "data-as-a-service" model. Rather than operating as a human expert marketplace, Snorkel now sells reinforcement learning (RL) environments and complete datasets, with payments to human experts accounted for as cost of goods sold rather than headline revenue.

---

## 18-Fold Revenue Growth in 12 Months

Snorkel reports an annualized revenue run-rate of $375 million -- an 18-fold increase over the past 12 months. This growth is driven by AI labs' insatiable appetite for high-end training data. The company launched commercially in 2019 after four years of research by co-founder and CEO Alex Ratner and his team at a Stanford AI lab.
