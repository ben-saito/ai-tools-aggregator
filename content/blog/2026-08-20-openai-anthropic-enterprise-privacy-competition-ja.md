## The Privacy Race in Enterprise AI

Both OpenAI and Anthropic have been positioning privacy as a key selling point for enterprise customers, particularly those in regulated industries like healthcare, finance, and government. Anthropic has emphasized its Constitutional AI approach and has marketed Claude as a more privacy-respecting alternative.
