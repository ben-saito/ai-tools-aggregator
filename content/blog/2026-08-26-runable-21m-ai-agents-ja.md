# Runable Raises $21M to Help Small Businesses Get Customers via AI Agents

Runable has raised $21 million in Series A funding to bet that AI agents can go from building businesses to growing them. The Series A was co-led by Susquehanna Venture Capital and Nexus Venture Partners, with existing investors Together Fund and Array VC participating.

---

## From AI Infrastructure to Business Outcome Agents

Founded in 2025, Runable initially built browser technology to scrape data at scale before pivoting to AI agents. Co-founders Kumar and Saksham Sarda noticed users wanted more than infrastructure -- they wanted outcomes. "In the end, a business doesn't require Codex or Claude Code or anything. They require real outcomes," Kumar told TechCrunch.

The shift helped Runable go from zero to a $2 million annualized revenue run rate within three weeks of launching payments in March. Runable's agent allows users to build websites, apps, presentations, and other content through natural language prompts while handling underlying infrastructure including deployment and analytics.

---

## Targeting Small Businesses in Crowded Market

Runable targets small businesses in a market that includes AI giants Anthropic and OpenAI as well as coding platforms Cursor, Lovable, and Replit. The company's goal is to help small business owners get a certain number of customers rather than separately setting up websites, analytics tools, and advertising campaigns.

Runable reports that 60%-70% of its 1 trillion-plus token usage in the last 90 days came from paying customers, indicating strong product-market fit for its outcome-based approach.
