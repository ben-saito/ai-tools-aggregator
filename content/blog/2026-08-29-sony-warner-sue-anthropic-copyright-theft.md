# Sony Music and Warner Sue Anthropic Over Copyright Allegations

---

Sony Music Entertainment and Warner Music Group filed a federal lawsuit against Anthropic on August 29, 2026, alleging the AI company conducted a "brazen campaign" of intellectual property theft through illegal piracy for AI training purposes.

## Background of the Lawsuit

Both Sony Music and Warner are seeking damages under US copyright law. According to TechCrunch reporting, this lawsuit is one of the broadest copyright infringement cases filed against Anthropic this year. The music labels allege that Anthropic used their copyrighted songs without licenses for AI model training.

## Scale of Alleged Piracy

The lawsuit targets the breadth of Anthropic's use of pirated training data. Using copyright-protected material for AI training has been the subject of multiple lawsuits since 2024, involving companies such as Genentext, Nvidia, and Microsoft. Anthropic has not publicly commented on the specific allegations, but the company has previously argued that its training data was obtained lawfully under fair use principles.

## Industry Implications

This lawsuit signals that copyright claims against AI companies over training data have reached a new peak. With both Sony Music and Warner filing suit simultaneously, the music industry's legal strategy against AI companies has entered a new phase. Securing training data directly impacts model performance, so such lawsuits are expected to increase, making the legal definition of fair use a critical issue for the AI industry.
