# Disney Offers $20M for Character.AI Tech Amid Ongoing Regulatory Scrutiny

---

Disney has offered $20 million to acquire technology from Character.AI, the chatbot startup linked in public discourse to user interactions that raised concerns about mental health impacts, particularly among younger users. The proposed acquisition comes as Character.AI has faced continued scrutiny following high-profile incidents and regulatory attention.

The deal would give Disney access to Character.AI's conversational AI infrastructure, potentially for use in theme park experiences, customer service applications, or content creation tools. Character.AI's technology allows for the creation of interactive characters that maintain extended conversations with users.

---

## Background on Character.AI's Challenges

Character.AI gained rapid adoption for its ability to let users interact with AI versions of fictional characters, celebrities, and original creations. However, the platform also became controversial when some users, including teenagers, developed what clinicians described as unhealthy attachments to AI companions. Several highly publicized incidents involving young users contributed to regulatory scrutiny.

The company's AI safety practices were reviewed by multiple state attorneys general, and the startup implemented changes including enhanced content moderation and restrictions on certain types of character interactions. The Disney offer comes after a difficult period for the company's valuation.

---

## Disney's AI Strategy

The acquisition represents Disney's most significant move into conversational AI for consumer applications. The company has experimented with AI in various capacities — from theme park animatronics to content production — but Character.AI's technology could enable a new generation of interactive experiences where guests converse directly with AI-driven characters.

The deal is subject to regulatory approval and due diligence. Terms of the proposed transaction remain confidential, though the reported $20 million figure represents a significant discount to Character.AI's previous private market valuations.
