# AI Hedge Fund Situational Awareness Faces SEC Probe

The AI hedge fund Situational Awareness — founded by a former OpenAI researcher and built around AI industry investments — is now the subject of federal subpoenas, TechCrunch reported on August 25, 2026. The fund went from "the talk of Wall Street" to a target of SEC enforcement faster than its leveraged bets on AI-adjacent stocks unraveled.

---

## From Star Fund to Federal Subpoenas

Situational Awareness was among the most closely watched vehicles for investors seeking exposure to the AI boom through a researcher-led vehicle with insider knowledge of the industry. The fund reportedly held significant positions in Anthropic alongside its public equity portfolio.

The forced unwind of its public portfolio in late July 2026 — driven by plummeting leveraged bets on AI-adjacent stocks — was the first sign of trouble. Now, federal investigators have issued subpoenas as part of a probe whose scope and allegations remain unclear.

---

## Prior Coverage

This is the third major development in the Situational Awareness story tracked by this publication:

- **July 31, 2026:** Initial reporting revealed the fund's large Anthropic holdings and AI-focused strategy
- **August 1, 2026:** The fund was forced to unwind its public equity positions after leveraged bets failed
- **August 10, 2026:** The fund closed a $400 million investment in AI chip startup Source Foundry

The SEC probe represents a significant escalation and raises questions about the fund's disclosure practices and whether investor communications about its AI thesis may have drawn regulatory scrutiny.
