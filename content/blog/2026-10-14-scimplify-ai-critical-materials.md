Indian startup Scimplify has raised $25 million in a funding round to expand its AI-powered platform for producing specialty chemicals and critical materials, with semiconductors, defense, and energy emerging as its fastest-growing markets.

The company's platform uses machine learning models to optimize chemical synthesis pathways, allowing manufacturers to reduce dependence on Chinese suppliers for materials like rare earth compounds, advanced polymers, and high-purity reagents used in chip manufacturing.

Scimplify's AI system predicts reaction yields, identifies optimal temperature and pressure conditions, and flags contamination risks — capabilities that historically required years of lab trial and error. By automating much of this process, the startup says it can cut development time for new chemical products from months to weeks.

The company counts several Tier-1 Indian conglomerates among its customers, along with defense contractors and energy companies seeking domestic supply chains for materials that currently flow primarily from China. India has been actively promoting domestic chemical manufacturing as part of its broader push for technology self-reliance.

The $25 million round was led by a consortium of strategic investors from the chemicals and defense sectors. Scimplify said it will use the funding to expand its lab infrastructure and hire additional ML engineers and chemists.
