# Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 Bring AI Recap and Health Sensing Upgrades

Apple's newest smartwatches arrive with a dual focus: AI-powered memory features that transcribe and summarize daily conversations, and a redesigned health-sensing system that measures vital signs 60 times per minute.

---

## Audio Intelligence: Live Rewind and Siri Recap

The headline AI features are "Live Rewind" and "Siri Recap." Live Rewind lets users rewind 15 seconds of conversation by double-pressing the Digital Crown, displaying the transcript and allowing it to be saved to Siri's new standalone app. Siri Recap continuously monitors ambient conversation and generates summary notes — meeting takeaways, key points from a conversation — processed entirely on-device.

Apple explicitly states these features do not create or store audio recordings.

---

## Redesigned Health Sensing System

The new hardware introduces a health-sensing system with increased electrode surface area for improved ECG readings and a ring of redesigned photodiodes that capture light more efficiently. Continuous heart rate readings now occur every 5 seconds throughout the day — 60 times more frequently than previous generations.

This enables a new "readiness score" feature, borrowed from smart rings like Oura. Each morning, the watch analyzes recent activity, vitals, and sleep data to produce a single score indicating how prepared the body is for the day.

---

## Health Age: Comparing Metrics to Your Actual Age

Apple also introduced a "Health Age" feature that compares key health metrics — VO2 max, sleep quality, heart rate variability, and other biomarkers — against the user's chronological age. The data comes from Apple Watch sensors and, when available, blood test results integrated via the Health app.

---

## Pricing and Availability

Apple Watch Series 12 starts at $399. Watch Ultra 4 starts at $799. Both are available for pre-order starting September 9, with general availability in October 2026.
