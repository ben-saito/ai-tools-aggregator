# Apple HomeView: Siri-Powered Smart Home Hub Launches Tuesday

---

Apple is set to announce a new smart home hub called the HomeView on Tuesday, according to Bloomberg's Mark Gurman. The device represents Apple's most serious entry into the smart home market, an area where the company has historically lagged behind competitors like Amazon and Google.

## A Siri-Powered Device

Gurman described the HomeView as a Siri-powered device featuring a display. Unlike Apple's previous forays into home automation through HomeKit and the Home app on iPhones and Macs, this is dedicated hardware built specifically for smart home control. The timing suggests Apple may use next week's product event to formally introduce the device to consumers.

## Apple Smart Home Strategy

Apple has long faced criticism for its fragmented approach to home automation. While HomeKit provides a solid software foundation, the lack of a flagship hardware product has allowed Amazon Echo and Google Nest devices to dominate households worldwide. The HomeView appears designed to address this gap directly, giving Apple a physical presence in the smart home that competitors have established for years.

The device is expected to leverage Apple's strengths in privacy and on-device processing, differentiating it from cloud-dependent alternatives. Siri integration would allow voice control of all HomeKit-compatible devices, from lights and thermostats to locks and security cameras.

## Market Context

The smart home hub market has evolved significantly since the early days of proprietary protocols and limited compatibility. Matter, the new smart home standard backed by Apple, Google, Amazon, and Samsung, has simplified cross-platform device control. Apple positioning the HomeView as a Matter-compatible hub would align it with the broader industry push toward interoperability.

## What This Means for AI in the Home

The convergence of AI assistants and smart home control represents a natural evolution. The HomeView's Siri integration suggests Apple is thinking beyond simple voice commands toward more proactive, intelligent automation. The question remains whether Apple can deliver an experience compelling enough to convince consumers already invested in Amazon or Google ecosystems to switch—or whether the real opportunity lies in attracting the substantial number of households that haven't yet adopted any smart home system.
