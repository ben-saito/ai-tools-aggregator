# TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 Day 2: AI Takes Center Stage as Nvidia, Waabi, and Robotics Leaders Converge

---

Day 2 of TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 is underway at San Francisco's Moscone West, bringing together over 10,000 founders, investors, and technology leaders for a day focused on AI, robotics, and venture investment. With 300+ startups exhibiting in the Expo Hall and a packed schedule of keynote sessions, the event highlights the continued dominance of artificial intelligence across the startup ecosystem.

---

## AI-Powered Networking and Startup Pitches

One of the distinguishing features of Disrupt 2026 is the AI-powered matchmaking system that connects attendees based on their interests and goals. The system, which runs across the event platform, analyzes attendee profiles and meeting preferences to suggest relevant connections throughout the day.

Startup Battlefield pitches continue on the Pitch Showcase Stage, with enterprise-focused startups presenting their solutions to a panel of judges and live audience. The startup competition has drawn entries across AI, robotics, developer tools, and enterprise software.

---

## Day 2 Speaker Highlights

Today's speaker lineup emphasizes AI and robotics:

- **Nvidia** — The chip giant returns to Disrupt with updates on its AI hardware ecosystem and partnerships
- **Waabi** — AI autonomous driving startup presenting its approach to full autonomy
- **Flock Safety** — Public safety technology company integrating AI into law enforcement tools
- **Ricursive Intelligence** — Industrial AI and robotics company
- **Benchmark** — Venture capital firm presenting its full partnership

Sessions will cover robotics, AI hardware, public safety applications, and venture investment strategies.

## Expo Hall and Networking Opportunities

The Expo Hall features 300+ startups across 200,000+ square feet of exhibition space. Attendees can see products demonstrated live, speak directly with founding teams, and discover tools relevant to their businesses.

Conference passes provide access to main stage sessions, interactive roundtables, and Q&A breakouts. Expo+ passes focus on networking and discovery, while discount pricing remains available at 50% off the original walk-up rate.

## Key Themes: AI Infrastructure and Hardware

Day 2 sessions particularly focus on AI hardware and infrastructure — reflecting the industry's ongoing attention to compute capacity and the supply chain for AI training and deployment. The convergence of AI with robotics and physical systems continues to be a major theme, with multiple sessions addressing how AI models interact with the real world.
