# Nvidia Senior Manager Linked to Supermicro Scheme Smuggling AI Servers to China

An Nvidia senior manager has been indicted in connection with a scheme to smuggle AI servers to China, according to reports. The indictment comes after Jensen Huang publicly scolded Supermicro for related activities.

The case highlights the intensifying scrutiny around AI chip exports to China and the increasingly complex supply chains designed to circumvent export controls.

---

## Context

Nvidia GPUs are at the center of US export control efforts, with the company required to design specialized chips for the Chinese market that comply with regulatory requirements.
