# Google Announces Gemini 4 Argon, But Public Access Is Not Yet Available

Google has announced Gemini 4 Argon, its latest flagship AI model, but the system remains inaccessible to external users. Internal company engineers are reportedly using the model extensively, according to Google's announcement. The Argon variant shows strong performance on software engineering benchmarks — DeepSWE v1.1 reports 77% accuracy — positioning it as a direct competitor in the coding-assistant domain.

The announcement follows Google's pattern of previewing models before broad availability, a strategy that has drawn both anticipation and criticism from developers awaiting access.

---

## Technical Profile

Gemini 4 Argon builds on the Gemini 3 architecture with reportedly significant improvements in reasoning and long-context tasks. The 77% on DeepSWE v1.1 places it among the top coding models in closed evaluations, though public benchmarks and real-world developer feedback have not yet been published.

Google has not disclosed release timelines, API pricing, or context window specifications.

---

## Competitive Landscape

The software engineering benchmark space has grown competitive with models from Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT series), and specialized players like Devin (Cognition). Gemini 4 Argon's strong showing on DeepSWE v1.1 suggests Google is targeting the same developer-tooling market.
