# Meta Launches Pocket: Vibe-Code Gaming App Now Available in US

Meta officially launched Pocket, its experimental AI-powered app for creating and sharing interactive games, to all users in the United States on August 20, 2026. The app was previously tested quietly in Brazil starting last month.

Pocket allows users to generate small interactive games through AI prompts, which are then published to a scrollable feed. The app is based on Meta's acquisition of the team behind the vibe-coded gaming platform Gizmo earlier this year.

Interactive experiences created in Pocket respond to touch and phone tilt, include sound effects, and can incorporate clips from favorite songs. Users can also import photos from their camera roll to personalize games.

The app represents Meta's push into AI-driven content creation, enabling non-developers to create and share gaming experiences through natural language prompts. Unlike traditional app development, Pocket users don't need coding skills to build interactive content.

Pocket's US launch marks Meta's first major expansion of the platform beyond its initial test market. The app is available for download on iOS and Android in the US.
