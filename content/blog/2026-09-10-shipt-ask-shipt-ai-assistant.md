# Shipt Launches Ask Shipt AI Assistant, Joining the AI Cart Race in Grocery Delivery

Shipt, the same-day delivery platform owned by Target, announced on September 9, 2026 the launch of Ask Shipt — an AI-powered shopping assistant that converts natural language prompts into customized ready-to-buy carts. The launch puts Shipt alongside Instacart, Uber Eats, and DoorDash in the race to embed conversational AI into grocery shopping.

---

## Ask Shipt: From Prompt to Cart

Ask Shipt allows customers to describe what they need conversationally rather than searching and selecting items individually. Users can specify constraints like budget, dietary needs, or event type ("a Saturday tailgate for 25 people with brunch items") and receive a complete cart. The tool is integrated directly into Shipt's existing ordering flow.

Shipt emphasized that the AI assistant draws on its same-day delivery infrastructure — items sourced from local Target stores — to fulfill orders within hours. This differentiates Shipt from pure-play grocery aggregators that rely on third-party retail partners.

---

## Competitive Landscape

The simultaneous announcement of Shipt's Ask Shipt and Instacart's Clementine on the same day reflects the delivery sector's coordinated push toward AI-native shopping experiences. Both platforms face similar challenges: high cart abandonment rates, low average order values, and customer fatigue with manual item selection.

Uber Eats and DoorDash have each introduced AI features in 2026 — including predictive ordering and smart reorder suggestions. The cumulative effect is pressure on all delivery platforms to make AI-assisted shopping the default experience rather than a premium feature.

---

## Strategic Context for Target

Shipt's AI push aligns with Target's broader effort to leverage its physical footprint as a competitive advantage in grocery delivery. Same-day fulfillment from nearby Target stores gives Shipt a speed and availability edge over grocery-only competitors that depend on centralized warehouse models. The Ask Shipt assistant is designed to make that advantage more accessible to casual users who might otherwise default to larger grocery chains.
