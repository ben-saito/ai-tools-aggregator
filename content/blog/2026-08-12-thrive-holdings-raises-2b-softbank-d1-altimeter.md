# OpenAI-Backed Thrive Holdings Raises $2B from SoftBank, D1, Altimeter

Thrive Holdings, backed by OpenAI, has raised $2 billion in new funding at a $12 billion valuation from investors including SoftBank, D1 Capital Partners, and Altimeter Capital.

---

## Enterprise AI Focus

Thrive Holdings specializes in bringing AI solutions to enterprise customers. The large funding round, backed by prominent investors, signals strong market confidence in the enterprise AI space.

---

## Enterprise AI Market Growth

Demand for enterprise AI services has grown explosively in recent years. From business automation to data analysis and customer service, AI adoption across industries continues to accelerate, with Thrive Holdings positioning itself as a key player in the space.
