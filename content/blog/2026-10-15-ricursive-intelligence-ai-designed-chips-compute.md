# Ricursive Intelligence is betting on AI-designed chips to help solve the compute crisis

---

Ricursive Intelligence founders Anna Goldie and Azalia Mirhoseini believe more efficient AI chip design could help solve the compute crisis. The founders, who previously worked at Google DeepMind and Intel, are building an automated chip design pipeline that uses AI to explore the design space of AI accelerators much faster than traditional EDA tools.

The core insight is that the current semiconductor supply chain is optimized for general-purpose computing, not AI workloads. As AI models demand more FLOPs per dollar, there's growing interest in domain-specific architectures that can achieve better performance per watt for matrix multiplications and attention computations.

Ricursive's approach uses reinforcement learning to optimize chip floorplans for specific model architectures. By co-designing hardware and software together, the startup claims it can reduce the design cycle from years to months.

The compute crisis refers to the growing gap between AI model training demands and available hardware. Frontier labs are reportedly spending billions on compute, and the economics of AI deployment remain challenging. If AI-designed chips can deliver even modest improvements in efficiency, the compounding effect across millions of deployments could be significant.

The startup raised a seed round from Sequoia and Benchmark and is currently working with several cloud providers to validate its approach.
