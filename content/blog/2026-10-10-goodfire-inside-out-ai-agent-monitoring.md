# Goodfire Launches "Inside-Out" AI Agent Monitoring at Fraction of Cost

---

## Overview

Google is embedding agentic AI capabilities directly into its Gemini platform, starting with business users. The move represents a significant step in the trend toward AI systems that can plan, execute tasks, and delegate work across multiple business applications and systems.

---

## Key Details

Google's Gemini is evolving from a conversational AI assistant into a full-fledged AI agent capable of autonomous task completion. The agent can now:

- **Plan and execute** multi-step workflows across business apps
- **Delegate tasks** to sub-agents and coordinate complex operations
- **Work across systems** including email, calendars, documents, and enterprise software
- **Reason through** ambiguous instructions and take initiative on user behalf

This development reflects the broader industry shift toward agentic AI — systems that don't just respond to queries but actively accomplish goals with minimal human intervention.

---

## Industry Context

The agentic AI market is heating up as major labs compete to move beyond chat interfaces. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind are all pursuing similar directions, building AI systems that can use tools, browse the web, write and execute code, and manage files autonomously.

For enterprise customers, agentic AI promises significant productivity gains by automating routine workflows that currently require human oversight at every step.
