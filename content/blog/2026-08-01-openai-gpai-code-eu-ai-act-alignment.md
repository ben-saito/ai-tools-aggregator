# OpenAI Publishes EU AI Act GPAI Code Alignment Summary as Enforcement Deadline Nears

OpenAI has published a detailed overview of how its safety, security, and transparency practices align with the European Union's General-Purpose AI (GPAI) Code of Practice. The company contributed to and endorsed the GPAI Code, which was developed through a multi-stakeholder process alongside the Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content. Both codes are central to the EU AI Act's regulatory framework for general-purpose AI models, with compliance obligations approaching for GPAI providers operating in the European market.

The alignment summary covers several domains: risk mitigation processes, technical safeguards deployed during model training and inference, incident response procedures, and transparency reporting mechanisms. OpenAI states it has implemented documentation practices that map to the GPAI Code's transparency requirements, including model capability evaluations, known limitation disclosures, and abuse pattern tracking. The company says it will continue updating its practices as the GPAI Code evolves through future revision cycles.

The EU AI Act entered into force in 2024, with GPAI provisions taking effect in stages. Providers of GPAI models with systemic risk characteristics face stricter obligations, including adversarial robustness testing, cybersecurity assessments, and incident reporting to authorities. OpenAI's disclosure signals that it considers its models subject to these heightened requirements and is preparing documentation for regulatory review.

Industry observers note that the GPAI Code remains a voluntary framework in some respects, but regulatory pressure is mounting. The European AI Office has indicated it expects GPAI providers to demonstrate alignment even where participation in formal code-working groups has been limited. OpenAI's publication positions the company to respond to enforcement inquiries with documented evidence rather than general statements of intent.

The Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content addresses watermarking, content attribution, and detection mechanisms for AI output. OpenAI's summary notes that its synthetic media detection tools and usage policies reflect principles outlined in the transparency code, though critics argue that industry-wide adoption of content attribution standards remains incomplete.
