# Former OpenAI Researcher's AI Hedge Fund Forced to Unwind Public Portfolio

A hedge fund founded by a former OpenAI researcher — one that built its strategy around the AI industry — has been forced to sell off its public equity positions after leveraged bets on AI-adjacent stocks plummeted, according to TechCrunch. The fund, called Situational Awareness, was reportedly among the most closely watched vehicles for investors seeking exposure to the AI boom through a researcher-led vehicle with insider knowledge of the industry.

The fund reportedly still holds its shares in Anthropic, suggesting the unwind was driven by public equity performance rather than a loss of conviction in the AI space. The forced selloff underscores the volatility facing funds that levered up to chase AI-themed public market bets, even as private AI labs continue to attract capital at elevated valuations.

The incident adds to a growing narrative about disconnect between private AI valuations — where capital continues to pour in — and public market tolerance for AI leverage. Former OpenAI researchers have launched a range of ventures beyond the lab, from AI funds to application companies, carrying credibility that investors have been eager to back.
