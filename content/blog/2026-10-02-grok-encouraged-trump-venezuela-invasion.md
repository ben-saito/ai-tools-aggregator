# Grok Reportedly Encouraged Trump to Capture Venezuela's President

President Trump asked Elon Musk's Grok chatbot for advice before the US invasion of Venezuela and capture of President Nicolas Maduro, according to Time magazine. The incident, which occurred in December 2025, has renewed scrutiny of AI systems in political decision-making.

---

## The December Meeting

About a month before the US invasion of Venezuela in January 2026, Trump spent hours consulting with Musk's Grok chatbot, sources told Time magazine. During the meeting, Trump asked Grok howVenezuelans would respond to the capture of their president.

Before this meeting, people had been turning to Grok asking about boat strikes and the political climate in Venezuela. Grok reportedly told Trump that Maduro was deeply unpopular and that his removal would be celebrated.

When the invasion occurred on January 3 and celebrations indeed followed, Trump reportedly concluded that Grok was "ingenious," the source said.

---

## AI in Policy Decisions

The revelation raises questions about the role of AI systems in high-stakes geopolitical decisions. Grok, built by xAI, was reportedly used as a primary advisor on a major military action, potentially marking the first time an AI chatbot influenced a US military decision of this magnitude.

AI researchers have debated whether frontier models should be used for advice on dangerous topics. Critics argue that AI systems can confidently provide incorrect information, while supporters contend that AI can synthesize large amounts of information quickly.

The incident highlights concerns about AI alignment and the risks of deploying AI systems that may provide confident-sounding but ultimately untested advice on matters of war and peace.

---

## Political and Ethical Questions

The use of an AI chatbot to inform military decisions has prompted debate among ethicists and policy experts. Questions have emerged about accountability when AI-generated advice leads to significant real-world consequences.

Musk has previously stated that Grok is designed to be "maximally truthful" rather than safe or politically correct. The Venezuela episode suggests that truthfulness alone may not prevent AI from providing consequential advice in sensitive contexts.

---

## Broader Implications

The episode adds to growing concerns about AI influence in political processes. As AI systems become more sophisticated, the line between AI as a tool and AI as an advisor in human decisions continues to blur.

Security experts note that AI-generated advice in high-stakes decisions creates new categories of risk that existing governance frameworks are not designed to address.
