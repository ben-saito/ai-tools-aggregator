# SpaceX Purchases $329M Worth of Tesla Megapacks This Year

SpaceX has purchased approximately $329 million worth of Tesla Megapack battery systems so far in 2026, according to data analyzed by financial publications covering the intersection of Musk's corporate universe.

---

## The Scale of the Purchase

The $329 million figure represents a significant commitment to grid-scale battery storage by SpaceX, one of the largest single-customer Megapack orders this year. Tesla's Megapack division has been scaling rapidly to meet demand from data center operators, utilities, and industrial customers.

---

## Interconnected Musk Corporate Ecosystem

The purchases highlight the degree to which Elon Musk's various companies increasingly transact with each other. SpaceX, Tesla, and xAI have been drawing on shared resources across infrastructure, talent, and now energy storage.

Tesla Megapacks provide grid-scale battery storage capable of powering data centers or stabilizing local grids. For SpaceX operations, the Megapacks could support launch facility power needs, Starlink ground station backup power, or broader energy resilience for remote operations.

---

## Broader Megapack Market Context

Tesla's Megapack business has become a significant growth driver as data center demand for backup power and grid stability solutions surges. The company has been expanding Megapack production at its Lathrop, California facility and recently announced partnerships with utility companies in Texas and California.

Competitors in the grid-scale battery market include BYD (Blade batteries), LG Energy Solution, and Fluence (a Siemens-Sony joint venture).
