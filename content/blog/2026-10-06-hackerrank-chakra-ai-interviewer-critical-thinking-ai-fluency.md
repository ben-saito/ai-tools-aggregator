# HackerRank launches Chakra, an AI interviewer that evaluates candidates on critical thinking and AI fluency

---

## Overview

After around six months in beta, HackerRank is making Chakra generally available to its customers on Monday. The startup says the AI interviewer has already conducted more than 500,000 interviews, with Snowflake, Snorkel, and Capgemini among its early testers.

---

## From Output Evaluation to Process Evaluation

AI has been a staple in job interviews for some time, with companies using voice agents and other automated tools to screen candidates and make the hiring process more efficient. Job seekers, meanwhile, have increasingly gained their own AI tools to help them navigate interviews, sometimes without employers knowing.

With Chakra, HackerRank is betting AI can change not only how interviews are conducted, but also what employers can measure. Beyond whether someone arrives at the right answer, the startup wants to assess harder-to-capture signals such as critical thinking and judgment, as well as what it calls **"AI fluency"** — how well a candidate frames a problem for AI, judges its output, and steers it toward a solution.

"The previous modality of evaluation was evaluating the output," HackerRank co-founder and CEO Vivek Ravisankar said in an interview. "Now, because of AI, anybody can produce an artifact." The question for employers, he said, becomes whether they can understand the thinking behind it.

---

## How Chakra Works

In practice, a Chakra interview is designed to look more like doing the job than taking a traditional coding test. A candidate gets a task involving a realistic scenario and can use AI tools to work through it. The system then evaluates both the output and the process — how the candidate prompted the AI, whether they caught errors, and how they iterated.

Ravisankar told TechCrunch that Chakra is changing the basic structure of the hiring process itself. What previously involved three separate rounds — a recruiter screen, a technical test, and an onsite interview — can now be compressed into a single AI-moderated session that generates a unified evaluation signal.

---

## Cheating Detection Inverted

Giving candidates access to AI during an interview might seem to make it easier to cheat. However, HackerRank says it found the opposite. Suspicious-activity flags — such as pasting a prompt from outside the interview environment — dropped significantly when candidates knew AI was allowed and being actively monitored.

---

## AI Fluency as a Hiring Criterion

The broader bet is that **"AI fluency"** becomes a core hiring criterion. As AI tools proliferate in the workplace, the ability to work effectively with an AI assistant may prove as important as the ability to work alone. Chakra is designed to surface that skill during the hiring process rather than discovering it on the job.
