## What Comes After RSI? Self-Sufficient AI

Recursive self-improvement (RSI) — where AI systems improve their own capabilities — has long been theorized as a key step toward transformative AI. But researchers are increasingly asking: what comes after RSI? The answer likely involves humanoid robotics and physical infrastructure that allows AI systems to interact with and modify the world.

The bottleneck may not be algorithmic but physical — RSI requires the ability to manipulate the environment, not just generate text or code.

---

## DeepMind Contemplates the Path to Superintelligence

Google DeepMind researchers have published a paper exploring how we might transition from building general intelligences to building artificial superintelligence (ASI). The paper defines ASI as "a system that exceeds the performance of large human-expert collectives on virtually all tasks and domains of human activity."

Notably, the authors suggest a single ASI could consist of "a collective of millions of instances that interact with the world in parallel" — similar to how today's large language models already operate at massive scale.

The paper explores what advantages digital intelligences have over biological ones: faster input/output speeds, superior working memory, substrate independence, lossless replication, and the ability to operate continuously without fatigue.
