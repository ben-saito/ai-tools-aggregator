# Russell Vought to Gain Veto Power Over All NIH Grants Under Reported Restructuring

---

## Overview

According to multiple reports, Russell Vought, a prominent Trump administration official, is set to receive veto authority over all National Institutes of Health (NIH) grant decisions. The pending move has drawn sharp criticism from scientific and public health advocates who warn it could politicize biomedical research funding decisions.

---

## Institutional Concerns

The NIH has long operated with a degree of independence in making funding decisions based on scientific merit. Grant peer review processes have historically been insulated from political interference to ensure the most promising research receives support regardless of political considerations.

Critics argue that granting veto power to a political appointee over the entire NIH grant portfolio could fundamentally alter the agency's research prioritization framework. The move follows broader efforts to restructure federal science agencies and align their activities with administration priorities.

---

## Scientific Community Response

Research institutions and scientific organizations have expressed alarm at the reported decision. The potential for political consideration in grant decisions could affect research ranging from infectious disease preparedness to cancer treatment development.
