# Amazon's Texas Data Center Could Become Largest U.S. Climate Polluter

Amazon is planning a data center in Pecos County, Texas, paired with an on-site natural gas power plant that could become the largest source of climate pollution in the United States — permitted to release 33 million tons of carbon dioxide per year, more than any existing power plant in the country, according to The New York Times.

AI has already significantly impacted Amazon's carbon emissions, which were up 16% last year — moving in the wrong direction for a company that pledged to reach net-zero by 2040. The situation could worsen as Amazon and other tech companies back the development of massive natural gas plants to power AI compute infrastructure.

An Amazon spokesperson confirmed the data center will be powered by new on-site generation that "won't raise electricity costs for Texas families," while claiming the company's climate commitment "hasn't changed."
