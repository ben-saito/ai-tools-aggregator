# Nscale Seeks $3.5B Ahead of Potential IPO

Nscale, the UK-based AI infrastructure company, is in talks to raise up to $3.5 billion ahead of a potential public listing. The company -- founded just two years ago -- recently signed a massive $45 billion deal with Anthropic and is now pursuing additional financing to support its rapid expansion.

---

## Pre-IPO Financing Push

According to Bloomberg, Nscale is exploring two parallel funding tracks. The first involves selling $1.5 billion in convertible notes -- a form of debt that converts to equity at a later date -- to a group of investors. The second is an additional $2 billion investment from Nvidia, which already participated in Nscale's Series B round earlier this year.

The convertible note approach is common among late-stage companies preparing for IPO, as it allows them to raise capital without immediately diluting existing shareholders.

---

## From Series A to $45 Billion Deals

Nscale has grown rapidly since its founding. In December 2024, the company raised a $155 million Series A. Just three months later, in March 2026, it closed a $1.1 billion Series B led by investment fund Aker -- described at the time as the largest Series B in European history.

The company's profile rose sharply after announcing its $45 billion agreement with Anthropic. Reports emerged that Nscale has been telling potential investors it has approximately $203 billion in revenue projected from signed customer leases -- though this is forward-looking contracted revenue, not current sales, according to The Information.

---

## The AI Infrastructure Race

Nscale's positioning reflects a broader trend in the AI industry: AI labs are racing to secure long-term GPU compute agreements. Anthropic has pursued a multi-vendor infrastructure strategy, partnering with Google Cloud, Together AI, Oracle, and now Nscale to ensure access to training and inference capacity.

Nvidia's deepening involvement with Nscale -- through both the Series B and the potential $2 billion additional investment -- reflects the chipmaker's strategy of extending its influence beyond hardware into the broader AI platform ecosystem.

Nscale has not yet filed its IPO paperwork, but the company has indicated a listing could come as soon as later this month.
