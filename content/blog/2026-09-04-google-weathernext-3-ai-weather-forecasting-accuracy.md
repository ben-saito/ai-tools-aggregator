# Google DeepMind WeatherNext 3: The Most Accurate AI Weather Model Yet

Google DeepMind and Google Research have released WeatherNext 3, a new artificial intelligence model for weather forecasting that has achieved the highest accuracy score among leading models tested on Operational WeatherBench, a benchmark built by the startup Brightband. The model is already being integrated into Google Search, Google Maps, and the Gemini chatbot.

---

## Beating Government Supercomputers

WeatherNext 3 outperformed not only AI models from Microsoft, Nvidia, and the European Center for Medium-Range Weather Forecasting (ECMWF), but also traditional physics-based forecasting systems operated by the US National Weather Service and the ECMWF. The latter result is particularly notable: traditional weather forecasting relies on supercomputers solving mathematical equations that describe atmospheric physics, an approach that has been refined over decades.

The shift toward AI-based weather prediction began when the ECMWF released more than fifty years of historical weather data in 2018. Researchers used this dataset to train deep learning models that could learn the patterns in atmospheric behavior without explicitly encoding physical laws.

---

## How WeatherNext 3 Works

WeatherNext 3 analyzes temperature, wind speed, and humidity data to generate predictions across multiple time horizons. Unlike traditional models that require extensive high-resolution observational data, WeatherNext 3 can make accurate predictions from sparser input data, reducing the computational infrastructure needed for weather forecasting.

The model builds on techniques from the original WeatherNext announced in August 2026, but introduces architectural improvements that allow it to better capture atmospheric chaos. Small errors in initial conditions compound exponentially in weather prediction -- a phenomenon known as sensitive dependence on initial conditions -- and WeatherNext 3 appears to handle this more effectively than its predecessors.

---

## Commercial Deployment

Google is rolling WeatherNext 3 across its consumer products. Users of Google Search will see AI-generated weather forecasts when searching for conditions in specific locations. Google Maps will incorporate the model's predictions into routing decisions that account for weather along travel routes. Gemini will have access to the model through Google's cloud API, allowing developers to build applications on top of WeatherNext 3.

The model is also being made available to researchers and enterprises through Google Cloud Platform, where it can be accessed via API at rates Google says are competitive with other AI APIs.

---

## The Broader AI Weather Race

The success of WeatherNext 3 reflects intensifying competition among major technology companies to apply AI to scientific domains. Weather prediction is particularly attractive because accurate forecasts have clear commercial value, the data needed to train models is publicly available, and the validation process is objective -- the weather either happens or it does not.

Microsoft and Nvidia have both invested in weather AI research, and the ECMWF itself has been developing its own machine learning forecasting capabilities. The result is a rapid improvement cycle where new model versions are released on timescales measured in months rather than the years that characterized traditional weather model development.
