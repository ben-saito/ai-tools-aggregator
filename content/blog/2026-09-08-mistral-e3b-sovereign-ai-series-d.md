# Mistral Raises €3B Series D at €21B Valuation — Europe's Largest-Ever Tech Fundraise

French AI lab Mistral has closed a €3 billion Series D round, which the company says is the largest equity fundraising ever completed by a European technology company. The round values Mistral at €21 billion and was led by Samsung Electronics, with EQT-managed Scaleup Europe Fund and existing investor PSG Equity joining as co-leads.

---

## Overview

The funding will be used to scale Mistral's compute capacity, build infrastructure, accelerate commercial growth, and expand its international footprint. Luxembourg's Grand Duchy also joined as a new backer, with many existing European backers doubling down.

Mistral said it will use the funding to pursue a strategic shift aimed at addressing concerns in Europe and elsewhere about dependence on the United States for AI technology — particularly as AI regulation and products in the US intensify.

---

## Sovereign AI Infrastructure: 1 GW by 2030

In addition to raising capital, Mistral in August unveiled tools that let customers choose which regions their AI queries are processed in. The company also started hosting third-party open-weight AI models, including Chinese ones, positioning itself as a neutral infrastructure layer.

Mistral is targeting 1 GW of compute capacity in Europe by 2030. The company described its frontier research as "the foundation underpinning its infrastructure, products and sovereignty" — an indirect response to critics who interpreted its hosting of Chinese models as a sign it was becoming a mere inference provider.

---

## Geopolitical Backing: Macron's "Third Way in AI"

France's President Macron posted on X that the round reflected France and South Korea's goal of "building a third way in AI." Samsung's entry into Mistral's cap table has the explicit blessing of French authorities, signaling state-level support for the company as a European AI champion.

Dutch chipmaker ASML is a major partner and investor alongside Samsung, giving Mistral deep ties to critical semiconductor infrastructure.

---

## Global Ambitions Beyond France

Mistral operates in 20 countries, and its go-to-market strategy focuses on helping governments and corporations leverage AI and preserve digital sovereignty. The capital required to compete with leading US labs isn't available in France alone, but Mistral appears to have found a pragmatic international coalition to do so.
