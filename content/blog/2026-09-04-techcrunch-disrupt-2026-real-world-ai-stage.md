# TechCrunch Disrupt 2026: The Real World AI Stage Spotlights Nvidia, Robotics, and Extinct Animals

TechCrunch has announced a new stage at its annual Disrupt 2026 conference: the Real World AI Stage, focused on the intersection of artificial intelligence and the physical world. The stage will showcase applications of AI in robotics, autonomous systems, and even efforts to bring extinct species back to life.

---

## Where Digital Meets Physical

The Real World AI Stage represents TechCrunch's recognition that AI is no longer confined to screens and data centers. Sessions will explore how AI systems are increasingly interacting with the physical environment -- from warehouse robots to self-driving vehicles to synthetic biology.

Nvidia, a major sponsor of the stage, will present its latest developments in robotics and physical AI. The company has been investing heavily in platforms that enable AI models to understand and manipulate the real world, building on its dominance in AI training hardware.

---

## Robots and the Factory Floor

One key theme will be the deployment of AI-powered robots in manufacturing and logistics. Unlike chatbots that operate entirely in software, these systems must navigate physical spaces, handle objects with precision, and respond to real-time sensory data.

Sessions will cover advances in computer vision, tactile sensing, and reinforcement learning that are making industrial robots more adaptable. Speakers will address the gap between laboratory demonstrations and real-world deployment, where variability in environments creates challenges that pure software AI cannot solve.

---

## De-Extinction and Synthetic Biology

Perhaps the most striking announcement is the inclusion of de-extinction projects on the agenda. Companies and research groups working on resurrecting extinct species -- using AI to analyze ancient DNA, simulate developmental biology, and design surrogate host systems -- will present their progress.

The intersection of AI and synthetic biology extends beyond novelty projects. Researchers will discuss how machine learning accelerates protein design, genetic circuit engineering, and the creation of biological systems that never existed in nature. Applications range from medicine to materials science to environmental restoration.

---

## The Broader Disrupt 2026 Agenda

The Real World AI Stage joins the Builders Stage -- focused on practical strategies for scaling startups -- as two new additions to TechCrunch Disrupt 2026. The conference has historically been a venue for major technology announcements and high-profile industry conversations.

The emphasis on physical AI reflects a broader shift in the AI industry. After years of focus on language models and software applications, companies and researchers are increasingly tackling the harder problem of embedding AI in physical systems that must interact with an uncontrolled, dynamic world.
