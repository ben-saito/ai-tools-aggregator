# Oracle Issues Force Majeure Notice on New Mexico Stargate Data Center

Oracle has sent a force majeure notice regarding its New Mexico Stargate data center project, TechCrunch reported September 24, 2026. The notice would allow Oracle to delay payments should the facility miss its 2028 target to come online.

---

## Force Majeure Context

A force majeure clause excuses contractual obligations when extraordinary events prevent performance. In infrastructure projects, such notices typically cite supply chain disruptions, permitting delays, or force majeure events beyond the company's control.

---

## Stargate Overview

The Stargate data center initiative represents Oracle's push to compete with AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud in AI infrastructure. The New Mexico facility is part of a broader build-out targeting AI training and inference workloads.

---

## Payment Implications

The force majeure notice relates specifically to payment obligations, not necessarily construction timelines. Oracle may be protecting itself from penalties if the facility comes online after the contractual deadline.

---

## AI Infrastructure Race

The Stargate project reflects the massive capital being deployed in AI data center construction. Companies are racing to secure power, land, and networking capacity to host the next generation of AI models.
