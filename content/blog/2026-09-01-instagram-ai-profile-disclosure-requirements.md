# Instagram Implements New Disclosure Requirements for AI-Generated Profiles

Meta's Instagram announced new policies on August 31, 2026 requiring creators to label AI-generated profiles, with penalties for non-disclosure including reduced reach. The platform is renaming its existing "AI creator" label to "AI-generated profile" to make the disclosure requirement clearer to users.

---

## New Disclosure Policy

Under the updated guidelines, creators who feature AI-generated people on their profiles must apply the new "AI-generated profile" label. The platform defines AI-generated profiles as accounts where the person depicted was substantially created or generated using AI tools.

Accounts that fail to properly label AI-generated profiles will see their reach reduced as a penalty. In contrast, creators who use the new label will not be penalized simply for having an AI-generated person as their profile subject.

---

## What Does Not Require Labeling

Instagram clarifies that the AI-generated profile label is not required for everyday uses of AI. Creators who use AI for photo editing, caption polish, graphics creation, or other creative enhancements do not need to apply the label. The requirement specifically targets profiles where AI generates the central figure depicted.

---

## Background: Growing Frustration Over AI Influencers

The policy change comes in response to increasing user frustration with AI-generated influencers that appear to represent real people. Users have reported discovering that seemingly authentic profiles were actually entirely AI-generated, often without clear disclosure.

This follows a broader trend of AI-generated content proliferation across social media platforms, where synthetic influencers, product reviewers, and personalities have grown in number and sophistication.
