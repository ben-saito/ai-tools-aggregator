# Harvard Law Dropout Raises $6M for Blue Voice, Building a 'Harvey for Police Officers'

A Harvard Law dropout has raised $6 million in seed funding for Blue Voice, an AI legal assistant designed specifically for police officers. The startup is positioning its product as "Harvey" — a reference to the AI legal assistant from the TV drama Suits — adapted for the unique legal environment that law enforcement officers navigate daily.

---

## The Product: Department-Specific Legal AI

Blue Voice is trained on department-specific laws, local ordinances, protocols, and guidelines that general-purpose AI tools cannot access on the public internet. Unlike broad legal AI assistants, Blue Voice is built around the specific regulatory and procedural framework that police officers encounter in the field, giving it a distinct advantage over generic AI tools when it comes to providing relevant, jurisdiction-specific guidance.

The product comes at a time when law enforcement agencies are under increasing scrutiny over AI-assisted decision-making, and when the legal landscape for police officers — including qualified immunity, use-of-force standards, and departmental policies — is becoming more complex.

---

## Market Context

The legal AI space has grown significantly, with general-purpose tools like Harvey (the original) serving lawyers. Blue Voice is targeting a specific vertical within that space: law enforcement officers who need real-time legal guidance during interactions with the public. The company argues that generic legal AI tools are insufficient for this use case because they lack the training data specific to police procedures, local ordinances, and department-level policies.
