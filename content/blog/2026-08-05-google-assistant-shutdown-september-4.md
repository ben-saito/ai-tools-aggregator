# Google to Kill Assistant on Phones September 4, Gemini Takes Over

Google will retire Assistant on mobile devices starting September 4, 2026, ending the voice assistant that launched in 2012 alongside Android 4.1. The retirement was originally planned for late 2025 but got a brief reprieve as Google shored up Gemini's capabilities. Now that Gemini is ready, the old Assistant will be phased out over several weeks.

---

## Beyond smartphones

The shutdown covers more than phones. Smartwatches, headphones, and cars running Android Auto that rely on phone-connected Assistant will all be forced to transition to Gemini. Most Google smart home devices have already made the switch.

Assistant will persist on a few platforms for now. Android TV set-top boxes, smart TVs, and vehicles with Google built-in are not yet on the retirement list. Google says these will transition to Gemini-only in the future but has not set dates.

---

## Who is affected

Most users will lose Assistant on their phones within the next month or two. Users with older devices that do not meet Gemini's minimum specs, or those in regions where Gemini is not supported, may get a temporary reprieve. Otherwise, the choice narrows to talking to the gen AI-based Assistant or disabling voice control entirely.
