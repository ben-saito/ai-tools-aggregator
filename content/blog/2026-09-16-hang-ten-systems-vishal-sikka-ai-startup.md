# Former Infosys CEO Vishal Sikka's AI Startup Hang Ten Raises $85M, Expands Enterprise AI Consulting

Hang Ten Systems, an AI startup founded by former Infosys CEO Vishal Sikka just four months ago, has expanded its seed funding by another $53 million, bringing total funding to $85 million. The new investment was led by Temasek's early-stage investment platform Xora and came just five weeks after the initial $32 million seed round.

---

## Fast Enterprise Adoption

Founded in May 2026, Hang Ten advises large companies (typically over $10 billion in annual revenue) on AI strategy and helps build and modernize software. The startup has already signed multiple seven-figure enterprise contracts, including a multimillion-dollar deal to build a mission-critical software system within 25 days of the first customer meeting.

Current customers include Fresenius Kabi, Saudi Aramco, and Siemens Energy, spanning the US, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia.

---

## The Build Part Has Become Near-Zero Marginal Cost

Sikka argued that AI is fundamentally shifting software development focus from writing code to defining requirements and validating system performance. "The build part itself has basically become close to zero marginal cost, close to zero time," he told TechCrunch.

Hang Ten's competitive edge lies in adapting AI to complex enterprise software development processes rather than building another AI model. Co-founder Sanjay Rajagopalan explained that engineers use an in-house framework called "Hobie" that packages reusable AI skills for regulated industries.

---

## Investors and Expansion Plans

Investors in the expanded seed round include Mayfield, Aramco Ventures, Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan, Micron CEO Sanjay Mehrotra, and Yahoo co-founder Jerry Yang. The startup plans to use the funds to expand engineering, consulting, and sales teams across the US, Europe, Middle East, Australia, and India.

Hang Ten currently has 20-25 employees and is competing with both AI model developers like OpenAI and Anthropic as well as traditional systems integrators.
