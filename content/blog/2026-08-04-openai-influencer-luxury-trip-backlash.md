# OpenAI Influencer Luxury Trip Draws Backlash as Critics Call Out Environmental Irony

OpenAI's first-ever influencer brand trip is sparking online backlash as tensions over the use of AI continue to grow. The company took a group of influencers to a luxury retreat in upstate New York for what was called "Summer Club," complete with farm-to-table dinners, wellness activities like beekeeping, and classes on how to better use OpenAI products.

---

## A Luxury Retreat Amid AI Criticism

The videos shared by attendees were aesthetic and lighthearted — one creator posted about making a website, while another photographed a brochure for a painting class. But the comments were far less positive. Influencers who posted about the trip on TikTok and Instagram faced criticism from followers suggesting they had "sold their soul for a nice hotel room," or asking if they could produce content reconciling the trip with the environmental impact of AI data centers.

The timing of the trip added to the criticism. OpenAI is set to strike a $500 billion deal to build a data center in Ohio, during a period when the socio-ecological impact of AI infrastructure has taken center stage. Commenters noted the irony of OpenAI hosting a brand retreat at a luxury resort nestled in nature while simultaneously expanding energy-intensive data centers.

Other critics pointed to OpenAI's $200 million contract with the Department of Defense as a reason the brand trip evoked ire.

---

## OpenAI's Growing Influencer Strategy

The move into influencer marketing follows OpenAI's hire of Charles Porch, described by Vanity Fair as a "celebrity whisperer," as its first VP of global creative partnerships. Porch joined from Instagram, where he was VP of global partnerships.

It is worth noting that OpenAI is not the first AI lab to work with influencers. Anthropic reportedly hosted an influencer brand dinner for Claude, and Microsoft Copilot hosted an influencer brand trip to the Super Bowl earlier this year. However, none of those other AI trips seem to have struck as big of a nerve as OpenAI's Summer Club event.

One influencer who attended took to LinkedIn to write that she thought people "would be interested in the behind-the-scenes tips and tricks" of using OpenAI technology, but did not anticipate the backlash. "I didn't for one second consider it would be controversial," she wrote.

---

## Broader Implications for AI Brand Management

The backlash highlights the growing tension between AI companies' public relations efforts and the broader societal concerns surrounding AI development. As AI labs increasingly turn to consumer-facing marketing, they face a more skeptical audience than traditional tech companies might encounter.

The incident also underscores the paradox at the heart of AI companies' environmental messaging: while many publicly commit to sustainability initiatives, the construction of large-scale data centers continues to draw scrutiny from environmental advocates.
