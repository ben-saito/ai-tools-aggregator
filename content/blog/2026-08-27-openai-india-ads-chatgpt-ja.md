# OpenAI to Show Ads on ChatGPT Free and Go Tiers in India

OpenAI has more than 100 million weekly active ChatGPT users in India, and the company is now monetizing this base through ads on the free and lower-priced Go tiers.

---

## India's Large Free User Base

With over 100 million weekly active users in India, OpenAI has built a substantial consumer presence in one of the world's most price-sensitive markets. Most Indian users are on the free tier or the entry-level Go subscription, making them attractive targets for advertising.

The decision reflects OpenAI's ongoing efforts to diversify revenue beyond API sales. While enterprise customers generate significant B2B revenue, consumer-tier ads represent a new growth vector.

---

## Ad Model Implications

The shift to ads in India follows a broader pattern of tech companies testing monetization strategies in price-sensitive markets before rolling them out globally. How ads perform in India will likely inform OpenAI's approach to advertising in other regions.

For users, the introduction of ads means the ChatGPT experience on free tiers will increasingly resemble other ad-supported online services. The company has not disclosed how prominently ads will be displayed or how they will be integrated into the conversational interface.
