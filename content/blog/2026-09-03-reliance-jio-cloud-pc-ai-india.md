# Reliance Jio Turns Aging PCs into AI-Ready Machines for $11

Mukesh Ambani's Reliance Jio is expanding its JioPC cloud service to all internet users in India, offering a way to transform aging computers into AI-capable machines without hardware upgrades. The service streams a virtual PC from Jio's cloud infrastructure, with plans offering up to 8 virtual CPUs, 16GB of RAM, and 1TB of storage.

---

## JioPC: Cloud Computing for the Masses

Originally launched in July 2025 for Jio broadband subscribers, JioPC is now available as a standalone subscription for any internet user in India, regardless of their telecom or broadband provider. The service targets users who cannot afford hardware upgrades but want access to AI-powered computing capabilities.

Jio is positioning JioPC as an "AI-ready PC" experience, streaming a full desktop environment from its cloud data centers. At approximately $11 for two months, the service aims to democratize access to AI tools in a market where hardware costs remain a significant barrier.

---

## The Cloud PC Opportunity in Emerging Markets

Jio's approach reflects a broader trend: delivering AI capabilities via cloud infrastructure rather than requiring local hardware. For emerging markets where device upgrade cycles are longer and hardware costs are prohibitive, cloud-based AI delivery could accelerate adoption significantly.

The service streams virtual PCs with specifications that would cost significantly more to purchase as physical hardware in India, Jio said.

---

## Strategic Context

Reliance Jio has been aggressive in building out its cloud and AI service portfolio. JioPC represents an extension of the company's strategy to bundle connectivity with computing services, creating a recurring revenue model beyond traditional telecom.
