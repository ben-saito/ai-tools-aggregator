# June Emerges from Stealth with $20M Pre-Seed Led by Marc Benioff's Time Ventures

A startup called June has emerged from stealth with a $20 million pre-seed round to tackle what it sees as the core bottleneck in enterprise AI adoption — not model quality, but deployment complexity.

---

## The AI Implementation Gap

June's founding thesis is paradoxical: as AI tools proliferate, the demand for professional services to implement them grows rather than shrinks. "The industry's answer to AI implementation is, 'let's hire more and more and more people,'" CEO Efrat Rapoport told TechCrunch.

Rapoport is a former Salesforce executive who previously co-founded Bonobo AI, a voice-to-text company acquired by Salesforce in 2019. Her cofounders — Ohad Hen, Barak Goldstein, and Idan Tsitiat — also came from Salesforce's AI initiatives. After watching enterprise customers struggle to deploy AI reliably at scale, the team decided to build June.

## The Funding

The $20 million pre-seed round was led by Marc Benioff's Time Ventures, with participation from Michael Dell, Aaron Levie (Box), and George Kurtz (CrowdStrike). June declined to disclose its valuation.

## Product Focus

June's product aims to simplify the AI deployment problem — specifically the gap between having capable AI models and getting them to work reliably in production enterprise environments. The company is positioning itself as an alternative to the common pattern of hiring additional human staff to manage AI toolchains.
