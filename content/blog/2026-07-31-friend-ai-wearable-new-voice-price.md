# Friend, the Lonely AI Wearable, Returns with a New Voice and a Much Bigger Price Tag

Friend, the AI-powered wearable designed to act as a conversational companion, has relaunched with an upgraded voice system and a significantly higher price point.

The original Friend device gained attention for its unusual pitch — a wearable explicitly designed to combat loneliness through ongoing AI conversation. The new version promises more natural dialogue and faster response times, though the core proposition remains the same: an always-listening companion that chats with users throughout their day.

---

## What's New in Version Two

The most visible change is the voice system. Friend says it has rebuilt the speech synthesis pipeline from the ground up, reducing the robotic quality that characterized the first generation. The device now maintains context across longer conversations and can reference prior interactions within the same day.

Battery life has also improved, according to the company, though the wearable still requires nightly charging. The companion app has been redesigned with a cleaner interface that surfaces conversation highlights and memory fragments the AI has collected.

---

## The Price Increase

The original Friend launched at $99. The new version enters the market at $349, a substantial jump that reflects both the hardware improvements and the increased compute costs of running the updated model. A subscription tier, priced at $9.99 per month, unlocks access to longer conversation histories and advanced personalization features.

The company says the pricing reflects the actual cost of providing a genuinely useful AI companion, not just a gimmick. Whether consumers agree will be a test of the market's appetite for dedicated AI social devices.

---

## Market Context

The Wearable AI category has struggled to find a sustainable product-market fit. Previous entrants, including Rabbit and Humane's Ai Pin, failed to reach mass adoption despite significant press coverage. Friend is betting that a narrower focus — loneliness and emotional support rather than general-purpose assistance — will resonate with a specific but sizable audience.

Preorders for the new Friend device are open now, with shipping expected to begin in September 2026.
