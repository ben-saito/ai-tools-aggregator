# Harvard Law Dropout Raises $6M for Blue Voice to Build AI Legal Assistant for Police Officers

A former Harvard Law student has raised $6 million in seed funding for Blue Voice, an AI-powered platform that provides real-time legal and policy guidance to police officers in the field. The round was led by SignalFire and Las Olas VC.

---

## From Law School to AI Policing

David Lawrence was a Harvard Law student when an on-campus shooting involving a police officer sparked intense controversy over police conduct. Witnessing the incident, Lawrence recognized that most policing errors occur when officers lack instant access to department rules and protocols.

Realizing the gap, Lawrence dropped out of Harvard Law and teamed up with Amit Patankar, a Harvard MBA and former Google engineer, and Michael Gropman, a retired Boston police deputy chief, to launch Blue Voice — a Boston-based AI startup that gives officers on-the-spot access to department-specific policies and legal guidance.

Before Blue Voice, an officer who could not recall a specific protocol — such as the exact seventh step required at a crime scene — had few reliable options. They could flip through a 15,000-page manual, wake up a supervisor in the middle of the night, or search generic tools like Google or ChatGPT that lack police-specific training.

---

## The Problem with Generic AI Tools

The issue with existing tools is twofold: traditional methods consume too much time in critical situations, while consumer AI models deliver incorrect answers up to 30% of the time, according to Lawrence. This error rate is unacceptable in law enforcement contexts where a wrong answer can have serious legal consequences.

Blue Voice differentiates itself by being trained exclusively on department-specific laws, local ordinances, protocols, and guidelines that general-purpose AI systems cannot access on the public internet. The platform also provides officers with detailed school maps during active-shooter emergencies.

The company reports that its platform now answers a question every minute. Officers can rely on the tool because it always points them directly to original regulations rather than generating responses autonomously — a key trust factor when lives and legal outcomes are at stake.

---

## The AI Legal Assistant Market

Blue Voice enters a growing market for specialized AI tools tailored to professional workflows. Unlike general-purpose large language models, Blue Voice is built specifically for law enforcement use cases, with citations to source material that officers can verify in the field.

The $6 million seed round will be used to expand Blue Voice's training data coverage, integrate with more police department systems, and scale its customer base beyond Boston-area departments.

---

## Outlook

As police departments face increasing scrutiny over operational errors and legal liability, tools that help officers make better-informed decisions at the point of action are gaining attention from both law enforcement agencies and investors. Blue Voice's approach — grounded in verifiable source material rather than generative AI speculation — addresses a specific gap that generic AI assistants cannot fill.
