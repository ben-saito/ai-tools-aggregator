# US Data Centers Projected to Consume More Natural Gas Than Germany and Japan Combined by 2035

A new report from BloombergNEF projects that by 2035, US data centers will consume approximately 18 billion cubic feet of natural gas per day, surpassing the combined consumption of Germany and Japan.

---

## The Scale of Energy Demand

The AI industry boom has created unprecedented demand for power infrastructure. Data centers are now the second-strongest driver of natural gas demand growth, trailing only LNG exports in terms of projected consumption increases.

The forecast has nearly doubled from BloombergNEF's prediction just nine months ago, reflecting the rapid acceleration in AI infrastructure buildout by major tech companies.

Onsite power generation is a key driver. Meta, Microsoft, Google, and Amazon have all announced plans for natural gas power plants that will bypass the electrical grid entirely, generating power directly at data center facilities.

---

## Natural Gas Plant Announcements

Facilities such as these will consume 2.9 billion to 3.4 billion cubic feet per day by 2035, roughly equivalent to all US data center natural gas consumption today. Meta has announced a 7.5-gigawatt natural gas plant in Louisiana, while Microsoft and Google have unveiled plans for gigawatt-scale gas plants in Texas.

The shift marks a significant reversal for tech companies that had previously invested heavily in wind and solar renewable energy.

---

## Energy Security Concerns

The concentration of power generation at individual data center sites raises questions about energy security and grid resilience. Rather than drawing from diversified grid sources, hyperscalers are locking in dedicated natural gas supply arrangements.

This approach provides reliability benefits but also exposes AI operators to natural gas price volatility in ways that traditional utility contracts do not.
