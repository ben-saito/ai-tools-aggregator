# Microsoft Unveils Surface Laptop Ultra: AI-Native PCs with Nvidia RTX Spark Chips Hit the Market

---

Microsoft has officially revealed the specifications and pricing for its new Surface Laptop Ultra, the company's first **AI-native PC** designed from the ground up to run AI models and agents on Windows 11. The announcement, made at an event in San Francisco during Tech Week, marks a significant milestone in the PC industry's shift toward AI-integrated hardware.

## AI-Ready Hardware: The RTX Spark Partnership

Back in June, Nvidia announced partnerships with Microsoft and several other PC makers to develop **AI- and agent-ready PCs** based on its newly announced RTX Spark chip. Until now, detailed specifications remained scarce. The Surface Laptop Ultra changes that — delivering a machine explicitly engineered for on-device AI workloads.

The device comes in two base configurations:

- **Entry model**: Starting at **$2,600**
- **High-performance model**: Starting at **$3,700**

Buyers can configure both models with increased memory and storage, with top-tier configurations reaching approximately **$5,900**. Notably, Microsoft has already indicated that the highest-end configuration is **out of stock**, suggesting strong early demand for AI-capable hardware.

## Why AI-Native PCs Matter for Developers

Traditional PCs treat AI as a cloud-mediated experience — queries get sent to remote servers, and results return with latency and privacy tradeoffs. AI-native hardware like the Surface Laptop Ultra changes this equation fundamentally:

- **On-device model inference**: Foundation models and agents can run locally, reducing round-trip latency
- **Privacy-preserving AI**: Sensitive data never leaves the device
- **Agent-ready architecture**: The hardware is optimized for the multi-step, tool-calling patterns that define modern AI agents
- **Offline capability**: Full AI functionality without internet connectivity

## The Broader Industry Shift

Microsoft's entry into AI-native PCs reflects a broader industry movement. Both Intel and AMD have announced dedicated Neural Processing Units (NPUs) in their latest silicon, while Qualcomm has been pushing ARM-based Snapdragon X chips purpose-built for on-device AI. The question is no longer **whether** AI capabilities will be baked into consumer hardware, but **how deeply** those capabilities will be integrated.

## Pricing and Availability

The Surface Laptop Ultra is available now through Microsoft and authorized retailers. The entry-level $2,600 configuration is positioned competitively against Apple's MacBook Pro lineup, which has been emphasizing its own neural engine capabilities for on-device AI tasks.
