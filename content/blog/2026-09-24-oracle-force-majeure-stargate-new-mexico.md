# Oracle Sends Force Majeure Notice on New Mexico Stargate Data Center

Oracle has issued a force majeure notice on its New Mexico Stargate data center project, according to a TechCrunch report published September 24, 2026. The notice, sent to Blue Owl Capital, would allow Oracle to delay payments should the facility miss its 2028 target to come online.

---

## What Is a Force Majeure Clause?

Force majeure clauses, common in energy and commodities contracts, excuse a party from contractual obligations when events outside its control get in the way. Oracle is not seeking to exit the project, but is using the clause as a protective measure given ongoing construction challenges.

The notice also comes as AI data center construction faces growing scrutiny nationally, and as developers take on steep costs to build capacity for customers like OpenAI.

---

## Oracle Maintains Confidence in Timeline

Oracle maintains that it does not expect a delay. "Project Jupiter remains on our planned schedule," Oracle said in a statement to CNBC. "We are fully committed to New Mexico and confident in our path forward."

Blue Owl Capital, whose unit received the notice, said in its own statement to CNBC that "this notice does not change the financial commitments to this multi-year project."

Neither Oracle nor Blue Owl immediately responded to TechCrunch's requests for comment.

---

## Context: AI Infrastructure at a Crossroads

The force majeure filing highlights the massive capital requirements and execution risks facing AI infrastructure projects. Stargate — a joint venture involving Oracle and OpenAI — represents one of the largest AI data center builds in the United States, with targets that have proven challenging to meet amid supply chain constraints and power infrastructure limitations.
