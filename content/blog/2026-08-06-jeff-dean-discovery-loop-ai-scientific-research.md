# Jeff Dean and Top Google Researchers Launch Discovery Loop to Accelerate Scientific Discovery with AI

Jeff Dean, one of Google's most celebrated executives and a pioneer of modern AI infrastructure, is leaving Alphabet to co-found Discovery Loop, a public benefit corporation that aims to use AI to automate and accelerate the scientific research process. The founding team includes Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals — all highly influential figures in Google's AI and deep learning history.

---

## A Dream Team for AI-Driven Science

Jeff Dean joined Google in 2000 and became synonymous with the company's technical infrastructure, overseeing MapReduce, BigTable, TensorFlow, and ultimately the entire AI research organization. Sanjay Ghemawat is the engineer behind landmark systems like MapReduce, BigTable, Spanner, and Go. Quoc Le was a founding member of Google Brain and a pioneer of large-scale distributed training. Oriol Vinyals is a senior research scientist at DeepMind known for his work on sequence-to-sequence models and neural machine translation.

Discovery Loop's stated goal is to move beyond slow, sequential human iteration in science and replace it with AI systems that can initiate, conduct, and iterate thousands of experiments simultaneously. The company describes its core innovation as automating "complete experimental loops" by leveraging massive computational scale.

---

## Recursive Self-Improvement and AI-Generated AI

A notable aspect of Discovery Loop's vision is its interest in using AI to create more powerful AI — a form of recursive self-improvement that would reduce human involvement in the loop entirely. While the concept has been discussed in AI safety circles for years, Discovery Loop appears to be one of the first well-funded startups to explicitly pursue it as a commercial strategy.

The company argues that science and engineering have advanced society tremendously, but the traditional research process remains bottlenecked by slow human iteration cycles. Discovery Loop's approach seeks to remove that bottleneck through high-throughput AI experimentation.

---

## Funding from Top-Tier VCs

Discovery Loop has secured backing from Radical Ventures and Khosla Ventures as co-leads in its initial funding round, with participation from Kleiner Perkins, Lightspeed, and Doerr Capital. Alphabet is also an investor, though the amount of the round was not disclosed.

---

## Implications for AI and Scientific Research

The founding team's pedigree makes Discovery Loop one of the most closely watched AI startups in recent memory. If the company can demonstrate that AI-driven experimentation can reliably accelerate scientific discovery, it could represent a paradigm shift in how research is conducted across fields including biology, chemistry, materials science, and climate science. The involvement of researchers who built the foundational infrastructure of modern AI adds credibility to the technical claims.
