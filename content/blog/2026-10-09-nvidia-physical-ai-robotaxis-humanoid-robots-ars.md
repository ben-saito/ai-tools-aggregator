# Nvidia's Physical AI Push Targets Safer Robotaxis and Humanoid Robots

---

Nvidia is making a major push into what it calls 'physical AI' — AI systems that interact with the physical world rather than just processing text or images. At a recent event, the company demonstrated how its simulation platforms are being used by robotaxi companies to test safer autonomous driving and by robotics firms building humanoid machines. Nvidia's DRIVE Thor and Jetson Thor platforms power the vision compute for several leading physical AI programs.
