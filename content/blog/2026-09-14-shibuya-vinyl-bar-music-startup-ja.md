# A Vinyl Bar in Shibuya is a Startup Offering Fun Music Apps Without Any AI Prompting

Former Spotify executive's company raises $5.5M pre-seed to build experimental music creation tools that involve users in the creative process

---

## From Streaming Access to Active Music Creation

A new Tokyo-based startup is reimagining how people interact with music — and deliberately avoiding AI prompting as a core design principle. The company, founded by Mohanan M Zonoozy, former head of innovation at Spotify, has raised a $5.5 million pre-seed round from Mantis VC, SV Angel, Boxgroup, Quiet Capital, ERA, Common Metal, Gold House, Liquid 2 Ventures, Breakers VC, Systemic Ventures, and former Spotify executives.

Zonoozy left Spotify in 2024 with a clear thesis: while streaming solved the access problem for music, it diminished the creative interaction that made music meaningful to younger generations.

"I think when I looked at emergent behaviors and younger audiences, like everyone's growing up playing with culture through games, through filters, through creation tools," Zonoozy told TechCrunch. "And I just felt like there was something interesting about going back to that."

---

## Experimental "Singles" as Album Building Blocks

The startup releases creation tools as experimental "singles" — each one part of a larger album concept. The common thread across all apps and websites is using different interactive elements to create or modify music. Unlike AI-assisted music tools that generate tracks from text prompts, this approach puts the user directly in control of the creative parameters.

Prior to this venture, Zonoozy founded Bubbl, a video clipping and remixing startup that was acquired by Cricket Wireless. The new company represents his return to independent founding after the acquisition.

---

## The Philosophy: No AI Prompting Required

In an era where AI-assisted creation dominates music tech headlines, the company's deliberate avoidance of AI prompting stands out. The tools are designed to feel like games or creative toys rather than AI pipelines — emphasizing human agency and tactile interaction over algorithmic generation.

The Shibuya location serves as both a physical space for testing these interactive music experiences and a reference point for the company's roots in Tokyo's music and startup culture.

---

## Funding and Investor Backing

The $5.5 million pre-seed round brings together a notable mix of investors focused on consumer products and creator tools. The involvement of multiple former Spotify executives signals industry confidence in the founding team's understanding of music platform dynamics and user behavior.

---

## Implications for Music Tech

This approach represents a counter-trend in music technology. While most new music startups lean heavily on AI generation and prompt-based interfaces, this Shibuya-based team is betting that active user involvement and game-like creative interactions will resonate with audiences who grew up as digital native creators rather than passive consumers.

The strategy also sidesteps the ongoing debates around AI music training data and rights issues that have complicated launches for tools like Suno and Udio.
