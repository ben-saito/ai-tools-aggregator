# Stanford Study: AI Is Hitting Entry-Level Jobs Hardest

A new Stanford University study has found that AI is disproportionately affecting entry-level jobs, with young workers in AI-impacted fields experiencing a 19% decline in employment compared to more AI-resistant occupations.

The research adds quantitative weight to what labor economists have been observing anecdotally: the automation wave triggered by advances in generative AI is hitting newcomers hardest.

---

## Key Findings

- Youth employment in AI-exposed fields down 19% compared to resistant occupations
- The employment trends identified last year are persisting and expanding
- Entry-level positions proving more vulnerable than expected
