# Brad Lightcap, OpenAI's Longtime COO, Departing to 'Start Something New'

Brad Lightcap, OpenAI's chief operating officer and one of the San Francisco company's longest-serving executives, is leaving to "start something new," according to a report published August 11, 2026. Lightcap, who has been with OpenAI since its early days, told staff he was "excited to help you all advance the mission from a different vantage point."

---

## A Key Operator Behind OpenAI's Scale

Lightcap joined OpenAI in its founding years and helped build the operational infrastructure that supported the company's rapid growth from a research lab into one of the most valuable AI companies in the world. As COO, he oversaw the company's business operations, fundraising, and go-to-market functions as OpenAI commercialized ChatGPT and struck deals with enterprise customers and cloud partners.

His departure comes as OpenAI continues to navigate the transition from a research-focused organization to a full-scale commercial enterprise. The company has raised billions in capital and completed a reported $7 billion employee tender offer, valuing OpenAI at eye-watering multiples despite ongoing debates about AI safety and regulatory scrutiny.

---

## What Comes Next

Lightcap did not disclose the specific venture he is planning. The phrase "different vantage point" suggests he may take an investor or advisory role rather than joining another AI company directly. His departure is likely to trigger speculation about succession planning and organizational restructuring at OpenAI as the company prepares for its next chapter without its longtime operational chief.

OpenAI has not yet named a replacement for the COO position.
