# Pangram Raises $9M to Detect Both AI Text and AI Images

---

## Pangram's AI Detection Technology

TechCrunch reported that AI detection startup Pangram has raised $9 million in a seed funding round. The company simultaneously released Pangram 4, an AI text detection model, and a research preview of an image detection model.

Pangram 4 learns "AI-like" statistical patterns from large language model outputs and returns the probability of whether text was generated by AI or created by humans. Unlike traditional prompt-embedding detection, it can make judgments from output results alone.

---

## Current State of Image Detection

The research preview for images detects watermarks and statistical traces from image generation AIs such as Stable Diffusion and DALL-E. While not as advanced as text detection, AI-generated images are becoming increasingly sophisticated, making visual distinction difficult.

Pangram's CEO told TechCrunch: "We are entering an era where it is difficult to believe information," emphasizing the social importance of detection technology.

---

## AI Detection from a Developer Perspective

AI detection technology becomes important in scenarios such as:

- **Code review**: Evaluating AI-generated code from a licensing perspective
- **Content moderation**: Assessing the reliability of user submissions
- **Academia and journalism**: Disclosing AI-assisted creation

Since detection accuracy is not 100%, designing policies for how to handle "AI-generated facts" remains a challenge for developers and organizations.
