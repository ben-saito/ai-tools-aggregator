# OpenAI Reportedly Completes $7 Billion Employee Tender Offer

OpenAI has reportedly completed a $7 billion employee tender offer, TechCrunch reported on August 10, 2026. The tender offer allows employees to sell their equity stakes at a significant valuation, providing liquidity without requiring an IPO.

---

## Tender Offer Details

The $7 billion tender offer is one of the largest secondary share sales in Silicon Valley history. OpenAI, valued at over $150 billion in its most recent funding round, has been working to provide liquidity to employees and early investors through structured secondary transactions.

Secondary market transactions have become increasingly common among late-stage startups seeking to retain talent without going public. OpenAI has pursued this route as an alternative to a traditional IPO, which would require extensive public disclosure and regulatory scrutiny.

---

## Implications for AI Industry

The tender offer signals strong investor confidence in OpenAI's commercial trajectory. Unlike many AI startups still operating at losses, OpenAI has shown significant revenue growth through its ChatGPT subscriptions, API licensing, and enterprise partnerships.

The completion of the tender offer also positions OpenAI favorably for potential future IPO scenarios, as employee equity becomes more evenly distributed and understood by institutional investors.

---

## Background

OpenAI has been at the center of AI industry growth since the launch of ChatGPT in late 2022. The company has raised billions in primary funding rounds while simultaneously offering secondary liquidity events to early employees and investors seeking to monetize their stakes.
