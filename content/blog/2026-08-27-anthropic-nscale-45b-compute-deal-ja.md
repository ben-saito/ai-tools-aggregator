# Anthropic Nscale Deal Continues Compute-Gobbling Streak

Anthropic signed a multi-year contract with infrastructure provider Nscale on August 26, in a deal reported by Bloomberg to be worth $45 billion over six years. Nscale, founded only in 2024, will supply Anthropic with compute from its flagship data center in West Virginia, using NVIDIA's next-generation Vera Rubin chip system.

The Vera Rubin system combines six different chips working in concert and is considered cutting-edge in chip design. Compute capacity is expected to power Anthropic's services starting in late 2027.

This deal is the latest in Anthropic's aggressive compute capacity buildup over the past eight months. Earlier this month, Anthropic signed a $10 billion deal with AI cloud startup Volta, securing six years of cloud compute from a data center in Norway. In July, it signed a $5 billion compute-related deal with AMD.

In May, Anthropic revealed a large computing deal with SpaceX, drawing capacity from two different SpaceX data centers reportedly providing $1.25 billion worth of capacity each month. In April, Anthropic expanded its partnership with Amazon, gaining access to an additional 5 gigawatts of compute, and also expanded its relationship with Google and Broadcom.

The race to gobble up compute capacity is ongoing, with Google, OpenAI, and Meta all following similar strategies. Nscale has already cut deals with Microsoft and other major players.

---

## Background: The Compute Arms Race

Anthropic's heavy investment in compute reflects intensifying competition with OpenAI and other frontier labs. Securing compute capacity for foundation model training has become a critical differentiator in the AI race.

NVIDIA's Vera Rubin architecture is notable for its coordinated multi-chip design, engineered to efficiently handle large language model training and inference.

---

## Industry Impact

Anthropic's compute-buying spree creates challenges for smaller AI labs. As compute access becomes a key differentiator, the gap between resource-constrained companies and well-funded frontier labs may widen.

The emergence of new infrastructure providers like Nscale is significant. A company founded in 2024 landing contracts with Microsoft and Anthropic signals intensifying competition at the infrastructure layer.
