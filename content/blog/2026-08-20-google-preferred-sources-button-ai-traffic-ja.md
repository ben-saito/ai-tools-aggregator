# Google Gives Publishers New "Preferred Sources" Button to Combat AI-Driven Traffic Losses

Google on August 20, 2026 announced a new "Preferred Sources" button that allows readers to designate their favorite publishers across Google Search, Discover, and Google News. The move aims to help publishers combat declining web traffic caused by AI search features like AI Overviews and AI Mode.

As AI-powered search increasingly surfaces answers directly rather than linking to external websites, publishers have seen significant drops in referral traffic. Google's new feature lets readers explicitly select preferred sources, potentially boosting visibility for those publishers in AI-generated results.

The Preferred Sources feature was first introduced in May 2026 for Google's AI experiences. According to Google, over 345,000 unique sources had already been selected by users across the web as of July 2026.

Publishers can embed the new button on their own websites, making it easier for readers to designate them as a preferred source. However, it remains uncertain whether this will meaningfully offset the traffic losses from AI search, and the impact varies significantly by publisher.

With AI services increasingly consuming content without attribution, publishers face existential pressure on their advertising revenue models. The Preferred Sources button represents Google's attempt to balance AI functionality with publisher sustainability, though its long-term effectiveness remains to be seen.
