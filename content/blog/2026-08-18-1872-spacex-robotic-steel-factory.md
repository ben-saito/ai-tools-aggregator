# Former SpaceX Engineers Launch 1872, Building Robotic Factory for Steel Parts

A startup founded by former SpaceX engineers called 1872 officially launched on July 22, 2026, opening a robotic factory in Cincinnati, Ohio to automate manufacturing of steel parts for AI data centers and small modular nuclear reactors.

---

## Addressing Skilled Labor Shortages

The push for automation is driven by a growing shortage of skilled labor in the US manufacturing sector. The American Welding Society estimates the country will need 320,500 new welding professionals by 2029 due to retirements and surging demand from AI data center construction.

CEO Dan Summers, who led the team integrating and fabricating Raptor engines for SpaceX's Starship Super Heavy booster, said: "We may achieve 80% autonomous operations, and we may decide that it makes sense to stop there because there's just diminishing returns to go to full 100%."

---

## SpaceX Heritage

1872's three cofounders — Summers, Brian Mongilio, and Michael Grant — all previously worked at SpaceX. The Raptor team's experience with "intelligent software" for tracking and manufacturing gave them the foundation to apply similar approaches to steel fabrication.

The company is initially focused on automating the production of steel skids — rectangular steel frames that serve as movable foundations for modular buildings — targeting customers building AI data centers and small modular nuclear reactors.
