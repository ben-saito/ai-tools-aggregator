# Instinct AI's Product Recommendations Trigger User Backlash

Instinct AI, the buzzy AI agent startup that recently raised a $1 billion Series C at a $10 billion valuation, has launched a new product recommendation feature called Instinct Selections — and users are not responding positively.

---

## The Feature

Instinct Selections, announced September 30, curates personalized product and travel recommendations by partnering with human experts — "local chefs, designers, architects, travel guides" — rather than relying solely on AI-generated suggestions from web training data.

Founder Noah Shinn described the vision on X: "The next time you're looking for a restaurant, Instinct can pull from a list handpicked by chefs who know the hidden gems in your area." The feature extends to shopping and travel recommendations, with human taste as the differentiating factor.

The company did not disclose the identities of its human partners.

---

## User Backlash

The rollout triggered negative reactions from users who received unsolicited product suggestions they had not requested. Instinct's AI agent began pushing recommendations to users proactively, without a prior conversation or search context.

The backlash highlights the tension between AI platform monetization strategies and user experience expectations. Users accustomed to requesting recommendations on demand are apparently resistant to receiving them unprompted.

---

## Revenue Model Pivot

The feature represents Instinct's first explicit move toward a revenue model beyond the core AI agent subscription. By introducing affiliate-style recommendations with human curation, Instinct is experimenting with a discovery commerce approach common in consumer apps but not yet widespread among AI agent platforms.

The company has not disclosed terms of its commercial relationships with recommended brands or partners.

---

## Relationship to Existing Platform

The announcement comes shortly after Instinct disclosed that over 50% of transactions on its platform are now travel-related, suggesting the company is building toward a broader lifestyle platform rather than remaining a pure-play AI agent developer.
