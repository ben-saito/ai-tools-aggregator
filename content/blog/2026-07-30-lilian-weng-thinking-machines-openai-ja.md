# Lilian Weng Leaves Thinking Machines, Returns to OpenAI

Lilian Weng, co-founder of the AI safety-focused startup Thinking Machines, has left the company and rejoined OpenAI. The move comes just months after her departure from OpenAI, where she previously served as VP of AI Safety Research.

---

## From OpenAI to Thinking Machines and Back

Weng's career trajectory has drawn significant attention in the AI safety community. She left OpenAI earlier in 2026 to co-found Thinking Machines, a startup dedicated to building safe and beneficial AI systems. However, her return to OpenAI after a brief stint at her own company marks an unusual career reversal in the competitive AI safety field.

According to reports, Weng cited health reasons for her initial departure from Thinking Machines before accepting the role at OpenAI.

---

## Implications for AI Safety Research

Weng's return to OpenAI underscores the intense competition for top AI safety talent. Both Anthropic and OpenAI have been aggressively recruiting researchers with deep expertise in AI alignment and safety, reflecting industry-wide recognition that safe AI development is critical as capabilities advance.
