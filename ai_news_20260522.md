**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19192  
**Date:** May 18, 2026  
**Authors:** Zhang, Guijia; Zheng, Hao; Yang, Harry  

Researchers formalize "hallucination-to-action conversion" — when false visual claims trigger unauthorized privileged actions. They propose Evidence-Carrying Multimodal Agents (ECA), which require typed certificates from constrained verifiers before granting tool access. Results show 0% unsafe-action rate on 200-task pipeline (vs 100% for naive agents).

### 3. Uncertainty and Exploration: Volatility vs Stochasticity
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19215  
**Date:** May 19, 2026  
**Authors:** Piray, Payam  

New research reveals that volatility and stochasticity — both increasing uncertainty — drive optimal exploration in opposite directions. The paper introduces CAUSE (Cause-Aware Uncertainty-Sensitive Exploration), showing volatility enhances exploration while stochasticity suppresses it, with implications for computational psychiatry.
